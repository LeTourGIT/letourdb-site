04

Session Summary

what moved and why

Monday extended the Nasdaq 100's run to five straight gains and back-to-back record closes (31,076.44, +0.87%), while the S&P 500 (+0.66% to 7,773.95) finished 0.32% short of its 13/08/2026 record close of 7,798.99. What the rally is ignoring matters more than what it is celebrating: the 10-year Treasury yield printed a fresh 52-week high at 5.315% and the 30-year 5.670%, pushing the 2s10s spread to roughly 49bps. This is a bear steepener. The front end is anchored by Friday's +29,000 payrolls print, which pulled October Fed-hike odds lower; the long end is repricing term premium, and ISM services prices paid at 74.0 (highest since July 2022) gave it no reason to stop. Equities are trading the Fed. Bonds are trading supply and inflation. Both cannot stay right.

The regime table says the same thing in price. SPX and NDX are Bullish with 5day and 20day trends Up; the Dow and Russell 2000 are still Bearish, both under their 50MA (DJI -2.79%, RUT -3.32%) and 5.67% and 7.21% below their August closing highs. Over 20 days XLK is +7.29% and every other SPDR sector is negative — financials -7.26%, utilities -7.22%, real estate -7.42%. Levered balance sheets and long-duration cash flows are being marked to a 5.3% ten-year; mega-cap AI, which funds capex from cash flow, is not. That is the whole explanation for IWF +4.00% against RSP -3.60% and RPV -5.23% over the same 20 days.

Geopolitics set the day's largest stock moves. Flavio Bolsonaro's first-round lead in Brazil (47% to Lula's 45%, against polls that had Lula ahead) triggered a wholesale re-rating of Brazilian ADRs: XP +30.93%, STNE +20.84%, NU +13.03%, PBR +11.50%, SID +6.90%. In the Gulf, the Strait of Hormuz remains shut and Saudi Aramco's CEO warned that global stockpiles are "scarily thin" and may need two years to rebuild after any reopening. Crude itself eased (WTI -0.72% to $89.27, Brent -1.08% to $100.26), yet OIH +3.14% topped the ETF board and Oil & Gas Drilling vaulted from rank 115 to rank 36 in the Smart Money table in one session. Equity investors are paying for the duration of the rebuild cycle, not the spot price.

M&A did the rest. Schneider Electric's $22.6B all-cash bid sent PTC +33.49% on $5.68B turnover and repriced the design-software shelf: BSY +7.82%, TEAM +4.66%, ADSK +4.51%, with IGV +1.19% (5day +4.07%). A strategic buyer paying cash with the long bond at a 52-week high tells you where private-market value sits for a group whose ETF is still -5.40% over 1year. C.H. Robinson's sale of Global Forwarding to RXO (implied $5.8B) cut the other way — CHRW -10.85%, the day's worst large-cap, while the buyer RXO rose +22.54%: the market thinks the seller gave up the growth asset. After the close Option Care Health jumped +21.41% (⚠ PRELIMINARY) on reported McKesson / CD&R takeover talks. Three deals in one session: corporates are not waiting for cheaper money.

Healthcare was two tapes in one sector. Vaxcyte's clean Phase 3 OPUS-1 readout for VAX-31 sent PCVX +30.70% and took MRK -3.30% and PFE -1.40% with it as the market priced share loss in the pneumococcal franchise; IHE (5day -4.82%) and PPH (5day -3.47%) extended their slide. Against that, HLTH +3.01% made the +3% board, TEM +9.03%, ILMN +7.56%, MRNA +6.95%, TMO +3.35% — and 15 of the RS Top 20 are now healthcare names. Yet Biotechnology & Medical Research ranks 113 of 139 on 10day industry ROC. Leadership sits in genomics tools and catalyst-driven single names, not in the group.

Breadth improved, but from a hole. SPX advancers beat decliners 315 to 183 and the share of members above the 20MA jumped to 34.73% from 26.35%; T2108 has lifted from 19.00% on 30/09/2026 to 27.15%. Still, only 26.75% of the S&P 500 is above its 50MA with the index a third of a percent from a record, and new 20day lows (77) outnumber new 20day highs (40). Either breadth repairs upward to meet the index — the 30/09 washout argues it can — or the index keeps being carried by a list that shrinks. Wednesday's Fed minutes and the first Q3 reports (PEP, DAL, STZ, LEVI) are the tests.
