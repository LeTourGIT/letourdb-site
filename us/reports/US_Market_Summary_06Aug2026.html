04

Session Summary

Thursday was a two-front rotation session dressed up as a quiet down day. Headline losses were small, but underneath, two structural repricings ran in opposite directions: high-multiple software was violently de-rated, while capital surged into hard assets, energy and financials on a fresh Hormuz escalation. VIX fell 4.2% — the signature of orderly rotation, not fear.

The software story is the inverse of this cycle's earlier enterprise-software surge. This time even clean beats were punished. Datadog (-19.0%) beat and raised full-year guidance yet collapsed after flagging that its largest AI client expects lighter second-half usage — the market read it as direct evidence of AI cannibalizing consumption-based SaaS. HubSpot (-19.1%) cut its outlook into a wall of downgrades; Figma (-14.9%) beat but froze operating income as AI inference costs eat margin; AppLovin (-19.7%) posted its first guidance miss since IPO. The common thread: AI has flipped from a software tailwind to a simultaneous margin cost and demand threat. IGV -1.87% confirms it was sector-wide.

The second front is geopolitical. Iran's parliament published a draft plan to ban US and Israeli ships from the Strait of Hormuz, impose tolls up to 7% of cargo value and fine violators 20% — Washington rejected it outright, demanding unimpeded transit. Crude bid immediately (WTI +2.74% to $78.88, OIH +1.82%), Energy was the only green SPDR sector, and the oil→inflation impulse pushed Treasury yields up, de-rating rate proxies (homebuilders ITB -2.39%, utilities, REITs) and hitting airlines (JETS -2.65%) on fuel cost. This is the mirror image of the peace-signal oil selloffs earlier this cycle — the Hormuz premium is being repriced in, not out.

Where did the money go? Into hard assets. Smart Money rotation shows a clean precious-metals and mining cluster surging — Gold to rank 5, Precious Metals rank 6, Specialty Mining rank 13, Integrated Mining rank 15, Mining Support rank 2 — with the physical complex confirming (gold +0.81%, silver +0.98%, zinc +3.21%, copper +2.03%). Fintech stayed entrenched at rank 1 (Chime +20.6%), insurance led financials (MetLife +3.8%, Allstate +4.0%), and quietly, biotech/pharma is re-rating (Insmed +33.9%; seven biotech names in the RS top 20).

The setup into Friday: a genuine theme rotation with the index barely moving. The NDX's Bearish downgrade is the flag to watch — if software and memory keep bleeding, the Nasdaq's regime crack widens; if the after-hours software bounce (Atlassian +33%, Cloudflare +16%, Doximity +73%) holds, Thursday reads as a guidance-specific purge rather than a sector top. Hormuz headlines remain the wildcard.
