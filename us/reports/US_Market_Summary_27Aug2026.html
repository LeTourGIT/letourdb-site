Session Summary

Thursday was a mega-cap engine running against a stalled chassis. The S&P closed +0.72% and the Nasdaq 100 +1.43%, but decliners beat advancers 347 to 151 — one of the widest gaps between index direction and internal breadth of the cycle. The advance was manufactured by a handful of software and semiconductor earnings names; beneath them, the average stock fell.

The catalyst chain is clean. NVIDIA's beat ($68B turnover, +8.74%) removed the "AI capex isn't paying off" overhang and short-covered the infrastructure layer. Simultaneously Salesforce (+22.58%) and CrowdStrike (+20.50%) detonated the "AI disrupts software" thesis. The result: IGV +7.74%, CIBR +7.61% and XLK +3.16% were the only three ETFs above +3%, and XLK was the only green sector — every other sector closed red between -0.2% and -1.4%.

That configuration is textbook funding rotation. Capital was sold out of defensives and retail to buy the AI-software complex: Staples -1.38%, Healthcare -1.13%, Discretionary -1.09% led the downside while retail was wrecked (BURL -7.64%, BBY -4.44%, DLTR -3.92%). The losers list is the mirror image of the gainers — nothing was added to the market, it was rearranged into technology.

The geopolitical cross-current is a Hormuz re-escalation — the mirror of this year's peace-proposal cycle. A tanker attack, Iranian ship-seizure warnings ahead of Bessent's sanctions push, and soaring shipping rates pushed WTI +2.15% to $83.60. Energy the sector barely moved (XLE -0.22%), but Smart Money tells the truer story: shipping and marine industries have surged to the very top of institutional flow (ranks 1, 2, 16). The freight-rate trade is showing in capital rotation before ETF prices.

Overlaying everything is Jackson Hole. Chair Kevin Warsh keynotes Friday with Treasury's Bessent intervening in the bond market — "complicating things" and leaving the dollar and long end "on edge." The market's answer was to crush volatility (VIX -4.60% to 14.51) and lean into the winners. That is a complacent posture into a binary macro event with breadth already this thin.
