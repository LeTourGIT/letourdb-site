04Session summary

Monday was a rates shock with a geopolitical trigger. Trump rejected Iran's offer to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days, crude spiked and the oil-to-inflation loop fired straight into Treasuries: the 10yr printed 5.24% (highest since 2007) and the 30yr 5.56% (highest since 2004), with October hike odds near 70% barely a week after the Fed's first hike in three years. Losses were pared from roughly -1% at midday on reports the White House is open to nuclear sanctions relief — the index lows were bought, the rate path was not.

The transmission map is the key read. Damage concentrated wherever duration lives: utilities (XLU -0.66%, 20day -8.14%), REITs (XLRE -0.51%, VNQ 5day -3.43%), mortgage REITs (REM 20day -14.19%), small caps (RUT RSI-15 33.07) and non-yielding metals (SLV -5.49%, GLD -3.94%). The only green SPDRs were XLV, XLP and XLE — non-rate defensives plus the oil hedge. When bond proxies fall alongside growth it is a discount-rate problem, not a growth scare.

AI split in two on the same headline. OpenAI's second training pause in three months hit the physical supply chain — QCOM -7.17%, CRDO -8.67%, INTC -5.67%, MOD -11.63% (thermal), BE -8.95% (power), SOXX -2.08%. Cyber caught the other side: PANW +4.63%, OKTA +3.58%, ZS +3.28%, CRWD +2.82%, and CIBR was the day's best ETF (+0.71%). Agents escaping sandboxes is a security budget line item. NVDA +1.68% on a $150B buyback extension held the index together — capital return propping the leader while its suppliers were sold.

Software now carries a new risk premium. MDB -18.46% lost its CEO to Meta's new enterprise platform, and the platform itself repriced workflow incumbents (TEAM -4.90%). IGV only -0.55% says this is selective, not yet a sector de-rating.

⚠ Structural breadth divergence: SPX Adv/Dec 162/334 (1:2.06) with only 24.6% of members above their 50MA while the index itself sits +0.56% above its own. T2108 24.0%, 128 new 20day lows vs 22 highs. RSP -0.65% beat IVV -0.76% — for once the decline was megacap-led, stripping the cover that has hidden the broad correction for weeks.

Setup: VIX at 16.07 is not pricing the rate tail. T2108 near 24% on SPX and RUT is washed-out territory where reflex rallies start, but rallies inside rising yields have been sold all month. PCE, Micron and Friday's payrolls decide whether the 10yr breaks 5.30%.
