Session Summary

Macro → sector → stock — the chain that moved the tape.

Monday's damage was invisible at the index level and total beneath it. Iran hardened its stance on the Strait of Hormuz — denying direct US talks and setting conditions for reopening — and the market repriced the entire energy complex in one session: WTI +4.40% to $82.28, Brent +4.04%, USO +7.16%. It is the exact mirror of the Iran peace-signal sessions earlier in the cycle — the same Hormuz variable, now running the other way. With the US SPR below 300 million barrels (lowest since 1983), the release valve that capped prior spikes is gone.

The rotation was not confined to oil. The dominant institutional story is a wholesale move into hard assets. Silver ripped +3.32% (SLV 5day +13.25%), gold +1.39%, and the Smart Money board is now almost entirely mining and metals: Mining Support Services vaulted to rank 1 from rank 134 nine days ago, with Precious Metals, Gold, Specialty Mining, Integrated Mining, Uranium and Aluminum filling the top nine. The 10day hot-sector leaders confirm it — Alcoa, Century Aluminum, Kaiser Aluminum, Endeavour Silver, USA Rare Earth. MP Materials +6.95% on the DoD's rare-earth investment.

The funding source was the semiconductor and optical complex. "Chip stock rebound fails" showed up in the tape: XSD −3.54% and JETS −3.38% were the only two ETFs below −3%. Coherent cratered −14.24%, Lumentum −8.61%, Ciena −5.99%, Corning −4.78% — the entire AI-optical interconnect layer sold; Astera Labs −5.07% and Marvell −4.65% carried the semis. That is what flipped NDX to Bearish. Crucially it was not all tech: software and security held up (DDOG +11.48%, RBRK +8.70%, PANW +5.82%, CRWD +5.01%). Two rotations stacked on one flat tape — within tech toward software, and out of tech into materials.

Rate-sensitives were collateral damage: Utilities −1.10% and Real Estate −1.29% were the worst SPDRs, tracking a "higher for longer" narrative and a 10year yield rising into this week's CPI. Nvidia's $500B financing push with Wall Street asset managers is the one megacap AI headline holding the structural bull thesis together — but it did not stop the semi tape from breaking. Breadth ratified the caution: SPX advancers/decliners 228/269, and Russell 2000 a brutal 627/1296. When the broadest index is 2:1 negative, the "flat day" framing collapses — this was distribution dressed as a quiet session.
