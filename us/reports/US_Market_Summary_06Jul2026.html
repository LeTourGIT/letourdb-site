Session Summary

Monday was a chip-led relief rally that reversed the prior week's AI-complex unwind. CNBC's session headline said it plainly — the S&P closed higher as chips staged a comeback while Iran halted strikes on Israel. The names at the epicentre of the drawdown were the epicentre of the recovery: AMD +6.61%, QCOM +5.80%, ANET +8.31%, CRDO +9.77%, WDC +7.14%, ALAB +6.48%, DELL +4.43%, NTAP +6.08%. A V-shaped snapback in the exact leaders that led the sell-off — classic wash-out mechanics, not fresh accumulation.

Macro de-risked in tandem. OPEC+ approved a further output increase just as Hormuz exports recover, and Brent has posted its biggest monthly decline since 2020 — WTI sits at $68.68, a world away from spring's $100+ war premium. Falling oil + a Fed-pause narrative (weak jobs, FOMC minutes on deck) is the goldilocks cocktail risk assets want. VIX collapsed -3.59% to 15.57. That is why energy (XLE -0.17%) sat out the rally while every growth-cyclical sector caught a bid.

The risk-on signal was loudest in the highest-beta corners. Crypto ripped — ETHA +5.37%, IBIT +3.58%, Ether ETFs the best-performing group (+13.9% over 5day). Miners followed (IREN +13.11%, BMNR +8.29%); power/electrification joined (Bloom Energy +8.92%, FCEL +5.76%, VRT +5.97%); autos ran (RIVN +8.11%, TSLA +6.69%, CARZ +3.38%). When crypto, chips, power and autos all lead one session, that is beta being re-embraced wholesale.

The laggards tell the rotation story. The defensive complex was sold to fund the beta chase — Staples XLP -1.05%, Healthcare XLV -1.09%, Utilities XLU -1.01%, Real Estate XLRE -0.87% all red on a green tape. Auto-parts retailers were hammered on an idiosyncratic thread (ORLY -6.66%, AZO -6.38%), while space/sat names (RKLB -7.34%, ASTS -5.27%) and Solstice (SOLS -15.14%) took stock-specific hits. Microsoft's 4,800-job cut signals megacap cost discipline arriving even as AI hardware re-rates.

Breadth was healthy but not euphoric. SPX advancers trailed decliners 219 to 278 — a green index on 2:3 negative breadth, the cap-weight fingerprint of a few chips doing the lifting. But deeper internals are constructive: T2108 67.6%, 67.2% of SPX above both 20MA and 50MA, 98 new 20day highs vs 17 lows. Russell breadth near-even (991/982) with 347 new highs — small-caps quietly broadening. A durable base-building bounce, provided chip leadership doesn't fade into FOMC minutes.
