Session Summary

Wednesday delivered the most geopolitically significant session of the 2026 bull cycle — a genuine regime-change day, not a routine ATH extension. The Axios pre-market report of a 14-point US-Iran memorandum of understanding closing in sent simultaneous signals in two opposite directions: oil collapsed (USO -7.09%, XOP -6.24%) while 9 of 11 SPDR sectors rallied and 15+ ETFs exceeded +3% gains. This is not a contradiction. It is capital reallocation happening in real time — out of oil-war-premium and into the post-Hormuz infrastructure rebuild trade.

The semiconductor earnings double sealed the session's narrative. AMD's Q1 print (+38% revenue, data centre +57% YoY) landed at the open, and ARM's AH report (+9.5% on record $1.49B quarterly revenue, data centre royalties doubling) closed the day. Architecture licensing (ARM) and compute silicon (AMD) confirming in the same earnings window is the AI stack validation event. Add FIVN's software AI print from Friday, GLW's physical optical infrastructure deal with NVDA from Wednesday's session, and the Pentagon's AI spending confirmation from last week — and every layer of the AI value chain has been validated in a single earnings cycle.

Corning's NVDA partnership (+17%, three dedicated US optical factories, 10x capacity expansion) is the session's strategic sleeper. This is not a glass company story — it is the physical AI data centre infrastructure above the chip layer being explicitly built on US soil. The 60day Communications & Networking sector leader (+36%) has been accumulating for two months; now it has a commercial contract confirmation. VIAV, CIEN, and the optical interconnect cohort re-rate from here.

The energy collapse (-4.12% XLE, -6.24% XOP, -7.09% USO) is the Iran MOU trade unfolding at maximum velocity. Goldman's 4-6 month normalisation estimate remains the base case for actual Hormuz reopening — but the market is pricing the probability shift, not the outcome. Five energy sub-themes dominated Smart Money's top 16 through last week; those positions are now unwinding in orderly fashion into copper (+6.85%), uranium (+7.49%), construction (+10.95% STRL), and paper packaging (Smart Money surge from #74 to #6 in one day). The rotation is supply-chain-logical: if Hormuz opens, global shipping normalises, packaging pricing power restores, and the electrification infrastructure buildout accelerates as fossil fuel risk premium fades.

SPX breadth improved materially: 316 advancers vs 183 decliners (1.7:1 positive) versus Friday's 172/326 (2:1 negative). T2108 at 57.6%, 50MA participation at 54.8%. The risk concentration sits in NDX RSI-15 at 78.89 — the deepest overbought reading of this entire cycle — and FOMC Thursday adds a binary event to a market at technical extremes.
