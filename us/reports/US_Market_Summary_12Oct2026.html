04Session Summary

Monday was a rotation day dressed up as a flat one. SPX finished -0.06% at 7,807.24, but beneath that print sat a 1.6-point gap between the Dow (+0.38%) and the Nasdaq 100 (-0.71%), and a 4.6-point gap between energy exploration (XOP +1.45%) and semiconductors (XSD -3.55%). Capital didn't leave the market — it walked out of AI hardware and into oil, defensives and value.

The catalyst chain started in Asia. China's CXMT announced mass production of its fifth-generation DRAM platform with DDR5 server memory slated for year-end, while reports that Apple asked suppliers to cut iPhone component orders on soaring memory costs hit the demand side at the same time. Memory names led the slide — MU -3.88% on $23.3B turnover (the day's heaviest tape), SNDK -5.97% on $12.9B, WDC -2.60% — and the selling cascaded through the entire AI-infrastructure stack: optical (LITE -9.78%, CIEN -9.95%, COHR -7.87%, AAOI -8.17%, GLW -6.00%), connectivity silicon (SMTC -10.15%, CRDO -7.25%, ALAB -5.29%) and power (MPWR -4.84%, BE -6.50%). Eleven of the 15 biggest $500M+ turnover losers were AI-hardware names. This is crowded-position de-grossing, not a fundamental break — but Smart Money confirms it is institutional: Semiconductors slid from rank 13 to 109 over three sessions (87 → 109 today), Semi Equipment & Testing sits at 73 (rank 2 nine days ago), Computer Hardware at 98.

Geopolitics supplied the second leg. Houthi drone and missile attacks on Saudi Arabia continued after the Riyadh airport strike that killed 12, Trump said he is considering joining Saudi Arabia against the Houthis, and Iran is still reviewing the US Hormuz proposal. WTI +3.20% to $93.22, Brent $106.04. The 'energy ceasefire' in Ukraine is already disputed. Energy (XLE +1.43%) was the best SPDR sector and Smart Money has four energy sub-industries in the top 16, with Oil & Gas Drilling surging from rank 134 nine days ago to 11.

The receiving side of the rotation was textbook late-cycle defensive: staples (XLP +1.17%), medtech (IHI +2.03%, MDT +3.90%, BSX +2.60%, ABT +2.23%), insurance (IAK +1.06%), utilities and REITs. Software was the notable exception within tech — CRM +2.09% on BofA's double upgrade (Underperform → Buy, PT $160 → $285), ACN +2.78%, DT +2.76%, META +3.38% on its Muse Wonder AI model. The market is re-pricing AI from 'buy the picks and shovels' to 'buy the platforms that monetise it' — the AI-disruption discount on software is unwinding while the AI-capex premium on hardware compresses.

Breadth tells you this was rotation, not liquidation: SPX advancers beat decliners 318 to 179 (1.78:1) on a down close — a positive divergence where cap-weight tech dragged the index while the median stock rose. But the structural picture is weak: only 36.8% of SPX names sit above their 50MA and T2108 is 39.2% while the index is 1.33% above its own 50MA. Generals are holding the line; the army is still regrouping. RUT is worse — 132 stocks down 4%+ vs 29 up, 304 new 20day lows vs 146 highs.

Setup into the week: bank earnings Tuesday, CPI Wednesday, TSM Thursday. With the 10year at 5.24%, a hot energy-led CPI is the clear tail risk; TSM is the referendum on whether today's AI-hardware unwind is a positioning flush or the start of a capex-cycle re-rating.
