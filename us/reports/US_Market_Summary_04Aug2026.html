📝 Session Summary

The defining event was geopolitical: the Strait of Hormuz reopening. WTI crashed −7.24% to $75.31 and Brent −7.24% to $78.88 as OPEC+ signalled it would step up supply and the White House stepped back from escalation. That single de-risking event collapsed the oil-war risk premium embedded in equities for weeks — and the index response was violent and broad: Dow +900 points, SPX above 7,700 for the first time, NDX +3.32%. This is a risk-premium repricing, not an earnings-driven melt-up.

Semiconductors were the transmission mechanism. SOXX +6.80% and XSD +6.14% led, with XLK +4.98%, QTEC +5.32% and IGV +4.70% behind — 17 ETFs cleared +3% and not one breached −3%. Micron memory-share gains and SK Hynix earnings drove INTC +10.84%, MRVL +12.81%, ALAB +12.65% and SNDK +10.84%. AMD is the tell: revenue +50% with data-center sales doubling, yet the stock FELL — the semis bar is now priced for perfection, and when beats stop being enough, leadership narrows from here.

Palantir was the signature name at +29.45%, fusing government and commercial AI acceleration; paired with Leidos +10.01%, the government-AI-spend leg now confirms alongside the infrastructure leg. Wayfair +29.97% and Zebra +26.47% extend the beat-and-rip regime into consumer-discretionary and enterprise hardware — earnings strength consistent with the broad tape.

Breadth confirms rather than diverges — the critical distinction from prior narrow ATH prints. SPX advancers led decliners 356/141 (2.5:1), RUT 1441/476 (3:1), DJI 25/5, NDX 83/19. T2108 at 67.0% and % above 50MA at 67.4% put structural participation firmly in healthy territory. When the tape gaps up AND breadth expands AND small-caps lead (RUT +1.85%, 300 stocks up >4%), the move has internal support.

The rotation is unambiguously risk-on and cyclical. Smart Money surged into Mining Support Services (+112 ranks), Home Furnishings (+105/+103), Software (+74) and Pension Funds (+76), while Construction & Engineering exited to #12 from #1 three days ago — Sterling Infra −11.42% is the stock-level proof. Utilities funded the move (NRG −15.48%, VST −8.15%) as the AI-power trade unwound back into core semis. One caution flag: Michael Burry publicly called "a major top, possibly a 1987-type fall" — sentiment worth logging as the tape gaps into resistance on a still-Bearish regime.
