📝 Session Summary

Thursday was the relief rebound after Wednesday's hawkish Warsh-Fed sell-off, and the tape split along a single fault line: the silicon layer ripped while the human-services layer broke. Semiconductors fuelled the entire comeback — XSD +7.26% and SOXX +6.62% (both EXTREME >5%) topped nine ETFs above +3% against zero below −3%. INTC +10.64% on reports Apple agreed to build chips with Intel in the US; SNDK +11.54%, MU +8.70%, ALAB +11.31%, GLW +11.13%, KLAC +8.73% and ENTG +13.62% completed a near-pure chip sweep.

The mirror image was Accenture −17.97% — its worst session on record — after cutting FY26 revenue-growth guidance to 3–4%, missing on revenue and printing Q3 bookings down ~2% YoY. The read-through hammered the IT-services complex: CTSH −10.49%, IBM −5.05%, Gartner (IT) −4.56%, and even the #1 RS name RXT −4.12%. With Cognizant exiting the Nasdaq-100 on 22 June, the market is repricing labour-arbitrage consulting as the loser of the AI build-out — paying for picks-and-shovels, not headcount.

Overhead, two macro locks turned at once. The US and Iran signed an interim peace deal, ending the 3-month war, reopening the Strait of Hormuz and waiving sanctions on Iranian oil. WTI fell to a 3.5-month low at $75.46 — prewar pricing — and the Navy ended its blockade. That peace dividend is the second engine under the rotation: Smart Money's #1 industry is Marine Port Services, with Airlines surging #32→#14 and Hotels #21→#11 on cheaper fuel and reopened trade. Defense was the casualty — NOC −5.21%, LHX −5.86%.

Against that, new Chair Kevin Warsh delivered a hawkish FOMC debut: rates held at 3.50–3.75% but 9 of 19 officials now pencil a 2026 hike to quell energy-driven inflation, and the easing bias was stripped. That is why gold fell −2.55% and silver −5.04% even as the geopolitical premium faded — higher-for-longer real rates, not just peace, are pressuring metals. Breadth confirmed the rebound was real: SPX Adv/Dec 262/237 positive, RUT a powerful 1403/460; only DJI was negative (14/16). No cap-weight illusion — a broad, small-cap-led bounce.
