04Session Summary

Black Friday's half-session (1pm ET close) delivered SPX +1.60% and NDX +2.60%, capping a +5.6% week. That is the S&P's best since April 2025 and the Dow's best since October 2022. Jensen Huang's reinstatement at Nvidia sparked it on Monday (NVDA +31%); the market gave half back midweek and resumed on Friday. The critical detail: yields rose 8-9bp across the curve while stocks rose. The 10yr is near 4.94% and a 25bp hike on 09/12 is ~99% priced. Equities rallied against tighter financial conditions, which is short-covering after the 20/11 crash, not a liquidity tailwind.

Leadership came from AI hardware's most-sold names: memory/storage (SNDK +15.96%, MU +7.60%, WDC +7.22%), optical (COHR +14.07%, LITE +7.98%), connectivity (ALAB, CRDO, MRVL, ANET) and servers (DELL). XSD rose +5.04% and SOXX +4.76%. But the Excel shows what this is not. Semiconductors rank 124 of 137 in Smart Money (10day ROC -13.73%) and Semi Equipment ranks 123. Not one semiconductor sits in the RS Top 20, and 14 of 15 top gainers still carry negative 10day returns. This is a reflex bounce from the bottom of the rotation board. Re-accumulation is only confirmed when semis climb that board.

Breadth is the genuinely constructive signal. SPX printed 405/94 (4.3:1), NDX 93/7 and RUT 1,642/268, the third ≥2.9:1 session in four. SPX T2108 has gone from 39.0% (20/11) to 62.8%, +23.8 points in four sessions. The share above the 50MA went from 42.8% to 63.4%, and new 20day lows collapsed from 173 to 2. That is a breadth-thrust profile, and thrusts mark durable lows more reliably than price does.

The structural tell is equal-weight vs cap-weight: RSP 20day -0.75% vs IVV -5.31%. The "S&P correction" is a cap-weighted megacap-AI event, with 63.4% of members above their 50MA while the index sits 4.09% below its own. NDX is where the damage lives (47% above 50MA, T2108 46%). RUT is weakest structurally (T2108 40.4%; 652 stocks -25% QTR vs 416 +25%).

Cross-asset: gold +2.35% and miners +7% (SIL +7.44%, GDX +7.16%) rose with yields. That is a geopolitical/fiscal hedge bid after the Kuwait drone strike and two Hormuz ship attacks. Oil fell anyway (WTI -1.59%, -21% over 20 days) into Sunday's OPEC+ meeting. Crypto stayed out (IBIT -0.62%, Blockchain rank 136). Today's risk-on was selective, not speculative-liquidity driven. Laggards were pharma and staples (VRTX -5.24%, ABBV, PG, KO): the defensive funding leg was sold to buy beta.
