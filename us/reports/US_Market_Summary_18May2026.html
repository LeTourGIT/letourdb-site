Session Summary

Monday's session was defined by two structural repricing events colliding simultaneously: the NextEra/Dominion utility mega-merger confirmation and a hardening Iran/Hormuz macro backdrop. Neither was priced in Friday's close. Both reset the week's institutional narrative away from last week's tech ATH celebrations.

The NextEra-Dominion deal — $66.8B all-stock, the largest utility acquisition in history and the second-largest transaction of 2026 — is fundamentally a declaration that power, not chips, is the binding constraint on AI buildout. PJM grid capacity prices for 2026-27 were approved at $329/MW-day versus $29/MW-day the prior year, an 11x repricing that the market has been pricing in through the Smart Money Heavy Electrical Equipment theme (#4, ROC +16.42%). The deal gives NextEra dominant control of Northern Virginia's data center corridor — the most AI-dense power market in the world. Dominion +11.5%: correct. NextEra -4.0%: also correct — this is a dilutive all-stock deal with 2028 regulatory close risk and a $6.5B termination fee overhang. The read-across: Bloom Energy (#12 Smart Money, +2.5% RS), GEV, AES as secondary beneficiaries of the AI-power thesis M&A repricing.

On the macro side, the IEA's chief Fatih Birol delivered the clearest official supply-crisis statement of the war cycle at G7 Paris: commercial oil inventories are now weeks from depletion. Strategic reserve releases are running at 2.5M bbl/day, but "are not endless." Spring/summer demand acceleration for diesel, jet fuel, and fertilizer will accelerate the draw. This contextualises Trump's escalatory "Clock is Ticking" language as a negotiating position under real inventory constraint, not merely rhetorical pressure. OIH +3.04%, XLE +1.92%, XOP +1.19% — energy services is the one sector where the fundamental thesis and the price action are pointing in the same direction without ambiguity.

Technology's -1.08% session is a NVDA pre-earnings de-risk, not a trend break. SOXX -2.49%, SOXX 5day -6.92% — but VIX -3.31% to 17.82 tells you this is orderly positioning, not panic. KeyBanc raised the NVDA price target to $300 today on 150-200k Blackwell GPU quarterly shipments. Wednesday is now the most consequential remaining earnings event of the May season. A strong NVDA guide raise breaks the 5day NDX downtrend immediately and re-validates the AI capex cycle narrative that higher yields have been challenging since May 15. A miss or cautious guidance confirms the semi consolidation has further to go and hands the energy/utility complex the dominant institutional narrative through June.
