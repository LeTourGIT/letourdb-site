Session summary

Thursday was a rotation, not a liquidation. The S&P 500 fell 1.21% — its worst session since July — yet nearly two of every three constituents rose (334 advancers, 165 decliners). That is an inverse breadth divergence: a narrow AI-megacap cohort dragged the index lower while the broad market rallied. Meta's non-voting share class to fund AI, Lam Research's slightly-light guide and CoreWeave's debt worries combined into one message — the AI build is now funded by equity issuance and leverage, and the market repriced the cost of that capital.

Semiconductors took the full hit. SOXX −6.79% and XSD −6.15% were extreme moves and the SOX closed at its lowest since 29/07. The damage tracked the capex supply chain precisely: equipment (LRCX −11.76%, ENTG −13.12%), optical (LITE −11.54%, COHR −12.51%, AAOI −11.96%, CIEN −10.71%), connectivity (ALAB −11.11%, MTSI −15.87%), PCB (TTMI −27.67%) and power/electrical (NVT −12.63%, FPS −21.54%). WDC, BE and FIX extended it after the bell.

The money did not leave equities. The 10-year collapsed from 5.13% to 4.96% and the bid flowed straight into housing (ITB +5.39%, LEN +8.69%), regional banks (KRE +2.53%), REITs, insurers and staples (XLP +3.31%, led by PG's beat). XLP up 3.3% on the day XLK fell 3.3% is the cleanest growth-to-defensive mirror of 2026. Inside tech, software and IT services rallied — ROP +9.73%, ADBE +6.07%, ACN +7.69% — bought with the proceeds of semiconductor selling.

Geopolitics added the second leg. Brent reached $112.80, a five-month high, after reported attacks on Saudi infrastructure and the US pulling diplomats from the kingdom. Energy equities did not follow (XLE −0.87%, OIH −4.21%, CRAK −2.25%): with the Defense Production Act invoked for refining, margin upside is capped by policy. Gold fell 2.8% on a day yields plunged — forced selling across every crowded long, from AI to metals to crypto.

The forward read: falling yields, strong labour data and improving breadth (SPX T2108 51.6% → 65.8% across five sessions) are not a bear-market backdrop. This is a leadership change concentrated in the AI-capex cohort that carried index returns all year — so the index can keep falling while the average stock rises. Size positions to that divergence, not to the headline print.
