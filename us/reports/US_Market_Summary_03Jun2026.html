📝 Session Summary

Wednesday's session erased Tuesday's ATH-adjacent optimism in a single morning shock: Iran launched ballistic missiles at Kuwait's international airport and Bahrain, killing at least one at the terminal and threatening to extend targeting to the Bab Al-Mandeb Strait. The market had been riding back-to-back record closes; the geopolitical flare-up hit risk assets immediately, driving WTI back above $95 (+1.81%), Treasury yields higher on inflation expectations, and sector rotation hard into Energy (+1.29%) while hammering Technology (-1.00%), Financials (-1.15%), and Communications (-1.31%). CENTCOM confirmed most missiles failed to reach targets, but the market was not waiting for a damage assessment.

The breadth story is where the session becomes structurally alarming. The Russell 2000 breadth print is a regime-level signal: 422 advancers vs 1,451 decliners, with 200 individual stocks dropping 4% or more in a single session. That is not index-level noise — that is systematic small-cap liquidation. SPX breadth deteriorated materially: 200 advancers vs 297 decliners with new 20day lows (62) exceeding new 20day highs (53). The SPX 50MA participation has dropped to 49.4%, crossing below the neutral 50% threshold. T2108 at 45.6% is in bearish territory. Three consecutive deteriorating breadth readings are flashing a condition where the bull market's internal scaffolding is being quietly removed even as index levels hold above key MAs.

After hours, the megacap semiconductor earnings wave arrived. AVGO delivered Q2 FY2026 at +48% YoY with a Q3 guide of $29.4B above consensus — but fell 6.5% on a slight revenue whisper miss. CRWD beat Q1 FY2027 by 25% on EPS, reported record ARR, and announced a 4-for-1 split — then fell 7% on a 98% pre-earnings positioning flush. VEEV beat and raised FY27 guidance and fell 2.6%. The aggregate message: AI fundamentals intact; the price action is a positioning correction, not a fundamental deterioration.
