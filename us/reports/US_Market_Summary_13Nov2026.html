04

Session Summary

The Nvidia governance story graduated from overhang to event. CNBC headlines confirm Jensen Huang has departed; the board told CNBC it has not yet hired a search firm, and HSBC downgraded the stock, saying it will struggle until a new CEO is named. NVDA fell 2.01% to $162.85 on $59.6B of turnover — more than double any other name on the tape — and MarketWatch tallies $657B of market cap shed this week in a losing streak not seen in 14 years.

NVDA fell less than its ecosystem — that is the insight. AMD -8.75% on $26.4B turnover, AVGO -4.76%, MRVL -4.68%, CRDO -5.94%, COHR -6.00%, TER -5.38%, SNDK -5.59%, WDC -5.00%, DELL -7.18%. Most were up +5% to +19% on the week coming in (AMD 5day +18.93%, AVGO +18.16%). The market is not selling Nvidia the company; it is repricing the AI capex flywheel Huang personally sold to hyperscalers, sovereigns and Washington. The highest-beta expressions of "capex keeps compounding" were liquidated first — SOXX -3.67% and XSD -3.20% were two of only five ETFs to breach -3%, and none breached +3%.

Earnings piled onto the hardware stack. CSCO -6.24% after its fiscal Q1 — the third straight report the market has sold. AMAT -2.74% after fiscal Q4 and another -2.09% AH, with LRCX -3.36% and KLAC -3.17% in sympathy. Equipment, networking, memory and custom silicon all red together = the whole AI hardware chain de-rated as one trade. Neoclouds took the hardest hit (NBIS -9.92%, CRWV -6.13%, IREN -6.72%): that is where the leverage sits, and AI borrowers now drive nearly half of long-dated US IG issuance.

Underneath, the tape was healthier than the index. SPX advancers 313 vs 184 decliners (1.70:1) on a -0.40% close — yesterday was 123/375. This is an inverse cap-weight divergence: the median stock rose while AI mega-caps dragged the index. RSP +0.20% vs VOO -0.38% (+58bp equal-weight edge); RPV +0.65% vs RPG -1.47% (+212bp value-over-growth). Staples led (XLP +1.42%: PEP +2.24%, KDP +2.60%, KMB +2.09%) and Smart Money confirms it is structural — Non-Alcoholic Beverages rank 2, Personal Products rank 3, Household Products surging to rank 10 from rank 91 three days ago.

Within tech, money rotated up the stack rather than leaving. IGV -0.26% vs XLK -2.01%; APP +3.92%, U +5.24%, ADBE +1.90%, META +2.90%. Cerebras +11.22% is the cleanest "AI without Nvidia" hedge on the tape. AI demand is not being questioned — the pricing power and roadmap credibility of the incumbent supply chain is. VIX +3.21% to 19.30, still below 20, confirms rotation over liquidation. Next week is the binary: NVDA reports Tuesday 17/11 — its first print without Huang — and the week is capped by the Anthropic IPO.
