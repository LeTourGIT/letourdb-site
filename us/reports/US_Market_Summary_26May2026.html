📝 Session Summary
⚠ BREADTH DIVERGENCE — SPX ATH 7,519.12 with only 245 Advancers vs 254 Decliners. MU's ~5% index weight at +19% is mathematically lifting SPX. Cap-weight distortion confirmed.

Tuesday's return from Memorial Day produced a clean SPX ATH (7,519.12) but the session had one overwhelmingly dominant catalyst: Micron Technology surging ~19% intraday to breach the $1 trillion market cap threshold for the first time in its history. UBS analyst Timothy Arcuri tripled his price target from $535 to $1,625 — the most aggressive single analyst revision of the semiconductor cycle — citing Micron's entire 2026 HBM4 capacity being sold out under long-term supply agreements and DRAM prices up 58-63%. This is not a momentum trade re-rating; it is a structural repricing of AI memory from commodity to essential infrastructure, a transition analogous to how NVDA re-rated from a gaming GPU maker to AI compute backbone in 2023. The corollary was immediate: SNDK +9.3%, SOXX +6.10% (largest one-day gain of the recent cycle), XSD +4.69%, with MRAM, NVTS, ALAB and AEHR all confirming the breadth of the memory complex re-rating.

The macro backdrop amplified the rally. Treasury yields retreated materially — the 10-year dropped ~8 basis points to 4.485% — as bond markets reopened after the long weekend pricing increasing Iran peace probability. Secretary Rubio confirmed talks were progressing but required "several more days" on the wording of an initial agreement. This dual signal — yield compression plus peace probability rising — created ideal conditions for the growth-and-rate-sensitive memory complex. WTI fell ~2.37% to ~$94.31, a five-week low, with the implied Hormuz risk premium continuing to erode. Airlines (JETS +4.17%), cruise/travel names, and ground transport surged as the direct oil-inverse expression of the peace trade.

The breadth warning is the crucial counterpoint. SPX printed an ATH with 245 advancers vs 254 decliners — a 0.97:1 Adv/Dec ratio that is definitionally negative on a record close day. This is the same cap-weight distortion mechanism observed on 1 May when AAPL (+3%) drove SPX to ATH against negative breadth. T2108 at 53.4% and 50MA participation at 55.2% represent only modest structural support — the market's foundation is improving but not confirmed broad. Three consecutive weeks at SPX RSI-15 above 67 without a meaningful pullback creates asymmetric risk for week 2 of June.

The Smart Money rotation table delivered the session's sharpest signal: Miscellaneous Fintech Infrastructure jumping from #97 to #1 with a 10day ROC of +44.07% — the hottest reading in the entire rotation table. Blockchain/DeFi infrastructure is catching the AI plus crypto wave simultaneously. This is capital positioning quietly, outside the semiconductor headline that is absorbing all the oxygen. Passenger Transportation surging from #137 to #8 is the cleanest Iran peace expression in the Smart Money data — these are cruise ships, ground logistics, and transit operators repricing for a world with oil under $90.
