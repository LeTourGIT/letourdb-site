📝 Session Summary

Friday capped Memorial Day week with all three major indices at fresh ATHs — Dow above 51,000 for the first time ever — driven by Dell Technologies' extraordinary Q1 FY27 print that redefined AI infrastructure expectations for the entire cycle. Dell's $43.8B revenue (+88%), $16.1B AI server sales (+757% YoY), and $51.3B backlog with guidance raised ~$27B was not just a beat — it was a structural re-rating event. HPE +12.76%, NTAP +22.39%, and SMCI +11.60% confirm that the AI infrastructure tide is lifting all enterprise boats simultaneously.

The second catalyst was Snowflake's Q1 FY27 beat (+33% revenue, $6B AWS partnership), which directly demolished the "SaaSpocalypse" thesis suppressing software valuations all year. CIBR +6.41%, IGV +6.25%, and OGIG +3.45% — the only three ETFs above +3% — represent institutional repricing of the AI application layer above the infrastructure layer. Palantir +8%, ServiceNow +6%, Oracle +6%, and Palo Alto +3% confirm this was institutional conviction, not retail momentum. SNOW's AWS partnership proves enterprise AI spend is landing in software margins, not just server capex.

Iran diplomacy reached its most advanced stage since the February 2026 conflict: Rubio met Pakistani FM Dar face-to-face in Washington — the first direct US-Pakistan mediation session — following reports of a tentative framework pending Trump's final approval. WTI fell -1.61% to $91.16 (5day: -9.02%), the largest sustained weekly oil decline since the conflict began. Rubio's language was disciplined ("slight progress, some movement") — not a confirmed deal, but the directional signal is now backed by physical diplomatic meetings. Airlines #13 and Ground Freight #9 in Smart Money are pre-positioning for Hormuz reopening.

Costco's -3.84% on a slight EPS miss resets the consumer staples floor. At 52x trailing earnings, in-line is structurally a miss — the entire premium consumer complex (XLP -1.80%, XRT -1.89%) is exposed to identical valuation discipline. XSD -2.78% within tech confirms capital is rotating from semiconductor equipment and fabless hardware upward into AI software applications.
