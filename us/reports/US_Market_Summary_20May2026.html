📝 Session Summary

Wednesday delivered a structural macro break. Trump announcing US-Iran negotiations were in "final stages" sent WTI crude -5.66% to $98.26 — the first sub-$100 close since the war escalation — unwinding the energy premium that has dominated the macro tape since February. Treasury yields cooled on the back of the same signal (30-year had touched 5.19% Tuesday), giving rate-sensitive tech and growth stocks their largest relief day in weeks. The Dow Jones crossed 50,000 for its first clean close — a round-number milestone that arrived on the back of Iran hopes, not NVDA (which is not in the Dow).

Semiconductor stocks led the index recovery. XSD +5.14% and SOXX +4.74% reflect the NVDA halo — institutions pre-positioned ahead of what consensus expected to be a clean beat. NVDA closed +1.22% on the session before reporting Q1 FY27 results AH: revenue $81.62B (+85% YoY, vs est $79.2B), EPS $1.87 (vs est $1.76), Data Center revenue $75.2B (+92% YoY). Q2 revenue guide of $91B demolished the $87B consensus. The market's response was the defining story of the night: little changed AH (~flat/slight dip), confirming that even a historic beat against a $91B guide cannot overcome a whisper number that had already priced perfection.

Airlines (JETS +6.57%) are the session's most underappreciated signal. The Iran peace trade — not the NVDA beat — was the dominant institutional thesis. JETS leading at +6.57% over XSD's +5.14% means the broadest beneficiary of a Hormuz resolution just beat the AI chip complex on the day NVDA reported its best quarter ever. Homebuilders (ITB +4.53%, XHB +4.28%) are the second-order play: Treasury yield compression translating directly into mortgage rate expectations.

The RUT -1.01% divergence from large-cap gains is the session's structural warning. Small-caps are disproportionately leveraged to domestic economic conditions and have not participated in the large-cap recovery since the April ATH cycle. RUT 5day and 20day trends are BOTH DOWN — regime divergence from SPX/NDX/DJI. This is not sector rotation within small-caps. Watch RUT vs SPX ratio as a leading indicator of breadth normalization into the Iran resolution.
