Session Summary

Two macro forces collided into the August close and both point the same way: higher rates and higher oil. Fed Chair Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole tone has markets pricing better-than 80% odds of a September rate hike — not a pause — while renewed Middle East attacks pushed WTI +1.42% to $86.30 and USO +4.09%. Rising crude into a hawkish Fed is the textbook stagflation-lite cocktail, and the tape traded exactly to script: rate-sensitive and oil-consuming sectors sold, real assets and energy bought, long-duration growth spared only at the very top.

Energy was the unambiguous winner. XLE +2.04% led by a wide margin, backed by OIH +2.60% (services) and CRAK +2.03% (refiners) — the whole crude value chain repricing higher together. SLB +4.83% and LNG +3.32% led names; XLE's 20day is now +8.79%, confirming a multi-week structural bid rather than a headline pop. The mirror image was oil-input consumers: JETS -2.33%, RCL -3.82%, CCL -3.51%, every fuel-cost loser repriced in the same move.

The day's dominant single-stock story was the California utility rout. Edison International EIX -23.07% and PG&E PCG -20.06% collapsed on heavy turnover ($1.3B and $2.0B), and crucially both sit near -27% on the 5day — an escalating wildfire-liability de-rating that accelerated Monday, not a one-session shock. The pair dragged XLU -1.17% and hit insurer Aon -9.53%, evidence the market is pricing a genuine catastrophe-liability event, not a stock-specific fluke. Read it as idiosyncratic California liability compounding the rate-driven sell in defensives.

Underneath the red tape, capital rotated hard into crypto and real-asset hedges. Circle +9.65%, Coinbase +5.31%, MSTR +4.42%, BMNR +6.39% and IREN +4.70% led a broad digital-asset bid (IBIT +1.34%, ETHA +1.67%), corroborated three ways: Blockchain & Cryptocurrency is the number-one 10day Hot Industry, Smart Money ranks it 4th (up from 9day rank 128), and Misc Fintech Infrastructure sits at Smart Money rank 1 (ROC +43.36). Alongside crypto, Coal, Gold and Specialty Mining all climbed the Smart Money board — the classic inflation-hedge rotation you would expect with oil bid and a hawkish Fed. RS leadership, meanwhile, is dominated by biotech (roughly half the top 20), the defensive-growth corner that leads when the cycle signal turns cautious.

Breadth confirmed rather than diverged: SPX 136 advancers vs 359 decliners is internally consistent with a down day, but the magnitude is a warning — 128 new 20day lows swamped 22 new highs, and only 38.8% of SPX names sit above their 20MA. T2108 at 46.2% says participation has thinned to where the index is propped by a shrinking mega-cap cohort. No ETF moved beyond ±3% either way: selling was broad but shallow.
