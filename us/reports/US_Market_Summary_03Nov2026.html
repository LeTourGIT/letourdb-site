04Session Summary

Election Day delivered an index bounce rather than a market rally. SPX +0.35% to 7,568.92 broke a three-session losing streak and NDX +0.66% to 28,180.26 led on a rebound in AI semis (SOX +2.6%, XSD +3.42%, SOXX +2.50%). Underneath, 312 SPX members fell and only 187 rose, the equal-weight RSP lost -0.33%, and just four of eleven sectors finished green. Monday ran the other way: 345 advancers against 154 decliners while the index fell. Two sessions in a row where the index and the average stock moved in opposite directions is a market in rotation, not one picking a direction.

The chip bounce was positioning ahead of AMD, and AMD delivered: record $13.4B revenue, Data Center +97% and an accelerating Q4, with the stock +5% AH. ARISTA (+9.5% AH) and SMCI (+7.2% AH) followed. The chain is AI-capex validation → semis (XSD, FN +15.45%, LSCC +10.98%, CRDO +7.78%) → the power layer (ETN +7.00%, RRX +11.81%, BE +9.05%, TLN +7.29%) → crypto-to-compute miners (WULF +14.53%, IREN +11.08%). Electrical Components & Equipment surged to rank 9 in Smart Money from rank 50 a day earlier and rank 119 nine days ago. Capital is buying the AI power bottleneck, not just the GPU.

The other side of the AI trade is disruption. DT -14.69% (5day -26.23%), IT -13.19%, TRI -9.87%, SHOP -8.74% and DOX -44.6% AH all point the same way: businesses that sell human analysis, monitoring or content are being de-rated as AI substitutes for them. The market is now charging for AI exposure on both sides. It pays for owning the picks and shovels and penalises businesses that AI can replace.

Defensives broke. XLP -2.07% and XLB -2.01% were the worst sectors on an earnings cluster (SYY -9.33%, ADM -6.82%, KDP -5.65%, BALL -7.14%) together with yield pressure on bond proxies. Consumer non-Cyclicals still has the strongest 10day sector ROC (+5.71%) and Non-Alcoholic Beverages is rank 2 in Smart Money, so this was the most crowded defensive trade being hit at its most extended point. If Wednesday's chip follow-through holds, expect that money to keep leaving staples.

Geopolitics stayed in the background. Iran and Qatari mediators held a third day of talks, Rubio said Trump wants the conflict resolved peacefully, and a reported US strike lifted crude only briefly. WTI closed -0.66% at $96.44 and Brent -1.11% at $104.13. Energy (XLE -0.85%, FANG -3.70%) lagged, but the 60day leadership in tankers and integrated oil shows the structural Hormuz premium is intact.
