📝 Session Summary

Tuesday delivered the macro shock the market had been pricing as tail risk: April CPI printed 3.8% headline (3yr high, beat 3.7% consensus), driven by Iran-war energy costs that accounted for over 40% of the monthly increase. Gasoline +28.4% YoY. Core CPI +2.8% (+0.4% MoM), also above the 2.7% estimate. Real wages fell 0.5% in April — the first month wages have failed to outpace inflation in three years. The yield on the 10yr Treasury climbed to ~4.42% (week high), and CME FedWatch now prices roughly 27-30% probability of a rate hike by December. The Fed's "higher for longer" framework is no longer a constraint — it is the base case.

Simultaneously, President Trump declared the US-Iran ceasefire "on massive life support," rejecting Tehran's counter-proposal (sanctions relief, reparations, Hormuz authority). Reports emerged that Trump is meeting his NSC to weigh resumed military operations. WTI rose +3.4% toward $101.63. Saudi Aramco CEO warned the market is losing ~100M barrels of supply weekly. Two independent macro negatives — energy-driven CPI and geopolitical re-escalation — landed on the same session. The market's resilience (SPX only -0.16%) is its own signal: institutions are not capitulating. They are rotating.

The rotation story was the session's structural insight. Healthcare led the day: XLV +1.96%, IHF (Healthcare Providers) +2.64%, IHI (Medical Devices) +2.42%, BBH +1.76%. This is not defensive repositioning — it is capital moving from a high-RSI, CPI-sensitive semiconductor complex toward an inflation-resistant, earnings-season-driven healthcare thesis. Consumer Staples XLP +1.28% and Financials XLF +0.78% (yield curve steepener benefit) added texture. Meanwhile, XLK -1.51% with semi ETFs SOXX -3.15%, XSD -4.26%, URNM -3.94%, URA -5.03% continued the rotation away from the semi/nuclear complex.

Gold tells the smartest story: GLD -0.40% (spot gold pressured by real yield spike on hot CPI) while silver SLV +0.71% and copper COPX +3.63% surged. Industrial metals decoupling from monetary metals = real-economy demand signal. Copper's 5day +16% on AI data center construction demand is the commodity market pricing AI capex duration. Smart Money Gold #8 SURGE confirms institutional inflation hedge accumulation began before today's CPI print — the acceleration is 5-9 days old.
