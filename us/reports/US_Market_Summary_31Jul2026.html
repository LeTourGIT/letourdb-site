Session Summary

Friday delivered one of the widest single-session dispersions of the year. The cap-weighted tape printed green — SPX +0.70%, NDX +0.60%, DJI +0.53% — but those gains were manufactured almost entirely by a handful of mega-cap earnings winners. Amazon +15.3% and Alphabet +6.7% did the heavy lifting, with Microsoft +3.0% and Meta +3.3% adding ballast. Strip them out and the market was heavy: SPX advancers trailed decliners 220 to 279, the Russell 2000 fell outright, and SPX new 20day lows (45) outnumbered new highs (21) on an up day.

The offsetting shock was Apple -7.35% — an iPhone and guidance miss that handed the most-valuable-company crown back to NVIDIA and single-handedly pinned XLK to -0.22% despite real strength beneath it (MPWR +8.4%, ANET +5.5%, SNPS +4.4%, COHR +5.6%). The concentrated damage was in the momentum and single-name earnings cohort: RBLX -26.9%, RDDT -21.0%, MTZ -18.9%, GDDY -16.7%, CTVA -11.9% and COIN -10.6% were all earnings air-pockets. This is dispersion, not contagion — but with NDX already Bearish, there is no cushion for a miss.

Geopolitics re-entered hard. Iran escalated — striking US assets in Kuwait and a first drone attack on Egypt — sending WTI +5.39% to $86.80 and Brent +2.35%. Crucially, energy equities lagged the futures (XLE +1.0%, USO +0.91%). The market is pricing a geopolitical risk premium, not a demand signal — corroborated by VIX collapsing -6.44%. When crude spikes 5% and the fear gauge falls 6%, traders are treating the oil move as a headline, not a regime change.

Beneath the surface, leadership has rotated defensive. The RS Top 20 is now dominated by healthcare and biotech — a stark contrast to the semiconductor-led leaderboards of the spring — while Smart Money industry rankings are reshuffling violently (Construction & Engineering to #1 from #130, Renewable Energy to #12 from #127), the signature of a market repricing leadership rather than trending. Tellingly, gold miners were dumped (GDX -3.49%, SIL -3.20%) as long-end yields backed up on the Fed-credibility debate — a rare session where crypto AND precious metals fell together.
