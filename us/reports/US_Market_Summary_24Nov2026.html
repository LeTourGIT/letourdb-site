04Session Summary

Tuesday was the hangover from Monday's Jensen Huang reinstatement. Nvidia's board reinstated Huang as CEO on 23/11 — less than two weeks after he resigned — and NVDA ran more than 30% in its best session since 2003, lifting SPX from 6,910 to 7,237 on 416 advancers. Today the tape answered the harder question — does a returning CEO change the AI hardware cycle? — with a no. NVDA gave back -5.86% on $60.0B of turnover (2.4x the next-largest print in the 16d universe), AMD -8.86%, MU -11.01%, AVGO -3.98%.

The trigger for the second leg was Analog Devices. ADI beat cleanly — Q4 EPS $4.06 vs $3.89 on $4.37B revenue (+42% YoY) — and still fell -17.37%. The market looked through the quarter to fiscal 2027: a Q1 sequential slowdown, holiday fab shutdowns pinching margins, and capex more than doubling for capacity that does not open until 2028. When a record beat is punished 17%, positioning — not fundamentals — is setting price. The contagion map was precise: analog peers TXN -11.25%, MPWR -10.77%, SMTC -16.97%; AI connectivity ALAB -13.88%, CRDO -13.07%, LITE -10.52%; AI hardware HPE -16.31%, DELL -11.83% into its print, SNDK -11.13%; neocloud CRWV -11.85%. SOXX -8.27% and XSD -7.84% both flag ⚠ EXTREME.

The money did not leave — it changed address. SPX printed 344 advancers vs 155 decliners (2.2:1 positive) while the index fell -0.50%: an inverse breadth divergence, where semiconductor cap-weight drags the index while the median stock rallies. RSP beat IVV by 1.02 points; pure value (RPV) beat pure growth (RPG) by 3.38. Healthcare was the receiving account — IBB +5.17%, HLTH +4.66%, BBH +4.46%, XLV +2.95% — led by INSM +14.13% after Sanofi agreed to buy it. Retail earnings supplied the second leg: ANF +19.69%, KSS +19.48%, DKS +14.59%, DLTR +11.18%, TGT +4.68%.

Macro layered a geopolitical premium onto energy and stripped it from metals. Iran said three tankers struck mines in Hormuz despite a US escort, and Houthis claimed strikes on a Saudi refinery: WTI +2.65% to $81.70, Brent +2.69% to $88.46. Yet gold fell -2.62% and silver -4.37% — a liquidation signature, not a safe-haven bid. When oil rises on war headlines and gold falls, metals are funding losses elsewhere.

Structural read: AI hardware has moved from a momentum trade to a capex-credibility trade. Semiconductors fell from Smart Money rank 13 to rank 118 in three sessions and Semi Equipment from rank 4 to 111 — ↓↓ EXIT velocities, not profit-taking. Until SOXX stops making lower lows, NDX rallies are supply, not demand.
