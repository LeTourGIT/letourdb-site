Session Summary

September opened the way August closed — under the twin weight of a hawkish Fed and an oil shock. Fed Chair Warsh's Jackson Hole message ("we have work to do" on inflation) has repriced the September meeting to a ~65% chance of a rate HIKE, up from ~40% a week ago. The 10year yield sits near 4.8% (highest since 2023) and the 30year near 5.28% (2006-07 levels), with the selloff global. This is the master variable for every rotation below: rising discount rates de-rate long-duration equity (high-multiple software) and bid hard assets.

That is exactly what printed. The indices closed green, but leadership was Materials (XLB +1.69%) and the precious-metals miners — SIL +4.67%, XME +3.19%, GDX +3.13% were the ONLY three ETFs above +3%, driven by gold +2.0% and silver +2.7%. Technology (XLK -0.02%) was flat only because a single name masked a rout underneath.

The tech story was a violent hardware-over-software rotation. DELL +15.81% (AI-server beat) was the day's top large-cap gainer and held XLK flat by itself, while the software complex was liquidated: PANW -9.28%, CRDO -20.04%, MDB -13.54%, DDOG -6.53%, PLTR -5.81%, CRWD -5.42%, NOW -4.32%, FTNT -4.52%, NET -4.47%. In a rising-rate regime the market pays for AI-capex hardware and sells high-multiple application and security software. This is a duration trade wearing a sector costume.

Breadth is the warning. The SPX advanced 298/201, but quarter-to-date internals are rolling over: only 37.2% of SPX names are above their 20day MA, T2108 sits at 45.4%, and — the key tell — the SPX printed 106 new 20day LOWS against just 39 new 20day highs on a green day. Russell 2000, despite leading at +1.13%, shows 233 new lows vs 148 highs. A green index built on more new lows than highs is being carried by a narrow rotating cohort while the median stock deteriorates.
