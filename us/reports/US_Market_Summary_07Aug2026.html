Session Summary

Friday was a dovish-repricing day. July payrolls unexpectedly FELL 23,000 against consensus for a ~83,000 gain, with the prior two months revised down a combined 103,000. Unemployment slipped to 4.1%, but internals were soft (government −53k, private +30k). The market read it cleanly — the labor market is cracking, the Fed's hand is forced toward cuts. Treasury yields plunged (10yr ~4.64%, 2yr 4.19%) and every rate-sensitive corner caught a bid.

That single print explains the day's three biggest structural moves. Gold and hard assets went vertical — GDX +7.11%, SIL +6.29%, XME +4.88%, URA +3.96% — the textbook falling-real-yield bullion regime. Smart Money confirms it: Gold, Precious Metals, Integrated Mining and Specialty Mining all SURGED up the rotation table. The 5day returns (GDX +21%, SIL +20%, REMX +17%) mark genuine accumulation, not a spike.

Software blew out on earnings — but selectively. Atlassian +35%, Twilio +25%, Doximity +33%, Cloudflare +5.6% and Datadog +2% powered IGV +3.29% and directly answered the "SaaSpocalypse" bear case that AI is gutting seat-based software. Yet the same session saw Trade Desk −22% and Figma −2.8% — the re-rating is dispersion, not a blanket bid. AI-additive platforms win; threatened models lose.

Semiconductors staged the rebound (XSD +4.66%, SOXX +2.02%, MCHP +14%, COHR +13%, GFS +7%), the mechanism behind NDX's +1.19% — but the regime still reads Bearish. This is repair off oversold, not restored trend leadership. Memory remains the soft spot (WDC −3.8%, 5day −20%; SNDK −3.7%), so the rebound is fabless/analog/optical-led, not broad silicon. Energy was the mirror image (XLE −1.13%, worst sector; WTI −7.4% on the week) as Iran halted strikes and opened Hormuz talks — the market has moved from "peak fear" to "pricing in." The energy-vs-metals split is the day in one image: capital leaving the fading war trade, crowding into the rate-cut/debasement trade.
