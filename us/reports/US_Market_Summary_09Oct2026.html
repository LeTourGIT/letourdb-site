📝 Session Summary

Friday was a sector-rotation day dressed up as a quiet index gain. The SPX added +0.59% to 7,811.54, 0.09% shy of its 06/10 record. Underneath, one corporate announcement repriced an entire industry. SpaceX agreed to buy a nationwide 800 MHz low-band spectrum portfolio from Grain Management (~$8B, pending FCC approval) to make Starlink Mobile "a major mobile carrier in the U.S." Low-band 800 MHz travels far and penetrates buildings: it turns direct-to-cell from a remote-area patch into a real terrestrial competitor.

The market voted immediately. VZ −10.14% (worst day since 2002), TMUS −13.27%, T −10.82%, CHTR −5.74%, CMCSA −2.59%, on $14.1B of carrier/cable turnover in sheet 16d. That is institutional de-risking, not retail panic. ASTS −10.48% is the subtle tell: the satellite challenger lost its moat to a better-funded satellite challenger. The mirror trade was tower REITs. CCI +15.60% and AMT +9.30% made XLRE (+1.86%) the best sector, on the logic that a fourth national carrier must lease steel. The tape is pricing a four-player market and lower wireless pricing power.

The second theme was a clean rotation inside tech, from chips into software/security. ZS +7.80%, SNOW +7.42%, DDOG +7.11%, NET +5.57%, OKTA +5.41%, PLTR +5.17% and PANW +5.09% traded $16.5B. CIBR +3.58% and IGV +2.81% (20day +10.98%) confirm it, and twelve software names printed 20day highs. Semiconductors collapsed to Smart Money rank 88 from rank 10 three days ago, a full institutional exit (INTC 5day −12.26%, SWKS −5.45%, SOXX 5day −5.01%). Profits are being taken on the hardware layer and redeployed into the application/security layer above it. That pattern usually marks a late-stage AI capex cycle, not a broken one.

Healthcare was the third leg. MRNA +14.21% hit an ATH of $225 on its Nasdaq-100 debut (replacing WBD): forced index demand into a stock already up sixfold in 2026, with Citi at Sell and an $80 target. HUM +11.56% emerged as the top beneficiary of the 2027 Medicare Star Ratings, lifting Managed Healthcare to Smart Money rank 11 from 135 nine days ago, while ALHC fell −13.38%. With IBB +3.00% and 14 of the RS Top 20 in healthcare, it is the most concentrated leadership group on the board.

Geopolitics kept crude bid. Trump says the Iran deal "isn't what he wants" with renewed strikes readied, and the Saudi-led coalition vowed a "firm" response to the Houthis. Brent +1.47% to $104.43, yet XLE −0.25% and refiners fell (CRAK −1.56%, VLO −2.26%). The Trump–Putin deal to ship Russian diesel into US and global markets compresses distillate cracks. Smart Money still shows Drilling (SURGE to rank 15) and E&P (rank 16 from 131) accumulating, so buyers are picking up oil exposure on red days.

Breadth was positive at the headline but weak underneath. SPX Adv/Dec was 317/183 and new 20day highs flipped to 52 vs 20 lows (from 26/63). Yet only 35.0% of SPX stocks are above their 50MA with the index a tick from a record. RUT closed +0.46% on 895 advancers vs 1,038 decliners.
