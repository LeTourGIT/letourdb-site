Your China hypothesis

Partly right, but not a chip export rule and not the day's trigger. No new BIS export control on China chips was found for 01/10. The live policy lever is the FCC's draft ban on imports of NEW Chinese optical transceivers (Reuters, 04/08/2026) — an import ban that hands share to Western suppliers. A note circulating 01/10, citing Morgan Stanley's Washington checks (unconfirmed), says action could surface as early as October, phased in from the 3.2T generation with a 65% US-content carve-out, and that the real bottleneck is lasers — Lumentum's core franchise. Test of the hypothesis: when the ban report broke on 04/08, AAOI (the purest ban beneficiary) led at +16% to +18%. On 01/10, COHR led and AAOI merely kept pace. That ordering says initiation, not policy.

Risks: 6.6% ADR, a $93.8B market cap that needs CPO timing to hold, and Chinese control of indium phosphide — if the FCC acts, Beijing's retaliation lands on the same supply chain the ban is meant to help.

Session Summary

Geopolitical lead · macro to sector to stock

GEOPOLITICS LED THE TAPE. Q4 opened under a double squeeze: Brent +5.56% to $102.21 and WTI +4.16% to $92.85 after Reuters reported Chinese refiners suspended October fuel exports, the US reportedly sent a third aircraft carrier to the Middle East, and Trump told TIME that resuming strikes on Iran after the midterms was 'possible'. The chain ran exactly as theory says: crude up, inflation expectations up, 10yr Treasury yield to 5.33% (highest since April 2002) and 30yr to 5.67% (highest since July 2002), equities flushed (SPX roughly -0.4% by late morning). Then yields backed off and every index clawed back to green. The close matters less than the path: equities are now trading as a derivative of the long end.

THE BOUNCE WAS BROAD, THE DAMAGE UNDERNEATH IS NOT REPAIRED. SPX advancers beat decliners 296/202 and equal-weight RSP (+0.47%) beat the cap-weighted index (+0.19%) because megacaps dragged: AAPL -0.81%, GOOGL -1.70%, AVGO -2.15%. That is the opposite of the usual cap-weight distortion. But only 25.2% of SPX stocks sit above their 20day MA, T2108 is 22.0%, and 193 stocks printed new 20day lows on an up day versus 32 new highs. The index is 1.70% below its 13/08/2026 closing high while the median stock is in a correction. SPX and NDX carry a Bullish regime; RUT and DJI have been Bearish for 11 sessions or more. This is a two-tier market, not a bull market with a breadth problem.

AI PLUMBING TOOK LEADERSHIP FROM AI COMPUTE. Bernstein launched coverage of US networking and optical with a 'structural winners' call: Outperform on LITE ($1,220 target), COHR ($350), CIEN, ANET and CLS; Market Perform on CSCO and GLW. The bid concentrated in optical components: COHR +10.90%, AAOI +8.12%, CIEN +7.77%, LITE +7.67%, versus GLW +4.33% and CSCO +1.05% (both Market Perform) and systems names ANET +0.44% and CLS +3.23% despite their Outperform ratings. The market bought component scarcity, not the coverage list. Add SNPS +12.78% (Investor Day: an AWS agreement above $1B and an OpenAI partnership), CDNS +6.22%, and MU +3.03% on $49.1B of turnover after record FQ4 revenue of $54.23B. Capital is moving along the AI stack from GPUs to interconnect, memory and design tools. AVGO -2.15% was the lone AI-semi loser.

IT SERVICES GOT ITS FIVE9 MOMENT. ACN +15.78%, the best day in its history, on FQ4 revenue of $18.68B (consensus $18.03B), EPS $3.29 (consensus $3.18), a record 141 bookings above $100M and FY27 growth guided at 3% to 6%. The market had priced Accenture as an AI casualty (1year -13.91% even after today). CTSH +5.99%, CRM +3.10%, NOW +2.80% and IGV +1.64% confirm the read-through: enterprises are paying integrators to deploy AI, not replacing them with it. IT Services climbed from rank 65 to rank 54 in Smart Money and Software from 61 to 55; both still carry negative 10day ROC, so this is short covering becoming accumulation, not yet a trend.

HEALTHCARE IS THE SOURCE OF FUNDS. XLV -1.32% was the worst sector and 9 of the 15 largest liquid decliners were healthcare (DHR -4.42%, AMGN -3.38%, TMO -3.34%, REGN -3.09%, VRTX -3.07%). Biotechnology collapsed from rank 56 to rank 106 in Smart Money in a single session. Yet the RS Top 20 still holds 14 healthcare names. RS is a 1month-to-6month measure; Smart Money is a 10day measure. When the two disagree this sharply, the leaderboard is describing where money was. With the 10yr above 5.3%, long-duration biotech is the natural casualty, and it is funding the re-entry into tech.

The September jobs report lands into a market where Fed officials are saying inflation takes priority over a stable labour market, so a hot number is a yield problem and a soft number is a growth problem.
