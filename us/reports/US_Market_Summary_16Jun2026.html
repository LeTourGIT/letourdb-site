📝 Session Summary

The dominant catalyst is geopolitical: a US–Iran peace deal (final agreed text confirmed via Pakistani mediators ~14 June, signing timeline still fluid) collapsed the war premium. WTI fell -6.02% to $75.83 — its lowest since early March and the first sustained break back toward pre-war levels. Brent -4.21%, USO -5.09%. The market now prices a Strait of Hormuz reopening as base case, not tail.

The second-order effect dominated the tape: a violent unwind of every war-premium beneficiary. Semiconductors — the cycle's AI-capex leadership — took the brunt: SOXX -5.92%, XSD -5.73% (both EXTREME). MRVL -9.78%, MPWR -9.29%, INTC -8.45%, AMD -7.30%, KLAC -7.44%, MU -6.18% on $46B turnover. This was positioning, not chip-specific news — capital that crowded into AI-infra as the safe-haven growth trade rotated out the moment the geopolitical hedge lost its rationale.

Where did it go? Into value and cyclicals. Financials led (XLF +1.47%: JPM +3.68%, COF, SCHW, SOFI) on a lower-oil / steeper-curve read. Critically, utilities held — TLN +5.26%, VST +3.32% — the market is separating AI-power-demand (intact) from AI-silicon-multiple (de-rating). Industrials caught the falling-input-cost bid; gaming/biotech (RBLX +8.06%, TTWO +6.35%, MRNA +6.27%) confirmed the move is breadth-broadening, not risk-off.

⚠ THE KEY DIVERGENCE: SPX closed -0.57% but advancers BEAT decliners 271/229 — a REVERSE cap-weight distortion, the mirror image of an ATH-on-narrow-breadth setup. The median S&P stock rose; a handful of mega-cap chips dragged the index down. T2108 at 68.2% and 101 new 20day highs vs 15 lows confirm the tape beneath the chip carnage is HEALTHY. NDX breadth (35/65) is the inverse — chip concentration makes the growth index structurally fragile. VIX only +1.30% (and -17% over 5days): orderly repositioning, not panic.
