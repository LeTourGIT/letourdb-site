Yet markets made new ATHs anyway, because the MoM deceleration (headline +0.4% vs +0.7% prior; core +0.2% vs +0.3% prior) was "less bad" than feared, and AI earnings momentum is overriding the macro drag. The structural message: both sides want the deal more than markets are pricing. But resolution is not confirmed — Trump's approval is still pending.
