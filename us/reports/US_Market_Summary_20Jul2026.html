Session Summary

Monday extended the great AI rotation — with a twist that rewired sector leadership. The headline wasn't a chip: Bitcoin miners re-rated violently as AI-infrastructure landlords. IREN +19.57% won a $2.8B AI-cloud contract and lifted its AI target above $4B (~85% contracted with Microsoft, Nvidia, Perplexity, Figure AI); Hut 8 signed a $9.8B, 15-year lease taking Beacon Point to a fully-contracted 1GW. APLD +7.99%, WULF +3.85%, BMNR +5.99% surged with them. The signal: the market is structurally decoupling miner equities from Bitcoin spot and valuing them as recurring-revenue AI-capex beneficiaries — the one corner of tech still bid.

Everywhere else the AI trade cracked. The semiconductor rebound failed intraday: MRVL +3.32%, CRDO +4.63%, TER +3.54% bounced but each still carries a 5day loss of −2% to −10% — dead-cat repairs, not accumulation. Use the 5day column to separate trend from short-cover: none of the chip 'gainers' has a positive 5day. The pressure is structural — TSMC's raised capex guide, cheaper Chinese open-source models, and reports of Meta renting out its own AI capacity (oversupply fear). BE −8.33%, ORCL −3.98%, DELL −3.65%, PANW −2.79% carried the AI hardware/software de-rate.

Capital that left tech rotated rather than fled. Energy was the second-best SPDR (XLE +0.45%) with crude at a one-month high despite an Iran ceasefire proposal — the 9th consecutive night of US strikes plus a Houthi blockade on Saudi shipping keeps a war premium bid even as WTI's headline print fell −1.64%. Smart Money confirms it: four of the top seven industries are energy. The rest of the bid went defensive — Consumer Staples and Managed Healthcare lead the 60day structural ranks, the textbook late-cycle hide.

Breadth made the risk explicit. SPX advanced just 163 vs 336 decliners — a 2:1 negative tape on a near-flat index — while RUT was brutal at 593/1364 with 88 stocks down >4%. This is distribution masked by cap-weighting: a few AI-infra winners plus green XLC/XLE held the headline indices near unchanged while the median stock fell. Layer on Jamie Dimon warning he 'wouldn't buy stocks or Treasurys,' rising 10yr yields, and threatened 50% Canada tariffs, and the risk-reward has shifted defensive. NDX's break below its 50MA into a Bearish regime is the level that matters into Tuesday.
