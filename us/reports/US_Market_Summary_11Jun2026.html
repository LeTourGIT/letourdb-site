📝 Session Summary

Thursday was a three-act geopolitical whipsaw that resolved violently risk-on. Act one: overnight reports of continued Washington–Tehran talks lifted futures off Wednesday's wreckage. Act two: Trump threatened fresh strikes and a takeover of Kharg Island — stocks knifed lower. Act three: the president called off military action and declared the Iran war "settled, subject to finalization," expecting a signing "in the next few days." The Dow surged 900 points, reversing Wednesday's 900-point loss almost to the dollar, and WTI collapsed -5.76% to $86.52 — an 8-week low that round-trips the entire war premium (crude traded $101.94 on 1 May). In one tape, the market moved from pricing escalation to pricing resolution.

The macro consequence is bigger than the index moves. The chain that has terrorised this market — Hormuz drives oil, oil drives the 4.2% CPI print, and inflation is new Fed Chair Warsh's first test — just had its first link snapped. Thursday's hot-headline/soft-core PPI confirmed Wednesday's CPI anatomy: the entire inflation overshoot is an energy problem, and energy just broke. Yields tumbled, the dollar weakened, and the rate-cut path that war inflation had foreclosed cracked back open — homebuilders (ITB +4.43%) were the first rate-sensitive cohort through the door, and Smart Money promoted Homebuilding to #4 the same session.

Semiconductors were the institutional epicentre. SOXX +8.39% and XSD +7.30% led 32 ETFs above +3% — the broadest flag day of the cycle. The memory complex detonated: MU +11.66% on $58B turnover (the day's largest single-stock liquidity event), SNDK +14.50% on $25B, with the equipment trifecta KLAC +12.92% / LRCX +12.65% / AMAT +11.19% confirming the layer below, and INTC +9.27% adding a BofA double upgrade. This is capital piling into the AI-capex receivers twenty-four hours after Oracle quantified how big the cheques are: $70B FY27 net capex against a $638B backlog. Oracle's pain is Micron's revenue — the Smart Money board printed it literally, re-ranking Semiconductor Equipment & Testing from #98 to #11 in a single day.

That is the session's defining bifurcation: semis +8% while software bled red on a +3.29% NDX day. ORCL -8.53%, ADSK -7.10%, ADBE -6.25%, WDAY -5.05%, with NOW, CRM, INTU and TEAM all down — IGV finished -0.72% against a roaring tape. The market is explicitly splitting the AI trade into payers (software carrying negative free cash flow to fund the buildout) and receivers (semis, memory, equipment collecting the spend). Adobe's after-hours beat-and-raise being sold -5.4% to 2019 levels confirms this is a terminal-value de-rating, not an earnings argument — and until a major software name gets paid for a beat, the rotation has no natural buyer on the other side.

Breadth was genuinely strong, with no asterisks: SPX 318/180 advancers, RUT a thumping 1,335/522 with 265 small caps up +4% or more, and T2108 rebuilt to 56.6% from 49.0% in one session. The subtle tell was gold +3.34% alongside a -12.5% VIX — not a haven bid, but an inflation/weak-dollar trade rallying in tandem with equities, pricing "Fed stays easy into hot headline inflation." Friday brings the SpaceX IPO, the largest in history, as the next liquidity test for a tape that is relieved but unhealed: 5day trends are still Down, VIX is still +26% on the week, and the treaty is unsigned.
