04Session Summary

Tuesday was a two-speed market wearing a flat SPX mask. SPX closed at 7,764.64 — six cents below Monday — while NDX rallied +0.82% to 30,732 and the Nasdaq Composite posted a record close. Beneath that, XLF fell -1.97%, the S&P bank index lost around 3%, and SPX Adv/Dec was a coin flip at 249/250. Two powerful trades cancelled out at index level: long AI hardware, short AI-disrupted incumbents.

Geopolitics set the macro tone. Trump described a "very good" meeting with Iranian envoys in New York and Iran reportedly says Hormuz could reopen within seven days if US pressure eases — though Trump also told the UN a deal would come after the November midterms. WTI fell -3.44% to $89.77; XLE -1.09% (5day -6.29%), XOP -1.70% (5day -9.06%). Smart Money is already voting: Oil & Gas Refining collapsed from rank 3 to 75 in three days and E&P from rank 6 (5days ago) to 124 — an institutional exit, not a dip.

The chip trade carried the tape. SOXX +2.40% for a sixth straight gain (5day +14.82%) with memory in front — SNDK +6.82% on $24.2B turnover, MU +5.00% on $31.5B — plus power and interconnect in MPWR +8.06%, ALAB +6.67%, VICR +19.85%, TER +4.49%. The Meta Muse agent launch lifted the AI trade, and the same headline is the knife in financials: AI advisers threaten fee-based wealth platforms, hence SCHW -6.11%, LPLA -7.45%, AMP -4.36%, with ADBE -4.52% and INTU -3.87% in software. AI is simultaneously the market's biggest buyer and its biggest seller.

The rate channel sealed it. Post-hike, falling oil is dragging the long end lower and flattening the curve — a direct bank-margin threat. The mirror image: ITB +3.21%, XHB +2.53%, LEN +6.38%, and Homebuilding vaulting to Smart Money rank 25 from 135. Rate-sensitive equity rallies while rate-sensitive lenders sell: the market is trading the 10-year, not Fed funds.
