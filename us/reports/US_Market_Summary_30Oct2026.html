04Session Summary

October closed with the Nasdaq Composite’s worst day since June (−2.86%) and NDX −3.46%, as Intel’s −28.58% collapse turned a week of mixed megacap earnings into a verdict on AI capex. Intel beat Q3 on every line, then guided Q4 revenue to $15–16B and gross margin to 38%. That the market wiped a quarter of its value on a beat says the AI-hardware trade was priced for perfection, and the first crack in guidance forced de-risking across the supply chain.

The chain ran exactly as a capex-digestion scare should: Amazon’s print revived spend worries → hyperscalers dipped only ~1.4–1.6% → their suppliers were hit hardest (SOXX −8.24%, XSD −6.30%, SNDK −13.47%, AMD −10.88%, QCOM −11.12%, VRT −5.46%, GEV −3.38%) → AI-networking momentum names flat-to-up on the week (AAOI −12.80%, COHR −11.34%, ALAB −10.93%) liquidated in a day. Software joined in: IGV −3.41%, CIBR −3.78%, DDOG −11.50%, APP −10.36%.

The other side of the tape was orderly rotation, not panic. XLE +1.91%, XLP +1.41%, XLU +0.67% and XLV +0.45% all closed green; RPV +0.47% and SCHD +0.48% led the style board while RPG −4.31% and QQQ −3.47% trailed. SPX printed 175 new 20day highs against 48 lows on a −1.82% day — defensives and energy are breaking out while tech breaks down. Equal-weight RSP (−0.79%) beat SPX by a full point; technology’s roughly one-third index weight at −4.89% explains most of the loss on its own (our estimate: ~1.5 of the 1.82 points).

Macro offered no cushion. The Fed’s 10–2 hawkish hold keeps December live, gold fell −3.62% to $3,875 — a one-year low despite Iran claiming four tankers hit mines in Hormuz — and a record $6.3B Binance hack pushed IBIT −5.14%. When gold, crypto and tech fall together, real-rate expectations are doing the work.

Next week stacks the catalysts: US midterms and Apple earnings. NDX RSI-15 at 35.62 and VIX still under 20 argue the flush is mature but not finished.
