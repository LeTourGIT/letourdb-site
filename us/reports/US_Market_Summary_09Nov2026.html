04Session Summary

Monday was an oil-led relief rally layered on top of a chip short-squeeze. WTI fell more than 4% to its lowest close since August after President Trump said a return to war with Iran is "highly unlikely" while talks progress, and reports of a possible preliminary uranium-enrichment deal (unconfirmed by US officials) revived the Hormuz-reopening scenario. The chain was textbook: crude down → inflation expectations down → cyclicals and small caps up → XLI +3.82% led all sectors, JETS +5.15%, and Airlines took rank 1 in Smart Money.

The second engine was semiconductors. SOXX +7.49% and XSD +6.93% after last week's ~13% SOXX collapse — AMD +13.26% (Oppenheimer upgrade), MPWR +16.60%, COHR +16.79%, TER +12.73%, AMAT +11.37%, ALAB +13.72%. NVDA fell -4.27% to its lowest close since April after its board opened an independent review of Jensen Huang's reported relationship with a suspected Chinese intelligence operative. The NVDA-vs-SOXX gap of nearly 11 points was the widest since January 2019. Capital did not leave AI — it left the single point of failure.

Context matters: SOXX is still -19.19% over 20days and -2.95% over 5days. This is a reflex rally inside a broken trend. BTIG notes the SOX has historically been higher 10 days after a fresh 200-day break only 47.4% of the time. Zero semiconductors sit in the RS Top 20 — price bounced, leadership did not.

Breadth was the real story: 420 SPX advancers vs 78 decliners (5.4:1), 47 stocks +4% vs 2 at -4%, T2108 up to 71.0% from 65.0%, and 69.2% of SPX names above their 50MA. On 05/11 only 73 names advanced. This is a breadth thrust — but inside a Bearish regime. Thrusts that start recoveries need follow-through within 3–5 sessions; Tuesday's CPI is the immediate test.

The bond market is the caution flag: 2-year +5bp to 4.57%, December hike odds 85% despite the oil collapse. Hot CPI breaks the oil→rates transmission and exposes Monday's cyclical leaders (airlines, small caps, homebuilders); cool CPI gives the thrust a macro anchor.
