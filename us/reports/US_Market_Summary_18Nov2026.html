05📝 Session Summary

Wednesday delivered a fifth straight SPX loss and a second consecutive close below the 200-day MA (-0.31%), but internals improved sharply beneath a mixed tape. Three forces collided: a second day of regulatory panic in managed care, hawkish Fed minutes, and a powerful idiosyncratic bid in cybersecurity — all into the evening pricing of Anthropic's record IPO.

Healthcare was the epicentre. CMS's 16 November proposal to codify and widen Medicare Advantage audits — with clawbacks reaching back to 2018 — turned from a one-day shock into a liability repricing. UNH -10.21% and HUM -12.98% took two-day losses to roughly 14% each; ELV -7.63%, CVS -5.20% and HCA -4.96% show the selling spreading from payors into providers. Until insurers can size the clawback, analysts can't model 2027 EPS — that uncertainty is what's being sold. Smart Money registers Managed Healthcare as an EXIT (-66 ranks to rank 71).

The minutes added a macro layer. Real assets took the hit: gold -3.22%, silver -4.55%, copper -3.60%, with miners falling about twice as far (COPX -6.31%, GDX -5.97%, SIL -5.89%, FCX -6.96%, NEM -6.05%). Gold's Smart Money rank collapsed 97 places to rank 115 — the hedge trade is being unwound as real-rate expectations reprice higher.

The bid came from cyber. DHS warnings on AI-powered attacks and a CISA emergency patch order for "Java4Shell" drove CIBR +4.72% — the only ETF above +3% — with OKTA +12.07%, PANW +9.90%, AKAM +8.34%, CRWD +7.95% and FTNT +5.87%; PANW then beat and raised. Retail earnings (TJX +8.05%, TGT +4.01%) made XLY (+1.79%) the top SPDR sector.

Breadth was the quiet positive — SPX Adv/Dec 235/264 from 75/424 Tuesday, RUT a positive 984/926 — but SPX new 20-day lows outnumber highs 63 to 11 and only 40.4% of members sit above their 20MA. Selling exhaustion in the broad tape, not accumulation.
