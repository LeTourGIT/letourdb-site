04Session Summary

Selling broadened across Wall Street into Nvidia's print and the S&P 500 closed at its lowest level since July, slipping under its 200day MA for the first time since April. All 11 SPDR sectors finished red, SPX and NDX logged a fourth straight loss, and the Dow fell roughly 850 points as price-weighted software and healthcare components sank.

The epicentre was enterprise software, not chips. Salesforce -9.45% (worst day since May 2024), Intuit -7.17%, ServiceNow -6.51%, Atlassian -5.98%, Workday -5.80%, MongoDB -5.77% and Datadog -4.44% — IGV -3.29% breached the -3% flag. Two forces compound: AI-disruption risk to seat-based models, and a liquidity event — Anthropic prices its IPO Wednesday, and the capital is being raised by trimming the software names most exposed to disruption. A rotation within AI, not out of it.

The split inside XLK proves it: SOXX +0.17% held flat while software cratered, and eight of the 15 top gainers are AI hardware (ALAB, COHR, CIEN, CRDO, LITE, AVGO, MRVL, GLW) with data-centre builders FIX and PWR alongside. Capital pre-positioned in picks-and-shovels while dumping the application layer — NVDA's +4.82% after-hours validated it within minutes of the close.

Macro offered no cover. Musalem's four-hike comment hit bond proxies (XLU -1.59%, XLRE -1.49%), Financials were worst (XLF -1.91%) on neocloud-credit fears and bank outages, and only Energy held up (XLE -0.52%) as Brent rose 1.55% on the third US carrier arriving in the Gulf.
