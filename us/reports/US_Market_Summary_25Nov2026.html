04Session Summary

The AI unwind resumed at the open — Nvidia fell as much as 8%, the Nasdaq Composite was down 4.6% and the Dow down 1,105 points (−2.2%) at the lows. By the close the Dow had flipped to +164 (+0.33%) at 50,182.95, the S&P 500 finished −0.25% and the Nasdaq −0.62%. A full-range reversal of that size into a holiday is a washout signature: Russell 2000 new 20day lows doubled to 406, yet RUT closed +0.71%. Sellers were exhausted, not finished.

What drove the turn was rotation, not rescue. AI leaders never recovered (NVDA −3.91%, MU −4.53%, ORCL −5.13%, SNDK −5.57%, LRCX −5.68%); capital moved into materials, real estate, energy, small caps and bonds. That is why the S&P 500 closed red with 370 advancers against 127 decliners — a 2.9:1 positive ratio on a down index. Equal-weight RSP gained +0.83% against IVV −0.27%: a 1.1pt equal-vs-cap spread, almost entirely explained by NVDA ($54.7B turnover), MU ($31.9B) and AMZN ($15.3B). It is the reverse of the narrow-ATH breadth warnings of the spring: the average stock is healing while the generals are being shot.

The geopolitical overlay kept energy and metals bid. Iran said three tankers hit mines in Hormuz as Treasury widened sanctions, and the Houthis claimed strikes on a Saudi refinery. Brent settled +3.51% at $94.61. Oil up plus a near-certain December hike squeezes AI multiples from both sides — a higher discount rate and higher input costs.

Smart Money confirms the price action. Semi Equipment (41 → 127), Semiconductors (78 → 128), Computer Hardware (42 → 111) and Construction & Engineering (20 → 77) all printed ↓↓ EXIT within two to three sessions, while Homebuilding (76 → 29), Forest & Wood (68 → 27) and Biotech (116 → 74) printed ↑↑ SURGE. The RS Top 20 holds zero semiconductors and 12 healthcare names. The AI-capex trade has handed leadership to housing, defensives and drug development.

Posture into a half-day Friday: the reversal bar is constructive for equal-weight and small caps, but all four indices remain Bearish with NDX more than 11% below its 50MA. The after-hours bid in SNDK, NBIS and CORZ says the knife-catchers are back; on thin Thanksgiving liquidity that can produce a sharp but untrustworthy AI bounce. Respect the regime until NDX reclaims its 20MA.
