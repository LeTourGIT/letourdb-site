📝 Session Summary

Thursday was a rates-driven, high-beta risk-on session. Governor Waller indicated support for holding steady (no hike) and VP Vance pressed for cuts. Yields fell, VIX collapsed −5.79% to 14.32, and capital rotated hard into the longest-duration assets on the board — crypto, software, and rate-sensitive financials and REITs. MarketWatch's roundups said it in a line: Tech, Financials, Consumer and Utilities all "up as rate fears recede."

Crypto was the loudest signal. IBIT +5.85% and ETHA +5.37% both cleared the ±5% EXTREME threshold and the equity proxies went vertical: MSTR +17.56%, HOOD +16.57%, CRCL +16.46%, COIN +10.14%, WULF +9.51%. This is a liquidity story, not a coin story — falling real yields plus a −5.79% VIX is the exact cocktail that reprices the highest-beta risk. Smart Money confirms it: Miscellaneous Fintech Infrastructure is entrenched at rank 1, up from rank 139 nine days ago — the fastest structural rotation in the file.

Software split the tape. SNOW +16.55% delivered the cleanest AI-monetisation print of the season and dragged IGV +3.41% with PLTR +7.71%, NOW +6.49%, ORCL +5.69% and CRWD +5.68%. But the same session saw the chip rebound fail: AVGO −2.74% sold a good number and CIEN −10.36% guided optical down, taking LITE and AAOI with it. The message is a rotation up the stack — out of merchant silicon and optical hardware, into asset-light software and application-layer AI.

Defensives were dumped. Food processing was the worst pocket (CPB −6.96%, GIS −3.25%, KHC −3.20%) and Consumer Staples was one of only three red sectors alongside Materials and Energy. When staples and energy are the only things falling on a +1% day, the market wants beta, not shelter.

But the internals carry a warning the headline indices hide. Advancers beat decliners cleanly (SPX 337/161), yet new 20day lows outnumbered new 20day highs on every index (SPX 55/73, NDX 15/24, RUT 157/194), and T2108 sits below 50 on SPX (49.4%), NDX (46.5%) and RUT (47.5%). A rally where more stocks make fresh lows than fresh highs is carried by the biggest, most liquid names re-rating — precisely the crypto/software beta that led. Combined with negative 15day ROC and Neutral regime tags, this is a powerful but not-yet-confirmed bounce. Friday's August jobs report is the binary catalyst.
