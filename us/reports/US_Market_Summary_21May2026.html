📝 Session Summary

Thursday's session delivered two competing earnings arcs arriving simultaneously: Nvidia's $81.6B Q1 FY27 record (+85% YoY), with a $91.0B Q2 guide, drove NDX +0.93% through the session. Against this, Walmart in-line, Deere's ag-cycle disappointment (2027 recovery), and Intuit's 17% workforce cut collectively pressed the broader market into a narrow but positive advance across all four indices. The NDX +0.93% vs Dow +0.20% spread is the AI-capex bifurcation in its purest expression.

The session's institutional surprise was clean energy. TAN +3.08% and ICLN +3.07% were the only two ETFs above +3% — the first simultaneous clean energy dual-breach of this cycle. FCEL +30.46% hit a new 20day high in the RS MOMO list (#12). Smart Money Renewable Energy Equipment surged to #6 (from #11 yesterday). This is Hormuz-resolution positioning accelerating: capital is pre-building clean energy exposure for the world after the blockade lifts, regardless of resolution timeline. The structural energy transition narrative is returning to the foreground as the Iran conflict extends.

USA Rare Earth (USAR) surged ~20%+ after the DOE selected it for up to $19.3M in Critical Materials Innovation funding for domestic REE separations. The sovereign validation — combined with the pending Serra Verde acquisition ($2.8B, Q3 2026 close) — transitions USAR from exploration-stage to government-funded processing infrastructure. REMX +0.55% today but 5day −4.16% (China rare-earth agreement noise from last week); the DOE catalyst is the structural floor signal, not the China diplomacy headline. Mining Support Services holds #1 in Smart Money for nine consecutive days at ROC +45.62% — this is the broadest confirmation of the domestic critical minerals thesis.

On the Iran front: a bogus draft-resolution report briefly lifted equities mid-session (Russell 2000 got the spike), but energy correctly saw through it. XLE −1.12%, XOP −2.34%. Iran's proposal to decouple Hormuz reopening from nuclear talks was rejected by Washington as removing leverage; the stalemate deepens. Oil remains structurally elevated and Hormuz remains contested. The 5day XLE +1.83% confirms Smart Money hasn't exited energy — today's negative is intraday noise, not a structural rotation out.

SPX breadth improved materially versus the April 1 ATH: 285 advancers vs 211 decliners (1.35:1 positive), Stocks +4% today = 14 vs 7 down >4%. This is a qualitatively healthier session than the 172/326 cap-weight distortion of 01 May. T2108 at 50.2% is borderline — not the confirmation of a sustained breakout, but not the deterioration of a distribution phase either.
