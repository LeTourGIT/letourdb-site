Session Summary

Two forces defined the session and neither showed in the index close. Above the tape: Nvidia's earnings. Below it: a macro regime hardening around sticky inflation, a hawkish Fed, and the highest long-end yields since 2007.

NVDA delivered an objectively extraordinary quarter and still fell — the defining paradox of late-cycle AI. The market's "next question" was answered after the bell by a software and cybersecurity blowout: Okta +19%, Salesforce +13%, CrowdStrike +11%, Nutanix +9%, Veeva +8%, Palo Alto +5%, ServiceNow +4%. Capital is migrating from the AI-infrastructure layer to the AI-application and security layer, where monetization now shows up in real enterprise P&Ls.

Intraday confirmed it: the speculative AI-power/quantum complex was dumped (IREN -6%, OKLO -6%, IONQ -5%) while cyclicals led — Industrials +1.09%, Energy +0.60% — and long-duration defensives sagged (Healthcare -1.00% worst). This is a rates-regime trade, not a growth scare.

The third force is geopolitical. The US-Iran conflict is in its sixth month; Bessent threatens an "economic D-Day" of sanctions while Iran threatens vessel seizures in Hormuz. Tanker rates and war-risk insurance are spiking — which is why Smart Money is pouring into shipping and transport, not crude. WTI finished +1.94% at $81.83 on a two-sided tape.
