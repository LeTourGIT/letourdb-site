04Session Summary

Two markets shared one ticker. NDX -1.39% to 27,989.82 confirmed a correction, 10.4% below its 06/10/2026 record. Yet SPX breadth ran 345/154 and RSP rose 0.77%. The index lost 0.52% only because its heaviest weights, AI hardware and AI financing, were liquidated again. This was a rotation day, not a risk-off day.

Geopolitics came first. Iran-Qatar talks reached day three, and WTI fell 4.06% to $96.69 (USO -7.22%). The chain ran cleanly: crude down, then hike-risk down, then long Treasuries bid, then fuel- and rate-sensitives up. ITB +4.56%, JETS +4.16%, UAL +4.80%, SHW +5.94% and CRH +6.36% led. Energy mirrored it: XLE -3.96%, with OIH -5.48% and XOP -5.01% EXTREME.

AI broke on deal structure and financing, not demand. In MRVL's all-stock LITE deal (4.5 shares per LITE), MRVL fell 12.72% and LITE 18.59%. ORCL fell 8.76% on a $20B raise. INTC -8.80%, AMD -7.52% and SOXX -3.79% extend a roughly 12% three-day semi drawdown. AMD, INTC, MRVL and LITE were the four heaviest tapes on the 16d list ($50B combined), all losers: institutional distribution.

The arb tells the story. 4.5 x MRVL $171.41 = about $771, against LITE's $684.40 close, a 12.7% spread. The announced value of about $1,132 has deflated roughly 32% as the acquirer currency collapsed. The market is saying Marvell overpaid, and it is pricing a long regulatory path.

After the close came the counter-argument. FN +10.42%, AEIS +9.74% and LSCC +8.93% beat in the very supply chain that was sold. AAPL fell 2.81% AH on a 45-46% margin guide; PLTR fell 2.99% AH. AI orders are intact; multiples and financing are not. With the midterms on Tuesday and VIX at 21.15, a peace deal extends the rotation, while a talks breakdown snaps it back.
