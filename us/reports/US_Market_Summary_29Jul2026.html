04Session Summary

A hawkish-by-omission FOMC detonated the market's most crowded trade. With inflation still sticky, holding the funds rate was read as the Fed falling behind — Gundlach flagged the bond market signalling exactly that — and the Dow shed ~1,100 points for its worst session since April 2025. Crucially this was not cap-weight distortion: SPX breadth was 179 advancers to 321 decliners and the Russell was a rout at 558/1366. Everything with a multiple and a capex story was sold.

The epicentre was semiconductors and AI-infrastructure. SOXX -5.38% and XSD -4.68% led an eleven-deep wall of sub-3% ETF breaches. KLAC -10.8%, MU -9.9% (dragged by SK Hynix's fumbled memory-cycle guidance), ENTG -9.4%, MKSI -9.7% and SMCI -9.7% were gutted, while VRT -17.3% and the crypto-miner complex (IREN -13.6%, APLD -12.8%, WULF -11.7%, CRWV -9.6%) confirmed the market is repricing the entire AI power-and-datacentre buildout. When the discount rate stops falling, the longest-duration cash flows get marked down first.

The mirror image was a textbook value rotation — CNBC literally ran "sell semis, buy ketchup." XLE +1.88% and XLP +0.34% were the only green sectors while XLK -2.64% and XLI -3.19% anchored the board. The names that rose were beat-and-raise, lower-beta compounders (MANH +21%, GRMN +16%, GEHC +12%, CTSH +11%) plus a de-rated software cohort (INTU, ADBE, DDOG, WDAY, NOW, SNOW all +4–6%). Money didn't leave equities — it moved down the beta and duration curve.

Geopolitics amplified the reflation fear rather than starting it. Iran attacked again and Trump promised a "beating," but parallel Hormuz de-escalation calls with Saudi Arabia and Oman kept it two-way. WTI +2.36% to $84.66 pushed XOP +3.41% — the only equity ETF above +3% — while gold +2.53% did its safe-haven job. Higher oil into a Fed-behind-the-curve narrative is the worst macro cocktail for long-duration tech, which is exactly how the tape traded.
