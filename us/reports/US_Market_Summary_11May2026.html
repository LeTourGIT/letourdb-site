⚠ BREADTH DIVERGENCE — SPX ATH at 7,412.84 with only 214 advancers vs 286 decliners (negative ratio). Only 41.8% of SPX constituents trade above their 20-day MA. Fourth negative Adv/Dec in five sessions at ATH.

Monday's session delivered the paradox that defines this bull market: new ATH closes alongside VIX +6.92%, negative breadth, and oil rising on geopolitical deterioration. SPX gained 0.19% to 7,412.84 — its third consecutive ATH close — while VIX jumped 6.92% to 18.38. These two facts co-existing encode the market's current message: semiconductor and AI-platform momentum is strong enough to overwhelm Iran risk, but that risk is not going away.

The catalyst architecture was semiconductor-led. XSD +3.85% and SOXX +2.39% were the session's only ±3% ETF flags. Smart Money rank #1 Semiconductors carries a 10day ROC of +22.43% — the single highest institutional capital accumulation rate of any industry group in the market. This is a capex cycle call validated across all sub-layers: memory (MRAM +47.68% on $40M DoD subcontract, MU +6.5%), compute (AMD, INTC +3.62%), handset AI (QCOM +8.42%), and optical (AAOI +24.14%). The Pentagon's DoD spending is now visible from hyperscaler AI (NVDA/MSFT/AMZN last Friday) all the way down to niche magnetoresistive memory (MRAM today) — the supply chain depth of government AI demand is being revealed one session at a time.

Monday.com's +21% provided the software counterpart. MNDY's Q1 print (revenue $351M +24% YoY, EPS $1.15 vs $0.93 estimated, AI driving 10% of net new ARR, record $49M operating profit) is the most important enterprise software datapoint of the day — not for its size, but because it extends the AI-additive pattern. Apple Friday, Five9 last Friday, Monday.com today: three consecutive sessions of an AI-is-additive-not-disruptive earnings print from enterprise software. The statistical pattern is now sufficient for institutional re-pricing of the SaaS disruption risk premium. BAND, RXT, and INOD all moved in sympathy.

The geopolitical backdrop hardened significantly. Trump declared the ceasefire "on life support" and called Iran's counter-proposal "TOTALLY UNACCEPTABLE," reversing Friday's peace-premium oil drop. WTI rose approximately 2% intraday. JETS -3.17% and XRT -3.61% are the honest read of Main Street embedded within Wall Street's ATH — airlines and retail face direct margin compression from sustained high oil and cautious consumers (DG -5.8% guidance miss). The Smart Money Gold SURGE (from rank #136 to #10 in nine days) and silver's +6.83% confirm institutional repositioning for Iran war duration, not resolution.
