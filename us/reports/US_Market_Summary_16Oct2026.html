04Session Summary

Friday ended the week the way it started: AI longs being liquidated. Stocks opened higher, then the selling built into the final hour. SPX lost 1.09% to 7,659 and the NDX shed 2.50% to 29,188. The VIX ripped 21.45% to 19.99 after an intraday 22.31, its highest since June. MarketWatch calls it the "AI burnout trade", but the mechanism is more specific than fatigue: it is funding cost. AI infrastructure has been built on debt, and with the 10-year above 5% this month for the first time since 2002, credit markets have started to price that risk. CoreWeave's credit default swaps widened all week, and the stock fell 11.91% on Friday (5day -25.21%).

Trace the chain. Neocloud and AI-hosting names fell hardest, because their equity sits beneath the most leverage: CRWV -11.91%, APLD -11.48%, IREN -11.19%, WULF -7.45%. Next came the hardware supply chain that sells to them: SMCI -9.86%, AAOI -9.75%, FLEX -8.16%, DELL -4.58%, VRT -3.88%. Then the semis: MU -5.63%, AMD -5.95%, INTC -5.79%, AVGO -4.52%. SOXX -4.14% made a ninth straight daily decline, the first such streak since 2006. Software did not escape. IGV -4.80% was the session's worst ETF, with TWLO -10.62%, OKTA -10.20%, SNOW -8.43%, PANW -7.63% and CRWD -7.02%. The application layer is no longer a refuge from the infrastructure layer.

The 9day Smart Money data shows the scale. Semiconductor Equipment & Testing was the top-ranked industry nine sessions ago and is now rank 137 of 139. Semiconductors went from rank 6 to rank 134, and Electronic Equipment from rank 11 to 128. The leadership table has been emptied top to bottom inside two weeks. Institutions that were overweight AI hardware have been distributing systematically.

Geopolitics supplied the other half of the tape. Iran escalated attacks in the Strait of Hormuz, Saudi Aramco facilities were hit earlier in the week, and Ukraine vowed to strike Russian refineries. Brent touched $110 and closed +4.31% at $109.69, with WTI +3.52% at $99.04. Capital went where it goes in a war tape: upstream energy (FANG +4.27%, EOG +3.53%, COP +3.37%, CVX +2.59%) and the defence primes (NOC +4.05%, RTX +3.73%, LMT +3.65%, LHX +3.33%). Ten of the top 15 institutional gainers came from those two groups.

The defining feature is the breadth mismatch. SPX fell 1.09%, yet advancers and decliners were dead even at 248/252. Equal-weight RSP fell only 0.42%, so the cap-weighted index underperformed it by 68bp. SPLV rose 1.04%, XLV traded at record highs intraday, and Visa and Mastercard hit records. SPX logged 171 new 20day highs against only 31 new lows. This is a megacap AI drawdown masking a rotation into defensives that is working. For traders: keep AI longs light, own the rotation, and wait for NDX RSI-15 to reach the low 30s before buying the dip.
