04Session Summary

Tuesday ran on two shocks. October CPI came in a tenth hot on both headline (+0.4%) and core (+0.3%) — the third straight 0.3% core print. With Fed Governor Waller having said overnight that such a print puts a 50bp December hike on the table, the 2Y jumped 18bp to 4.75% and the 10Y briefly traded above 5%. That is a bear flattener, and bear flatteners de-rate long-duration growth first.

The second shock was Nvidia. The Huang / suspected Chinese intelligence story has added a governance discount and fresh China-export policy risk, with Congress signalling tighter China sales. NVDA fell -5.47% on $72.5B of turnover — by far the largest dollar flow in the market — and is now -18.29% over 5day. XLK -1.15% (20day -13.94%) did most of the index damage on its own, while AVGO +3.91% shows money rotating inside semis rather than abandoning AI.

Underneath, the market was close to flat. SPX Adv/Dec was 246/248 and RSP (equal weight) finished -0.03% against SPY -0.48%. More striking: 69.0% of S&P 500 stocks sit above their 50MA and T2108 is 71.0%, yet the cap-weighted SPX is 2.02% below its 50MA and in a Bearish regime. That is a reverse cap-weight divergence — the average stock is in an uptrend while the megacap layer is in a downtrend. Over 20day RPV (pure value) is +3.71% and IWF (growth) -10.31%: a 14-point leadership swap in four weeks.

Where the money went: insurers (IAK/KIE +1.51%) and healthcare providers (IHF +1.41%) led the ETF board. Higher short rates lift insurers' float income, and managed care has become the market's preferred defensive growth. Smart Money confirms it — Reinsurance SURGED from rank 88 to 16 in three days, Personal Products from 85 to 4, and 10day leadership is travel and consumer (Airlines rank 1, Hotels & Cruise rank 6, Airport Operators rank 8), which benefit directly from cheaper fuel.

Where it left: every inflation and war hedge. GDX and SIL -5.43% (⚠ EXTREME), SLV -3.45% and gold futures -2.02%. Gold falling on a hot CPI reads as real yields up plus the Iran war premium fading. Precious Metals, Integrated Mining, Uranium and Marine Port Services all dropped out of Smart Money with ↓↓ EXIT signals, and long-duration themes (TAN -3.70%, ITB -3.28%, ETHA -3.27%) were sold for the same discount-rate reason.

After the bell CoreWeave beat and grew its backlog to ~$132.5B (+7.90% AH), reconfirming AI compute demand. Wednesday's test is whether NVDA can separate a governance problem from a demand story that CRWV just validated — and whether SPX 50MA participation holds near 69%.
