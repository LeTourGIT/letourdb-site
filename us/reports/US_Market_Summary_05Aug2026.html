Session Summary

Wednesday was a macro-driven regime rotation. ADP private payrolls printed just +44k for July vs +70k expected — the weakest in six months, down from +98k in June. JOLTS had already softened, and ISM Services came in at 54.1 with its employment sub-index in contraction (47.4). September rate-HIKE odds collapsed from ~67% to ~57%. Direction matters: under Chair Warsh (funds held 3.50-3.75% on 29 July, 9-3), the debate is hike-or-hold, so a soft print simply removes the next hike rather than pulling forward easing.
That repricing detonated precious metals. Gold +4% to ~$4,234 — biggest one-day since February — silver +3.9% to a six-week high. The equity beta was violent: GDX +7.39% and SIL +6.59% both cleared the +5% EXTREME line, with SLV/GLD +4.14%, GLTR +3.97%, XME +3.76%, COPX +3.25% completing a seven-ETF sweep. In the cash tape CDE +7.53%, HL +7.47% and NEM +6.71% led the gainers. Softer real yields + weaker dollar + easing oil inflation is the textbook bullion cocktail — expressed through the miners' beta.
The mirror image was megacap growth and semis as the funding source. AMD -7.04% on heavy turnover led a broad chip de-rate (ALAB -11.96%, LSCC -7.02%, XSD -3.12%, SOXX -2.12%), dragging NDX Bearish with its 20day trend flipping Down. VIX fell 4.18% — this is capital leaving duration-like growth for cyclicals, miners and value, which is exactly why the Dow printed a record even as SPX and RUT slipped.
Oil supplies the disinflation tailwind: Trump flagged "very good discussions" with Iran and a possible deal Wednesday, Hormuz talks "in final stages," crude ~-10% on the week despite a +0.55% WTI bounce. Falling energy costs feed the gold and rate-cut trade — but gutted energy equities (XOP -3.93%, IEO -3.56%, EOG -6.47%, DVN -4.45%). Oil up, oil stocks down = the tape pricing resolution, not the barrel. The catch: job-switcher wages accelerated to +7% (from 6.6%), a stagflation tension keeping Governor Cook "prepared to act." Friday's NFP (~+80k) is the fulcrum.
