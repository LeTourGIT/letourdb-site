Session Summary

Thursday delivered a broad, high-velocity relief rally. NDX led +1.73%, SPX +1.14%, with the VIX collapsing -12.82% to 15.44 — the single loudest signal of the day. A vol crush of this size is the market exhaling: 'peak fear' from the prior selloff and the Iran-oil premium is being priced out. Stocks rose on hopes rising inflation and oil prices are now contained, with WTI easing -1.06% to $101.19 despite continued Middle East escalation. Oil bid but capped is the goldilocks the tape wanted.

Semiconductors were the engine. XSD +4.06% and SOXX +3.39% led the ETF board, and the single-stock tape confirmed conviction: INTC +7.67% on $16.1B turnover (the day's heaviest by far), SMCI +9.5%, ALAB +9.06%, SWKS +6.69%, HPE +7.69%. Jensen Huang says NVDA will sell twice as many chips next year. Smart Money confirms the flow — Semiconductors rocketed to rank 13 (from rank 124 nine days ago) and Semi Equipment to rank 15 (from 136): a dated re-accumulation of the chip complex off oversold, not a one-day squeeze.

The second leg was precious metals. SIL +4.49%, SLV +3.37%, GDX +3.36% all cleared +3%, with silver (+2.35%) outrunning gold (+1.05%) — the late-cycle catch-up Morgan Stanley flagged. Metals rallying alongside a risk-on tape and a VIX crush is unusual — it says the bid is liquidity/debasement-driven, not a fear hedge. A power/energy-transition micro-theme rode shotgun: GNRC +18.3% (Smart Money's rank-1 Heavy Electrical Equipment), OKLO +11.3% and URA +3.17% (nuclear/uranium), TAN +3.9% (solar) — the AI-datacenter-power build expressed from generators to reactors.

Underneath the green, a decisive rotation ran the other way. Software and IT-services were sold into strength: CRM -3.07%, CRWV -4.16%, SPOT -3.4%, RDDT -3.03%. Capital is rotating out of software and into semiconductors — the mirror image of a software-surge session. Defensive telecom was dumped outright: TMUS -5.57%, CMCSA -3.46%, VZ -2.87%, T -1.82% — dragging XLC to the only meaningfully red sector. Quietly, 13 of the RS Top 20 are healthcare biotech (MRNA, MNOV, IOVA, CYPH, TWST) with TEM +14.85% and ILMN +7.1% leading intraday — the accumulation nobody is talking about while everyone watches chips.

The critical caveat: breadth improved on the day (SPX adv/dec 291/206, ~1.4:1) but the medium-term damage is intact. SPX has just 21% of members above their 20MA, 30.6% above the 50MA, T2108 at 27.8% — deeply oversold — and new 20-day lows still outnumber highs 87 to 25 (RUT: 246 to 110). An oversold bounce with real one-day participation, layered over a market that has not repaired its trend. Respect the bounce; do not yet call it a bottom.
