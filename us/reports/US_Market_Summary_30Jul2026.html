Session Summary

Thursday was a violent one-day relief rally stacked on a still-broken trend. Two catalysts did the work: Microsoft's cloud/AI earnings blowout (+15.5%, a $3.4T name) and a snap-back in the semiconductor complex that had been the epicentre of the recent drawdown. SOXX +8.50% and XSD +7.48% led all ETFs — yet both remain deeply negative on 5day (-8.5%/-7.7%) and 20day (-15.9%/-19.0%). This is an oversold bounce in real time, not a base breakout.

The mechanics matter. NDX ripped +3.36% while its own advance/decline split was a coin-flip 51/50, and SPX rose +1.66% on negative breadth (197 adv / 301 dec). A cap-weight melt-up: MSFT plus mega-cap semis (MU +18.4%, LRCX +18.0%, SNDK +26.0%, ALAB +20.0%) mathematically carried the tape while the median stock stalled. META -7.95% proves leadership is selective even inside big tech.

The most important tell is the VIX: down -17.2% from 20.63 to 17.09. Volatility above 20 into this session means the prior tape was genuinely fearful — an Iran escalation scare and a contentious Fed hold had lifted the premium. Thursday unwound it as the US-Iran conflict paused for talks and WTI held calm at $83.89. Falling oil + falling VIX + rising mega-cap tech is the textbook relief-rally signature.

Underneath the tech headline, a genuine hard-asset rotation broadened. Gold +1.11%, Silver +2.50%, and the miners (GDX +4.36%, SIL +4.46%, XME +4.39%, COPX +4.41%) all bid, and Smart Money confirms it: Gold vaulted to #9 (from #133 nine days ago), Precious Metals to #10, Integrated Mining to #14, Uranium to #15. Alongside Renewable Energy surging to #2 and FinTech/crypto to #1 (IREN +30.5% led all gainers), the capital-flow story is a rotation into real assets and the energy transition — arguably a more durable signal than the one-day chip squeeze.
