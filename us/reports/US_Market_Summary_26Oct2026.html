04 · Session Summary

Two shocks pulled in opposite directions and the US tape chose AI. Anthropic’s S-1 (Nasdaq: ANT) disclosed hyper-growth — revenue of $4.6bn in 2025 and $16.3bn in H1 2026 alone — alongside steep losses, and that was enough to re-ignite an AI trade that had bled all month. The SOX gained ~2.8% but remains ~12% lower for October. At the same time Brazil delivered an emerging-market crash: Lula’s re-election sent the Ibovespa -13.88% with a circuit-breaker halt and the real -8.8%. The US absorbed it cleanly — damage stayed inside Brazil-linked ADRs (XP -48.36%, NU -16.95%, PBR -13.99%).

The composition of leadership tells you what kind of rally this was. The biggest tech gains came from the most beaten-down AI hardware names — LITE +8.19%, COHR +5.83%, TTMI +6.73%, AAOI +6.56% — many with negative 5day returns. SOXX (+2.85%) and XSD (+2.07%) carry 20day returns of -12.3% and -12.2%. That is short-covering into a catalyst, not fresh accumulation. The RS Top 20 contains zero semiconductors. Genuine accumulation is in software/cybersecurity (CIBR 5day +8.52%, IGV 5day +5.09%, RPD +20.25%, NTSK and ZS at new 20day highs) and biotech.

The underappreciated headline is Meta: -1.9% after flagging up to $95bn of new equity to fund AI data centres. Put it beside the Anthropic IPO, SpaceX’s AI-infrastructure plans and Microsoft’s 60,000-employee buyout offer, and AI is moving from free-cash-flow funding to capital-markets funding. That is bullish for suppliers — Celestica’s $40bn 2027 guide confirms the order book — but it is a late-cycle supply signal: more AI paper to absorb in the same week the Fed debates a hike.

Breadth confirmed the rally at the large-cap level: SPX advancers 289 vs decliners 208 (1.39:1), reversing Friday’s 135/364. T2108 rose to 66.8% from 63.2%. But the Russell tells the other story — 966/954 is a coin-flip, only 38.2% of members are above their 50MA, and 665 RUT stocks are down 25%+ for the quarter vs 318 up. Large caps are recovering; small caps are not.

Geopolitics remains the oil floor: Trump says he will soon decide whether to “make a deal” with Iran or “finish it off”, and Saudi Arabia intercepted Houthi missiles and drones. Brent held $109.82. Defence caught the bid — PPA +2.65%, ITA +2.44%, LMT +5.13% on a Morgan Stanley upgrade.

The week ahead is the most event-dense of the quarter: FOMC Wednesday 28/10 (~12.7% hike odds), GDP and inflation Thursday, MSFT/GOOGL/AMZN earnings, a hawkish-hold BoJ, then US midterms on 03/11. Monday bought the bounce. It did not repair a single regime.
