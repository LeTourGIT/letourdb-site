Session Summary

Thursday delivered a fresh S&P 500 record — the first close above 7,800 (7,798.99, +0.65%) — on a clean disinflation catalyst rather than a mega-cap melt-up. July CPI eased for a second straight month to 3.4% YoY and PPI cooled more than expected to 4.7% (helped by lower energy and food), pushing money-market odds of a September Fed hike down to ~40% from ~50%. In a Fed debating whether to tighten further — not cut — "less-bad inflation" is the risk-on trigger, and it fired straight into long-duration growth and rate-sensitive assets at once.

The session's signature was a software + storage surge paired with an AI-hardware fade — a genuine intramarket rotation, not a broad tech trade. IGV +3.10% was the only ETF above +3%. WDAY +17.78% (best day on record) ripped on Silver Lake take-private talks; HUBS +14.46% on a double beat plus a $1B buyback; and the move spread across MDB +7.87%, PATH +9.31%, TTD +7.93%, TEAM +6.92%, NET +6.22%, INTU +7.04% and FIG +10.95%. Storage rode alongside (SNDK +13.67%, WDC +7.31%, HPQ +6.90%). Against that, the AI-infrastructure complex was sold: CSCO -8.40%, CBRS -11.85%, COHR -7.99%, LITE -5.58%, AAOI -5.79%, ANET -3.27% — and AMAT, which beat and guided 30%+ equipment growth, fell ~3% after hours as free cash flow collapsed ~80%. Capital is paying up for application-layer cash flows while punishing any blemish in AI capex names that have already doubled.

Crucially this was a broad advance. SPX breadth ran 316 advancers vs 183 decliners with 87 new 20day highs against just 10 new lows, T2108 at 66.4% and 68.8% of members above their 20day MA; the Russell 2000 confirmed with 1,139 advancers and 316 new highs. This is the opposite of the fragile mega-cap ATHs seen earlier in the cycle — which is why no index is overbought even at a record.

The rate-relief signal reordered the sector board in a rate-sensitive direction: XLC +2.07% led (NFLX +5.43% on Ackman's stake, plus Meta), with XLRE +1.42% and XLP +1.08% outrunning XLK +1.01%. The laggard was the metals complex — XLB -0.51% worst as COPX -3.15%, GDX -2.96% and SIL -2.88% were dumped and gold slipped ~1.2% off a 10-week high. That reversal sits in direct tension with the 10day Smart Money board, where Mining and Precious Metals still dominate — a hot rotation taking a session breather while software takes the baton.

The macro tape was not uniformly benign. Even as front-end hike odds fell, the US sold 30-year bonds at the highest yield in roughly a quarter century — a deficit-driven long-end warning that keeps NDX volatility Elevated. Oil kept falling (WTI $81.25, Brent $87.07, both -2%+) on soft demand even as the US-Iran war grinds on, yet energy equities held up (XLE +0.05%, 5day +4.99%). Net: a healthy, broad record close with a clean rotation underneath — but with long-end yields and an unresolved war as the two live tail risks into next week.
