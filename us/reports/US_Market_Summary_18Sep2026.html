Session Summary

Friday was a semiconductor rebound bolted onto a deteriorating tape. The S&P closed +0.17% and the Nasdaq 100 +0.67% — but the average stock fell hard. SPX advancers 155 vs 344 decliners, Russell internals 688/1220, T2108 at just 23.6%, and only 19% of SPX names above their 20-day MA. A textbook cap-weight illusion: a handful of mega-cap chips hold the index green while breadth implodes.

The macro spine is the 16 September FOMC. The Fed hiked 25bp to 3.75–4.00% — first hike since 2023, unanimous 12-0, dot-plot signalling one more this year. The 10-year pushed to ~5%, highest since 2007. Rising rates repriced the entire value complex: utilities −1.42%, materials −1.42%, homebuilders (LEN −4.10%), steel (NUE −6.32%, STLD −4.11%). That is why RUT and DJI flipped Bearish while NDX clings to Bullish — duration-light mega-cap tech is the only hiding place from the yield move.

The day's leadership was memory/storage, and the catalyst is concrete. SanDisk +10.99% led the entire market on $31.8B turnover — the session's single largest — ahead of its S&P 100 inclusion effective 21 September (with Dell, Palo Alto, Arista), a forced-buying event for index funds. Layered on: spot NAND +~5% on the week as Nvidia's Vera-Rubin ramp tightens supply, plus SanDisk's Investor-Day 80% gross-margin / $93.9B backlog thesis. WDC, STX, MU, MRVL and LITE rode sympathy. Semiconductor equipment joined hard — LRCX +6.98%, AMAT +6.51%, TER +5.19%, ONTO +5.36% — but all with negative 5-day numbers: a violent mean-reversion bounce off oversold, not fresh accumulation. QCOM −5.82% is the tell — this was a memory/equipment/optical bid, not an all-chips bid.

Smart Money confirms the rotation was pre-positioned: Semiconductor Equipment surged to rank 4 from 9-day rank 112 (+108) and Semiconductors to 8 from 101 (+93). Institutions built the chip position for over a week; Friday was the payoff. Yet the structural RS list runs a different clock — the RS Top 25 is dominated by biotech/pharma/medical (TEVA, MRNA, CYPH, IOVA, TWST, NEO), and today's chip movers are absent from it. A defensive structural core alongside a cyclical bounce.

Geopolitics cut the other way for energy: Iran halted strikes on Israel and Trump signalled the war is nearing an end — WTI −5.63% to $95.47, Brent −4.96%. The war-risk premium is bleeding out of crude even as the Fed hikes on that same energy-inflation. Crypto ripped on the risk-on tone (VIX −4.08% to 14.81): ETHA +7.85%, IBIT +6.28%, MSTR +16.39%, COIN +11.66% — real rotation (Blockchain to Smart Money rank 3), but concentrated; the median stock never joined.
