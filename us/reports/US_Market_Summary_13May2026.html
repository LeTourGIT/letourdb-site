📝 Session Summary

Wednesday's session was a paradox: SPX and NDX printed fresh ATHs while being systematically sold beneath the surface. The catalyst pairing was extraordinary — a double-barrelled inflation shock (CPI +3.8% YoY Tuesday, PPI +6.0% YoY Wednesday — the latter its worst print since 2022) sent the 10Y Treasury to 4.47% and pushed the 20Y and 30Y back above 5%. In any other market environment, that macro backdrop strangles equity multiples. Instead, SPX rallied +0.58% and NDX +1.20%. Markets are front-running a geopolitical resolution narrative: President Trump and NVDA CEO Jensen Huang arrived in Beijing for the Trump-Xi state visit, with AI export controls and Hormuz resolution both on the diplomatic agenda. Markets are pricing the resolution premium, not the current inflation data.

The earnings side amplified the bifurcation. BABA delivered cloud revenue of $6.13 billion (+38% YoY) alongside a surprise $2.5 billion dividend and a commitment to exceed its $55 billion AI investment plan. Nebius added confirmation: AI infrastructure revenue at $399 million (eightfold year-over-year) validated the hyperscale demand thesis that NVDA is expected to confirm next Wednesday (20 May). Cisco's after-hours print (+15% AH) completed the night — a 33% single-quarter guidance raise from a name that is now critical-path AI infrastructure.

The Semi complex continued its institutional accumulation. AAOI +18.49%, ATOM +18.44%, WOLF +16.53%, QUIK +14.59%, LWLG +14.34%, TSEM +22.61%. The 60day industry ROC for Semiconductors sits at +64.30% — the single most dominant structural ROC reading in the dashboard. The Trump-Jensen Huang China visit is a direct semi/AI export catalyst; the market is pricing a reduction of Export Control overhang as part of the diplomatic rapprochement.
