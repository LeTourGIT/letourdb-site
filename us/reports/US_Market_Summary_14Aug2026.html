04

Session Summary

Friday was a rotation dressed as a down day. The headline indices closed a hair red — SPX -0.17% to 7,785.76, NDX -0.13%, Dow -0.20% — but the internals tell the opposite story: Russell 2000 +0.51%, SPX advancers 248 vs 244 decliners, and VIX down 2.60% to 14.25. When the tape sells the index but not the market, the loss is a cap-weight illusion.

The stress is concentrated and diagnosable. AVGO -5.94% and AMAT -5.12% (earnings) led an AI-infrastructure de-rate, joined by a broad software/fintech unwind — ORCL -3.65%, WDAY -3.76%, TWLO -4.50%, NET -4.55%, PATH -4.02% — and a crypto flush (COIN -3.53%, CRCL -5.01%, HOOD -3.83%, MSTR -4.18%). This is the same cohort that took July's AI wreck; the positioning tape confirms the exit, with Appaloosa reportedly selling AI-memory while other desks bought. Crowded-trade unwind, not thesis break.

Underneath, the opposite trade went on with conviction. Energy XLE +1.39% led the SPDRs and the hard-asset complex ran hot: REMX rare earths +3.18% (the day's only +3% ETF), GDX gold miners +1.93%, XME metals +1.62%, OIH oil services +2.62%, MP Materials +5.53%. Gold +1.20% to $4,432, WTI +1.29% to $82.40. The catalyst is geopolitical: Iran is now framed as an indefinite war, the Strait of Hormuz remains blocked with traffic near three-month lows, and Washington is tightening the squeeze. Oil at $82 sits far below May's $102, but the direction has re-inverted — escalation is again the marginal price-setter.

The most important nuance is inside semiconductors. This was not a blanket chip selloff. Equal-weight XSD +1.72%, AMD +6.50%, and memory — SNDK +7.39% (5day +35%), WDC +4.41% — rallied hard even as cap-weight SOXX finished flat and AVGO/AMAT bled. Capital is rotating from the AI-infrastructure mega-caps toward memory/storage and second-tier logic. Both the RS and Smart Money lists corroborate: Computer Hardware sits at Smart Money rank 9, the only tech-adjacent industry in the top 10.
