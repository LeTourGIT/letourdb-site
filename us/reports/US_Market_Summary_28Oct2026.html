04Session Summary

Wednesday was an FOMC day that equities treated as a sideshow. The Fed held at 3.75–4.00% by 10–2, with Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland’s Beth Hammack both dissenting for an immediate hike. The statement upgraded growth to “expanding at a strong pace”, and Warsh again refused forward guidance. That is a hawkish hold, with December live. The indices closed green anyway (SPX +0.27% to 7,743.98, NDX +0.55%) and VIX fell 3.48% to 16.91. The real reaction was in duration: XLU -0.84% and XLRE -0.73% were the worst two sectors, and ITB fell 1.51%.

The dominant story was AI capex paying the physical layer. Vertiv +16.48% after revenue +61%, organic orders more than doubling and a $650M guide raise. FLEX +11.61%, CLS +8.41%, SNDK +7.66%, MU +5.86%, ENTG +6.14%, AAOI +6.85%, LITE +5.52% and KLAC +3.33%, plus Oracle +10.73% on investor-day targets. Every dollar of hyperscaler capex is someone’s revenue line, and on Wednesday the market paid the recipients.

The mirror image was software, especially security. F5 fell 20.43% on guidance and a downgrade. FTNT fell 5.90% in session and then 25.71% AH on a beat-and-raise. NOW fell 3.00% and then 18.10% AH on a 2-cent miss, and CIBR lost 1.58%. The market is drawing a hard line between AI suppliers, which get paid, and seat-based or security software, which is de-rated on any blemish. Selling crowded winners on good news is distribution.

After the bell the AI ledger split in two. Alphabet’s Cloud grew 103%, but record capex of $51.4B and -$9.1B of free cash flow drew a ~10% AH drop. Microsoft’s Azure grew 48% with capex below Street, and the stock rose 2.88%. The market is pricing return on AI capital, not its size. Even so, GOOGL’s ~$300B 2027 capex path is the most bullish number of the night for VRT, MU and the optical chain.

Then came M&A. Bloomberg reported Marvell in advanced talks to buy Lumentum (~$83B): LITE +8.65% AH, MRVL -8.40% AH. Lumentum’s optical capacity is reportedly sold out into 2029, so designers are buying supply. Smart Money front-ran it: Communications & Networking jumped from rank 57 to rank 15 in a single session.

Breadth was thinner than the tape. SPX had 246 advancers against 254 decliners on a +0.27% close. New 20day lows more than doubled (17 → 37) while new highs rose (65 → 80). Both tails expanding points to earnings-driven dispersion, not a broad bid. The SPX regime upgraded to Neutral Bull, while NDX remains Neutral Bear and sits on its 50MA into GOOGL’s gap.
