Session summary

macro → sector → stock

Thursday was about the AI trade's revenue denominator. The FT and CNBC reported OpenAI's annualised revenue at September-end at about $50B, roughly $20B below the ~$68B the market had been using. OpenAI told investors the figure was "lower than widely reported". That matters well beyond one private company. OpenAI is the anchor tenant underneath Oracle's backlog, CoreWeave's contracts, and much of the GPU, optical and power orders those contracts pay for. A smaller revenue base repriced the whole chain.

The selling went through every layer of the build-out at once. Silicon: INTC -5.34% on $12.7B turnover, ALAB -9.21%, SOXX -3.35%, XSD -3.68%. Optical: AAOI -13.58%, COHR -9.63%, GLW -6.38%, LITE -5.62%. Neocloud: CRWV -7.77%, IREN -7.70%, ORCL -5.48%. Power: VST -6.35%, BE -6.34%, OKLO -6.11%, with URA -3.43% and URNM -3.72%. When optical, power and nuclear fall together on one revenue headline, all of them were priced off the same demand assumption.

The critical read is that capital rotated rather than left. SPX fell 0.47% but advancers beat decliners 337 to 162, a full reversal of Wednesday's 136/361. Equal-weight RSP gained 0.60% while SPY lost 0.43%. Pure Value RPV +1.33% against Pure Growth RPG -1.45% is a 2.78-point style spread. This is an inverse breadth divergence: the index is down while the typical stock is up. Money is reallocating, not de-risking.

It went to energy and defensives. Trump said an Iran deal "isn't what he wants" as the U.S. readies renewed strikes, and Brent rose to $103.85. A $12.71 Brent-WTI spread puts the Hormuz premium in the seaborne barrel, which is why refiners (MPC +4.77%, VLO +4.65%, PSX +3.67%) and tankers lead. Staples (XLP +2.11%) took the defensive bid as PEP rallied on a beat with a trimmed guide. IT services (ACN +5.96%, CTSH +5.15%) rallied as the inverse AI trade: slower AI revenue means slower disruption of labour-intensive services.

Rates make the rotation sticky. With the 10yr near 5.3% and a Fed still hiking, AI equities lose the multiple support that justified buying them ahead of revenue.
