04 Session Summary

Friday was a textbook rotation dressed as a quiet up-day. Headline prints were benign — SPX +0.42% to 7,575, NDX +0.33%, Dow +0.29% — and VIX collapsed 5.1% to 15.03. Beneath the surface, three simultaneous capital rotations reshaped leadership, and the Russell's -0.49% was the tell that this was a redistribution of risk, not a broad advance.

Rotation one, within technology. Megacaps and semis ripped — META +5.97% on cloud/AI-capex news, NVDA +4.03%, AMD +2.04%, CSCO +2.54%, AMAT +2.35%, LITE +2.07% — while high-multiple software and cybersecurity were dumped: CRWD -5.66%, ZS -5.34%, PANW -3.67%, FTNT -3.80%, DDOG -4.26%. That split is why XLK closed up only +0.23% despite housing the day's biggest winners — a quality-and-liquidity preference paying up for tangible AI infrastructure and memory (SNDK +3.10%, new 20day high) and selling the richest-multiple corner.

Rotation two, into financials. The Smart Money board is unambiguous: Fintech #3, Reinsurance #4, Diversified Investment Services #5, Financial & Commodity Market Operations #7 (+25 ranks in 3 days), P&C Insurance #14 — five financial sub-industries in the top 14. Circle +4.97% and fintech's slot as a 10day-hot industry confirm real accumulation. With Chair Warsh's Fed keeping bond vol elevated, capital is positioning in rate-sensitive financials and names that monetise volatility itself.

Rotation three, out of biotech and pharma. Healthcare (XLV -0.82%) was the sole red sector, and it was ugly: MRNA -10.83%, IONS -9.37% (5day -28.79%), ALNY -4.49%, BBIO -4.75%, GILD -3.72%, IBB -2.68%. The mirror of the crowded-momentum unwind in the RS list, where biotech leaders ABSI (-7.59%) and SLS (-5.55%) sold hard. Only 2 of the RS Top 20 printed new 20day highs — the momentum book is digesting, not breaking.

The geopolitical backdrop turned sharply worse yet the tape shrugged. Iran's ceasefire collapsed, tankers were hit in the Strait of Hormuz, and Washington revoked Iran's sale authorization — and WTI still fell -1.22% to $71.51. Markets have moved 'from panic to pricing in,' past peak fear. The nuance: spot crude fades, but the toll-risk trade lives on the water — oil services OIH +5.74% (5day) and shipping BOAT +4.09% (5day) are where the Hormuz premium is now expressed.
