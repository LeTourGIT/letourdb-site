04Session Summary

Wednesday was a cost-of-capital session. September core CPI ran hot (0.3% MoM, 2.6% YoY), pushed the 10-year to ~5.33% intraday and lifted October hike odds from ~21% to above 50% before settling near 37%. With the Fed meeting later this month and December hike pricing already elevated, the market repriced the asset most exposed to a rising discount rate: debt-funded AI infrastructure.

The selling was surgical. All 15 of the session's largest $500M-turnover losers sit in the Technology sector, and they map the AI build-out layer by layer: neocloud and AI hosting (CRWV -15.46%, DOCN -14.76%, IREN -12.18%, WULF -12.64%, APLD -9.43%), optical interconnect (AAOI -11.82%, CIEN -10.19%, LITE -9.28%, CRDO -9.62%) and servers/storage (DELL -11.27%, Everpure -9.86%). CoreWeave's bonds tumbled alongside the equity while bond-market voices questioned the long-term prospects of some hyperscalers. When credit starts pricing the AI trade, equity multiples follow — this was the first session where the financing chain, not the demand chain, was the catalyst.

Healthcare and staples absorbed the capital. XLV +2.53% and XLP +2.22% were the only SPDR sectors up more than 1%, and 10 of the 15 top gainers are healthcare, with WMT, KR and PM from staples and LMT from defence. Value beat growth by 3.7pts (RPV +0.24% vs RPG -3.46%). That is institutional rebalancing from long-duration growth into cash-flow certainty, executed in size — $3.8B in LLY, $3.1B in UNH, $3.0B in WMT.

Geopolitics stayed in the background but did not fade: Iran said it is "counting the moments" until it can strike US warships, Saudi Aramco facilities were attacked, and a container ship sank in the Strait of Hormuz. Front-month WTI held $94 and Brent $105 — high enough to keep headline CPI hot, which is why the energy shock and the rate shock are now the same trade.
