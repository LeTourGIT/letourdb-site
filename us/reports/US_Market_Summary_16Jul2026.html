📝 Session Summary

Thursday was a textbook rotation dressed up as a down day. The headline — SPX -0.51%, NDX -1.62% — hides the most important fact on the tape: 368 S&P advancers against just 131 decliners, a 2.8-to-1 positive breadth reading on a red index. This is the mirror image of a cap-weight distortion: the mega-cap growth cohort fell hard enough to pull the index down while the median stock rose. Small-caps (RUT -0.06%) and the Dow (-0.20%) barely moved.

Capital flowed into defensives, energy and value. Consumer Staples (XLP +2.80%) was the day's best sector — PM +4.95%, MDLZ +4.60% — with Health Care (+2.22%) close behind on an ABT +10.71% earnings blowout, and Real Estate (+2.02%) catching a duration bid. Smart Money confirms it: Tobacco surged to #4, Tires & Rubber and Appliances both posted 80-plus-rank surges into the top 15.

The other side was brutal. The semiconductor/AI-hardware complex was routed: XSD -5.63% (an EXTREME move, -11% on the week), with MRVL, ALAB and CRDO all down 22–23% over five days. Three catalysts converged — TSMC's higher capex guide, a report that Alphabet's Gemini 3.5 Pro is delayed, and a hawkish Dallas Fed (Logan calling for 'modestly higher' rates) that punishes long-duration growth.

Financials told the rotation in miniature: Goldman (-4.91%) and Morgan Stanley (-4.45%) sold off despite record AI-trading-driven quarters, while regional banks (CFG +4.61%) were bid. The RS leaderboard has already re-sorted — biotech and healthcare now hold 9 of the top 20 slots. VIX +6.76% to 16.73 is doing exactly what it should in a rotation: rising modestly on repositioning, not spiking on fear.

📰 Key Headlines

Rotation
AI-trade unwind, not a market top. SPX -0.51% masks a two-tape session: 368 advancers vs 131 decliners (2.8:1 POSITIVE) with staples +2.80%, healthcare +2.22% and REITs bid, while NDX -1.62% and XSD -5.63% mark a wholesale semiconductor de-rate (MRVL/ALAB/CRDO all -22% to -23% on the week). Capital rotated OUT of AI/high-beta INTO defensives, energy and value — a healthy broadening, not a breakdown.
Earnings
Banks blow out, then sell the news. Goldman (record $20.98 EPS) and Morgan Stanley (record $6.3B equities trading) crushed estimates earlier in the week on AI-driven flow — yet GS -4.91% / MS -4.45% led financials lower on the 16th. Regional banks (CFG +4.61%) were bid instead. ABT +10.71% and CTAS +7.22% led an old-economy earnings cohort.
Macro
Hawkish Fed + cooler CPI collide. Dallas Fed's Logan called for 'modestly higher' rates even as CPI eased to 3.5% (biggest drop since Apr 2020). The mix — sticky-but-cooling inflation with a hawkish dissent — pressures long-duration growth (semis, mega-cap tech) while rewarding cash-flow defensives; VIX +6.76% to 16.73 reflects the repositioning, not panic.
Tech
Two cracks in the mega-cap growth story. Alphabet slipped on a report its Gemini 3.5 Pro model is delayed; Netflix fell -7.88% AH on narrowed guidance and a decision to disclose fewer engagement metrics. TSMC's higher capex guide also spooked the semi tape. The AI-infrastructure premium is being repriced — ALAB, CRDO, MRVL, WDC, SNDK all down hard.
Geopolitical
Iran draws a Hormuz 'red line'. Iran warned the U.S. it will retaliate if Trump carries out strike threats, framing Hormuz as a red line. WTI held near $78.89 (-0.84%) — energy equities are being bid on Smart-Money rotation (6 oil sub-industries in the top 16), not on a crude spike, which makes the energy bid a positioning call rather than a fear trade.
