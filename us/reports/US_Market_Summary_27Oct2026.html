04Session Summary

A two-speed tape into the Fed. Tech and small caps rallied (NDX +1.29%, RUT +1.16%) as chips bounced for a third session and October's AI-debt anxiety eased ahead of the hyperscaler prints; Anthropic's IPO filing added a sentiment kicker. But SPX's +0.60% came on 244 advancers vs 255 decliners and equal-weight RSP managed only +0.11% — a 0.49pp cap-weight premium. Megacap weight carried the index, not participation.

Geopolitics set the energy tone. Trump said he will soon decide between a deal with Iran and escalation; oil eased on the diplomacy reading, WTI dipping below $100 intraday before settling at $100.23 (-0.56%). The damage hit the sub-sector most dependent on dislocation: refiners (VLO -9.24%, MPC -7.36%, PSX -7.03%, CRAK -3.73%). With Brent flat at $109.77, the market is not pricing an end to scarcity — it is pricing an end to the refining windfall.

The oil complex split along the value chain. OIH +1.61% (5day +6.23%) held, Oil & Gas Drilling SURGED +30 ranks to rank 9 and Oil Services +50, while Refining collapsed to rank 112 (rank 11 nine days ago). Capital is keeping the multi-year supply-rebuild trade and exiting the short-duration margin trade. XLE -2.05% was the worst sector, but with a +10.84% 20day return this is rotation inside the 60day leader, not an exit.

Earnings dispersion was violent: SOFI +13.42% and PNR +12.80% on beat-and-raise; BA -10.69% on a delivery cut; XYL -14.21% on a guidance cut (water peer PNR rallied — execution, not theme). The quieter signal came from the services layer: ACN -4.88%, CTSH -4.50%, ADBE -3.97%, INTU -3.37% and the data/ratings group (TRU, TRI, FDS, MORN -4% to -5%). The market is separating AI picks-and-shovels (CRDO, ALAB, DELL, WDC) from seat- and labour-based models AI can compress — Microsoft's buyout offer to 60,000 staff is the macro version of the same story.

Hard assets joined the bounce — URNM +5.20%, REMX +4.84%, XME +4.68%, COPX +3.89% — helped by China's strongest industrial-profit growth in three months. But 20day returns (REMX -16.35%, COPX -10.29%, URA -10.44%) mark this as short-covering in October's losers, and the RS Top 20 holds zero semiconductor or materials names to say otherwise.

Setup for Wednesday: a Fed hold at 3.75-4.00% is expected, with at least two more hikes priced by mid-2027 and the 10year near 4.99%. The asymmetric risk is a hawkish signal re-igniting the 'AI capex on expensive debt' fear just before the hyperscalers report. After hours already tested the semi bounce (STX -6.27%, TER -6.39%, NXPI -8.93%), yet VIX fell -4.73% to 17.52 — options are not hedging the event, and that complacency is the risk.
