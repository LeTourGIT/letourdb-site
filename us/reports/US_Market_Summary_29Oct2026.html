Session Summary

Thursday was the session the AI trade changed its exam. Alphabet's Q3 showed Google Cloud revenue doubling to $30.7B — and the stock still suffered its worst day ever (-12.64%, ~$500B of value), because free cash flow was -$9.1B for a second straight quarter and 2027 capex is heading toward ~$300B. The market is no longer paying for AI revenue growth on its own; it is demanding cash conversion. Amazon confirmed the pattern after the bell: AWS +39%, record margin, trailing FCF of -$42.4B.

Software took the second blow. ServiceNow -26.95% (worst day since its 2012 IPO) and Fortinet -20.09% turned a two-cent EPS miss and a service-revenue guide trim into a factor de-rating. IGV -3.52%, CIBR -2.71% and TEAM -7.28% say the market is pricing AI as a disruptor of seat-based software economics. MRVL -13.24% on reported all-stock talks for Lumentum added dilution fear to semis (SOXX -1.55%, MU -4.98%).

Underneath, the tape was strong — the gold nugget of the session. SPX fell -0.30% yet advancers beat decliners 404 to 96, 20MA participation jumped from 69.4% to 75.4%, T2108 rose to 70.2% and new 20day highs nearly doubled to 156. That is rotation out of a handful of cap-heavy AI names into everything else: low-vol SPLV +1.67%, healthcare XLV +2.40%, real estate XLRE +2.21%, homebuilders ITB +3.49%, defence ITA +3.16%.

Macro reinforces it: GDP +3.4% and core PCE 3.1% arrived a day after two FOMC members dissented for a hike. A Fed that cannot ease and a 10yr near 5% de-rate cash-burning growth and reward cash generation today. WTI near $103 on Iran brinkmanship ahead of the 3 November midterms keeps the inflation impulse alive.

Positioning: index-level risk skews lower into Friday's AMZN/INTC gap, but breadth this positive under a falling index reads as rotation, not distribution. Cautious on megacap AI and software; constructive on the broadening layer — equal-weight, small caps, healthcare, housing, defence — that Smart Money and the RS list already reward.
