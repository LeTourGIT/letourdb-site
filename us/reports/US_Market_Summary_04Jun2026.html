📝 Session Summary

Thursday rewrote the AI trade's short-term narrative in a single session. Broadcom's quarter was technically stellar — AI revenue $10.8B (+143% YoY), EPS beat, Q3 revenue guidance $29.4B (+84% YoY) — yet the stock fell -13% because the market had priced a guidance upgrade after a 40% YTD run. CEO Hock Tan reaffirmed the $100 billion FY2027 AI semiconductor target but declined to raise it. At that valuation entry point, reaffirmation was read as a ceiling. The result: ~$250 billion in AVGO market cap evaporated in one session, dragging SOXX -2.10% and XSD -0.83% while unlocking capital for the rest of the market.

The rotation that followed was broad, healthy, and structurally constructive. Healthcare led at +3.07% — XLV's strongest session in months. Not a resolution — fighting continued on 4 June — but a diplomatic architecture that shifts the probability distribution for regional stability. Airlines, cruise names, and industrials added a secondary rotation layer.
