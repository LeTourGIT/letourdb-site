📝 Session Summary

The dominant driver was geopolitical de-escalation. After weeks of Iran/Hormuz escalation that spiked oil and compressed risk appetite, the tape shifted from "peak fear" to "pricing in": Trump stepped back and OPEC+ stepped up supply, collapsing the war premium. Brent fell −7.35% and the USO oil ETF −8.66% — one of the largest single-session energy repricings of the year. Treasury yields fell as the oil-into-inflation channel unwound, and a broad four-index relief rally followed (SPX +1.48%, NDX +1.78%, DJI +1.32%, RUT +1.73%) with VIX down to 15.86 and its 5day reading off −15.1%.

Critically, this was not a narrow, cap-weighted melt-up. Breadth confirmed at every level: SPX advancers led 337/163 (better than 2:1), the Russell an emphatic 1,526/404, and 21 SPX names closed +4% against just 2 down 4%. Participation is real. But breadth confirming a bounce and regime confirming a trend are different things — and only the former is present. NDX remains −2.09% below its 50MA; a +1.78% session repairs price, not structure.

Leadership tells the rotation story. The rally was powered by the AI-datacenter/compute-power complex and aerospace-defense, not defensives. CoreWeave +19.49% led on AI-cloud demand; Oracle +9.22%, Vertiv +8.89%, Lumentum +9.24%, Coherent +9.60% and Applied Optoelectronics +16.85% confirmed the "power and optics for AI" sub-theme. A defense/space cluster fired alongside — Axon +9.12%, Rocket Lab +8.44%, Boeing +8.03%, AST SpaceMobile +7.70% — while First Solar +10.28% and the uranium/solar ETFs (URA +4.45%, TAN +3.87%) round out an "energy for AI" narrative: as the oil trade dies, capital rotates toward the power sources feeding compute.

Beneath the surface, Smart Money is undergoing its own regime change. Fintech is the new #1 industry (10day ROC +36.35%, up from rank #88 nine sessions ago), with Financial & Commodity Market Operators at #3. Construction & Engineering surged to #4 (from #130 three days ago), Heavy Machinery to #6, Airlines to #13 (from #137) on the de-escalation reopening trade. The single most important structural fact: zero energy in the Smart Money top 16 — a complete reversal from the five-energy-sub-theme dominance of prior weeks. The oil crash didn't just move a sector; it evacuated a capital-flow regime.

After hours, Palantir delivered the megacap headline: +11.85% on a blowout quarter with US commercial revenue up nearly 150%. Snap +10.52%, ON Semi +3.82%, Advanced Energy +9.62% and Ultra Clean +6.20% extended the semiconductor-capital-equipment recovery into the evening. The one megacap blemish was Marriott −6.97% — a consumer-services warning worth watching against the airline strength.
