Session Summary

Monday delivered a green screen concealing a fracturing market. The Nasdaq 100 closed at a record 30,482 (+2.83%) — its strongest single session of the recent AI leg — while the S&P added +1.49% to 7,764. But the Dow (+0.71%) and Russell 2000 (+0.52%) advanced only grudgingly and remain in Bearish regimes below their 50-day averages. One tape, two markets.

The catalyst was narrow and specific: Meta's Muse agent (+11.34% for META) triggered a repricing of the CPU-demand thesis. Intel +12.14%, AMD +9.95%, Qualcomm +9.29% and Astera Labs +12.36% led an SOXX +4.93% to a fifth consecutive gain — with no chip company reporting anything. XLC (+3.56%, lifted by Meta) and XLK (+2.77%) were the only sectors with real thrust; defensives (XLP -1.06%, XLU -1.07%) and the entire energy complex (XLE -2.88%) went the other way.

Beneath the indices, internals flash a warning not seen clearly since 1999. The S&P closed higher yet registered 129 new 20-day lows against just 31 new highs — more than four new lows per new high, on an up day at index highs. The Russell told the same story (286 vs 112). Only 49.6% of SPX members sit above their 200-day average and T2108 is a soft 25% — the classic Hindenburg-style divergence where a cap-weighted index levitates on a handful of mega-caps while the median stock slides. Advancers did beat decliners (SPX 288/210), so this is deterioration in the tails — but the new-low expansion at new highs is the tell.

Capital rotation confirmed the split. Smart Money's fastest movers were Miscellaneous Fintech Infrastructure (rank 1, SURGE) and Blockchain & Cryptocurrency (rank 2) — validated by IBIT +6.5% and ETHA +4.67% as crypto ripped risk-on. Simultaneously oil broke lower (WTI -2.15% to $91.95) as the market priced "past peak fear," crushing refiners (MPC -5.30%, VLO -4.84%, PSX -4.17%) and integrated names (XOM -3.20%, COP -3.26%, CVX -2.79%). The freight tape (UPS -4.35%, FDX -2.59%, CSX -2.36%, UNP -3.49%) is the quiet worry: freight weakness plus a Bearish Dow is a cyclical demand signal the AI headline is drowning out.

The macro backdrop is dense: the Trump–Xi summit (Sep 23–25, Xi's first White House visit in 11 years) puts trade, AI, rare earths, fentanyl and Taiwan on the table with the tariff truce expiring in November — a tech-heavy state-dinner guest list (Huang, Cook, Altman, Pichai, Musk) signals the agenda's center of gravity. Net: a low-VIX (14.87) mega-cap AI melt-up riding on the weakest internals in a year.
