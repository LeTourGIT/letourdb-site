Session Summary

Two independent catalysts collided to produce a textbook rotation day — growth and AI-hardware out, value-defensives and energy in. The headline losses on SPX (-0.45%) and NDX (-1.77%) mask a market that, beneath the mega-cap tech layer, was remarkably resilient.

Catalyst one — semis cracked. Samsung posted an 18-fold jump in Q2 operating profit (₩89.4tn, ~$58B) yet fell ~7%: a pure sell-the-news on a sector priced for perfection. Compounded by a report that China's DeepSeek is building its own AI inference chip and pre-positioning ahead of SK Hynix's Nasdaq listing Friday, the result was XSD -6.34% and SOXX -5.13% (both EXTREME). INTC -9.66%, ALAB -11.52%, TER -9.59%, MRVL -7.45%, WDC -7.86%, SNDK -7.26% — memory, equipment and fabless design sold as one.

Catalyst two — geopolitics reversed the energy trade. Iran struck three Hormuz tankers (Saudi Wedyan, Qatari LNG Al-Rekayat, and a third) and the US Treasury revoked General License X authorising Iranian oil sales, wind-down to July 17. The mirror image of May's peace repricing: WTI +4.77% to $72.22, Brent +4.85%. XLE +2.84% led all sectors — OXY +5.88%, DVN +5.08%, COP +4.69%, XOM +3.85%, LNG +3.62%.

The rotation was clean. Capital fled semis and crypto miners (WULF -8.87%, IREN -9.33%) into three defensive havens — healthcare (GILD +5.21%, REGN +4.07%, IHE +3.16%), real estate (XLRE +1.35%) and utilities (XLU +0.88%) — plus energy. Rising yields (30-yr above 5%, 10-yr ~4.51%) under a hawkish Warsh Fed lifted the discount rate on long-duration growth exactly as the semi valuation air-pocket opened.

The most important intra-tech signal: software SEPARATED from silicon. NET +8.60%, FIG +5.27%, WDAY +4.10%, ACN +3.78%, DOCN +4.32% — the application layer is being re-rated as an AI beneficiary, not a casualty, for the first time this cycle. Meanwhile SpaceX joined the Nasdaq-100 and fell (~16% Monday, extending Tuesday); RIVN -18.12% was the worst large-cap, dragging autos.

The breadth picture is the tell: SPX advancers led decliners 283 to 216 on a DOWN day — a reverse cap-weight distortion where heavyweight semis pulled the index red while the median stock rose. This is rotation, not liquidation.
