04Session Summary

Geopolitics set the tone. Iran's foreign minister used the UN to offer a Strait of Hormuz reopening within seven days — plus restarted nuclear talks — if Washington lifts its blockade, waives oil sanctions and accepts a ceasefire covering Lebanon: the collapsed June MOU on a faster clock. Crude extended its slide (WTI -7.9% on the week) and relief flowed straight into fuel-sensitive equities: Airlines jumped to rank 4 in Smart Money from rank 98 nine days ago (AAL +3.90%, DAL +2.63%, JETS +1.83%), with BKNG +4.15% and ABNB +4.02% riding the travel bid. Energy was the mirror image — XLE -0.89%, DVN -3.78%, LNG -2.80%. With Saudi Arabia reported sceptical, oil is pricing probability, not resolution.

The deeper regime is the bond market. The 10yr touched ~5.23% — highest since June 2007 — a week after the Fed's first hike in three years, with ~2:1 odds of another in October. Hence the split tape: SPX/NDX Bullish, RUT/DJI Bearish, and equal-weight RSP -4.67% over 20days against a flat IVV. Falling oil is really a rates trade — the only near-term lever that can cap yields.

Inside tech, capital rewarded product and punished execution risk. MSFT +3.66% on $19.7B turnover after its Copilot overhaul was the biggest SPX contributor. Chips broadened rather than led: equal-weight XSD +2.67% beat SOXX +1.17% as ON +5.54%, MCHP +5.36%, QCOM +3.97% and CRDO +7.65% ran while NVDA, AMD and MU sat flat. Money moving down the semi cap-curve is a cycle-extension signal, not exhaustion.

Cyber was the fault line. ZS -10.06% on a surprise CRO exit ahead of its 6 October investor day spread to PANW -3.89%, OKTA -5.54%, CRWD -2.90% and GEN -6.29%; CIBR -2.17% was the weakest ETF and Software slid to rank 41 in Smart Money. The week's winners were trimmed too — META -3.33% after a ~13% week, INTC -3.45% after +13.26% over 5days. Profit-taking in winners while laggards get bid, with VIX -5.11%, is rotation — not risk-off.

Breadth improved on the day but the structure is the story. SPX Adv/Dec flipped to 327/171 from 173/326, yet only 26.6% of members sit above their 50MA, T2108 is 25.4% and 20day lows outnumber highs 131 to 36 — with the index ~0.7% below its ATH. A megacap roof over a washed-out floor. T2108 near 25% is where breadth thrusts are born, but the trigger has to be the 10yr rolling over.
