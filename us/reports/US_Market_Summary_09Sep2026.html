Session Summary

Wednesday was a broad, orderly risk-off session on a single macro pivot: oil is back. WTI jumped +2.61% to $96.64 and Brent reclaimed $100 (+2.32%) after Houthi rebels struck a Saudi Aramco refinery and the US Navy attacked three Iranian oil tankers near Kharg Island. This is the mirror image of the spring "Iran peace" trade — the geopolitical premium priced OUT months ago is being priced back IN, hard. OPEC+ left October output unchanged.

The second-order effect moved equities. Oil above $100 revives inflation fears 48 hours before two prints the Fed's next decision hinges on, feeding a bond sell-off. Rising yields fall heaviest on the longest-duration equity — so the day's defining divergence sat inside technology. XLK closed flat (0.00%) while software ripped (Cloudflare +10.5%, Datadog +7.2%, Meta +6.6%, Fair Isaac +5.4%, HPE +5.1%) and the data-centre build-out was taken apart (Vertiv -9.6%, CoreWeave -4.9%, IonQ -5.8%, AST SpaceMobile -5.6%). This is a quality rotation within AI — capital exiting the cash-hungry physical layer and concentrating into profitable software. Semis split the difference (Marvell +4.3%, Astera +4.1% green), so it is a capital-intensity sort, not a semiconductor exit.

Breadth confirms the selling was broad: SPX advancers 98 vs decliners 402 (1:4.1) on a -0.48% tape, with 177 new 20day lows against 20 highs and T2108 at 31.2%. The median stock had a far worse day than the cap-weighted index.
