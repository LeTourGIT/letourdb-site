📝  SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Tuesday 9 June returned from the Queen's Birthday public holiday long weekend into the
teeth of a post-NFP hangover — but the damage was comparatively contained. XJO fell -0.24%
to 8,604.2, a surface reading that masks a fascinating internal divergence: XJO Adv/Dec
was 116:76 (tilted to advancers) while XAO Adv/Dec was 193:266 (decliners dominated in
the broader universe). Translation: large-cap selectivity was firmly in force. Institutional
flows found specific pockets of quality while the broader market sluiced lower.

The session's defining move was Pro Medicus (PME) +9.1% to a new 20-day high at $165.79.
This was a direct rebound from global healthcare SaaS peers' re-rating following Monday's
chip bounce — PME's 14.77% 10day return now makes it the top-10 performer in the ASX 200
RS rankings. Critically, a PME at a 20-day high while XJO sits 2.12% below 200MA is a
market-within-a-market signal: institutional capital is not rotating out of Australian
healthcare IT despite the macro headwinds.

Materials (XMJ -2.32%) was the session's anchor, the worst outcome for any major sector
following Friday's sharp commodity selloff — copper -4.0%, gold breaching its 200-day for
the first time since October 2023 (ending a 660-day streak). The context matters: these are
not panic moves but a deliberate repricing of the "tight money, falling real rates" thesis
that had underwritten the commodity/gold bull run. Hot US jobs → higher-for-longer USD →
commodity price compression. For BHP, RIO, and FMG, the AUD at 0.7045 partially offsets
in local currency terms, but the XMJ -5.08% over 5 days reads as genuine institutional
exodus, not seasonal noise.

Construction & Engineering was the stealth winner of the session — SRG Global (SRG) +2.88%
to a new 20-day high, with the Smart Money #5 rank and 8.59% 10day ROC confirming
sustained institutional accumulation. The DDR (Dicker Data) +1.34% further cemented
Computer Hardware's dominant Smart Money #1 position (ROC +25.67%, SURGE from rank 57
nine days ago). This is the most explosive institutional rotation in the current cycle —
Computer Hardware stocks are embedding a structural position, not a day trade.
