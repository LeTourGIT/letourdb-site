📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Wednesday delivered the most geopolitically significant session of the 2026 bull cycle.
Axios reported at pre-market that the U.S. and Iran were closing in on a 14-point,
one-page memorandum of understanding that would formally end the war, open the Strait
of Hormuz, and impose a 12-15 year moratorium on Iranian uranium enrichment. The
market's interpretation was immediate and decisive: USO -7.09%, WTI collapsing toward
$95 in after-hours, Brent losing over 4%, and the energy sector (-4.12% XLE, -6.24%
XOP) in free-fall. This is the Hormuz resolution trade the Smart Money rotation has been
pre-positioning for across the past two weeks.

The semiconductor complex confirmed what the RS rotation has been signalling. AMD
surged 17% intraday after reporting Q1 revenue of $10.25B (+38% YoY), crushing the
$9.89B consensus. Data centre revenue drove the beat — the exact metric the entire
sector needs as validation that AI infrastructure spending is not decelerating. ARM
was up 13% into its AH earnings print, then added 9.5% post-close on a record quarter
($1.49B revenue, $0.60 EPS vs $0.58 consensus) with data centre royalties more than
doubling YoY. Two separate earnings reports in the same session confirming AI
infrastructure demand from different layers of the stack — architecture licensing (ARM)
and fabless compute (AMD) — is as close to consensus-confirming evidence as earnings
season delivers.

The structural story from Corning (GLW +17%) cannot be understated. NVDA's partnership
to build three dedicated optical manufacturing facilities in North Carolina and Texas —
10x-ing Corning's US optical capacity — represents the first announced phase of the
'physical AI infrastructure' build. Optical interconnects are the data-pipe of AI data
centres; Corning's gain signals institutional re-pricing of that supply chain. The
60day Communications & Networking leader (sector ROC +36%) is being confirmed in
real-time.

Energy's historic single-day collapse (-4.12% XLE, -6.24% XOP, -7.09% USO) is not
finished. The Axios MOU terms include a gradual 30-day lifting of both the US naval
blockade and Iran's Hormuz restrictions. Goldman estimated 4-6 months to normalise
supply chains even after ceasefire — now the market is pricing the resolution scenario
directly. The five energy sub-themes that crowded into the Smart Money top 16 last week
are under acute rotation pressure; watch Renewable Fuels (#8, ROC +9.18%) as the
structural transition play.

SPX breadth: 316 advancers vs 183 decliners. 46 stocks +4%+ today. A genuine
broad-based session — not a cap-weight distortion. T2108 at 57.6%, 50MA participation
at 54.8% provide structural depth. The risk is concentrated in NDX RSI-15 at 78.89 —
a reading only exceeded at cycle peaks.
