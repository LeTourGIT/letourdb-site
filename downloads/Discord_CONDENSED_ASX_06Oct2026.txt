📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
A third straight gain (+0.57% to 8,735.7), but a top-heavy one. The
ASX 100 advanced 63/29; the Small Ords fell 0.27% on a 72/72 split.
BHP +1.57%, RIO +1.09%, CSL +0.95% and the big four (+0.56% to
+0.78%) did the lifting: index-level buying and short-covering, not
a broadening appetite for risk.

Leadership was the rate-peak trade. REITs (XRE +1.11%) bounced from
34-month lows after CBA's Comyn called RBA hikes "probably
finished"; ex-Goodman (GMG -0.65%) the typical REIT rose 2% to 3%.
The 5.41% 10-year has not endorsed it and auction clearance is under
50%, so treat it as positioning until yields follow.

Technology (XIJ -2.90%) fell on the day the Nasdaq set a record: WTC
-2.54%, NXT -2.16%, WBT -12.98%, ELS -11.55%, MP1 -5.70%, with no
Section 2b announcements behind any of them. Gold developers were
liquidated too (TCG -10.87%, PDI -6.67%). This year's leaders are
being sold while laggards are bought: rotation, not accumulation.

Materials (XMJ +0.91%) rose despite iron ore near US$91.05/t (-5.64%
over 5 days): a weaker AUD (-3.41% over 20 days) and an aluminium
bounce (AAI +4.14%) are doing the work.

Company news: EQ Resources (EQR -11.39%) lodged record cash
generation at 10:09 [PS], traded about 6% higher early and was then
sold, which is distribution in a stock already -12.50% over 5 days.
Stakk (SKK +4.35%) ran on its 09:39 IBM deal. After the close Dicker
Data (DDR, halted from 09:50) agreed to acquire Sektor Group:
tomorrow's price discovery. RBA: cash rate 4.60%, next decision
03/11/2026, so no RBA Watch this week; late-October CPI decides
whether it is live.
