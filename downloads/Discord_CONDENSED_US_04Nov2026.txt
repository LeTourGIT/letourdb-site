📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Democrats won both chambers and the market priced three things at once: gridlock, an earlier
end to the Iran war, and a harder road for AI data centers. Oil collapsed (WTI -8.73%), the Dow
added 1,579 points (+3.05%) and equal-weight S&P closed at a record.

Cheaper fuel is a consumer tax cut, and capital went straight there. XLY +4.26% led, with ITB
+7.25%, JETS +6.70% and XRT +5.54%. Smart Money had been building this rotation for nine days
(Appliances rank 83 → 2, Homebuilding 31 → 3), so today was confirmation, not the start.

The AI trade split. Spenders were bought (MSFT +6.68%, AMZN +6.12%, AAPL +5.31%). Suppliers were
liquidated: VST -13.83%, VRT -12.89%, FIX -15.08%, MU -7.00%, SOXX -3.14%. A Democratic Congress may
challenge data-center expensing and permitting. Slower capex saves money for the hyperscaler and
costs revenue for the supplier. XLK at -0.01% hides the whole fight.

Gold +4.19% and silver +6.30% rose as oil fell. That is not a safe-haven bid. It reads as a
weaker-dollar and fiscal-credibility trade, and AUD/USD rose to 0.7004.

Regimes lag the thrust. Only the Dow is Bullish. SPX needs a close above its 50MA to flip, and NDX
needs semis to stop falling. Today was a breadth thrust inside a still-broken trend. A second day
of follow-through matters more than the size of the first.
