📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday was a rates-driven, high-beta risk-on session. Two Fed signals did
the heavy lifting: Governor Waller indicated support for holding rates steady
(no further hike), and Vice President Vance publicly pressed for cuts. Treasury
yields fell, VIX collapsed -5.79% to 14.32, and capital rotated hard into the
longest-duration assets on the board — crypto, software, and rate-sensitive
financials/REITs. MarketWatch's sector roundups told the whole story in a line:
Tech, Financials, Consumer and Utilities all "up as rate fears recede."

Crypto was the single loudest signal. IBIT +5.85% and ETHA +5.37% both cleared
the ±5% EXTREME threshold, and the equity proxies went vertical: MSTR +17.56%,
HOOD +16.57%, CRCL +16.46%, COIN +10.14%, WULF +9.51%. This is not a coin story
— it is a liquidity story. Falling real yields plus a -5.79% VIX is the exact
cocktail that reprices the highest-beta risk on the board, and Smart Money
confirms it: Miscellaneous Fintech Infrastructure sits entrenched at rank 1
(it was rank 139 nine days ago), the fastest structural rotation in the file.

Software split the tape. SNOW +16.55% delivered the cleanest AI-monetisation
print of the season — consumption re-accelerating is the revenue proof the
market has demanded — and dragged IGV +3.41% with PLTR +7.71%, NOW +6.49%,
ORCL +5.69% and CRWD +5.68% in tow. But the SAME session saw the chip rebound
fail: AVGO -2.74% sold a good number, and CIEN -10.36% guided optical
networking down, taking LITE -2.67% and AAOI -2.67% with it. The message is a
rotation UP the stack — out of merchant silicon and optical hardware, into
asset-light software and application-layer AI (IOT +5.33%, ZS, DOCU all bid AH).

The defensive side was actively dumped. Food processing was the day's worst
pocket — CPB -6.96%, GIS -3.25%, KHC -3.20% — and Consumer Staples (XLP -0.32%)
was one of only three red sectors alongside Materials (XLB -0.62%) and Energy
(XLE -0.74%, worst). When staples and energy are the only things falling on a
+1% day, the market is telling you it wants beta, not shelter.

But the internals carry a warning the headline indices hide. Advancers beat
decliners cleanly (SPX 337/161), yet NEW 20day LOWS OUTNUMBERED NEW 20day HIGHS
on every index (SPX 55 highs vs 73 lows; NDX 15 vs 24; RUT 157 vs 194), and
T2108 sits below 50 on SPX (49.4%), NDX (46.5%) and RUT (47.5%). A rally where
more stocks are making fresh lows than fresh highs is a rally built on the
biggest, most liquid names re-rating — precisely the crypto/software beta that
led. Combined with negative 15day ROC across all four indices and the Neutral
Bull/Bear regime tags, this reads as a powerful but not-yet-confirmed bounce.
The August jobs report (due Friday) is the binary catalyst that decides whether
the dovish repricing extends or reverses.
