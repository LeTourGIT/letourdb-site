📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The ASX 200 shed 37.5 points (-0.41%) to 9,044.7, its third consecutive decline and the lowest close since 09/11. Yet this was not a broad sell-off. Seven of eleven sectors finished higher; the damage was concentrated almost entirely in Materials (XMJ -2.17%, 5day -3.61%), which on its own subtracted roughly half a percent from the index. Strip out the miners and the ASX 200 was flat to slightly up.

The trigger was China. October industrial output, retail sales and fixed investment all missed estimates, and the big iron ore names took the hit: BHP -2.80%, FMG -2.90%, RIO -2.21%, MIN -3.63%. Gold miners were sold alongside them (EVN -2.92%, NST -2.32%) as bullion edged lower (-0.22% to US$3,867), and copper equities were punished hardest -- CSC -4.83%, DVP -5.40%. PLS (+1.05%) was the lone large-cap resources name in the green.

The money that left resources went straight into defensives and quality growth. IT led (XIJ +1.07%) on XRO +4.72%, now +14.14% in five days off its 12/11 result. Real Estate (+0.82%) and Staples (+0.69%) followed, and the tell is the 20day-high list: CBA, WBC, TLS, TCL, QBE, IAG, BXB, CPU and a cluster of REITs all printed new 20day highs. XJO new 20day highs more than doubled from 11 to 25 on a down day -- leadership is broadening even as the index slips.

Stock-specific news did the rest. Elders (+7.49%) rallied on its FY26 result and is now an RS-92 leader at a 20day high. New Hope (-5.93%) was the session's biggest institutional loser after pairing its quarterly with an inaugural hybrid securities raise -- coal sits dead last (rank 78/78) in Smart Money rotation, so the market is in no mood to fund it. GQG (-6.38%) extended a brutal -30.89% week with no same-day announcement.

Forward look: Technology One and Catapult report tomorrow (17/11), Infratil on 18/11. Post-close, Mastermyne lodged an AGM guidance update (16:08) and EMU withdrew its Badja sale announcement (16:31) -- neither is in today's close. Five trading halts remain open (HLX, GSS, EPX, AOK, VEN). With US futures soft and RBA at 4.85% until 07-08/12, the path of least resistance is more defensive rotation unless China signals stimulus.
