📝 SESSION SUMMARY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────
Monday 07/09/2026 looked quiet on the surface — XJO +0.06% to 9010.9, XAO
+0.05% to 9200.6 — but the internals tell a narrower story. Decliners outnumbered
advancers on the 200 (85/106), the All Ords (180/251) and the 100
(32/64); only the Small Ords were near even (70/71). New 20day
lows outran highs on the XJO (13 vs 6). This was a mega-cap-propped flat tape, not
a broad advance — the kind of session where the index masks rotation underneath.

Energy (XEJ +1.78%) was the clear leader, and the driver is offshore: Brent has pushed to ~$97.39
(+1.15%, +11% on the month) as the US and Iran trade strikes on tankers and Tehran threatens a
restricted maritime zone beyond the Strait of Hormuz. That Hormuz risk premium lifted the ASX energy
complex — Whitehaven Coal (WHC +7.03%, ASX100, 20day high) and Tamboran (TBN +9.09%) on its first
Beetaloo gas sales — and, unhelpfully for the RBA, it is exactly the imported-inflation channel the
Board has been warning about three weeks out from its 29 September decision.

Underneath, this was a resources-and-agri-up, tech-retail-property-down session. The speculative
resource tier ran hard on drilling and corporate catalysts: Iondrive (ION +29.41%) on a US$243m NPV
upgrade, plus a cluster of gold explorers (SLS, BNZ, LRV). Bubs (BUB +30.00%) was the standout, its
permanent US FDA infant-formula approval a genuine fundamental re-rating. Ingenia (INA +14.79%, ASX200)
jumped on rejecting a non-binding takeover approach. The other side: Info Tech (XIJ -2.60%, 5day -6.37%)
was the worst sector — Audinate (AD8 -9.43%) leading it down — while retail (SUL, MYR) and property
(Peet PPC -7.37%) sagged as the Bathla $3.4b developer collapse kept housing-sector stress in the headlines.

Gold is the quiet structural story. The gold PRICE eased (spot ~$4,477, -0.82%), yet gold miners own
relative-strength leadership across BOTH tiers — spec (ION, NMG, BNZ, AUC) and institutional (VAU, SBM,
RSG, GMD, ALK). Miners holding RS leadership on a down-price day says the pullback is being bought, not sold.
