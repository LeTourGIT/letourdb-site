📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
De-grossing, not a fundamental break. Three forces hit a fragile tape at once.
NVDA fell another 4.1% into Tuesday's after-close print, its first under
interim CEO Colette Kress. A reported US move to close the remote/cloud-access
loophole for advanced AI chips hit the whole chip stack (SOXX -5.41%, XSD
-5.15%). And the VIX jumped 18.70% to 22.91 as traders paid up for event
insurance. SPX posted its third straight loss; the Dow shed 739 points.

The turnover column shows institutional selling. MU -7.62% on $19.5B and SNDK
-8.58% on $10.2B: ~$30B of memory changing hands in one session. Optical
(LITE, CIEN), connectivity (ALAB, CRDO, MRVL) and AI power (BE) all fell 7-9%
together. When every layer of a theme sells off on one policy headline, the
market is cutting the multiple on the whole capex chain, not just pricing a
quarter of NVDA risk.

The index is weaker than its constituents. SPX -1.91% vs equal-weight RSP
-1.01% (90bp) and RPV -0.53% vs RPG -3.28% (275bp), yet 65.0% of SPX names
still hold their 50MA while the index sits 3.30% below it. The generals are
being shot while the infantry holds. Small caps are the real warning: RUT
flipped to Bearish, only 37.0% of its names are above the 20MA, and new lows
beat new highs 331 to 60. With the 10-year near 5%, both ends of the barbell
are losing support.

The money went to staples, managed care and utilities: only XLP (+0.27%) and
XLU (+0.18%) closed green, and Consumer non-Cyclicals is the only sector
positive on both the 10day and 60day ROC. Gold, silver, Bitcoin and Ether all
fell with equities, and WTI dropped 3.29% despite Iran striking a US base.
When hedges and risk assets fall together, positions are being liquidated for
cash.
