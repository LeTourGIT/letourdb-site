lowest close since August after Trump called a return to war with Iran

down → cyclicals up: XLI +3.82% led, JETS +5.15%, Airlines took Smart
Money rank 1, RUT +3.15%.

Chips ripped ex-NVDA: SOXX +7.49%, AMD +13.26%, MPWR +16.60%, COHR

after its board opened a review of Jensen Huang's reported tie to a
suspected Chinese intelligence asset — an ~11-point NVDA/SOXX gap, the
widest since 2019. Capital didn't leave AI; it left the single point of
failure. But SOXX is still -19.19% over 20days and zero semis sit in the
RS Top 20: price bounced, leadership didn't.

Breadth was the real tell: 420 SPX advancers vs 78 decliners, 47 names
+4% vs 2 at -4%, T2108 up to 71.0% from 65.0%. On 05/11 only 73 names
advanced — a 15%→84% swing in three sessions is the signature of a
capitulation-and-reversal. Thrusts like this need 3-5 days of
follow-through to mark a bottom.

The caution flag is rates: the 2-year rose 5bp to 4.57% and December
hike odds hit 85% despite the oil slide. Equities priced disinflation;
bonds want October CPI to prove it first. Hot CPI hits exactly Monday's
leaders — small caps, airlines, homebuilders.
