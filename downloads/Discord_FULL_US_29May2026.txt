📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday capped Memorial Day week with all three major indices closing at fresh ATHs — Dow
above 51,000 for the first time ever — driven by an extraordinary Dell Technologies
earnings beat that redefined AI infrastructure expectations for the entire cycle. Dell's
Q1 FY27 report ($43.8B revenue, +88% YoY; AI server revenue $16.1B, +757% YoY;
$51.3B backlog; FY27 guidance raised by ~$27B) was not just a beat — it was a
structural re-rating event. HPE +12.76%, NTAP +22.39%, SNDK +3.25%, and SMCI
+11.60% all confirm that Dell's result is being read as a cycle acceleration signal,
not a Dell-specific share gain.

The second independent catalyst was Snowflake's Q1 FY27 (+33% revenue YoY,
$6B AWS partnership announcement), which directly neutralised the "SaaSpocalypse"
thesis that had suppressed software valuations all year. CIBR +6.41%, IGV +6.25%,
and OGIG +3.45% — the only three ETFs above +3% — represent a structural revaluation
of the AI application layer above the infrastructure layer. Palantir +8%, ServiceNow +6%,
Oracle +6%, and Palo Alto +3% confirm this was institutional, not retail speculation.
SNOW's $6B AWS partnership is the AI monetisation inflection the market had been
waiting for — it proves enterprise AI spend is landing in software margins, not just
server capex.

Iran diplomacy continued to mature as the geopolitical backdrop. Rubio met Pakistani
FM Dar in Washington directly — the first face-to-face US-Pakistan mediation session —
following reports on 28 May that a tentative US-Iran framework had been agreed pending
Trump's final sign-off. WTI fell another -1.61% to $91.16 (5day: -9.02%), the most
sustained oil de-escalation since the conflict began. Rubio's language was measured
("slight progress, some movement") — this is not a confirmed deal, but the directional
signal is now reinforced by physical diplomatic meetings rather than press releases.
Brent at $91.70 is beginning to price genuine re-opening probability, not just hope.
