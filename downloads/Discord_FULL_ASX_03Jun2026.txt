📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Wednesday 3 June delivered a constructive but internally divided session.
XJO added +0.70% to 8,785.7 as Energy and Materials led, anchored by the
overnight US AI chip catalyst (Marvell +32%, HPE +25%) and selective gold
strength via Northern Star's resource upgrade. The index posted its best
close since 28 May, recapturing 8,785 — but breadth was negative (84
advancers vs 104 decliners in XJO), a red flag under a green headline.

The day's macro set-piece was the Q1 GDP print: +0.3% q/q, missing the
+0.5% consensus and slowing sharply from Q4 2025's +0.9%. The annual rate
of 2.5% also missed 2.7% expectations. The miss lands squarely in the
"confirms the RBA pause" camp — markets moved the June hold probability
above 95% after April CPI softened, and today's GDP print reinforces it.
Critically, real GDP per capita fell 0.1% in the quarter, meaning the
three RBA hikes in 2026 are already doing their job of compressing demand.
The silver lining: business investment jumped 6.0% q/q, its highest share
of real GDP since 2015, driven by data centre machinery (+0.7ppt alone).
The AI capex boom is now measurable at the national accounts level.

Northern Star (NST) was the institutional standout: +5.3% on a resource
and reserve upgrade that confirmed 88.9 million total resource ounces —
the kind of balance sheet de-risking that re-rates a mid-cycle gold miner.
The Gold Smart Money ranking (#27 today, surging from #70 five days ago)
is catching up with the RS data. Acrow (ACF) +9.5% on revenue milestone
in Industrial Access is a pure ASX domestic construction theme, distinct
from the Hormuz infrastructure-spending narrative but consistent with it.

Megaport (MP1) remains in a trading halt after announcing four AI
infrastructure contracts worth A$459M TCV and a A$827M equity raise to
fund GPU and networking infrastructure. This is the most direct ASX proxy
for the US AI hyperscaler capex theme: the capital raising dilutes near-
term but the contract TCV confirms the demand pipeline is real. When MP1
comes out of halt, expect significant volume and price discovery.

The XSO "Bullish" regime against XJO/XAO/XTO "Neutral Bear" is the most
analytically important signal of the session — small-caps are leading the
recovery. Historically this regime divergence resolves with large-caps
following if macro headwinds (RBA, Hormuz) don't deteriorate. The 13-day
window to the 16 June RBA meeting is the next test.
