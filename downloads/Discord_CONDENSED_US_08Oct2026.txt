📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
A reported ~$20B gap in OpenAI's annualised revenue hit the denominator of the AI-capex
trade. Every layer sold at once: silicon (INTC -5.34%, ALAB -9.21%), optical (AAOI -13.58%,
COHR -9.63%), neocloud (CRWV -7.77%, ORCL -5.48%) and power (VST -6.35%, BE -6.34%, OKLO
-6.11%). But money rotated rather than left. Breadth was 2:1 positive and value beat growth
by 2.78 points (RPV vs RPG). Energy (Iran deal rejected, Brent $103.85) and staples (PEP,
STZ, PM) absorbed the flow. With the 10yr near 5.3%, a 24-year high, and a Fed signalling
another hike, AI equities are losing multiple support and revenue visibility together.

The tell is where the money went. Refiners (MPC, VLO, PSX) and tankers lead because
the Hormuz premium sits in the seaborne barrel (Brent-WTI spread $12.71). IT services
(ACN +5.96%, CTSH +5.15%) rallied as the inverse AI trade: slower AI revenue means
slower disruption of labour-intensive services. CMG +6.21% on a Starbucks takeover report.
