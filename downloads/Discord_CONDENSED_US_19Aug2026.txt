📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Wednesday was the cleanest sector rotation of the cycle. The index level barely
moved but internals were violently reallocated OUT of the crowded AI-semiconductor
trade and INTO healthcare, hard assets and crypto — on three catalysts at once.

Catalyst one, and the day's defining event: Moderna closed +176.97% (nearly tripling)
after Merck/Moderna's intismeran + Keytruda succeeded in the Phase 3 INTerpath-001
melanoma trial — the first-ever positive late-stage result for an mRNA / neoantigen
cancer vaccine. Prior Phase 2b data had cut recurrence-or-death risk 49%. MRNA added
~40bn in market cap, MRK rose +12.60%, and the Nasdaq Biotech Index printed a record.
This is platform validation, which is why it lifted the entire genomic-medicine complex.

Catalyst two, the macro engine: on the day US public debt hit 40 trillion dollars,
Treasury Secretary Bessent moved to "at least double" long-end buybacks (9 Sep-4 Nov).
The 30yr yield fell from a 19-year high of 5.26% to as low as 5.18%. Suppressed yields
plus a softer dollar is rocket fuel for gold, silver and rate-sensitives — and a
direct complication for Warsh's hawkish Fed ahead of his Jackson Hole keynote next week.

Catalyst three: the SEC's proposed "Regulation Crypto Assets" framework plus the
risk-on tape sent BTC toward 68-69k and ETH up double digits (IBIT +5.96%, ETHA
+9.97%, MSTR +12.68%, COIN +9.55%). Caveat: those equities remain 35-39% underwater
YTD — short-covering off summer lows, not a confirmed turn.

The funding source was tech. XLK -1.07% was the worst sector and NDX the only red
index. The optical/semi names that led all spring were crushed — AAOI -7.02%, LITE
-5.23%, LRCX -6.33%, TER -6.09% — with hardware (WDC -6.87%, DELL -6.64%) and steel
(STLD -7.53%) alongside. Critically, breadth was POSITIVE on every index (SPX 283/216,
RUT 1106/812) and VIX fell -6%: the signature of healthy rotation, not de-risking.
