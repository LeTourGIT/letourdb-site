📰 KEY HEADLINES
---------------------------------------------------
🔻 NVIDIA / AI: Nvidia fell 4.1% for a third straight decline and is ~24% lower
   month-to-date, on pace for its worst month since April 2022. It reports
   fiscal Q3 after Tuesday's close, the first print under interim CEO Colette
   Kress following Jensen Huang's 12/11 resignation; the board says it is not
   considering bringing Huang back.
🏛️ POLICY / CHIPS: Reports that Washington is moving to curb remote (cloud)
   access to Nvidia's advanced AI chips by Chinese users of offshore data
   centres hit the whole chip stack: SOXX -5.41%, MU -7.62%, SNDK -8.58%.
   Closing the cloud-rental loophole removes a source of incremental demand
   that equipment and memory names were pricing in, and adds to Washington's
   broader scrutiny of Nvidia's influence.
🌍 GEOPOLITICAL: Iran struck Bahrain in an attack on a US base, NATO member
   Estonia is closing its Russia border after attempted sabotage of a drone
   maker, and China confirmed a historic Xi-Trump-Putin summit. Markets sold
   oil (-3.29%) and defence (ITA -2.46%) into the escalation, which signals
   that the summit's de-escalation optionality is being priced over near-term
   conflict risk.
🏦 RATES / FED: Fed Chair Warsh says the Fed is starting to work out how AI
   reshapes the economy, while MarketWatch flags bond-market volatility
   'exploding higher'. With the 10-year at ~5% after last week's refunding and
   RUT now Bearish, the rate channel is what is breaking small caps,
   independent of the AI unwind.
🍗 EARNINGS / DEFENSIVES: Tyson +8.72% on a FY27 beef-recovery guide and AECOM
   +6.83% AH on record margins and an in-line FY27 EPS guide. Both are non-AI
   earnings streams with improving visibility, which is the profile capital is
