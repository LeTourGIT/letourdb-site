📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
October closed with the Nasdaq Composite's worst day since June (-2.86%) and
NDX -3.46%, as Intel's -28.58% collapse turned a week of mixed megacap earnings
into a verdict on AI capex. Intel beat Q3 on every line, then guided Q4 revenue
to $15-16B and gross margin to 38%. That the market wiped a quarter of its value
on a beat tells you the AI-hardware trade was priced for perfection, and the
first crack in guidance triggered forced de-risking across the supply chain.

The chain ran exactly as a capex-digestion scare should: Amazon's print revived
spend worries → hyperscalers dipped only ~1.4-1.6% → their suppliers were hit
hardest (SOXX -8.24%, XSD -6.30%, SNDK -13.47%, AMD -10.88%, QCOM -11.12%,
VRT -5.46%, GEV -3.38%) → AI-networking momentum names (AAOI -12.80%, COHR
-11.34%, ALAB -10.93%) that were flat-to-up on the week liquidated in a day.
Software joined in (IGV -3.41%, CIBR -3.78%, DDOG -11.50%, APP -10.36%).

The other side of the tape was orderly rotation, not panic. XLE +1.91%, XLP
+1.41%, XLU +0.67% and XLV +0.45% all closed green; RPV +0.47% and SCHD +0.48%
led the style board while RPG -4.31% and QQQ -3.47% trailed it. SPX printed 175
new 20day highs against only 48 lows on a -1.82% day — defensives and energy
are breaking out while tech breaks down. Equal-weight RSP (-0.79%) beat SPX by
a full point: technology's roughly one-third index weight at -4.89% explains
most of the index loss on its own (our estimate: ~1.5 points of the 1.82%).

Macro offered no cushion. The Fed's 10-2 hawkish hold on Wednesday (two
dissents for a hike) keeps a December move live, gold fell -3.62% to $3,875
— its lowest in a year despite Iran claiming four tankers hit mines in Hormuz —
and a record $6.3B Binance hack pushed IBIT -5.14% and ETHA -4.47%. When gold,
crypto and tech all fall together, real-rate expectations are doing the work.

Next week stacks the catalysts: US midterms and Apple earnings. NDX RSI-15 at
35.62 and VIX still under 20 argue the flush is mature but not finished.
