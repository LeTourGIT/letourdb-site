📝  SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday 18 September was a study in why the ASX cannot simply import US risk appetite.
Wall Street staged a sharp post-Fed-hike relief rally overnight (SPX +1.14%, NDX +1.73%,
VIX -12.8%) — but the XJO finished dead flat at 8,731.2 (-0.01%). The reason sits 11 days
out: the RBA meets 29 September with ~82% of a +25bp hike to 4.60% now priced. A local
central bank about to tighten, freshly reinforced by the Fed's 16 Sep move to 4.00% and
the BoJ's 31-year-high hike, is a hard ceiling on any relief bounce.

Materials (XMJ +1.60%) was the only sector to lead with conviction, and its engine was
precious metals. Gold in record territory (~US$4,434, +0.85%) drove a wall of ASX gold
re-rates — PDI +12.36% [ASX200], and the entire institutional RS leaderboard (SBM [H],
TCG, SX2, ALK [H]) is now gold. Rare earths added the catalyst layer: ARU +11.76% [ASX300]
on a binding offtake extension (09:23, price-sensitive) — the session's cleanest news-driven
institutional move — dragging BRE +13.65% and DVP +11.84% with it.

Underneath the resources bid, the rate-sensitive complex bled. Real Estate (XRE -1.34%)
was the worst sector — the purest read on the RBA overhang — with Consumer Staples
(-1.04%), Energy (-1.10%, WTI lower) and Financials (-0.59%) all red. AFG -7.37% [ASX300],
a mortgage aggregator, was rate-sensitivity in its rawest form. The day's disaster was
GEM -17.39% [ASX300], G8 Education gapping to a multi-year low on a weak trading update
and lowered attendance guidance atop an existing impairment/occupancy overhang.

Breadth confirms this was resources-narrow, not broad: XJO adv/dec was only 107/89, new
20day lows (23) outnumbered highs (8) nearly 3:1, T2108 sits at 32.5%, and just 41% of
XJO names hold their 200MA. This is a market being held up by a single sector while the
rate-sensitive two-thirds erode ahead of a near-certain hike.
