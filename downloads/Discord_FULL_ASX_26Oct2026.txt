📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
A resources-led rebound lifted the ASX 200 +0.17% to 8,809.9 after
Friday's liquidation session (41 XJO stocks down 4%+). Materials
(XMJ +1.71%) did all the heavy lifting on a two-pronged rotation:
lithium (PLS +8.40%, VUL +10.63%, MI6 +7.58%, LTR +6.25%, ELV +4.12%)
and gold miners (PDI +7.34%, GGP +7.03%, OBM +6.76%, NST +2.77%).
None of the lithium majors lodged news — this is sector flow and short
covering after XMJ fell -6.22% over 5day, not a fundamental re-rating.

BlueScope (BSL +2.94%, new 20day high) upgraded 1H FY27 guidance at
08:29 — a clean steel-margin beat in a tape that badly needed one.
FMG +2.17% and BHP +1.04% added weight, but iron ore at $90.83 (-6.4%
over 20day) caps how far the big miners can run.

Under the surface the rate trade dominated. RBA Assistant Governor
Sarah Hunter said rates may need to rise further and November hike
odds cleared 60% — Financials, Utilities, Discretionary and Staples
(XSJ -1.54%, the weakest sector) all closed red. Stock-specific damage
was brutal where regulators or reviews hit: TUA -30.49% (Singapore
IMDA show-cause notice) and OFX -24.51% (strategic review update).

This is peak quarterly season: 81 Appendix 4C/5B filings from 70
companies, 46 of them Materials. EIQ +17.05% (4C), LOT +12.12%
(Kayelekera first export) and TCG +7.87% (high-grade extension) were
the filings the market rewarded. Eleven trading halts remain open —
each is a pending catalyst for Tuesday or later.

Structurally nothing is fixed: XJO breadth 111/84 is only 1.3:1, new
20day lows (28) still outnumber highs (15), and just 33.5% of the XJO
sits above its 200MA. The test is 8,830-8,845 (200MA/50MA) into the
September inflation data and the 03/11 RBA decision.
