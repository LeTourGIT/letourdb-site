📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The defining event was geopolitical: the Strait of Hormuz reopening. WTI crashed
-7.24% to $75.31 and Brent -7.24% to $78.88 as OPEC+ signalled it would step up
supply and the White House stepped back from escalation. That single de-risking
event collapsed the oil-war risk premium embedded in equities for weeks — and the
index response was violent and broad: Dow +900 points, SPX above 7,700 for the
first time, and NDX +3.32%. This is a risk-premium repricing, not an earnings-driven
melt-up: the macro overhang lifted, and every risk asset gapped to catch up.

Semiconductors were the transmission mechanism. SOXX +6.80% and XSD +6.14% led,
with XLK +4.98%, QTEC +5.32%, IGV +4.70% and IYW +4.05% behind — 17 ETFs cleared
+3% and NOT ONE breached -3%. Micron memory-share gains, SK Hynix earnings and a
re-rating of the AI-capex complex (INTC +10.84%, MRVL +12.81%, ALAB +12.65%,
SNDK +10.84%, SMCI +10.65%) drove the tape. AMD is the tell to watch: revenue
+50% with data-center sales doubling, yet the stock FELL — the semis bar is now
priced for perfection even as the group rips. When beats stop being enough,
leadership gets narrower and more selective from here.

Palantir was the signature single name: +29.45% on a blowout that fused government
and commercial AI acceleration. Paired with Leidos +10.01% (defense-IT) and the
Pentagon-AI thesis, the government-AI-spend leg is now confirming alongside the
infrastructure leg. Wayfair +29.97% and Zebra +26.47% show the beat-and-rip regime
extends beyond tech into consumer-discretionary and enterprise-hardware — a breadth
of earnings strength consistent with the broad-tape rally.

Breadth CONFIRMS rather than diverges — the critical distinction from prior narrow
ATH prints. SPX advancers led decliners 356/141 (2.5:1), RUT 1441/476 (3:1),
DJI 25/5, NDX 83/19. T2108 at 67.0% (SPX) and % above 50MA at 67.4% put structural
participation firmly in healthy territory. When the tape gaps up AND breadth
expands AND small-caps lead (RUT +1.85% with 300 stocks up >4%), the move has
internal support — this is accumulation, not a mechanical cap-weight lift.

The rotation signature is unambiguously risk-on and cyclical. Smart Money surged
into Mining Support Services (+112 ranks), Home Furnishings (+105/+103), Software
(+74) and Pension Funds (+76), while Construction & Engineering EXITED to #12 from
#1 three days ago — with Sterling Infra -11.42% as the stock-level proof. Utilities
were the funding source: NRG -15.48%, VST -8.15%, CEG -2.36% as the AI-power trade
unwound and capital rotated back into core semis. The one caution flag: Michael
Burry publicly called 'a major top, possibly a 1987-type fall' — sentiment worth
logging as the tape gaps into resistance on a regime the model still rates Bearish.
