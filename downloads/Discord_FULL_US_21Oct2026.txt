📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The macro backdrop is an energy shock with a rates echo. Brent tagged $110
intraday on a reported attack on a Saudi Aramco field and stalled Hormuz
diplomacy (Iran's foreign minister saw little reason to expect progress), while
the 10year Treasury held above 5.1%. Treasury Counselor David Zervos argued
energy, not sticky inflation, is driving yields — a framing that matters,
because an oil-driven yield spike fades when oil does, whereas an inflation-
driven one forces the Fed. Putin hosting Witkoff and offering to help on Iran
is the first credible diplomatic off-ramp in weeks; oil's failure to break
lower on Gulf flows resuming tells you the market wants proof, not promises.

Against that backdrop, AI hardware ripped. Super Micro +9.55% on a revenue
guide raise to $18.5-19.5B (from $14.5-15.5B) lifted the full server and
optical chain: CRDO +6.28%, CIEN +5.67%, BE +7.13% (AI power), APLD +8.54% and
WULF +6.24% (AI-hosting conversions of crypto miners). SOXX +2.07% and XSD
+2.06%. SpaceX's first public-company profit — with its AI segment ($7.6B)
now out-earning Starlink and launch combined — reinforced the "AI revenue is
real" narrative heading into Meta. This is a capex-confirmation trade: every
AI infrastructure name rallied on evidence that end-demand is still rising.

The second engine was cybersecurity. A critical PHP flaw (Google, OpenAI and
Amazon all responding) drove ZS +7.84%, OKTA +7.54%, CIBR +2.72% (top equity
ETF of the day) and fed the broader software bid: MDB +6.71%, DDOG +6.50%,
IGV +1.77%. Smart Money confirms this is more than a headline pop — Software
SURGED from rank 85 to rank 46 in three days, and eight of the RS top 30 are
software names. Event-driven cyber demand is landing on a group that was
already being accumulated.

The losers tell the other half of the story. Communication Services was the
worst sector (XLC -1.11%): AT&T -6.45%, T-Mobile -2.54% and Verizon -2.20% on
fears that Starlink Mobile becomes a real wireless competitor, Crown Castle
-3.07% on the tower read-through, and Netflix -5.31% to a 52-week low after
cutting its ad-revenue guide. Medtech was hit hard inside a flat healthcare
tape — DXCM -6.39%, ISRG -2.26%, SYK -2.23%, BSX -2.35%, ABT -1.89% — while
life-science tools and CROs surged (CRL +9.33%, MEDP +6.43%, DHR +4.29%).
That is a rotation within healthcare, from device multiples to research spend.

Breadth was positive but thinning. SPX advancers 281 vs 218 decliners (1.29:1)
versus 402/98 on Tuesday, and NYSE ran only 1.37:1 — this was a narrower,
leadership-driven day after a broad Tuesday. But the structural gauges kept
improving: SPX % above 20MA 73.6% (from 64.2% two sessions ago), T2108 61.4%
(from 51.6%), 114 new 20day highs vs 16 lows. Large-cap breadth is healing;
small-cap breadth is not (RUT 933/977, 30.6% above 50MA, 656 stocks down 25%
in the quarter vs 317 up).
