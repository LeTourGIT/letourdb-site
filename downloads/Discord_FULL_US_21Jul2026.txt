📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Tuesday was a violent mean-reversion session — a memory-chip snapback fused with
a broad commodity bid, layered over a quiet de-rating of the market's most-crowded
quality compounders. The headline indices all closed green (SPX +0.89%, NDX +1.93%,
DJI +0.74%, RUT +1.53%) and VIX collapsed -8.58% to 17.05, but the internals tell
a rotation story, not a broad-advance story. This was money leaving high-multiple
defensives and re-entering beaten-down cyclicals.

The engine was memory. Micron +12.17% led after Morgan Stanley flagged memory prices
could rise ~25% on sustained AI demand, and JPMorgan's Matejka called the group
oversold with no meaningful supply additions before 2028. That read-through lit the
whole complex: SanDisk +14.27%, Western Digital +12.51%, Intel +8.64%, plus the
equipment layer (Teradyne +12.07%) and optics (AAOI +15.76%, Coherent +11.15%,
Lumentum +9.41%). SOXX +5.45% / XSD +5.44% were the semi ETFs, but note the 5day
column is still NEGATIVE (SOXX -2.68%, XSD -3.38%) — the PHLX had fallen ~20% over
the prior month. This is oversold repair off a real correction, not a fresh capex
breakout. SK Hynix ADRs +14% into its 29 July print make that the binary confirm.

The second leg was commodities and hard assets. Copper Miners +5.93%, Silver Miners
+5.77%, Gold Miners +4.88%, Silver +4.12%, and the uranium pair (URA +4.09%, URNM
+3.98%) all ripped — the strongest ETFs on the board sat OUTSIDE equities entirely.
Oil repriced HIGHER, not lower: WTI +2.68% to $84.39, Brent +3.03% to $91.23, as the
US struck Iran and Houthis threatened Saudi shipping while mediators pushed a 10-day
ceasefire. This is the mirror image of the spring's peace-signal repricing — the
geopolitical risk premium is back IN the tape. Energy Smart Money confirms it: five
oil sub-industries sit in the top nine, Refining & Marketing #1.

The offset — and the tell that this is rotation — was the quiet destruction of
crowded compounders. Danaher -10.99% (Q2 beat but cut core-revenue growth to 3-4%
on a bioprocessing timing gap and a 9pt margin contraction) and MSCI -10.14% (in-line
revenue but a slight EPS miss and RAISED expense guidance) dragged the entire
Professional Information Services cluster: S&P Global -3.81%, Equifax -3.93%, Moody's
-2.89%. Software was sold in sympathy: Figma -8.70%, Okta -4.51%, Workday -4.11%,
CrowdStrike -3.70%, Datadog -3.20%, Adobe -3.23%. IGV -1.25%, CIBR -1.32% and OGIG
-1.53% were the only meaningful ETF decliners. The market paid up for cyclical torque
by selling the names it had hidden in — a classic late-cycle risk-appetite flip.

Breadth confirms the narrowness beneath the green. SPX advanced on 229 up vs 268 down
— a NEGATIVE ratio on an up day, the mega-cap semi complex mathematically lifting the
tape. NDX was a dead-even 51/51. Only the Russell showed genuine broad participation
(1318/643, +4% stocks 199 vs -4% just 36) — small-caps embraced the risk-on impulse
far more evenly than the cap-weighted majors. New 20day lows still outnumber highs in
SPX (45 vs 29): the repair is early. Tomorrow night's GOOGL and TSLA prints are the
next regime test — the read-through on AI demand and auto margins will decide whether
this bounce becomes a trend or fades back into the downtrend it interrupted.
