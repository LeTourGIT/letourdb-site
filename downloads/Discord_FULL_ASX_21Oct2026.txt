📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The ASX 200 finished +0.16% at 8,831.3, but the session was a
red-to-green escape: the index traded negative for most of the day
and only turned positive around 3:40pm. The day's defining event
was NEXTDC (NXT -41.72% to $5.35) — out of a trading halt at 08:35
with an M3 Melbourne power delay and an FY27 reset that cut
revenue guidance to A$475m–500m (from A$615m–640m) and EBITDA to
A$245m–265m (from A$385m–410m). Roughly $2.9bn of market value
vanished, and XIJ (-3.32%) closed as the worst sector despite XRO
(+5.02%) and WTC (+2.92%) rallying.

This is a pivotal Australian AI-infrastructure data point: power,
not demand, is the binding constraint. Jemena paused grid works
after community opposition and the next supply step is deferred to
FY2028, so NXT has frozen new developments and land buys and
flagged asset recycling. The contagion ran straight through the
build chain — GMG -2.23%, SXE -7.06%, SYL -5.43%, SHA -4.10% and
MYG -9.74% — while MP1 (+5.00%) and IFT (+1.94%, data-centre
update at 12:33) caught a relative bid. With the ABC and CNBC both
leading on the story and a Google Cloud outage in the same news
cycle, the market is re-pricing data-centre timelines, not just
one stock.

The index was carried by the rate-beneficiary complex. Financials
(XFJ +0.96%) — NAB +1.86%, WBC +1.14%, CBA +0.91%, GDG +4.76% —
and Comm Services (XTJ +2.23%, SEK +4.37%, CAR +3.45%, REA +2.73%)
did the heavy lifting, and TCL (+3.01%) printed a clean
September-quarter traffic update. Deputy Governor Hauser's warning
that inflation is 'materially' worse than the August forecast
pushed November hike pricing above 40% (some measures above 50%)
for the 03/11 decision. Banks and insurers rallying into a hawkish
RBA is coherent: margin and float income win, while REITs (XRE
-0.49%) lose.

Materials (XMJ -1.04%) was the anchor: BHP -1.61%, RIO -1.48%, NEM
-1.86% and EVN -1.62% with iron ore pinned near US$91/t and the
sector -7.88% over 20 days. October quarterlies split the gold
tape — NST +1.79% and WGX +2.21% were rewarded on production
delivery while PDI (-4.66%) and TCG (-4.67%) were sold without
news. Energy (XEJ +0.49%) was supported by Brent at US$110 and
BPT's Q1 (+5.49%).

Breadth tells two stories. XJO advancers beat decliners 113/73 and
new 20day highs (41) more than doubled new lows (19) — a genuine
improvement from yesterday's 69/115. But across the All Ords only
29% of stocks sit above their 50MA and T2108 is 32.7%, and XSO
remains Bearish. Forward catalysts: CSL's post-close CEO
appointment (16:37), ALK results 23/10, a heavy quarterly-report
pipeline, and soft US tech futures (NQ -0.47%).
