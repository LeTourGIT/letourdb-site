Thursday's session was defined by two divergent earnings narratives arriving
simultaneously: Nvidia's extraordinary Q1 FY27 beat ($81.6B revenue, +85% YoY,

a cluster of "in-line or miss" reports — Walmart, Intuit, and Deere — that reinforced
the pattern of a market rewarding AI infrastructure spend while penalising
companies perceived as cyclically exposed or structurally cost-heavy.

The clean energy complex was the session's surprise institutional story. TAN +3.08%
and ICLN +3.07% were the only two ETFs above +3%, representing a structural
allocation shift into renewable assets that the Smart Money rotation data confirms —

program — validating the government's commitment to domestic REE separations
capacity. REMX, the rare earth ETF, +0.55% on the day but 5day -4.16% shows the
complex is still recovering from last week's China-agreement selldown. The DOE

pilot is not a grant but a sovereign validation of a chokepoint supply chain. The thesis
moves from speculative to structurally supported. REMX's 5day underperformance
is the buy-the-dip setup; the 1year +141.2% tells you where capital has already been.

elevated and Hormuz remains contested. The 5day XLE +1.83% still shows Smart Money
held energy through the week even as Thursday gave back ground.

healthier session than the cap-weight distortion of the 1 May ATH close. T2108 at 50.2%

sustained breakout. The NDX overbought reading (RSI-15 70.06) is the structural
