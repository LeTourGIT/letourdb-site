📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday delivered a broad, high-velocity relief rally. NDX led +1.73%, SPX +1.14%,
with the VIX collapsing -12.82% to 15.44 — the single loudest signal of the day.
A -12.8% vol crush of this size is the market exhaling: 'peak fear' from the prior
selloff and the Iran-oil premium is being priced OUT. MarketWatch framed it exactly
— stocks up on hopes rising inflation and oil prices are now contained, with WTI
easing -1.06% to $101.19 despite continued Middle East escalation (Houthi strikes
reaching Mecca, Saudi pipeline damage). Oil bid but capped = the goldilocks the tape
wanted.

Semiconductors were the engine. XSD +4.06% and SOXX +3.39% led the ETF board, and
the single-stock tape confirmed institutional conviction: INTC +7.67% on $16.1B
turnover (the day's heaviest by far), SMCI +9.5%, ALAB +9.06%, SWKS +6.69%, HPE
+7.69%. MarketWatch: Micron, Intel and other chips furthering strong comebacks;
Jensen Huang says NVDA will sell twice as many chips next year. Smart Money confirms
the flow — Semiconductors rocketed to rank 13 (from rank 124 nine days ago) and
Semiconductor Equipment to rank 15 (from rank 136). This is a hard, dated re-
accumulation of the chip complex off oversold, not a one-day squeeze.

The session's second leg was PRECIOUS METALS. SIL +4.49%, SLV +3.37%, GDX +3.36%
all cleared +3%, with silver (SI +2.35%) outrunning gold (GC +1.05%) and SLV
outrunning GLD — the classic late-cycle catch-up trade Morgan Stanley flagged
(questioning gold's role while another metal is set to outperform). Metals rallying
ALONGSIDE a risk-on equity tape and a VIX crush is unusual — it says the bid is
liquidity/debasement-driven, not a fear hedge. A power/energy-transition micro-theme
rode shotgun: GNRC +18.3% (Smart Money's rank-1 Heavy Electrical Equipment), OKLO
+11.3% and URA +3.17% (nuclear/uranium), TAN +3.9% (solar) — the AI-datacenter-power
build-out expressed through every layer from generators to reactors.

Underneath the green, a decisive ROTATION ran the other way. Software and IT-services
were SOLD into strength: CRM -3.07%, CRWV -4.16%, SPOT -3.4%, RDDT -3.03%, with OKTA
sliding in the RS table. Capital is rotating OUT of software and INTO semiconductors
— the mirror image of a software-surge session, and a tell that this bounce favours
cyclical hardware/AI-infra over high-multiple application software. Defensive telecom
was dumped outright in the risk-on move: TMUS -5.57%, CMCSA -3.46%, VZ -2.87%, T
-1.82% — dragging XLC to the only meaningfully red sector (-0.58%).

The RS leadership tells a quieter structural story the tape masks: 13 of the RS Top
20 are HEALTHCARE, overwhelmingly biotech (MRNA, MNOV, IOVA, CYPH, HELP, TWST, ARCT).
TEM +14.85% (Tempus AI), MRNA +8.55% and ILMN +7.1% carried it intraday, and HLTH
(Healthcare-AI ETF) was the day's number-one mover at +4.79%. Smart Money's Advanced Medical
Equipment industry SURGED to rank 7 from rank 111 nine days ago (+97 window). Biotech
is the accumulation nobody is talking about while everyone watches chips.

The critical caveat: breadth improved on the DAY (SPX adv/dec 291/206, ~1.4:1) but
the medium-term damage is intact. SPX has just 21% of members above their 20MA, 30.6%
above the 50MA, T2108 at 27.8% — deeply oversold — and NEW 20-day LOWS still
outnumber new highs 87 to 25 (RUT: 246 to 110). An oversold bounce with real one-day
participation, layered over a market that has NOT repaired its trend. Respect the
bounce; do not yet call it a bottom.
