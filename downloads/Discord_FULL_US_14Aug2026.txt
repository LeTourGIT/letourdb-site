📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday was a rotation session dressed as a down day. The headline indices
closed a hair red — SPX -0.17% to 7,785.76, NDX -0.13%, Dow -0.20% — but the
internals tell the opposite story: Russell 2000 +0.51%, SPX advancers 248 vs
244 decliners (essentially even), and VIX DOWN 2.60% to 14.25. When the tape
sells the index but not the market, the "loss" is a cap-weight illusion: a
handful of mega-cap AI names dragging a green median stock into the red.

The stress is concentrated and diagnosable. AVGO -5.94% and AMAT -5.12%
(the latter on earnings) led an AI-infrastructure de-rate, joined by a broad
software/fintech unwind — ORCL -3.65%, WDAY -3.76%, TWLO -4.50%, NET -4.55%,
PATH -4.02% — and a crypto-adjacent flush: COIN -3.53%, CRCL -5.01%, HOOD
-3.83%, MSTR -4.18%. This is the same cohort that took July's "AI wreck," and
the positioning tape confirms the exit: CNBC reports Tepper's Appaloosa SOLD
AI-memory names and rotated into the Magnificent Seven, while other desks
did the reverse. Crowded-trade unwind, not thesis break.

Underneath, the opposite trade is being put on with conviction. Energy XLE
+1.39% led the SPDRs, and the hard-asset complex ran hot: REMX rare earths
+3.18% (the day's ONLY +3% ETF), GDX gold miners +1.93%, XME metals & mining
+1.62%, OIH oil services +2.62%, MP Materials +5.53%. Gold printed +1.20% to
$4,432 and WTI +1.29% to $82.40. The catalyst is geopolitical: Iran is now
framed as an "indefinite war," the Strait of Hormuz remains blocked with
traffic near three-month lows (Iran publicly disputing Trump's normalisation
claim), and Washington is tightening the economic squeeze. Oil at $82 is far
below May's $102 spike, but the DIRECTION has re-inverted — escalation is
back to being the marginal price-setter, and Smart Money is positioned for it.

The most important nuance is INSIDE semiconductors. This was not a blanket
chip selloff. Equal-weight XSD +1.72%, AMD +6.50%, and memory — SNDK +7.39%
(+35% over five days), WDC +4.41% — all rallied hard even as cap-weight SOXX
finished flat (-0.06%) and AVGO/AMAT bled. Capital is rotating from the
AI-infrastructure mega-caps toward memory/storage and the second-tier logic
names. SanDisk's investor commentary this week and the memory-cycle turn are
the pull; AVGO/AMAT valuation air is the push. The RS and Smart Money lists
both corroborate: Computer Hardware jumped to Smart Money rank 9.
