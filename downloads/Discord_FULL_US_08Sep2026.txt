📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Tuesday was a rotation day dressed as a down day. Headline losses were modest but beneath
them capital moved hard along one axis: OUT of long-duration (software, healthcare, biotech,
high-multiple growth) and INTO hard, cash-flowing assets — energy, commodities, and the
picks-and-shovels of the AI datacenter buildout. Catalyst: a Hormuz escalation (US struck 3
Iranian oil tankers, a Saudi facility was hit, Iran seized an unmanned sub) sent WTI +3.0% to
$94 and pushed the 10yr above 4.8%. Rising real yields are kryptonite for distant cash flows.

The cleanest micro-story is FIBRE OPTICS. Corning (GLW +7.56%) signed a multi-year fibre-
optic supply deal with Verizon, and the read-through rippled through the optical complex:
Lumentum LITE +11.04%, Coherent COHR +7.10%, Ciena CIEN +6.32%, AAOI +5.7%. This re-prices
the whole optical-interconnect / fibre-buildout chain as an AI-bandwidth capex theme with a
named blue-chip customer behind it. CIEN did it on a still-negative 5day (-10.8%) — the kind
of trend-reversal footprint that precedes an RS-list entry. Watch LITE/COHR/CIEN hold.

The second cluster is AI PHYSICAL INFRASTRUCTURE — power and hardware. Bloom Energy
(BE +9.63%, 5day +34%) leads Heavy Electrical Equipment, which Smart Money dragged from
rank 122 to 3 in nine days. Datacenter-electrical (Vertiv, Eaton) drove Electrical Components
from rank 67 to 14 in a single day. Intel +9.05%, HPE +7.75%, Dell and the memory names
ripped ("memory stocks pressing summer highs"). CoreWeave +11.72%, WULF +8.2%, APLD +7.4%
round out the datacenter-capex trade.

What got sold tells the same story reversed. Software was the exodus epicentre: NOW -5.0%,
TTAN -18.7% AH, PATH -7.8%, TEAM -6.9%, ADBE -3.5%, CRM -3.9%, MDB -3.5%, ZS -4.6%.
Healthcare was the worst sector (XLV -2.52%): AMGN -10.1%, SYK -8.8%, BSX -5.9%, LLY -2.2%.
Travel/leisure (BKNG -6.7%, EXPE -7.9%, DASH -5.3%) and financials (XLF -1.38%) completed it.

Breadth confirms caution: SPX 145 adv / 354 dec, 114 new 20day lows vs 18 highs — internal
damage far worse than the -0.58% print. RUT uglier (590/1329, 219 new lows). T2108 36%, only
27% of SPX above 20MA. But ~60% remain above the 200MA everywhere: bending, not breaking.
The next 3-5 sessions of crude and the 10yr are the whole ballgame.
