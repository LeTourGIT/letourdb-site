📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The S&P 500 closed above 7,800 for the first time (+0.58% to 7,818.93),
clearing the 13/08/2026 high after 37 sessions, and the Nasdaq 100 made it
six straight gains. The bond market gave permission: the 10-year eased about
4bps to 5.27% as a soft payrolls print (+29k, per press reports) cooled bets
on a follow-up to the Fed's 16 September hike. Softer labour data → yields
ease → bond proxies bounce (XLU +2.98%, XLRE +1.06%) → record close with VIX
at 15.01.

The defining trade was power, not chips. Google signed a 20-year agreement
with Constellation Energy for 890 MW of new nuclear capacity. CEG +12.25%,
TLN +12.43%, VST +10.77%, with URNM +4.97% and URA +4.01% the only ETFs
above +3%. A hyperscaler PPA converts merchant power-price exposure into two
decades of contracted revenue: a re-rating from commodity generator to
infrastructure annuity. Vistra and Talen are not parties, so their gains are
a bet that the next contract is theirs. XLU is still -5.27% over 20day, so
this is a reversal from oversold, not yet a trend.

Technology split along a clear line. Marvell's analyst day sized the AI
interconnect market at roughly $65B growing 60-70% a year through 2030, and
optical names ran: CIEN +13.85%, FN +7.78%, ALAB +7.58%, AAOI +7.07%, MRVL
+5.81%. Semiconductor equipment was sold (KLAC -4.54%, LRCX -3.44%) and
storage fell on reports Toshiba will expand hard-drive output (WDC -6.93%).
SOXX -0.01% against equal-weight XSD +1.09% is a flat index hiding heavy
dispersion. Capital is leaving the layers where supply is being added for
the layers that are still the bottleneck: a mid-cycle rotation inside the AI
build-out, not an exit.

Healthcare paid for it. XLV (-0.17%) was the only sector lower, HLTH -6.39%
was the only ETF below -3% (⚠ EXTREME), and the laggards are AI-diagnostics
and genomics: TEM -13.85%, GH -8.83%, MRNA -7.75%, ILMN -6.86%. No single
catalyst surfaced; coverage describes profit-taking. This group is the RS
list (14 of the Top 20 are Healthcare, 11 of them fell). The most crowded
momentum cohort being sold on a record day is distribution funding the next
leadership, and the 20day High sheet shows where: cybersecurity and AI
networking.

Breadth was healthy on the day and alarming in structure. SPX Adv/Dec was
337/162 and new 20day highs beat lows 72 to 19. But only 29.4% of members
are above their 50MA; across 275 record closes since 2018 the prior low was
43.4%. Over 20day QQQ is +5.75% and RSP -2.03%. The Dow sits 5.2% and the
Russell 2000 7.8% below their August highs, both Bearish. A 10-year at 5.27%
is the wedge: megacap AI earnings outrun the discount rate, leveraged small
caps cannot. The constructive read: T2108 has risen every day from 19.0%
four sessions ago, a washed-out base turning up. The risk: no cushion if
FOMC minutes or CPI revive hike pricing.
