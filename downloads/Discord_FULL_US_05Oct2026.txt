Monday gave the Nasdaq 100 a fifth straight gain and back-to-back record
closes (31,076.44, +0.87%); the S&P 500 (+0.66% to 7,773.95) finished 0.32%
short of its 13/08/2026 record. What the rally ignored matters more: the
10-year yield hit a 52-week high at 5.315% and the 30-year 5.670%, taking
2s10s to ~49bps. A bear steepener: Friday's +29,000 payrolls cut October
hike odds at the front end; ISM services prices paid at 74.0 (highest since
July 2022) kept the long end selling. Equities are trading the Fed, bonds
are trading supply and inflation, and both cannot stay right.

The regime table shows it in price. SPX and NDX are Bullish; DJI and RUT are
Bearish, both under their 50MA and 5.67% and 7.21% below their August highs.
Over 20 days XLK is +7.29% and every other SPDR sector is negative. Levered
balance sheets and long-duration cash flows are being marked to a 5.3%
ten-year; mega-cap AI, which funds capex from cash flow, is not — hence IWF
+4.00% vs RSP -3.60% over 20 days.

Geopolitics set the biggest stock moves. Flavio Bolsonaro's first-round lead
in Brazil (47% to Lula's 45%, against the polls) re-rated Brazilian ADRs: XP
+30.93%, STNE +20.84%, NU +13.03%, PBR +11.50%. Hormuz remains shut and
Aramco's CEO called global stockpiles "scarily thin", with a two-year
rebuild after any reopening. Crude eased (WTI -0.72%, Brent -1.08%) yet OIH
+3.14% topped the ETF board and Oil & Gas Drilling jumped from rank 115 to
rank 36 in Smart Money. Equities are paying for the duration of the rebuild,
not the spot price.

M&A did the rest. Schneider Electric's $22.6B cash bid sent PTC +33.49% and
repriced design software (BSY +7.82%, TEAM +4.66%, ADSK +4.51%; IGV 5day
+4.07%). A strategic buyer paying cash with the long bond at a 52-week high
marks private-market value for a group still -5.40% over 1year. CHRW -10.85%
on selling Global Forwarding to RXO (RXO +22.54% — the market thinks the
seller gave up the growth asset). Corporates are not waiting for cheaper
money.

Healthcare was two tapes. Vaxcyte's Phase 3 win sent PCVX +30.70% and hit
MRK -3.30%; pharma ETFs extended their slide (IHE 5day -4.82%). Yet HLTH
+3.01%, TEM +9.03%, ILMN +7.56%, MRNA +6.95% and 15 of the RS Top 20 are
healthcare — while Biotech ranks 113 of 139 on 10day industry ROC.
Leadership is in genomics tools and single-name catalysts, not the group.

Breadth improved from a hole: SPX Adv/Dec 315/183, share above the 20MA up
to 34.73% from 26.35%, T2108 27.15% from 19.00% on 30/09/2026. But only
26.75% of members are above the 50MA with the index a third of a percent
from a record, and new 20day lows (77) beat highs (40). Either breadth
repairs upward or a shrinking list keeps carrying the index. Fed minutes
Wednesday and the first Q3 reports are the tests.
