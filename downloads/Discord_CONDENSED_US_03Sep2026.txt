📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
A rates-driven, high-beta risk-on session. Two Fed signals did the work: Waller
backed no hike, VP Vance urged cuts. Yields fell, VIX collapsed -5.79% to 14.32,
and capital rotated into the longest-duration assets — crypto, software, rate-
sensitive financials and REITs. This is a discount-rate repricing: when real
yields drop, the highest-beta cash flows move first and hardest.

Crypto was the loudest signal. IBIT +5.85% and ETHA +5.37% both cleared the ±5%
EXTREME line, and proxies went vertical: MSTR +17.56%, HOOD +16.57%, CRCL
+16.46%, COIN +10.14%. Smart Money confirms it — Fintech Infrastructure sits at
rank 1, up from rank 139 nine days ago, the fastest rotation in the file.

Software split the tape. SNOW +16.55% delivered the season's cleanest AI-
monetisation print (consumption re-accelerating) and dragged IGV +3.41% with
PLTR +7.71%, NOW +6.49%, ORCL +5.69%, CRWD +5.68%. But the chip rebound FAILED:
AVGO -2.74% sold a good number and CIEN -10.36% guided optical down, cracking
LITE/AAOI. The move is UP the stack — out of silicon/optical, into software.

Defensives were dumped: food staples were the worst pocket (CPB -6.96%, GIS
-3.25%, KHC -3.20%), and Staples/Materials/Energy were the only red sectors —
classic sell-the-safety when cuts are believed. But internals warn: advancers
beat decliners (SPX 337/161), yet NEW 20day LOWS OUTNUMBER NEW HIGHS on every
index (SPX 55/73, NDX 15/24, RUT 157/194) and T2108 is sub-50 on SPX/NDX/RUT.
A powerful but not-yet-confirmed bounce; Friday's jobs report is the decider.
