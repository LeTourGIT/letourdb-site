📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The XJO fell -1.04% (91.4 points) to 8,730.4, its sixth straight loss and a new 20day low. The XAO lost -1.07% to 8,836.6. Two blows landed: an overnight metals liquidation (copper -3.63% in futures, spot gold -3.3% overnight) set Materials up for another rout before the open, and then the ABS jobs report went off. Unemployment jumped to 4.9% against 4.7% expected, the economy shed 43,500 jobs, and the rate is now above the RBA's 4.75% NAIRU estimate. The market priced the cut it now expects (fully priced by September 2027) and still sold equities. That is the important tell: rate relief that comes from a growth scare is bearish for earnings before it is bullish for multiples.

Materials (XMJ -3.68%) did the damage again and is down -11.83% over 5days. The selling was correlated across commodities rather than about one: majors BHP -3.68%, RIO -4.06%, S32 -5.87% and FMG -2.45%; gold EVN -7.33%, EMR -8.17%, RMS -6.32%, NST -5.71% and KCN -7.75%; uranium PDN -8.16%. When copper, gold and uranium all liquidate together while the AUD slips, it is a USD and real-yield squeeze from a hawkish Fed (minutes flagged more hikes), not a China demand story. Iron ore at US$88.05 (-1.2%) adds a second, structural weight on FMG and the iron ore majors.

Underneath the headline was a clear defensive rotation. Comm. Services (XTJ +1.05%, TLS +1.55%), IT (XIJ +0.96%: NXT +4.30%, 360 +4.03%, MP1 +3.50%) and Healthcare (XHJ +0.83%: SHL +6.28% to a new 20day high, 4DX +5.12%) were the only green sectors. CBA (+0.44%) held up among the banks. Capital is hiding in earnings certainty, telco yield and AI infrastructure that tracks the Nasdaq; it is not buying the dip in cyclicals. Energy (XEJ -2.08%) followed Brent -1.7% and the uranium flush.

Breadth confirms the damage. On the XJO, decliners beat advancers 127 to 63 (0.50:1), 23 stocks fell 4% or more against 4 that rose that much, and new 20day lows (50) outnumbered highs (11) by almost 5:1. The broad market is worse: the XAO logged 136 new 20day lows and 75 stocks down 4% or more, with only 38.9% of stocks above their 40day MA (T2108). The XJO T2108 has slipped to 50.3%, the first test of the midpoint. Only 37.7% of XJO stocks are above their 20day MA, which is a short-term washout reading, but there is no capitulation volume signal yet to call a low.

Looking ahead: October CPI on 24/11 is the next hard catalyst before the RBA meets on 7-8 Dec. The cash rate is 4.85% after a 6-3 split hike on 3 Nov, and today's jobs data strengthens the case for a hold. US futures are firmer at report time (ES +0.30%, NQ +0.39%) and Anthropic trades from tonight. Any bounce needs to show up in breadth, not just in the index, before it is worth trusting. After the close, AGN lodged FDA feedback asking for an additional rat study (16:44), which is tomorrow's price action. ALQ reports on 23/11.
