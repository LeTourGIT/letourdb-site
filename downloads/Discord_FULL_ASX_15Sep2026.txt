📝  SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Tuesday 15 Sep dragged the ASX 200 down -0.88% to 8,672.5, a three-month low,
as a single macro force overwhelmed the tape: a Middle-East oil shock (Brent
~$107, WTI +10.5% over five sessions) feeding directly into inflation fear, with
US 10-year yields printing ~5% — the highest since 2007 — ahead of Wednesday's
Fed decision. This is a global-rates and energy story, not an ASX-domestic one.

Materials (XMJ -2.21%) was the worst sector, and the mechanism was specific.
US real yields at multi-decade highs cracked the gold price (gold -0.61%), and
the ASX gold-miner complex — the market's structural RS leadership — unwound hard:
RMS -4.76%, CYL -4.73%, OBM -5.26%, KCN -6.57%, SMI -8.04%, MEK -7.69%. This was a
leadership-group correction, the most damaging kind, not a rotation into new names.

The mirror image was defensive. Health Care (XHJ +1.50%) led every sector as
capital hid from duration and cyclicality: 4DX +8.72% and TLX +8.63% (both ASX200)
and HLS +7.06% drove it. Staples (+0.88%) and Discretionary (+0.87%, Life360 +5.02%)
filled out the risk-off leaderboard. Financials (XFJ -1.08%) led the index lower —
banks caught between a higher-for-longer NIM tailwind and rising bad-debt risk.

Energy (XEJ -1.62%) falling on a day oil surged is the session's sharpest tell:
this was broad de-risking and profit-taking after a huge multi-day crude run, not
a rejection of the energy thesis — Smart Money is still rotating INTO the oil complex
underneath. EQR +11.69% (US tungsten-plant JV) was the clean idiosyncratic winner.

Breadth confirms damage, not capitulation-and-turn: XJO Adv/Dec 78/116, only 35%
of XJO names above their 200MA, T2108 at 28%, and 52 new 20day lows against just 7
new highs. The RBA (29 Sep, cash rate 4.35%) now meets into a fresh imported-
inflation impulse — the local circuit-breaker is two weeks away.
