📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Monday 21 September was a study in a flat headline hiding a weak tape.
The XJO finished +0.01% at 8,731.9 after being down almost 0.6% early —
but decliners beat advancers 103 to 90, new 20-day lows (37) swamped new
highs (10), and T2108 sat at just 31%. Eight of eleven sectors closed red.

The rescue came from two defensive engines. Banks (XFJ +0.63%) rallied as
CBA and ANZ brought forward their RBA hike calls to 29 September, aligning
all Big Four on a move to 4.60% — a higher-for-longer path that lifts bank
margin expectations. Healthcare (XHJ +0.51%) provided the second leg, with
CSL back at 20-day highs and COH +5.27% as capital rotated toward quality
defensives and away from long-duration growth.

That growth cohort was hammered. Info Tech (XIJ -1.60%) slumped to a five-
month low, now down ~18% since 25 August — the purest expression of RBA
valuation anxiety. Materials (XMJ -0.66%) sold off as rare earths (LIN, BRE,
SRL), gold (RSG -8.49% on a soft Syama update) and Alcoa (AAI -5.79%) all
gave back recent gains; the ASX200 Resources index fell 0.69% and the All
Ords Gold index -0.88%.

The large-cap casualties were idiosyncratic. Perpetual (PPT -15.1%) rejected
EQT's further revised proposal, collapsing the takeover premium, while Telix
(TLX -11.7%) fell on an ITM radiopharma tie-up the market judged uncertain.
Against them, Ramelius (RMS +6.15%) rose on its four-year outlook and FY27
guidance, and IDP Education (IEL +17.4%) staged a violent short-covering
reversal off a fresh multi-year low with no lodged announcement.

The tape has not resolved: all four indices remain in Down trends, XJO is
below its 50MA (-2.79%) and 200MA (-1.02%), and only ~41% of XJO stocks sit
above their 200MA. The RBA decision on 28-29 September is the circuit breaker.
