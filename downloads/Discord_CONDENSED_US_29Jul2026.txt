📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
A hawkish-by-omission FOMC detonated the most crowded trade in the market. The Fed
(second statement under Warsh) held the funds rate steady, but with inflation still
sticky the tape read a hold as the Fed falling behind — Gundlach flagged the bond
market signalling exactly that. The Dow shed ~1,100 points for its worst session
since April 2025. Crucially this was NOT cap-weight distortion: SPX breadth was
179 advancers vs 321 decliners and the Russell was a rout at 558/1366. Everything
with a multiple and a capex story was sold.

The epicentre was semiconductors and AI-infrastructure. SOXX -5.38% and XSD -4.68%
led an eleven-deep wall of sub-3% ETF breaches. KLAC -10.80%, MU -9.94%, ENTG -9.44%,
MKSI -9.71% and SMCI -9.67% were gutted — Micron dragged lower after SK Hynix's
fumbled memory-cycle guidance, and VRT -17.26% plus the crypto-miner complex
(IREN -13.6%, APLD -12.8%, WULF -11.7%, CRWV -9.6%) confirmed the market is
repricing the entire AI power-and-datacentre buildout, not just chips. This is a
capex-cycle derating: when the discount rate stops falling, the longest-duration
cash flows in the index get marked down first.

The mirror image was a textbook value rotation — CNBC literally ran 'sell semis, buy
ketchup.' XLE +1.88% (oil) and XLP +0.34% (staples) were the only green sectors while
XLK -2.64% and XLI -3.19% anchored the board. The names that ROSE were beat-and-raise,
lower-beta compounders: MANH +21.3% (software execution), GRMN +16.2%, GEHC +12.2%,
CTSH +11.3%, plus a services/software cohort that had already de-rated (INTU, ADBE,
DDOG, WDAY, NOW, SNOW all +4-6%). Money didn't leave equities — it moved down the
beta and duration curve into cash-generative businesses that don't need a rate cut.

Geopolitics amplified the reflation fear rather than starting it. Iran attacked again
and Trump promised a 'beating,' but parallel Hormuz de-escalation calls with Saudi
Arabia and Oman kept it a two-way risk. WTI +2.36% to $84.66 and Brent +3.23% pushed
XOP +3.41% — the ONLY equity ETF above +3% today — while gold +2.53% did its
classic safe-haven job. Higher oil into a Fed-behind-the-curve narrative is the worst
macro cocktail for long-duration tech, which is precisely how the tape traded.
