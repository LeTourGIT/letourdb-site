📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Resources carried the ASX 200 to a third straight gain, +0.09% to 8,765.3, and they carried it almost alone. Materials (XMJ +1.57%) was the only sector to clear +1.5%, with the lift coming from gold producers — NST +3.97%, EVN +2.20%, CYL +6.57%, PNR +5.61% — and a supporting bid in the bulks (BHP +1.39%, RIO +0.79%, FMG +0.60%). The gold move is the nugget: COMEX gold slipped 0.25%, but AUD fell 0.24% to 0.7099 and the AUD gold proxy (GOLD.au) rose 0.65%. Local producers bank AUD prices against AUD costs, so a softer Aussie widens margins even on a flat metal — and gold equities are the market's cleanest hedge against a hike that compresses everything else.

Strip Materials out and the session was negative. Only three of eleven sectors finished green. Utilities (-2.21%) and Energy (-1.70%) led the losses: bond proxies were dumped with the 10yr near 5.25%, and oil fell a fifth session after Iran offered to reopen Hormuz within seven days if the US lifts its blockade. Both sectors were 60day leaders — this is profit-taking in the quarter's winners before the RBA, not a new downtrend. Financials (-0.56%) slipped as CBA and ANZ lost 0.84% and IAG fell 2.35% after the ACCC opposed its RAC Insurance acquisition (09:24, price sensitive).

Breadth did not confirm. XJO advancers beat decliners only 98 to 92 (1.07:1), and new 20day lows (23) outnumbered new highs (14) on an up day — a classic non-confirmation. Only 32% of XJO stocks sit above their 20MA, 36% above the 50MA and 38.5% above the 200MA; T2108 is 30%. Washed-out enough to support a bounce, not strong enough to call a bottom. The ASX 100 is the weak link (4 highs, 17 lows) while the Small Ords printed 20 highs against 9 lows.

Stock-level flow was driven by filings. TUA -23.39% after its FY26 Appendix 4E (08:26), SVL -14.29% on a $70M placement (09:23) and IEL -13.19% after rejecting Blackstone's upgraded $2.50 bid late Tuesday — the market is repricing the takeover floor away. On the other side, A1M +15.72% on a transformational copper acquisition (09:53) and BM1 +6.21% on a 56% resource upgrade at Mt Ida (08:17). CXO +12.00% had no filing: lithium sector flow after Albemarle's +3.4% overnight, ahead of FY results due Thursday.

The forward calendar is dense. Thursday brings August labour force data — the final hard input before the RBA's 29/09 decision, which futures price at ~90% for +25bp. The September flash PMI already softened (composite 50.8 from 52.7; manufacturing back in contraction at 49.3; first job losses since May). SOL, PMV, CXO and PNR report 24/09. After the close, OFX answered an ASX price query (16:29) following a 15:10 pause, and NMG lodged its annual resource and reserve statement (16:51) — both are tomorrow's catalysts. The level to hold is 8,700; the level to reclaim is 8,800 (the 10 September breakdown).
