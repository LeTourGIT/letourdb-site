📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Two shocks pulled in opposite directions and the US tape chose AI. Anthropic's
S-1 (Nasdaq: ANT) disclosed hyper-growth — revenue $4.6bn in 2025 and $16.3bn
in H1 2026 alone — alongside steep losses, and that was enough to re-ignite an
AI trade that had been bleeding all month. The SOX gained ~2.8% on the day but
remains ~12% lower for October. Simultaneously, Brazil delivered an emerging-
market crash: Lula's re-election sent the Ibovespa -13.88% with a circuit-
breaker halt and the real -8.8%. The US absorbed it cleanly — the damage stayed
inside Brazil-linked ADRs (XP -48.36%, NU -16.95%, PBR -13.99%).

The leadership composition tells you what kind of rally this was. The biggest
tech gains came from the most beaten-down AI hardware names: LITE +8.19%,
COHR +5.83%, TTMI +6.73%, AAOI +6.56%, SNPS +5.62% — many with negative 5day
returns. SOXX (+2.85%) and XSD (+2.07%) carry 20day returns of -12.3% and
-12.2%. That is short-covering into a catalyst, not fresh accumulation. The
RS Top 20 contains ZERO semiconductors. Where the RS data shows genuine
accumulation is software/cybersecurity (CIBR 5day +8.52%, IGV 5day +5.09%,
RPD +20.25%, NTSK and ZS at new 20day highs) and biotech.

The underappreciated headline is Meta: -1.9% after flagging up to $95bn of
new equity to fund AI data centres. Put it next to the Anthropic IPO, SpaceX's
AI-infrastructure plans and Microsoft's 60,000-employee buyout offer, and the
pattern is clear — AI is moving from free-cash-flow funding to capital-markets
funding. That is bullish for suppliers (Celestica's $40bn 2027 guide confirms
the order book) but it is also a late-cycle supply signal. When the
hyperscalers start issuing equity, the market has to absorb more AI paper at
the same time the Fed is debating a hike.

Breadth confirmed the rally at the large-cap level: SPX advancers 289 vs
decliners 208 (1.39:1), a sharp reversal from Friday's 135/364. T2108 rose to
66.8% from 63.2% and 71.4% of SPX members sit above their 20day MA. But the
Russell tells the other story — 966/954 is a coin-flip, only 38.2% of RUT
names are above their 50MA, and 665 RUT stocks are down 25%+ for the quarter
vs 318 up. Large caps are recovering; small caps are not.

Geopolitics remains the oil floor: Iran "deal or finish it off" rhetoric
and Houthi strikes on Saudi targets kept Brent at $109.82. Defence caught the
bid — PPA +2.65%, ITA +2.44%, LMT +5.13% on a Morgan Stanley upgrade.

The week ahead is the most event-dense of the quarter: FOMC Wednesday, GDP and
inflation Thursday, MSFT/GOOGL/AMZN earnings, BoJ (expected hawkish hold),
then US midterms on 03/11. Monday bought the bounce. It did not repair a
single regime.
