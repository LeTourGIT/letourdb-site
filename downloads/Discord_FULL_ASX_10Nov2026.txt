📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The ASX 200 rose 1.10% to 9,129.0, its highest close since August, as Wall Street's violent Monday rebound (Dow +1,100 points, S&P 500 +2.35%, best day since April) set a risk-on tone that held all session. This was not a narrow index lift: 173 XJO members advanced against 22 decliners (7.9:1), new 20day highs jumped from 26 to 63, and T2108 climbed to 74%. A move of this breadth after last week's US-led swoon is a classic breadth-thrust signature — buyers were not picking stocks, they were buying the tape.

Leadership was domestic-cyclical rather than resources-led. Industrials (XNJ +2.31%) and Real Estate (XRE +2.23%) topped the board: Qantas +6.34% on its 850-job restructure, Transurban +1.64%, Goodman +3.41%. Materials (XMJ +1.48%) sat just below the 1.5% resources-led threshold but owns the best 5day move of any sector (+7.87%), driven by critical minerals and gold rather than iron ore — Lindian +17.11% on a binding monazite offtake, IperionX +14.88%, Ora Banda +7.19%, while BHP managed only +1.13% with ore flat at US$92.71/t.

Company news decided the extremes. Monadelphous +15.30% on its pre-open business update (08:19, price sensitive) — the AGM-season guidance pattern repeating. On the other side, Life360 -15.32% after Q3 results (08:24) marks a third consecutive post-result sell-off for the stock, and Superloop -10.18% on its first formal FY27 guidance (08:16), with Aussie Broadband -2.39% alongside. The lesson for the tape: in a 7.9:1 up day, stocks that fell double digits on their own numbers are telling you exactly where expectations were too rich.

The regime picture strengthened again. XJO, XAO and XTO all print Bullish (MA10 > MA20 > MA50), and XAO only upgraded from Neutral Bull on 06/11. The small caps were the day's best index (XSO +2.29%, 15day ROC 4.91) and reclaimed their 50MA, yet XSO still prints Neutral Bear and sits 4.03% below its 200MA — an XJO/XSO regime divergence that is narrowing from below. One caution flag: Market Volatility Level flipped from Normal to Caution on all four indices today, with XJO/XTO RSI-15 at 68 and pushing toward overbought.

Macro context: the RBA lifted the cash rate to 4.85% on 03/11 (6-3 vote) and does not meet again until 08/12, so rate risk is imported tonight via US October CPI (~00:30 AEDT). US futures are mildly positive at report time (ES +0.12%, NQ +0.24%). No price-sensitive announcements were lodged after the close; five trading halts remain unresolved (RDN, WRX, RBR, PXX, DTZ), and formal results from ALL, XRO, ORI and GNC land Thursday 12/11.
