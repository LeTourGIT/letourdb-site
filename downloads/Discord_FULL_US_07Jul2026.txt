📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Two independent catalysts collided Tuesday to produce a textbook rotation day —
growth-and-momentum out, value-defensives-and-energy in. The headline losses on
SPX (-0.45%) and NDX (-1.77%) mask a market that was, beneath the mega-cap tech
layer, remarkably resilient.

Catalyst one: the semiconductor complex cracked. Samsung reported an 18-fold jump
in Q2 operating profit (₩89.4tn, ~$58B — larger than a recent Nvidia quarter), yet
the stock fell ~7%. That is a pure sell-the-news signal on a sector priced for
perfection. It compounded with a Reuters report that China's DeepSeek is building
its own AI inference chip (an Nvidia-dependency threat) and pre-positioning ahead
of SK Hynix's Nasdaq listing this Friday. The result: XSD -6.34%, SOXX -5.13% —
both EXTREME (-5%+) moves. INTC -9.66%, ALAB -11.52%, TER -9.59%, MRVL -7.45%,
WDC -7.86%, SNDK -7.26%. Memory, equipment, and fabless design all sold together.

Catalyst two: geopolitics reversed the energy trade. Iran struck three tankers in
the Strait of Hormuz (Saudi Wedyan, Qatari LNG carrier Al-Rekayat, and a third),
and the US Treasury revoked General License X — the waiver authorising Iranian oil
sales negotiated in the interim MOU, with a wind-down to July 17. This is the
mirror image of the early-May 'peace proposal' repricing: WTI +4.77% to $72.22,
Brent +4.85%. Energy was the day's best sector (XLE +2.84%): OXY +5.88%, DVN
+5.08%, COP +4.69%, XOM +3.85%, LNG +3.62%. The diplomatic understanding that
capped oil is now on shaky ground — with negotiators still 'working in good faith.'

The rotation was clean and directional. Capital fled semis/AI-infrastructure and
crypto miners (WULF -8.87%, IREN -9.33%) into three defensive havens — healthcare
(GILD +5.21%, REGN +4.07%, IHE +3.16%), real estate (XLRE +1.35%), utilities
(XLU +0.88%) — plus energy. Rising yields (30-yr above 5%, 10-yr ~4.51%) under a
hawkish Warsh Fed lifted the discount rate on long-duration growth exactly as the
semi valuation air-pocket opened. Note software SEPARATED from semis today: NET
+8.60%, FIG +5.27%, WDAY +4.10%, ACN +3.78% — the application layer is being
re-rated as an AI beneficiary, not a casualty, a critical intra-tech divergence.

SpaceX joined the Nasdaq-100 today and fell (~16% Monday, extending Tuesday) —
forced index buying met live litigation overhang. RIVN -18.12% was the single
worst large-cap, dragging autos (CARZ -4.53%). The breadth picture is the key
tell: SPX advancers led decliners 283 to 216 on a DOWN day — a reverse cap-weight
distortion where heavyweight semis pulled the index red while the median stock rose.
