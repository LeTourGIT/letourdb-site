📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The ASX 200 rose 155.8 points (+1.82%) to 8,726.4, snapping an eight-session
losing streak that had taken 6.4% off the index. The trigger was offshore:
Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO 11 days after his resignation,
and Wall Street posted its best day since April 2025. Ten of 11 sectors closed
higher; IT (XIJ +2.97%) led, Energy (XEJ -0.18%) was the only decliner.

Resources did real work (XMJ +1.72%), but the composition matters. Gold miners
led — RSG +4.39%, WAF +3.59%, BGL +3.37%, NST +2.99% — with FFM +7.23% on a
price-sensitive discovery release (08:27). The iron ore majors lagged: BHP
+1.35%, RIO +0.69%, FMG +1.94%. With iron ore ~US$90.67 near four-year lows,
the majors are a China-demand story the AI bounce does not fix. XMJ is still
-3.44% on 5day and -6.75% on 20day.

Breadth was the standout: 180 advancers vs 16 decliners (11.3:1) in the ASX 200,
395/51 in the All Ords, and new 20day lows collapsed from 73 to 10. That is a
breadth-thrust day. But the trend gauges barely moved: only 32% of ASX 200
stocks are above their 20MA, 39.5% above the 200MA, and T2108 is 47%. A
one-day thrust inside damaged structure needs a second day to matter.

Leadership was the most-beaten names, which is typical of relief rallies. IT
(5day -4.07%), Discretionary and REITs led; the Big 4 rose 1.7-1.9% to end a
five-session financials slide. Stock-specific catalysts carried the biggest
moves: SHV +17.07% on FY26 results, RWC +11.92% after major shareholders backed
the scheme, LOV +10.58% on its AGM trading update. TUA +32.35% had no ASX
announcement in the feed — treat the cause as unconfirmed.

Forward look: US futures are flat, so Wednesday opens without a fresh lead.
RYM and WEB report 25/11, FPH and TWR 26/11. PTX is in a trading halt. No
announcements were lodged after the close. The test is whether XJO can hold
8,700 and reclaim the 8,812-8,834 MA band before the RBA on 07-08/12.
