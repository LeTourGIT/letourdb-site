📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The ASX 200 added +0.76% to 8,783.2 — its second straight gain and
within reach of a one-month high — but the rally was built almost
entirely on index heavyweights. Financials (XFJ +1.56%) and
Materials (XMJ +1.52%) did the lifting: WBC +2.70%, NAB +2.26%,
MQG +2.05%, ANZ +1.96%, alongside BHP +2.45%, FMG +2.87%, RIO
+1.60% and BSL +3.51%. Strip those out and the market was red.

Resources: iron ore firmed ~0.7% to ~US$91.20/t on expectations of
Chinese mill restocking — enough to lift the majors, but this is
positioning, not a price breakout (iron ore -7.49% over 20days in
the 7d feed). Gold miners rode Friday's +1.5% gold futures move
(NST +2.50%). The other side of Materials was ugly: lithium and
rare earths were sold hard with no company news — PLS -3.71%, LYC
-3.14%, ELV -4.80%, GLN -9.26%, IXR -13.39%, BRE -4.89%. Even
ARU fell -3.03% on a binding offtake. That is de-risking of the
critical-minerals trade, not a fundamental event.

The banks' bid is the "higher-for-longer" trade: with the cash
rate at 4.60% and two of the Big 4 economists calling another hike
on 03/11, NIM support is outweighing credit fears for now. BOQ
(+2%) reports Thursday 15/10 — the first hard read on margins in
this rate cycle. US big-bank earnings Tuesday add a cross-check.

Communication Services (XTJ -1.94%) was the worst sector as the
global telco sell-off reached Australia: SpaceX's ~US$8bn purchase
of an 800MHz spectrum portfolio puts Starlink in direct competition
with mobile carriers. TLS -2.52%, TPG -4.90%. Real Estate (XRE
-1.38%) and Staples (XSJ -0.56%) bled as money rotated to cyclicals.

Breadth is the warning: XJO advancers/decliners 98/95 (near 1:1)
and XAO 189/255 — more stocks fell than rose on a +0.67% All Ords
day. New 20day lows (25) still edge highs (24) in the ASX 200 and
T2108 is just 32%. Watch tomorrow for after-close MSB (annual
report) and the resolution of seven trading halts.
