📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Monday 13/07/2026 was a flat-to-fractional session in name — XJO +0.03%
to 8,808.5, XAO -0.01% — but the internals were decisively negative. XJO
advancers trailed decliners 74/118 (0.63:1), and the All Ords
was worse at 156/292. This was an
index held aloft by a few large-caps, not a broad advance.

Energy was the story under the surface. With US-Iran strikes rocking the
Strait of Hormuz, the oil complex stayed bid and ASX energy names ran:
Ampol (ALD) +4.17%, Viva (VEA) +3.56%, Stanmore coal (SMR) +4.68%. The
Smart Money rotation table confirms it — Oil & Gas Refining vaulted to #1
(from #52 nine days ago) and Integrated + E&P oil both sit top-8. Energy
was also the best 5day sector at +4.10%. This is a genuine, multi-day
institutional rotation, not a one-session spike.

The offset was a gold-miner rout. Spot gold fell (-1.29% on the delayed
feed) and the ASX gold complex was dumped: Kingsgate (KCN) -12.83% led the
whole market lower, with St Barbara (SBM) -5.32%, Forrestania (FRS) -5.0%
and Viridis (VMM) -7.42% following. Gold cuts both ways though — Dateline
(DTR) +33.33% and Genesis (GMD) +3.70% bucked it on stock-specific catalysts.
Materials (XMJ -0.65%) wore the damage; iron ore itself was quiet at
$98.72 (+0.15%, delayed).

Technology (XIJ -2.49%) was the worst sector, dragged by a semiconductor
unwind — Weebit (WBT) -10.6%, Silex (SLX) -7.89% — mirroring the US chip
profit-taking even as the broad US indices closed green. Utilities
(-1.63%) and Staples (-0.91%) fell as rate-sensitive and defensive names
were sold. Financials (+0.67%, best 5day +3.0%) firmed ahead of US bank
earnings week. AUD slipped to 0.6935 (-0.28%), a mild tailwind for USD miners.
