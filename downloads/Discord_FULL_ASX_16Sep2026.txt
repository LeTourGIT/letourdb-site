Wednesday 16 September delivered a marginal +0.28% on the XJO to 8,696.5 — an oversold

Advancers beat decliners 110/76, but that sits against structural damage: only ~39% of

Leadership tells the real story. Energy (XEJ +2.19%) topped the board on the Hormuz oil
premium — Brent back near $109, WTI ~$104 — and Materials (XMJ +1.28%) rode a gold-miner
re-rating: PNR +9.30% on its Racetrack strike extension, SBM +8.18%, ALK +4.92%, BGL
+4.29%. Bullion near records plus elevated crude is a textbook late-cycle, war-premium bid,
and it is echoed in Smart Money: Electric Utilities at rank 1 and an Integrated Oil & Gas surge to rank 2.

sector on EDV -3.85%; Real Estate (XRE -0.57%) lagged with the 10-year UST at a 19-year high;
Financials (XFJ -0.37%) slipped as SUN -3.23% wore fresh debt-collector allegations. JHX
-5.23% [EARNINGS-style] was the large-cap casualty — its 2026 Investor Day underwhelmed on
the US-housing outlook. Life360 -5.46% and Telix -4.96% show growth/healthcare staying heavy.

Corporate action was live: RWC signed a Scheme Implementation Deed with Brookfield (a takeover),
and Anthropic's Queensland data-centre deal is a concrete read-through for the ASX AI-infrastructure
thesis (NXT). But everything is provisional until the Fed: with a hike near-certain overnight and
the RBA carrying its own hike bias into 29 September, the path of least resistance stays defensive.
