📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Bonds set the tape: the 10-year yield hit 5.36% intraday (highest since
April 2002) and Fed minutes showed a unanimous September hike with another
expected by year-end. A strong $39B 10-year auction limited the S&P 500 to
-0.22% after a -0.71% low.

⚠ BREADTH DIVERGENCE: SPX Adv/Dec was 136/361 one day after a record.
Equal-weight RSP fell -0.81%; megacaps (AAPL +0.91%, AMZN +1.42%, a $1.2T
Micron +4.06%) supplied the 59bp cushion. Self-funded megacaps are the
defensive asset when the risk-free rate is 5.3% — everything that borrows to
grow was sold: RUT -1.31%, CAT -5.75%, housing, REITs, regional banks and
debt-funded AI capacity.

Rotation went to large-cap pharma (XLV +1.03%, the only green sector), power
(NRG, VST) and storage hardware (Everpure, NetApp). Hard assets were
liquidated with the dollar at its highest since April 2025: gold, silver,
copper and uranium miners all fell 3% or more.

Next: Thursday's $22B 30-year auction tests whether the long end has found a
buyer; September CPI follows on 14/10. Futures price about 20% for a hike on
28/10 and about 70% by 09/12. VIX at 15.08 is pricing none of the breadth
damage.
