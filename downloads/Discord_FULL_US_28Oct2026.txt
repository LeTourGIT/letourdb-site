📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Wednesday was an FOMC day that the equity market treated as a sideshow.
The Fed held at 3.75–4.00% by a 10–2 vote, with Governor Lisa Cook and
Cleveland's Beth Hammack both dissenting for an immediate hike. The statement
upgraded growth to "expanding at a strong pace", and Warsh again refused to
offer forward guidance. That is a hawkish hold, with December live. The
indices closed green anyway — SPX +0.27% to 7,743.98, NDX +0.55% — and VIX
fell 3.48% to 16.91. The real reaction was in duration: XLU -0.84% and XLRE
-0.73% were the worst two sectors, homebuilders ITB -1.51%. Bond-sensitive
equity paid for the hawkish dissents; growth equity did not.

The dominant story was the AI capex chain paying out to the physical layer.
Vertiv +16.48% after revenue +61%, organic orders more than doubling and a
$650M guide raise. FLEX +11.61%, CLS +8.41%, SNDK +7.66%, MU +5.86%, ENTG
+6.14%, AAOI +6.85%, LITE +5.52%, KLAC +3.33% on earnings, and Oracle +10.73%
on investor-day targets. SOXX +2.13%. Every dollar of hyperscaler capex is
someone's revenue line, and Wednesday the market paid the recipients.

The mirror image was software, and especially security. F5 -20.43% on
guidance and a downgrade, FTNT -5.90% in session then -25.71% AH on a
beat-and-raise, NOW -3.00% then -18.10% AH on a 2-cent miss. CIBR -1.58%.
The market is drawing a hard line between AI suppliers (paid) and
seat-based and security software (de-rated on any blemish). Crowded
software winners are being sold on good news. That is distribution.

After the bell the AI ledger split cleanly in two. Alphabet posted Cloud
+103% to $30.7B, but capex hit a record $51.4B, free cash flow was -$9.1B
for a second straight quarter, and 2027 capex points to ~$300B — the stock
fell ~10% AH. Microsoft printed Azure +48% with capex BELOW Street, and rose
2.88% AH. The market is now pricing return on AI capital, not the size of
it. Yet GOOGL's $300B 2027 number is the most bullish line of the night for
VRT, MU and the optical chain.

Then M&A: Bloomberg reported Marvell in advanced talks to buy Lumentum
(~$83B). LITE +8.65% AH, MRVL -8.40% AH. With Lumentum's AI optical
capacity reportedly sold out into 2029, this is scarcity M&A — designers
buying supply. Smart Money front-ran it: Communications & Networking
jumped from rank 57 to rank 15 in one session (rank 113 nine days ago).

Breadth was weaker than the tape: SPX 246 advancers vs 254 decliners on a
+0.27% close, and new 20day lows more than doubled (17 → 37) while new
highs rose (65 → 80). Both tails expanding = earnings-driven dispersion,
not a broad bid. SPX regime upgraded to Neutral Bull; NDX still Neutral
Bear and sitting on its 50MA into GOOGL's AH gap.
