📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The dominant macro driver was geopolitical de-escalation. After weeks of Iran/
Hormuz escalation that had spiked oil and compressed risk appetite, the tape
shifted decisively from "peak fear" to "pricing in": Trump stepped back from
maximal rhetoric and OPEC+ stepped up supply, collapsing the war premium. Brent
fell -7.35% and the USO oil ETF -8.66% — one of the largest single-session
energy repricings of the year. Treasury yields fell in sympathy as the inflation-
via-oil channel unwound. The result was a broad, four-index relief rally: SPX
+1.48%, NDX +1.78%, DJI +1.32%, RUT +1.73%, with VIX down to 15.86 and its 5day
reading off -15.1% as hedges were unwound.

Critically, this was NOT a narrow, cap-weighted melt-up. Breadth confirmed the
move at every level: SPX advancers led decliners 337/163 (better than 2:1), the
Russell an emphatic 1,526/404, and 21 SPX names closed +4% against just 2 down
4%. This is the mirror image of the narrow-breadth ATH prints seen earlier in
the cycle — participation is real. But breadth confirming a bounce and regime
confirming a trend are two different things, and only the former is present.

The leadership tells the rotation story. The rally was powered by the AI-
datacenter/compute-power complex and aerospace-defense, not by defensives.
CoreWeave (CRWV) +19.49% led the tape on AI-cloud demand; Oracle +9.22%,
Vertiv (VRT) +8.89%, Lumentum (LITE) +9.24%, Coherent (COHR) +9.60% and Applied
Optoelectronics (AAOI) +16.85% confirmed that the "power and optics for AI"
sub-theme is where institutional money is concentrating. Alongside it, a defense/
space cluster fired: Axon +9.12%, Rocket Lab +8.44%, Boeing +8.03%, AST
SpaceMobile +7.70%. First Solar +10.28% and the uranium/solar ETFs (URA +4.45%,
URNM +4.43%, TAN +3.87%) round out a coherent "energy for AI" narrative — as the
oil trade dies, capital is rotating toward the power sources that feed compute.

Beneath the surface, the Smart Money rotation is undergoing a regime change of
its own. Fintech is the new #1 industry (10day ROC +36.35%, climbing from rank
#88 nine sessions ago), and Financial & Commodity Market Operators sit #3 — the
financials complex is where capital is building. Construction & Engineering
SURGED to #4 (from #130 three days ago), Heavy Machinery to #6, and Airlines to
#13 (from #137) on the de-escalation reopening trade. The single most important
structural fact: there is ZERO energy in the Smart Money top 16 — a complete
reversal from the five-energy-sub-theme dominance of earlier sessions. The oil
crash didn't just move a sector; it evacuated an entire capital-flow regime.

After hours, Palantir delivered the megacap headline: +11.85% AH on a blowout
quarter with US commercial revenue up nearly 150%. Snap +10.52% AH on an earnings
beat and strong guide, ON Semiconductor +3.82% AH, Advanced Energy +9.62% AH and
Ultra Clean +6.20% AH extended the semiconductor-capital-equipment recovery into
the evening. The one megacap blemish was Marriott -6.97% on a soft print — a
consumer-services warning worth watching against the airline strength.
