📰 KEY HEADLINES

💵 Wage growth picks up but real wages continue to go backwards
(ABC Business)
   Today's wage print lands two weeks after the RBA's fifth hike of
   2026 took the cash rate to 4.85%. Faster nominal wages with
   falling real wages is the worst mix for the RBA: it feeds services
   inflation without lifting spending. It keeps a December hike live
   and explains why REITs (XRE -0.61%) and discretionary (XDJ -1.05%)
   keep leaking.

🏢 Nvidia beats; chair says the board will not bring back Huang
(ABC Business · ABC World · MarketWatch · CNBC)
   The most-repeated story in the feed. Nvidia beat on revenue
   (US$115.8bn vs US$109.1bn est.), guided Q4 near US$130bn and added
   a US$150bn buyback, yet ES/NQ futures sat flat (-0.03% / +0.06%)
   at the ASX close. A blowout beat that cannot move futures says the
   AI discount is now about governance and valuation, not demand.
   That is why ASX tech (XIJ -2.31%) got no relief.

💎 Trump praises Quad as he unveils defence and minerals deals (ABC
Business · ABC World)
   This ran in both ABC feeds and is the cleanest explanation for
   rare earths outperforming a materials sector down 10% in five
   days: LYC +2.83% and BRE +4.12% (BRE also had a Pele discovery at
   08:48). Critical minerals are trading on policy and supply
   security, separate from the China-demand complex.

📅 Three things to watch as Trump, Xi and Putin meet in China (CNBC
· ABC World)

   meeting. For the ASX the read-through is iron ore and copper: iron
   ore sits at US$90.62 (-2.07% over 5day) after weak Chinese October
   activity data. Any stimulus or tariff signal from the summit is
   the swing factor for BHP/RIO/FMG, which are down 8-13% over 5day.
