📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Q3's final session was a bond-market story in an equity costume. A cooler-
than-expected core PCE lifted SPX roughly half a percent by mid-morning,
pricing out some October hike risk after September's Fed move. It did not
hold: the 10-year finished near 5.30% and the 30-year near 5.64% — highest
since 2002 — and a last-hour quarter-end reversal erased the gains. SPX
-0.25%, Dow -0.86%, RUT -0.39%; only NDX held green (+0.23%).

The regime split is now explicit: SPX and NDX Bullish, RUT and DJI Bearish
with 5day and 20day trends Down. SPX sits just +0.05% above its 50MA while
only 21.0% of its members are above theirs and T2108 is 19.0% — washout
territory. VIX 16.34 is pricing the megacap tape, not the constituent
damage underneath.

The mechanism is duration. Staples XLP -1.53% was the worst sector, then
Healthcare, Industrials, Financials and Real Estate; REM -2.57% was the
weakest ETF. Tech XLK +0.64% was the only green sector, and the bid was
software (IGV +1.20%), not chips (SOXX +0.21%). Twelve of the top fifteen
$500M-turnover gainers were Technology-sector names.

The AI-power complex took the rate shock hardest: BE -4.90% on $4.1B, CEG
-3.99%, VRT -2.83%, GLW -3.12%, and Heavy Electrical Equipment fell from
Smart Money rank 1 nine days ago to rank 83. Capex-financed models priced
on cheap long-dated capital break when the 30-year is 5.6%. JBL -10.03% is
the same lesson: 'beat but not enough' is punished like a miss.

Yet the AI demand layer strengthened. Micron beat the top of its own guide
and guided FQ1 to $61.5B, with $12.9B of customer prepayments on the
balance sheet. HPE booked the first AMD Helios order ($1.2B) and Google
launched Gemini 4. The market is not repricing AI demand — it is repricing
the cost of capital that finances it.

October setup: breadth at washout (T2108 19%, RUT 482 new lows vs 96
highs, RUT RSI-15 30.59) versus intact cap-weighted indices. It resolves
as a yield-reversal breadth thrust or a megacap catch-down through the SPX
50MA. Watch the 30-year: holding below 5.6% favours the thrust.
