📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday was a textbook leadership rotation. The headline indices masked everything:
SPX closed +0.05% (essentially flat), the Dow rose +0.46% to a record-area 51,947,
yet the Nasdaq 100 fell -1.15% and slipped into an outright BEARISH regime with
Elevated volatility. The single force behind the split was the semiconductor complex:
Intel's -7.89% post-earnings collapse anchored a broad chip wreck that took SOXX
-4.40% and XSD -5.55% — the ONLY two ETFs on the board below -3%, and XSD's -5.55%
trips the EXTREME MOVE threshold.

But underneath the mega-cap drag, the tape was healthy. SPX advancers led decliners
360 to 140 — a decisively POSITIVE 2.6:1 ratio on a flat index. This is the exact
inverse of a distribution day: normally you fear a green index on negative breadth
(cap-weight masking). Here we have a FLAT index on strongly positive breadth, which
means the median stock had a good day and only the largest AI-hardware names dragged
the cap-weighted print. T2108 at 61.4% and 55.2% of SPX names above their 20-day MA
confirm broad participation. Small caps (-0.35%) lagged only modestly.

The rotation had a clear destination. The 'sell chips, buy software' trade — a
recurring 2026 theme — reappeared in force: IGV software +1.01% against SOXX -4.40%
is a ~5.4-point one-day spread. ServiceNow +7.44%, Adobe +6.10%, VeriSign +7.04% and
IBM +3.65% led; the logic is that software monetises AI as a margin expander with a
fraction of the capex intensity, precisely as the bond market starts questioning
hyperscaler capex payback. Alphabet's earnings — another AI capex guide RAISE —
crystallised that anxiety: '1 hyperscaler down, 3 to go,' with MSFT/META/AMZN and the
Fed all landing next week.

Capital that left semis didn't leave the market — it rotated into value, cyclicals
and defensives. Real Estate (XLRE +2.22%) and Materials (XLB +1.93%) led the SPDRs;
Staples (+1.11%) and Health Care (+0.70%) were green; Technology (XLK -1.44%) was the
only materially red sector. The telco trio TMUS +5.67% / VZ +5.84% / T +5.10% is the
defensive-rotation signature. Digital Realty +11.01% is the nuance: AI-infrastructure
demand remains intact, but it is now being expressed through the power-and-real-estate
layer rather than the silicon layer — the thesis changed vehicles, it did not break.

Energy is the macro backdrop. WTI slipped -1.61% to $90.47 and Brent -2.32% to $98.38
on the session, but the 5-day tape tells the real story: spot WTI is up +9.96% over
five days as Middle East risk (Trump weighing a 'massive attack' on Iran; Pakistan
reportedly seeking to restart peace talks) keeps a war premium embedded. That premium
is powering the Smart Money board, where energy owns four of the top 16 industries.
