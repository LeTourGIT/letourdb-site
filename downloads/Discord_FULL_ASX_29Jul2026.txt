📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Wednesday 29 July was a CPI-driven relief rally. The June print landed at
3.8% headline (down from 4.0%), and — critically — trimmed mean
3.6% came in UNDER both the 3.7% consensus and the RBA's own 3.8%
May projection. The market read is clean: an August hike is off the table, the
cash rate stays 4.35% into the 10-11 August 2026 meeting, and the whole rate-
sensitive complex re-rated. XJO closed +1.01% at 9,038.6, a fresh push higher.

Healthcare (XHJ +4.24%) led every sector, powered by CSL +7.15% on its enhanced
plasma manufacturing update — the first hard operational catalyst for the
blue-chip in months, and enough to drag HLS +7.14% and EBO +5.06% with it.
But the more revealing leadership was consumer-discretionary: TPW +8.36%,
LOV +7.88%, APE +5.27%, DMP +5.28%, NCK +4.81% and REIT SGP +4.96% — a
textbook lower-for-longer-rates basket. The Smart Money table confirms it:
Homebuilding is entrenched at #1 and Insurance Funds, Auto Parts, Leisure,
Home Furnishings and Appliances all SURGED double-digit ranks this week.

RIO +3.67% on its 2026 half-year results gave Materials (XMJ +1.12%)
institutional ballast, though iron ore near US$99/t on softening Chinese steel
demand caps the upside. The clear losers were the AI/semiconductor complex:
MP1 -8.40% and WBT -8.33% (Weebit's ReRAM) are the local expression of the
global chip washout that took the Kospi down 10.8% overnight. LTR -7.92% on
an underwhelming June quarterly, and DVP -17.56% (which drew an ASX price-and-
volume query post-close) were the standout single-name blow-ups.

Structure check: this is a large-cap rally. XJO/XAO/XTO are all Bullish with
XTO overbought at RSI 70, yet XSO Small Ords remains Bearish, -7.95% below its
200MA. Only 45% of XJO names sit above their 200MA and quarterly -25% movers
(32) still outnumber +25% movers (27). The dovish CPI is a real tailwind, but
breadth beneath the mega-caps has not yet confirmed.
