Thursday was a three-act geopolitical whipsaw that resolved violently
risk-on. Act one: overnight reports of continued Washington–Tehran peace

market has moved from pricing escalation to pricing resolution in one tape.

oil → CPI → Fed Chair Warsh's first test — just had its first link snapped.

broke. Treasury yields tumbled in response. If the Iran signing lands, the
"Warsh trapped by war inflation" thesis dies with it — that is the macro
repricing underneath today's index moves.
