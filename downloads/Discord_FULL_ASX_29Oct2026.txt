📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The ASX 200 added 44.5 points (+0.50%) to 8,891.8 -- its highest close since 09/09/2026 -- and it did so on the day after an inflation shock. That is the tell. With a fifth RBA hike now ~85-87% priced for Tuesday, the market did not sell equities; it rotated inside them. Financials (XFJ +1.86%) and Real Estate (XRE +1.84%) led while Consumer Discretionary (XDJ -1.98%) and Staples (XSJ -1.77%) were the two worst sectors. That is the textbook hike trade: own margin beneficiaries, sell the household wallet.

The banks did the heavy lifting. CBA +2.1%, NAB +2.3%, ANZ +2.2% and WBC +1.9% -- all four printed new 20day highs, and with Westpac reporting 02/11 and NAB 05/11, this is pre-results positioning layered on top of a NIM tailwind. ASX Ltd (+3.0%) and Goodman (+2.3%) added breadth to the large-cap bid. The other side of the ledger was Wesfarmers (-4.0%), JB Hi-Fi (-2.9%, FY27 sales update 08:10) and Super Retail (-2.8%, trading update 08:07) -- three consumer bellwethers falling on the morning households were told rates are going higher again.

Resources were a drag rather than a driver. Materials (XMJ -0.65%) was hit by a lithium liquidation -- PLS -8.0%, ELV -9.4%, LTR -5.0% after its September quarterly, CXO -4.3% -- and Champion Iron's -17.3% on its Q2 FY27 result. Iron ore sat flat at US$90.70, down 6% in a month, so the big miners (BHP -0.9%, RIO -0.6%) had nothing to lean on. Lynas (+4.2%) was the exception: its quarterly kept the rare-earth strategic premium intact.

Breadth confirms the index but not the market. XJO advancers beat decliners 124/71 (1.75:1) and 20day highs (27) outnumbered lows (8), yet only 38.5% of ASX 200 stocks sit above their 200day MA. Further down, the XAO ratio was a thin 1.21:1 and the Small Ords remains in a Bearish regime with 31% of members above their 200day MA. Capital is concentrating in liquid large caps ahead of a binary RBA event -- classic defensive positioning dressed up as a rally.

Forward look: the RBA decides Tuesday 03/11; a hike is consensus, so the market-moving variable is the statement and the new SMP forecasts -- any hint of a sixth hike would hit XDJ/XRE again. Before that, US GDP and PCE tonight, Amazon after the US close, and WBC results Monday. Post-close, Deterra (16:55) lodged its September-quarter royalty update -- a read on BHP's Mining Area C volumes for tomorrow.
