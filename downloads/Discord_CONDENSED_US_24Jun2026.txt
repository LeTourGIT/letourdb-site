📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Wednesday was a regime-repricing day driven top-down by geopolitics. The US Energy
Secretary declared Washington has ended Iran's ability to close the Strait of Hormuz;
Trump said Iran assured no transit tolls, and tankers moved through as US crude briefly
dipped below $70. The 2026 war premium was priced out in one session: WTI -3.6% to $69.81
and Brent -4.2% — both back to pre-conflict levels.

That collapse rippled into rates: the 10-year yield fell below 4.5% as the oil-driven
inflation impulse faded. Lower yields ignited the rate-sensitive complex — homebuilders
exploded (ITB +6.15%, XHB +5.62%, PHM +7.24%, DHI +6.68%) on KB Home's +16.65% beat,
while airlines (JETS +4.17%, AAL +8.05%, UAL +7.40%) caught cheaper fuel AND a softer rate
path. Travel (BKNG +7.29%, EXPE +6.97%) and retail (HD, TGT) joined the bid.

The mirror image was a violent safe-haven / war-trade unwind — the Street pinned it on Fed
Chair Warsh's hawkish posture as much as Hormuz. With rate-cut odds fading and the
geopolitical bid gone together, every non-yielding hedge was hit: silver -6.4% (SLV -7.09%),
gold -1.6%, copper (COPX -4.76%), miners (GDX -3.95%, SIL -3.77%), and the whole digital-asset
block — Bitcoin back under $60,000 (lowest since Oct 2024). Crypto proxies were carnage:
MSTR -9.35%, COIN -5.10%, HOOD -5.87%, CRCL -6.21%. Space/defence (RKLB -10.21%, ASTS -6.67%)
and alt-asset managers (BX -5.90%, APO -6.13%) rounded out the losers.

The second story was MEMORY. Chips sold off all session as traders de-risked into Micron's
print — then Micron quadrupled revenue, posted $24.46 EPS vs $1.68 yr-ago, and ripped +14.84%
AH, dragging SNDK, WDC, STX, QCOM and LRCX up double digits. The intraday weakness was
positioning, not a thesis break. The Fed also cleared all big banks (capacity for $708B losses).
