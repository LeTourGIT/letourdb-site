Everything that borrows to grow was sold. Russell 2000 -1.31% (Bearish,
RSI-15 34.93) sits +0.24% above its 200day MA near 2,786. Caterpillar -5.75%
was the biggest drag on the price-weighted Dow — higher equipment-finance
costs plus an FTC/USDA farm-equipment inquiry naming Deere (-3.80%). The
same factor hit housing (ITB -2.59%), REITs (XLRE -1.29%), regional banks
(KRE -1.68%) and debt-funded AI capacity (IREN -6.27%, APLD -6.04%).

Rotation, not liquidation. Health Care (XLV +1.03%) was the only green
sector, and it was large-cap pharma — LLY +2.70%, AMGN +2.60%, PPH +1.22%
against IBB +0.12%. The second bid was power and storage: NRG +4.84%, VST
+3.88%, Everpure +3.71%, NetApp +3.20%. Six of seven names on the 20day-high
sheet are hardware, storage or networking: the AI trade has moved from chip
designers to whoever stores the data and sells the electrons.

Hard assets were liquidated. With the Fed hiking and the dollar at its
highest since April 2025, GLD fell -1.67% (20day -6.81%), GDX -3.13%, SIL
-3.54%, COPX -3.13%; uranium and ether completed a 0-versus-10 ETF ±3%
screen. WTI eased -1.41% to $88.94 but Brent held $100.95 — the $12 spread
is freight (a $76M supertanker charter, ten times pre-war), and Smart Money
is following it into tankers and refiners. Next: Thursday's $22B 30-year
auction and September CPI on 14/10.
