📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Tuesday 21 July closed effectively flat — XJO +0.02% to 8,793.3, XAO
+0.02% to 8,976.9 — but the flat headline masks a defensive, two-speed
session. Just 4 of 11 sectors finished green, yet the two that led (IT
+3.26%, Materials +1.33%) carry enough index weight to offset a broadly
red board. XJO decliners actually edged advancers 103 to 90.

Information Technology led on data-centre/AI capex demand: NXT (NextDC)
+7.74% and CAT (Catapult) +8.36% drove the sector even as US semis faded
overnight — the ASX expressing the AI-infrastructure thesis through
data centres rather than chips. Materials was carried entirely by
precious metals: gold +0.83% and silver +2.11% overnight lit up EVN
+5.63%, S32 +6.62%, SBM +5.88% and SLS +5.58%. This is a safe-haven
rotation, not a broad resources rally — iron ore slipped again (~US$98.6/t,
2nd day down on soft Chinese steel margins), and LTR -5.10% shows lithium
remains the sold end of the mining complex (LTR -14.79% over 5 days).

The red side of the board tells the rates-and-risk story. Financials
-0.71% saw platforms and lenders hit hard — HUB -4.18%, NWL -4.05%,
JDO -4.71%, HLI -10.11% — as rising bond yields pressured rate-sensitive
business models. Healthcare -1.05% was the outright laggard (MSB -4.53%,
CU6 -3.88%). Consumer Discretionary -0.84% (APE -3.82%) reflects caution
into the 11 August RBA meeting, where the cash rate sits at 4.35% and
Westpac still forecasts hikes to 4.85% while CBA/NAB/ANZ see a hold.

Underneath, the defensive posture is unmistakable: Utilities +0.22% and
the firm Energy tape (+0.40%) plus Real Estate +0.58% show cash being
parked in must-invest, lower-beta corners while the Hormuz oil premium
and RBA overhang keep risk appetite capped. AUD/USD 0.7022 (+0.36%) is
a mild tailwind but too small to move the miner-revenue maths materially.
