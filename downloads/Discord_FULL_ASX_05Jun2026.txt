📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday 5 June delivered a tale of two markets in a single flat headline: XJO closed
essentially unchanged (-0.06% to 8,625.1) as a dramatic rotational split carved the
market into winners and losers with conviction. Healthcare (XHJ +3.54%) led all sectors
by a wide margin — the first meaningful institutional re-engagement with a sector that
has suffered a -44.82% 1-year loss — powered by Pro Medicus (PME) surging 10.8%+ on
continued momentum from its A$44M US contract wins (Allegheny Health Network + TidalHealth).
WiseTech Global (WTC +7.9%), Xero (XRO +7.5%), and Life360 (360 +13.3%) amplified the
technology bid as offshore SaaS peers continued to make records overnight. The common
thread: companies with USD-denominated international revenue benefiting from a tech-sector
re-rating that explicitly excludes semiconductor hardware.

The other side of the ledger was equally decisive. Materials (XMJ -2.31%) and Energy
(XEJ -1.11%) both fell hard — a stark reversal from the year's dominant trend. BHP,
RIO, FMG and the resource complex turned south as iron ore slid to $101.96 (5day:
-6.49%) and WTI's fragile ceasefire-driven dip removed the Hormuz premium that had
underwritten the energy sector for months. Financials (XFJ -0.89%) remained under
structural pressure from the FWC's 4.75% wage decision — a direct compression to
bank-adjacent retail and hospitality sectors, with the added sting of an August RBA
hike now firmly in analysts' base cases.

The session's internal architecture tells the real story: Adv/Dec ratio of 94:100 for
XJO — barely even — confirms the headline flatness masked genuine conviction in both
directions. New 20day Highs (16) roughly matched 20day Lows (14), with T2108 at 39%
— below the 40% threshold that historically separates bear market rallies from genuine
recoveries. The XSO Neutral Bull regime divergence from XJO/XAO/XTO Neutral Bear is
the week's most important structural signal: small-cap relative resilience (XSO -0.17%
vs 50MA) against large-cap deterioration (-1.20% for XJO vs 50MA) sets up an important
test around the RBA meeting.
