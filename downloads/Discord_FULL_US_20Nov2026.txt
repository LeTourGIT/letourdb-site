📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The catalyst was a single headline: Reuters reported that SEC enforcement staff are examining potential accounting issues at Nvidia. NVDA was already down roughly 7% before the report hit and finished -14.01% at $125.53 on $79.5B of turnover — more than double the next-largest name in the 16d mover list (MU $35.3B). That is the seventh straight weekly loss, the longest run since 2008, and roughly -23% for the week. The distinction matters: the governance crisis that followed Jensen Huang's 12/11 exit attacked the multiple. An accounting inquiry attacks the denominator — the reported earnings every AI-complex valuation is anchored to. Investigations are not findings, but markets price the distribution of outcomes — the left tail just got fatter.

The transmission ran straight down the NVDA bill of materials, and the damage scaled with revenue dependence on Nvidia: memory (MU -13.89%, SNDK -16.05%), compute rival AMD -13.25%, connectivity (CRDO -15.20%, ALAB -14.77%), optics (AAOI -16.55%, LITE -13.94%, COHR -12.90%), data-centre power (BE -15.30%) and GPU hosting / bitcoin-miner conversions (IREN -17.97%, APLD -17.81%, WULF -14.94%). SOXX -10.55% and XSD -9.71% were the two worst ETFs on the board; XLK -7.27% is an EXTREME sector print. If Nvidia's top line is questioned, every supplier's order book is questioned with it — this was a re-underwriting of the whole AI capex chain, not a single-stock event.

Index damage is now structural: SPX's seventh straight loss leaves it -11.62% from its high (correction) and NDX -19.84% from peak — one ordinary down day from a bear market. DJI's downgrade to Bearish puts all four indices in the same regime. RSI-15 of 25.5 (SPX) and 27.2 (NDX) is washout-grade oversold — reflex-rally fuel, but only once forced sellers are exhausted.

The breadth read is the most important nuance of the session: participation actually IMPROVED versus Thursday even though the index fell twice as far. SPX Adv/Dec was 123/374 against 38/461 on 19/11, equal-weight RSP lost only -1.98% versus IVV -3.52%, and pure value RPV (-1.10%) beat pure growth RPG (-6.10%) by 5.0 points. Thursday was indiscriminate liquidation; Friday was a targeted exit from the cap-weighted AI leadership while capital hid in defensives — XLP +0.67% was the only positive SPDR, managed care bounced (UNH +2.84%, HUM +2.63%, HCA +3.31%) and telecom/staples (T, VZ, TMUS, GIS, CL, WMT) all closed green. Small caps got no such shelter: RUT Adv/Dec 276/1650 with 816 new 20day lows.

Cross-asset, only Treasuries and defensive equities worked. VIX +26.2% to 35.89 and yields fell sharply on haven demand. Gold failed as a hedge: XAUUSD -3.87%, GDX -5.00%, SLV -6.74%, and the Gold industry fell 114 Smart Money ranks in three days. That is margin-call selling of winners to fund losers; bitcoin (IBIT -5.87%) failed the same test. Oil held (WTI +0.76%) on Iran's strikes in Kuwait — a live tail, not today's driver.
