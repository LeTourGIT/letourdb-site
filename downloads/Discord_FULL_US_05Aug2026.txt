📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Wednesday was a macro-driven regime rotation. ADP private payrolls printed just
+44k for July against +70k expected — the weakest read in six months and down from
+98k in June. JOLTS openings had already softened Tuesday, and ISM Services came in
at 54.1 (vs 54.5) with its employment sub-index sliding into contraction at 47.4.
The market's read was immediate: CME September rate-HIKE odds collapsed from ~67% to
~57%. Note the direction — under Chair Warsh (funds rate held 3.50-3.75% on 29 July,
9-3 vote) the debate is still about whether to hike further, not cut. A soft labour
print simply takes the next hike off the table rather than pulling forward easing.

That single repricing detonated precious metals. Gold ripped +4% to ~$4,234 — its
biggest one-day advance since February — and silver +3.9% to a six-week high near
$61.85. The equity expression was violent: GDX +7.39% and SIL +6.59% both cleared
the +5% EXTREME threshold, with SLV +4.14%, GLD +4.14%, GLTR +3.97%, XME +3.76% and
COPX +3.25% completing SEVEN ETFs above +3%. In the cash tape, Coeur (CDE) +7.53%,
Hecla (HL) +7.47% and Newmont (NEM) +6.71% led the S&P/Russell gainers. Softer real
yield expectations + a weaker dollar + easing oil-driven inflation fear is the
textbook bullion cocktail, and institutions expressed it through the miners' beta.

The mirror image was the megacap-growth and semiconductor complex. AMD -7.04% on
heavy turnover led a broad chip de-rate: Astera Labs (ALAB) -11.96%, Lattice (LSCC)
-7.02%, and at the ETF level XSD -3.12%, SOXX -2.12%. This dragged NDX to -0.83% and
into a Bearish regime while its 20day trend flipped Down. The rotation is not risk-
off — VIX fell 4.18% to 15.81 — it is capital leaving expensive duration-like growth
and rebuilding in rate-sensitive cyclicals, miners, and value. That is exactly why
the Dow printed a fresh RECORD close (+0.49%, its best 5-day run since April 2025)
even as the S&P slipped and the Russell fell -0.59%.

The offsetting macro tailwind is oil. Trump flagged 'very good discussions' with
Iran and a possible deal as soon as Wednesday, with Oman-mediated Hormuz talks 'in
final stages.' Crude is down roughly 10% on the week on reopening hopes even after a
+0.55% WTI bounce today to $74.99. Falling energy costs reinforce the disinflation
read that is feeding the gold and rate-cut trade — but they simultaneously gutted
energy equities: XOP -3.93%, IEO -3.56%, XLE -2.07%, with EOG -6.47%, DVN -4.45% and
MPC -4.75% among the day's worst large-caps. Oil up, oil stocks down = the tape is
pricing the RESOLUTION, not the current barrel.

The wage data is the fly in the ointment and the reason this isn't a clean risk-on
melt-up. ADP showed job-stayer pay +4.4% but job-switcher pay accelerating to +7%
(from 6.6%). Slowing hiring with re-accelerating wages is a stagflationary tension —
and it is why Fed Governor Cook said she is 'prepared to act' on a HIKE. Friday's NFP
(consensus ~+80k) is now the fulcrum: a soft print extends the gold/value rotation,
a hot wage number hands the hawks their case and caps bullion above $4,200.
