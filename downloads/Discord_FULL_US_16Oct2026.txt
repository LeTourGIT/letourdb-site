📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday ended the week the way it started: AI longs being liquidated. Stocks
opened higher, then the selling built into the final hour. SPX lost 1.09% to
7,659 and the NDX shed 2.50% to 29,188. The VIX ripped 21.45% to 19.99 after an
intraday 22.31, its highest since June. MarketWatch calls it the "AI burnout
trade". The mechanism is more specific than fatigue. It is funding cost. AI
infrastructure has been built on debt, and with the 10-year above 5% this month
for the first time since 2002, credit markets have started pricing that risk.
CoreWeave's credit default swaps widened all week, and the stock fell 11.91% on
Friday (5day -25.21%). That repricing is spreading through the stack.

Trace the chain. Neocloud and AI-hosting names fell hardest, because their
equity sits beneath the most leverage: CRWV -11.91%, APLD -11.48%, IREN -11.19%,
WULF -7.45%. Next came the hardware supply chain that sells to them: SMCI -9.86%,
AAOI -9.75%, FLEX -8.16%, DELL -4.58%, VRT -3.88%. Then the semis: MU -5.63%,
AMD -5.95%, INTC -5.79%, AVGO -4.52%. SOXX -4.14% made a ninth straight daily
decline, the first such streak since 2006. Software did not escape. IGV -4.80%
was the session's worst ETF, with TWLO -10.62%, OKTA -10.20%, SNOW -8.43%,
MDB -7.89%, PANW -7.63% and CRWD -7.02%. "Nowhere to hide" is accurate inside
tech. The application layer is no longer a refuge from the infrastructure layer.

The 9day Smart Money data shows the scale: Semiconductor Equipment went from
rank 1 to rank 137 of 139 in nine sessions, and Semiconductors from rank 6 to 134.
The leadership table has been emptied top to bottom inside two weeks.

Geopolitics supplied the other half of the tape. Iran escalated attacks in the
Strait of Hormuz, Saudi Aramco facilities were hit earlier in the week, and
Ukraine vowed to strike Russian refineries. Brent touched $110 and closed +4.31%
at $109.69, with WTI +3.52% at $99.04. Capital went where it always goes in a war
tape: upstream energy (FANG +4.27%, EOG +3.53%, COP +3.37%, CVX +2.59%) and the
defence primes (NOC +4.05%, RTX +3.73%, LMT +3.65%, LHX +3.33%). Ten of the
top 15 institutional gainers came from those two groups.

The defining feature of the day is the breadth mismatch. SPX fell 1.09%, but
advancers and decliners were dead even at 248/252. Equal-weight RSP fell only
0.42%, so the cap-weighted index underperformed it by 68bp. SPLV low-volatility
rose 1.04%, and XLV traded at record highs intraday. Visa and Mastercard hit
records. The SPX also logged 171 new 20day highs against only 31 new lows.
This is a megacap AI drawdown masking a rotation into defensives that is working.
For traders, the takeaway is to keep the AI longs light, own the rotation, and
watch whether NDX RSI-15 reaches the low 30s before buying the dip.
