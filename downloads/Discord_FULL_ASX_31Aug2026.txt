📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Monday delivered a quiet index drift (XJO -0.18% to 9076, XAO -0.25%) that
masked violent single-name dispersion — the signature of FY26 August full-year
reporting season colliding with a sharp overnight gold selloff. Under the flat
headline, this was a defensive rotation: capital left gold, miners and tech and
moved into banks and staples.

The driver was offshore. Gold fell 2.88% Friday after Fed Chair Warsh's Jackson
Hole keynote read hawkish — US hike bets jumped, the 10yr yield and USD firmed.
That repriced the entire ASX gold complex: XMJ -2.02% was the worst sector, with
GGP -6.40%, BNZ -9.24%, PDI -6.26% and LRV -9.96% all sold. The cruel irony is
that gold miners ARE the market's institutional RS leadership — 8 of the top-10
ASX200/300 RS names are gold — so today the strongest cohort took the hardest hit.

Not all of Materials was red. Coal and lithium diverged higher: Coronado (CRN)
+25.00% led the whole market with no company announcement — a coal-sector move
(WHC, NHC, YAL, CRN all at 20day highs; Coal is Smart Money rank 5). Lithium names
bid too — GLN +9.72%, CXO +4.11%, LTR +2.51% (FY26 results) — as carbonate holds
+29% YTD on CATL restart delays. Treat CRN's +25% as unconfirmed until a catalyst
surfaces.

Financials (XFJ +1.14%) was the only clean leader — the mirror image of the gold
trade, banks absorbing the rotation out of rate-sensitive and momentum names.
Reporting-season winners and losers set the tails: BLS +15.22% (FY26 results +
FY27 guidance), KCN +4.71% (4E + maiden 10c dividend, the one gold name up),
PXA +9.43% (post-result re-rate); against NVX -12.00% (H1 miss), SLX -13.76%
(US-semi read-through) and Star Entertainment's A$307m FY26 loss reviving
survival doubts. Breadth was negative across ALL four indices — the quiet index
print flatters a session where decliners clearly outran advancers.
