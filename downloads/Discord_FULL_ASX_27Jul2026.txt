📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Monday 27 July delivered the ASX 200's best session in six weeks,
+1.39% to 8,894.0, as the US-Iran decision to pause missile strikes
over the weekend gapped oil sharply lower and unwound the war-risk
premium that had hung over markets for a fortnight. Brent fell below
US$92 and WTI sat at US$83.69 (-1.78%). Cheaper crude is a direct
inflation reliever for Australia, and the whole rate-sensitive complex
— REITs, industrials, discretionary — responded. Breadth was genuine:
166 advancers to 28 decliners on the XJO, a 5.9:1 thrust.

The paradox of the session is that ENERGY (XEJ -2.99%) was the only
red sector — the exact mirror of the bullish macro. Santos (-3.64%,
even with its first Barossa condensate cargo), Whitehaven (-3.61%),
Karoon (-7.82%) and the coal names all fell as the oil premium bled
out. This is textbook: the catalyst that lifts the index is the same
one that sinks its energy sub-index. Trade the mechanism, not the tape.

Materials (XMJ +2.42%) did the heavy lifting, and it was GOLD-led, not
iron-ore-led. Capricorn Metals +15.13% (Mt Gibson PFS, reserves to
5.2Moz), Bellevue +9.09%, Ora Banda +6.67%, with bullion above
US$4,100. On top of that, M&A fired: Evolution Mining's agreed takeover
of Carnaby Resources sent CNB +57.29%. The gold complex — not the
BHP/RIO iron-ore majors — is where the institutional RS now sits.

Information Technology (+4.52%) topped the board, but read it carefully:
this was Wisetech +7%, Megaport and BrainChip — domestic software and
data-centre names, plus 1414 Degrees +14.29% on a 1GW data-centre HoA.
It is NOT the semiconductor trade that just sank the US Nasdaq (SOX
-4.3% Friday). The two markets are pricing AI infrastructure through
different vehicles. Financials (+1.19%) quietly firmed with all Big-4
banks at fresh 20day highs and PPT fielding a revised takeover bid.

The caveat sits in the small-cap tape: XSO is Bearish, -8.1% below its
200MA, and today's leadership was concentrated in large-cap quality.
A single risk-on session on an external oil catalyst is not a small-cap
re-rating. It is also a heavy quarterly-reporting Monday (late-July 4C/5B
window) — dozens of small-cap resources filings and dividend notices.
