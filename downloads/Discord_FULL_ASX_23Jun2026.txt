📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The ASX 200 eased 0.33% to 8,787.0, the All Ords -0.48% to 8,988.3 —
a risk-off session imported wholesale from Wall Street's Mag-7 rout.
NOT broad-based selling: a tech-and-resources de-rate against a
defensive bid. Decliners swamped advancers 146 to 45 on the XJO
(3.2:1), yet Financials closed GREEN — the clearest sign capital
didn't leave the market, it rotated within it.

IT (XIJ -4.04%) was the epicentre, a direct read-through from
Alphabet -5% overnight. Technology One (TNE -7.10%, ASX200) led
domestic software lower; WiseTech extended its founder-probe slide.
High-multiple ASX software has no insulation when the global AI
bellwether de-rates on talent and valuation.

Materials (XMJ -1.38%, 5day -6.06%) was the second engine — the
lithium complex is unwinding. Chinese carbonate futures fell sharply
on CATL Jianxiawo restart speculation (the mirror image of May's
re-rating). Iluka (ILU -10.82%) was the institutional casualty, and
it fell DESPITE announcing a binding multi-year take-or-pay magnet
REO deal with a global automaker (~10% of production from 2028).
Classic sell-the-news in a tape with no critical-minerals appetite —
Alpha HPA (A4N -10.43%) and a string of silver/gold juniors confirmed.

Energy (XEJ -0.69%) was capped by Brent -2.75% overnight as a US-Iran
60-day peace roadmap drains the Hormuz premium — disinflationary for
the RBA, a revenue headwind for Woodside/Santos. With the RBA having
HELD rates last week, the domestic rate story now pivots to next
week's May CPI and labour data; globally the louder signal is the US
2yr at 4.23% reviving Fed hike bets (Centuria CNI -6.57%).

AUD/USD slipped 0.57% to 0.6961 — a quiet offset. A softer Aussie
cushions BHP/RIO/FMG USD revenue (~0.7% per 1c AUD fall), partially
blunting the commodity drag. Tomorrow's question: with ES/NQ futures
still red at the close, does the defensive rotation hold the 50MA, or
does a second leg of the Mag-7 unwind drag the index through support?
