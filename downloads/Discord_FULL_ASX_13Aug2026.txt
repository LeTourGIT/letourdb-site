📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday delivered a shallow -0.23% dip in the ASX 200 to 9,188.5 — a pause at record-high territory
rather than a reversal. With the trend structure fully Bullish (10MA > 20MA > 50MA) and RSI at 64.7,
the tape is consolidating a strong run while the market digests the busiest week of FY26 reporting season.

The session's character was defined by two heavyweight results dragging the index while the broad
market held up. Telstra's FY26 print sent Communication Services (XTJ -2.30%) to the bottom of the
board and CBA fell -2.15% the day after its own result, with home-loan applications reported down 15%
since May — a concrete sign mortgage demand is cooling. Yet Financials (XFJ) still closed +0.08% as
Suncorp (+3.28%) and other insurers absorbed the CBA drag: the bank weakness was name-specific, not sectoral.

Reporting-season dispersion was the real story. ASX Ltd (+9.03%), Origin (+5.33%, leading Utilities)
and Treasury Wine (+4.91%) were rewarded for their results, while IAG (-5.10%) and HomeCo Daily Needs
(-5.49%) were punished for theirs. This is a stock-pickers' tape — index beta is flat while single-name
moves are running 5-15%. Cleanaway (+15.19%) topped the board on a genuine corporate catalyst: a Process
Deed with EQT following a takeover approach, a reminder that M&A is live in ASX mid-caps.

Under the surface, the Smart Money rotation is emphatically risk-on: Renewable Energy Equipment, Aerospace
& Defence, Uranium, Semiconductors and Biotech all surged 50-80 rank positions in nine sessions, with Gold
entrenched at rank 3. That breadth of capital rotation — resources, defence, growth and clean energy all
bid simultaneously — is what underpins the record-high tape despite a soft index print today.
