📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The dominant catalyst is geopolitical: a US–Iran peace deal (final agreed text
confirmed by Pakistani mediators ~14 June, signing timeline still fluid) collapsed
the war premium. WTI fell -6.02% to $75.83 — its lowest since early March and the
first sustained break back toward pre-war levels. Brent -4.21% to $79.46, USO -5.09%.
The market is now pricing the Strait of Hormuz reopening as a base case, not a tail.

The second-order effect dominated the tape: a violent unwind of every trade that had
been a war-premium beneficiary. Semiconductors — the cycle's AI-capex leadership —
took the brunt: SOXX -5.92%, XSD -5.73%, both EXTREME (>5%) one-day moves. MRVL -9.78%,
MPWR -9.29%, INTC -8.45%, AMD -7.30%, KLAC -7.44%, MU -6.18% ($46B turnover). This was
not chip-specific news — CNBC's tape read 'chip stock rebound fails' — it was
positioning. Capital that had crowded into AI-infra as the safe-haven growth trade
rotated out the moment the geopolitical hedge lost its rationale.

Where did it go? Into value and cyclicals. Dow printed its first-ever close above
52,000. Financials led (XLF +1.47%: JPM +3.68%, COF +3.08%, SCHW +2.99%, SOFI +3.39%)
on a lower-oil/lower-inflation, steeper-curve read. Utilities (XLU +0.72%) and the
AI-power complex held — TLN +5.26%, VST +3.32% — a critical signal: the market is
separating AI-power-demand (intact) from AI-silicon-multiple (de-rating). Industrials
(XLI +0.65%, WWD/CMI) caught the falling-input-cost bid. Gaming/software (RBLX +8.06%,
TTWO +6.35%) and biotech (MRNA +6.27%) confirmed the move is breadth-broadening, not
risk-off.

⚠ THE KEY DIVERGENCE: SPX closed -0.57% but advancers BEAT decliners 271/229. This is
a REVERSE cap-weight distortion — the mirror image of the May ATH-on-narrow-breadth
setup. The median S&P stock rose; a handful of mega-cap chips dragged the index down.
T2108 at 68.2% and 101 new 20day highs vs 15 lows confirm the underlying tape is
HEALTHY beneath the chip carnage. NDX breadth (35/65) is the inverse — chip
concentration makes the growth index structurally fragile here. The rotation is
orderly: VIX only +1.30% (and -17% over 5days) — no panic, just repositioning.
