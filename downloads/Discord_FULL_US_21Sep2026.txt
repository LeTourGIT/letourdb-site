📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Monday delivered a green screen that concealed a fracturing market. The Nasdaq
100 closed at a record 30,482 (+2.83%) — its strongest single session of the
recent AI leg — while the S&P added +1.49% to 7,764. But the Dow (+0.71%) and
Russell 2000 (+0.52%) advanced only grudgingly and remain in BEARISH regimes
below their 50-day averages. One tape, two markets.

The catalyst was narrow and specific: Meta's Muse agent (+11.34% for META)
triggered a violent repricing of the CPU-demand thesis. Intel +12.14%, AMD
+9.95% (past $1T), Qualcomm +9.29% and Astera Labs +12.36% led an SOXX +4.93%
that logged a fifth consecutive gain. Critically, no chip company reported
anything — this was a second-derivative bet that agentic AI raises inference
and therefore CPU intensity. XLC (+3.56%, lifted by Meta) and XLK (+2.77%) were
the only sectors with real thrust; everything defensive (XLP -1.06%, XLU -1.07%)
and the entire energy complex (XLE -2.88%) went the other way.

Beneath the indices, the internals are flashing a warning not seen clearly
since 1999. The S&P closed higher yet registered 129 NEW 20-day LOWS against
just 31 new highs — more than four stocks hitting fresh lows for every one
hitting a high, on an UP day at the index highs. The Russell told the same
story: 286 new lows vs 112 new highs. Only 49.6% of SPX members sit above their
200-day average and T2108 is a soft 25% — the classic Hindenburg-style
divergence where a cap-weighted index levitates on a handful of mega-caps while
the median stock slides. Advancers did beat decliners today (SPX 288/210), so
this is deterioration in the TAILS, not yet the core — but the new-low
expansion at new highs is the tell to respect.

Capital rotation confirmed the split. Smart Money's fastest movers were
Miscellaneous Fintech Infrastructure (rank 1, ↑↑ SURGE) and Blockchain &
Cryptocurrency (rank 2) — validated by IBIT +6.5% and ETHA +4.67% as Bitcoin
and Ether ETFs ripped on risk-on. Simultaneously, oil broke lower (WTI -2.15%
to $91.95) as the market priced "past peak fear," and the refiner complex was
crushed — MPC -5.30%, VLO -4.84%, PSX -4.17%, with integrated names XOM -3.20%,
COP -3.26%, CVX -2.79% following. The transport tape (UPS -4.35%, FDX -2.59%,
CSX -2.36%, UNP -3.49%) is the quiet worry: freight weakness plus a Bearish Dow
is a cyclical demand signal that the AI headline is drowning out.

The macro backdrop is dense this week. The Trump–Xi summit (Sep 23–25, Xi's
first White House visit in 11 years) puts trade, AI, rare earths, fentanyl and
Taiwan on the table with the tariff truce expiring in November — the state
dinner guest list (Huang, Cook, Altman, Pichai, Musk) signals how tech-centric
the agenda is. Treasury's Bessent ruled out an AI "liability shield." And oil's
retreat came even as CNBC flagged fresh Iran escalation risk. Net: a low-VIX
(14.87) melt-up in mega-cap AI riding on internals that are quietly the weakest
in a year.
