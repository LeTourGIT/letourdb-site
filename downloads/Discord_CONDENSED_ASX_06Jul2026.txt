📝 SESSION SUMMARY
A quiet-looking session that wasn't quiet underneath. XJO slipped -0.15% to
8,831.0 and XAO -0.12% to 9,037.0, but decliners outnumbered advancers 112 to
74 (0.66:1). A flat index built on negative breadth is a distribution signal —
index calm masking rotation beneath the surface.
The session's headline act was gold M&A. Genesis (GMD) lobbed an unsolicited
binding A$5.6bn bid for Vault (VAU) — 0.7629 scrip + 47.5c cash — a 14.5%
premium the Vault board declared superior to the standing Regis (RRL) merger.
VAU surged +11.62% to A$5.09 [H]; GMD fell -4.13% on scrip dilution; Regis
rose on a strong June quarter and now has to 10 July to match. It's the latest
leg of an accelerating ASX gold consolidation wave (NST/De Grey, Gold Fields/
Gold Road) as record bullion funds production land-grabs.
Away from gold the tape was defensive and US-shaped. Healthcare (XHJ +1.50%)
led on CU6 +19% (Clarity Pharma) with CSL/COH/PME at fresh 20day highs — a
classic risk-management bid. IT (+0.93%) leaned on WTC +7.31% rebounding off
its recent A$30 sell-off, even as WBT -5.84% tracked the US chip slump.
Materials (-0.63%) and Utilities (-0.79%) lagged: iron ore has slid to ~US$98/t
(sub-US$100, -5.3% mth), pressuring the big miners, while bond-proxy utilities
keep de-rating as the long end stays firm. RBA held 4.35% in June after three
2026 hikes; next meets 11 Aug — still-restrictive policy is the weight on
rate-sensitive sectors. AUD/USD eased to 0.6933, a modest miner cushion.
