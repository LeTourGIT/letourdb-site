📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The 'Great Rotation' went from simmer to boil. The Dow tacked on 594.83
points (+1.14%) to a record 52,900.07 while the Nasdaq-100 fell -1.61% and
the S&P 500 finished dead flat — the widest Dow-vs-Nasdaq spread of the
cycle. This is not a market breakdown; it is a violent internal reallocation.
SPX Adv/Dec was strongly POSITIVE at 355/144 even as the cap-weighted index
printed flat — the exact inverse of a breadth divergence. The median stock
rose; the megacap-semi complex dragged the headline. Breadth is widening, not
cracking.

The driver was a second consecutive day of semiconductor liquidation. SOXX
-5.57% and XSD -6.74% led the tape lower, with Teradyne -13.6%, KLA -11.5%,
Onto -12.5%, Lam -10.2%, Entegris -11.2% — the wafer-fab-equipment and
test/metrology layer taking the deepest cuts. Memory collapsed alongside
(SanDisk -14.1%, WDC -9.9%; the DRAM ETF is pacing -15% on the week). The
mechanism is duration: soft June nonfarm payrolls (reported a day early ahead
of the July 4 close) pulled forward Fed-easing expectations, but the more
powerful effect is a re-rating of the AI trade itself — companies valued on
cash flows three-to-five years out get discounted hardest when the market
questions the capex cycle's durability.

Where did the cash go? Straight into near-term earners. Health Care XLV
+2.63% was the dominant sector (MRNA +10%, VRTX +6%, ISRG +5.9%, MDT +5%),
with Utilities +2.21%, Staples +2.03%, and the value/dividend complex (SPLV,
SDY, SCHD, IWD all +1-2%) catching the rotation. Precious metals ripped —
GDX +4.48%, SIL +3.9%, gold $4,133 +1.25% — as a hard-asset hedge. Defense
primes surged in unison (AVAV +10.7%, NOC +5.6%, HONA +8.7%). The one big-tech
survivor was Apple +4.84% on $23B turnover — a mega-cap ex-semi safe haven.

Smart Money confirms the flow in the plumbing: Home Furnishings Retailers,
Real Estate Services (surging 60→2 over nine days), P&C Insurance (33→3),
Healthcare Facilities, Discount Stores, Managed Healthcare and Fintech
(91→7 SURGE) now occupy the top of the rotation table. Every one is domestic,
value-tilted, or rate-sensitive. Not a single semiconductor industry sits in
the top 16. The rotation is institutional, multi-day, and accelerating.

Crypto diverged risk-on: ETHA +5.63%, IBIT +2.62% — digital assets rallied
while equities de-risked, a notable decoupling. Oil firmed (+1.1%) as Strait
of Hormuz traffic rebuilt and Washington and Tehran prepared fresh talks in
Qatar on Tuesday. Tesla -7% despite record Q2 deliveries (480k vs 407k est)
was the day's marquee sell-the-news, dragging CARZ -3.4%.
