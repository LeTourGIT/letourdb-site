📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday 26 June was a defensive grind that closed marginally green
(XJO +0.18% to 8,764.2) but masked one of the year's cleaner risk-off
rotations. The global chip rout dragged ASX IT (XIJ -1.17%) and Health
Care (XHJ -1.30%) to the bottom while capital that must stay invested
funnelled into Utilities (XUJ +1.01%) and Consumer Staples (XSJ +0.81%).
Defensives leading + growth trailing = capital preservation, not
conviction.

The defining stock story was the CONTINUED LITHIUM WIPEOUT. PLS -6.32%
(ASX50), LTR -5.68% (ASX200), ELV -8.94% (ASX200), CXO -7.27% and
PMT -5.83% all fell hard, extending 5day losses of 14–19%. Catalyst is
offshore: speculation that CATL's Jianxiawo mine could restart revived
oversupply fears; Chinese carbonate (~CNY169,000/t) is off a 2-month low
but far below the mid-May peak. Structural, not a one-day flush.

The offset was GOLD. After bullion slid sub-US$4,000 overnight to a
7-month low, it bounced +0.62% to ~$4,043 and the ASX gold complex
caught a strong bid — SLS +29.07% topped the board on a new 20day high,
KCN +4.46% firm. Gold strength + iron ore at ~US$105/t (highest since
January) is why Materials (XMJ +0.81%) printed green DESPITE lithium —
"resources" is not one trade.

Smart Money reinforced the domestic-cyclical/defensive tilt: Airlines
surged to #1 (from #30), Home Furnishings #2, Department Stores #3 —
pure ASX consumer-economy flows. Semiconductors rocketed to #8 (from
#72): local money front-running a chip-rout bottom — early, watch it.

RBA sits on a hawkish hold after three hikes since January; May headline
CPI eased to 4.0% but core re-accelerated to 3.6%, and Dep. Gov. Hauser
says inflation is "too high." June minutes due next week.
