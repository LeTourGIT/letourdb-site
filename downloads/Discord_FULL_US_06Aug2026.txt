📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday was a two-front rotation session dressed up as a quiet down day. Headline losses
were small (SPX -0.18%, NDX -0.39%) but underneath, two structural repricings ran in
opposite directions: high-multiple software was violently de-rated, while capital surged
into hard assets, energy and financials on a fresh Hormuz escalation. VIX FELL 4.2% — the
signature of orderly rotation, not fear.

The software story is the inverse of the enterprise-software surge earlier this cycle. This
time even clean beats were punished: Datadog (-19.0%) beat and RAISED full-year guidance yet
collapsed after flagging that its largest AI client expects lighter second-half usage — the
market read it as direct evidence of AI cannibalizing consumption-based SaaS. HubSpot (-19.1%)
cut its outlook and drew a wall of downgrades (Bernstein, Oppenheimer, Piper). Figma (-14.9%)
beat but froze operating income as AI inference costs eat margin. AppLovin (-19.7%) posted its
first guidance miss since IPO. The common thread: AI has flipped from a software tailwind to a
simultaneous margin cost AND demand threat. IGV -1.87% confirms it was sector-wide, not idiosyncratic.

The second front is geopolitical. Iran's parliament published a draft plan to ban US and
Israeli ships from the Strait of Hormuz, impose tolls up to 7% of cargo value and fine
violators 20% — Washington rejected it outright, demanding transit with no impediments. Crude
bid immediately (WTI +2.74% to $78.88, OIH +1.82%, XOP +1.13%), Energy was the ONLY green SPDR
sector (XLE +1.48%), and the oil→inflation impulse pushed Treasury yields up, de-rating rate
proxies (homebuilders ITB -2.39%, XHB -1.87%; utilities XLU -0.64%; REITs XLRE -0.86%). Airlines
(JETS -2.65%) took the fuel-cost hit directly. This is the mirror image of the peace-signal oil
selloffs earlier this cycle — the Hormuz premium is being RE-priced IN, not out.

Where did the money go? Into hard assets. Smart Money rotation shows a clean precious-metals +
mining cluster surging: Gold rank 5 (SURGE), Precious Metals rank 6, Specialty Mining rank 13,
Integrated Mining rank 15, Mining Support rank 2 — with the physical complex confirming (gold
+0.81%, silver +0.98%, zinc +3.21%, copper +2.03%, palladium +1.33%, REMX +1.32%). Fintech
stayed entrenched at rank 1 (Chime CHYM +20.6%), and insurance led financials (MET +3.8%,
ALL +4.0%). Quietly, biotech/pharma is re-rating (INSM +33.9%, six biotech names in the RS top 20).

The setup into Friday: a genuine theme rotation with the index barely moving. NDX's Bearish
downgrade is the flag to watch — if software and memory keep bleeding, the Nasdaq's regime
crack widens; if the AH software bounce (TEAM +33%, NET +16%, DOCS +73%) holds, Thursday reads
as a guidance-specific purge rather than a sector top. Hormuz headlines remain the wildcard.
