📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Wednesday delivered the clearest RBA-driven sell-off of the 2026
rate cycle. The ASX 200 shed 1.26% to 8,496.6 — its lowest close
since early April — as the 11:30am AEST release of the May board
minutes removed any residual ambiguity about the rate path. Eight
of nine board members backed May's hike; underlying inflation
projects above 3% through mid-2027; and the board flagged "de-
anchoring" risk in inflation expectations — the RBA's equivalent of
a forward commitment to hike again if needed.

The only standout was TNE (Technology One) +7.86%, which staged a
sharp reversal after Tuesday's initial sell-off on H1 results. The
market reconsidered overnight: ARR growth +28.8%, record H1 profit,
SaaS+ adoption momentum — the sell-off was a multiple recalibration
in a rising-rate environment, not a business quality question. TNE
single-handedly saved XIJ from a deeper decline, but a one-stock
story cannot mask a session where XJO Adv/Dec was 30/161 (0.19:1).

Materials (XMJ -2.12%) was the hardest-hit sector — reversing the
10day Smart Money leadership from early May. BHP, RIO, MIN lower
as SGX iron ore pulled back -1.2%. The RBA minutes explicitly
project that Hormuz-driven supply disruptions will raise inflation
in "globally traded goods" — that direct channel from oil shock to
goods inflation is the mechanism that justifies selling materials.

The XSO (-2.12%) underperforming XJO (-1.26%) confirms that
institutional rotation is now actively purging the speculative tier.
22 XSO stocks fell more than 4% today vs just 16 in XJO — the risk
capital withdrawal is most intense at the small-cap end.
