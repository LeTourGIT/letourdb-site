📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday 21 May delivered the ASX 200's strongest session in recent weeks
with a +1.47% advance to 8,621.7 — but the engine was data, not risk
appetite. April's unemployment print came in at 4.5%, a 4.5-year high
against expectations of a steady 4.3%, triggering an immediate repricing
of the RBA's June meeting: hike probability collapsed from ~60% to just 15%.
This is the single most consequential data point for the ASX since the March
CPI print sent rates expectations in the opposite direction three weeks ago.

The market's "bad news is good news" interpretation is textbook rate-sensitive
logic. Financials surged +1.45% — the same sector that led in red on every
prior RBA anxiety day now led green on the reprieve. REITs +2.21% were the
second-strongest sector, which is the most direct expression of rate relief.
When REITs and Financials rally together, the market is explicitly pricing out
a near-term hike — both sectors face structural headwinds at 4.35%+ and have
underperformed all year. One weak jobs print does not end the RBA's tightening
bias, but it removes imminent trigger pressure.

Separately, WTI crude -5.66% overnight to sub-$100 is the second pillar of
today's move. Iran deal proximity removes the energy inflation premium that
has been embedded in RBA models since February. Combined with the weak
employment data, the RBA now faces a fundamentally different set of inputs for
its June meeting than it did 48 hours ago. This is why Materials (+2.56%),
REITs (+2.21%), and Financials (+1.45%) all rallied together — different
beneficiaries of the same thesis: the rate hike cycle may be ending.

The Nvidia beat overnight was the third tailwind, confirming the AI
infrastructure capex cycle is intact and then some. The ASX's semiconductor
and data centre proxy names (WBT +12.98%, NXT +2.6%) responded directly.
