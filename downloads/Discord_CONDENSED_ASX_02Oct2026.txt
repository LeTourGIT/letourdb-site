📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The ASX 200 added 67.7 points (+0.79%) to 8,682.1, recovering
39% of Thursday's 174.9-point rout and finishing the week
+0.20%. The trigger was bonds, not equities: the US 10-year
yield reversed from 5.344%, its highest since 2002, to 5.24%,
and Wall Street turned a 0.45% loss into a 0.19% gain with VIX
calm at 16.39. Thursday was a discount-rate shock, so the relief
went to the longest-duration sector.

Technology (XIJ +4.45%, 5day +7.34%) is having its best week
since August off a six-month low. The names with evidence are
the ones to trust: Megaport +10.32% and DroneShield +6.41% on
contract wins, Data3 +7.76% to a second all-time high after
Thursday's 1H27 upgrade. WiseTech, Life360 and Xero rose on no
news, which is short covering. Real Estate (-1.65%) fell even as
yields eased, so the market is now pricing the property downturn
itself, and Healthcare (-1.10%) was sold without announcements
to fund the growth rebound.

Resource leaders were distributed into strength: EQ Resources
-10.11%, Macmahon -9.91%, DPM Metals -8.41% (Bosnian court
decision, 08:16, PS) and Benz -8.38%. Structure is unchanged:
Bearish on all four indices, T2108 at 24.5%, and 20day lows
tripling highs on an up day. The RBA is not this week's risk
(4.60% since 29/09, next decision 03/11); tonight's US payrolls
and Thursday's 8,614.4 close are.
