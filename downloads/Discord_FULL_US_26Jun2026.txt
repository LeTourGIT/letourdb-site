📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday's flat headline (SPX -0.05%) masked one of 2026's most violent sector
rotations. The semiconductor complex was routed — SOXX -5.64%, XSD -4.30% — with
ON -23.7%, WDC -13.2%, SanDisk -10.5%, and a wall of -7% to -9% prints across QCOM,
TXN, MPWR, ADI, TER and FLEX. Micron, fresh off a blockbuster record-margin quarter
the night before, fell 6.7% — the cleanest "sell the news" the tape can produce.

The trigger was financing, not fundamentals. Oracle logged its worst week since the
2001 dot-com bust as investors fixated on its ~$130B debt, 162% capex surge and
negative $24B FCF — the avatar of a broader anxiety over who funds the $725B+ in 2026
hyperscaler AI capex. Reports of an OpenAI IPO delay amplified it. The rout went
global: SK Hynix -8%, Samsung -5%, Advantest -10%, SMIC -7%.

Crucially, money rotated rather than left. SPX breadth was POSITIVE — 322 advancers
vs 177 decliners — the rare configuration where the cap-weighted index is dragged
down by its largest (tech) members while the average stock rises. Capital found three
homes: software (IGV +4.06%; NOW/SNOW/WDAY/DDOG all +8-10%, fading the "AI eats SaaS"
fear), healthcare (XLV +3.03%, MRNA +12.6%, LLY +7.1%, pharma IHE +3.32%), and
rate-sensitive defensives (Utilities, Real Estate, Staples all green).

The macro hardened the move. Minneapolis Fed's Kashkari flipped his 2026 dot from one
cut to ONE HIKE, citing Hormuz-driven cost-push with inflation above 4%. A hawkish
Warsh Fed lifts the discount rate on exactly the long-duration, capex-front-loaded
AI-hardware names being sold. And oil FELL (WTI -0.50%, USO -2.86%) despite fresh US
strikes on Iran — shipping is rebounding through Hormuz; the market moved from "peak
fear" to "priced in," draining the geopolitical premium that had supported energy.

Bottom line: a concentrated de-rating of AI-hardware duration, not a market break.
RUT +0.07% and the Dow held; T2108 sits at 65.4%; VIX fell to 18.41. But NDX has
flipped to Neutral Bull with negative 15day momentum — the leadership that carried
the tape all year is the leadership now under repair.
