📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The headline number lied. SPX +0.28% reads as a quiet day; underneath it was one of the broadest
participation sessions of the quarter. SPX advancers beat decliners 402 to 98 (4.1:1), a complete
reversal of Monday's 173/324, and new 20day highs jumped from 41 to 145. Equal-weight RSP +1.15%
beat the cap-weighted index by ~0.87pp, Russell 1000 Value +0.89% beat Growth -0.16% by over a full
point, and RUT +1.65% printed 1,485 advancers to 440 with 186 names up 4%+. This is a reverse cap-
weight divergence: the market rallied and megacap growth sat on the index.

The macro chain started overnight in the Gulf. Brent pushed above $110 — a five-month high — after
Houthi attacks on Saudi infrastructure and an IRGC threat against undersea cables in the Strait of
Hormuz, then faded on reports that Qatar and Pakistan had floated a compromise to Washington and
Tehran (Iran publicly denied a deal and said it has a 'new operational plan' if US strikes resume).
Lower oil pulled the long end down — 10yr 5.116% (-1.9bp), 30yr 5.425% (-4.7bp) — while the 2yr rose
2.6bp. Duration-sensitive equities took the bid: homebuilders ITB +2.80% on the same day pending
home sales hit a record-low 66.0, mortgage REITs REM +2.81%, REITs XLRE +1.70%, regional banks KRE
+1.96%. The market bought the rate path, not the housing data.

Healthcare closed at a record and was the session's only ±3% ETF cohort: HLTH +3.28%, BBH +3.17%,
IBB +3.16%, with XLV +2.37% (5day +5.99%). MRNA +6.69% (RS rank 1), RVMD +7.91%, BMY +4.84%, plus
Lilly and Merck pipeline strength. With the 10yr above 5%, healthcare is being bought as the one
growth cohort whose earnings do not depend on AI capex financing. The RS list confirms it
structurally: 11 of the Top 20 are healthcare, 10 of them biotech.

The other side of that trade is the AI infrastructure complex, which was liquidated for a second
straight week. Semi-cap LRCX -3.21%, KLAC -2.80%, AMAT -1.97% (each -12% to -13% over 5day), build-
out names FIX -4.57%, PWR -3.62%, ETN -2.43%, GEV -1.99%, and AI-pivot miner IREN -4.00% (5day
-23.5%). The trigger narrative was financing fatigue — Korean chip IPO Elice -32% on debut — and
Smart Money confirms the exit: Independent Power Producers fell from rank 11 to 56, Electric
Utilities from 39 to 79. Then the after-hours twist: SMCI pre-announced FQ1 revenue 27% above its
guide with higher margins (+15.6% AH) and SpaceX's AI revenue rose 830% (+8.1% AH). The demand side
of AI is not the problem — payback and financing are. Wednesday's open tests whether the hardware
layer (DELL, HPE, CRDO all +5-7% AH) can pull semi-cap off the floor.

Earnings dispersion is the tell for positioning. GE beat and raised EPS guidance to $8.00-8.10 yet
fell 3.67% on free cash flow -9.2% and margin mix; GM beat and fell 6.84% on a narrowed outlook and
cost pressure; UAL cut its 2026 profit outlook on fuel. Meanwhile 3M (+5.78%, second straight guide
raise plus an Amazon data-centre deal), Equifax (+11.33%) and Hasbro (+11.20%) were rewarded for
margin delivery. In a 5% long-yield world the market is paying for margin quality and cash
conversion, not top-line beats.
