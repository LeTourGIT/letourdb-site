📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Tuesday was the purest reporting-season tape: XJO 9,070 (-3.2 pts, flat) sitting on
violent single-stock dispersion. The ASX 200 fell just short of snapping a week-long
losing streak — six straight down sessions — but the story is beneath the surface.
Five of eleven sectors closed green, yet decliners swamped advancers 134 to 58 on the
XJO. A flat close was manufactured by a handful of mega-cap earnings winners; the
median stock had a poor day.

Health Care (XHJ +7.81%) was the engine. CSL +17.25% on its FY2026 result was the
largest single index contributor, joined by Pro Medicus +11.88% and Cochlear +7.58%
— a clean sweep of the healthcare majors beating on the same day. Not rotation; a
coordinated fundamental repricing, and it shows in Smart Money (Healthcare Facilities
+57 ranks to rank 10, Medical Equipment rank 9, Biotech rank 12).

The second headline was corporate: RWC +24.65% after disclosing a fourth, now
formalised, Brookfield approach — A$4.1bn at A$4.75/sh, process deed signed, four-week
exclusivity. The Board pulled its final dividend and RWC's own FY26 numbers (adj EBITDA
-12.8% on tariffs/copper) were softer than the tape — a control-premium re-rate, not a
beat. The standout ASX200 event of the day.

The offset came from rates and resources. Financials (XFJ -1.10%) was the heaviest drag
— an echo of Monday's NAB -4.7% on negative-gearing repeal risk and BEN -5.29% on
results — but the deeper driver is global: the US 30yr yield hit a 19-year high and
rate-sensitive Australian sectors sold in sympathy. Materials (XMJ +0.15%) was a wash:
BHP +2.65% vs SGM -11.04%, MAH -8.59% and LTR -7.31% as lithium's leadership passed to
gold. Gold is the new Smart Money rank 1 (from rank 25); RBA on hold at 4.35%, next
decision 29 September — the pressure on rate-sensitives is imported via the US long end.
