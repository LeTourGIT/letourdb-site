📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday delivered a fresh S&P 500 record — the first close above 7,800 (7,798.99,
+0.65%) — on a clean disinflation catalyst rather than a mega-cap melt-up. July CPI
eased for a second straight month to 3.4% YoY and PPI cooled more than expected to
4.7% YoY (helped by lower energy and food), pushing money-market odds of a September
Fed HIKE down to roughly 40% from ~50%. In a Fed that is debating whether to tighten
further — not cut — 'less-bad inflation' is the risk-on trigger, and it fired straight
into long-duration growth and rate-sensitive assets simultaneously.

The session's dominant story was a software + storage surge paired with an AI-hardware
fade — a genuine intramarket rotation, not a broad tech trade. IGV +3.10% was the only
ETF above +3% today. WDAY +17.78% (its best day on record) ripped on preliminary
Silver Lake take-private talks, HUBS +14.46% on a double earnings beat plus a $1B
buyback, and the momentum spread across MDB +7.87%, PATH +9.31%, TTD +7.93%, TEAM
+6.92%, NET +6.22%, INTU +7.04% and FIG +10.95%. Storage/hardware rode alongside:
SNDK +13.67%, WDC +7.31%, HPQ +6.90%. Against that, the AI-infrastructure complex was
sold: CSCO -8.40% (gross-margin guide), CBRS -11.85%, COHR -7.99%, LITE -5.58%, AAOI
-5.79%, GLW -5.32%, ANET -3.27%, and even AMAT — which BEAT and guided 30%+ equipment
growth — fell ~3% after hours as free cash flow collapsed ~80%. Capital is paying up
for application-layer cash flows and take-private optionality while punishing any blemish
in AI capex names that have already doubled.

Crucially, this was a BROAD advance, not a narrow one. SPX breadth ran positive at
316 advancers vs 183 decliners with 87 new 20day highs against just 10 new lows, T2108
at 66.4% and 68.8% of members above their 20day MA. Russell 2000 confirmed with 1,139
advancers vs 775 and 316 new highs. This is the opposite of the fragile mega-cap ATHs
seen earlier in the cycle — participation is doing the work, which is why no index is
overbought (SPX RSI-15 66.66) even at a record.

The rate-relief signal also reordered the sector board in a rate-sensitive direction:
XLC +2.07% led (NFLX +5.43% on Ackman's stake, plus Meta), XLRE +1.42% and XLP +1.08%
outran XLK +1.01% — long-duration and defensive rate-plays over pure tech beta. The
laggards were the metals complex: XLB -0.51% worst as COPX -3.15%, GDX -2.96% and SIL
-2.88% were dumped and gold slipped ~1.2% off a 10-week high. That metals reversal sits
in direct tension with the 10day Smart Money board, where Mining, Precious Metals and
Integrated Mining still dominate — a classic case of a hot 10day rotation taking a
session-level breather while software takes the baton.

The macro tape was not uniformly benign. Even as front-end hike odds fell, the US sold
30-year bonds at the highest yield in roughly a quarter century — a deficit-driven
long-end warning that keeps NDX volatility 'Elevated' and its regime only Neutral Bull.
Oil kept falling (WTI $81.25, Brent $87.07) on soft demand even as the US-Iran war
grinds on; energy equities held up (XLE +0.05%, 5day +4.99%), a divergence worth
watching. Net: a healthy, broad record close with a clean rotation underneath — but
with long-end yields and an unresolved war as the two live tail risks into next week.
