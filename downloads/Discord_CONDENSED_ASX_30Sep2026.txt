📰 KEY HEADLINES
-------------------------------------------
💵 RBA lifts rates to a 15-year high; banks pass it on
   The RBA's fourth hike of 2026 took the cash rate to 4.60%, and the major banks began passing it to borrowers within 24 hours (ABC Business ran four separate stories — the day's highest-priority theme). The statement leaned on Middle East energy prices running well above the August forecast assumptions. Mortgage stress now becomes a bank credit-quality story, not a NIM story.
🟢 August CPI 4.0% — below consensus; November hike odds collapse
   Headline CPI accelerated to 4.0% from 3.5% but missed the 4.1% forecast, and the trimmed mean rose just 0.2% m/m (3.6% y/y, flat for three months). Swaps cut November hike odds to ~20-25%. This was the trigger for XRE +3.68% — and the single data point Q3 CPI must now confirm.
🔴 US 30-year yield hits highest level since 2002
   CNBC and MarketWatch both led with the long end ('yields beat doves'): the 30-year scaled a 24-year high before easing, even as the Fed's Williams signalled openness to a pause. Hedge funds hold a record share of the Treasury market — a crowding risk. For the ASX, US duration is the ceiling on the rate-sensitive rally.
🛢️ Oil: Trump denies Iran sanctions relief; Qatar pushes peace talks
   Brent slid to ~$96 after the US ordered another SPR release and Saudi exports via the East-West pipeline recovered to about half capacity. Diplomacy remains unresolved, so the risk premium is volatile both ways. XEJ still rose +1.56% — energy equities had already discounted the flush.
🟢 China factory activity snaps a two-month contraction
   September's China PMI returned to expansion and Dalian iron ore rose ~1.3% in response. Combined with the US-China tariff cuts, it gives BHP/RIO/FMG a demand floor that the 7d iron ore price ($96.73) has yet to reflect.
