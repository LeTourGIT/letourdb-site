📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The XJO's +0.02% close at 8,795.2 is the most misleading print of the month.
Underneath it ran a 6.4-point sector spread — Consumer Discretionary +2.57%
and Financials +2.21% against Materials -3.85% — and a 2.5-point gap between
large caps (XTO +0.22%) and small caps (XSO -2.29%). Capital did not leave
the market; it left the resource tape and moved into bank and retail balance
sheets.

Resources led — downward. Gold slid to a three-month low of US$4,003 as
higher oil revived inflation and rate fears, and the gold miners' September
quarterlies landed straight into the rout: OBM -16.54% (08:16, price
sensitive), BC8 -15.83% (08:17), SRL -13.68% (09:06), ALK -10.88% (08:23) and
NEM -4.93% after its Q3 release (08:21). Where the quarter disappointed the
market showed no patience at a falling gold price — the margin cushion that
excused cost blowouts at ~US$4,280 a month ago is gone at US$4,000. The majors added
weight: BHP -3.64%, RIO -2.68% (despite its $1.9b Kangwinan bauxite
commitment), FMG -2.06%, S32 -5.28%, with iron ore soft at US$90.73 (20day
-6.6%). Lithium (PLS -8.07%, IGO -7.38%, LTR -7.25%) fell with no
company news at all — that is de-risking, not information. XMJ 5day -7.69%
and 20day -11.12% says this is a trend liquidation, not a one-day shake.

The money went to the banks and the shopping centre. CBA +4.35% to $164,
NAB +3.25% and WBC +3.11% all printed new 20day highs, riding the 10bp drop
in the AU 10yr and the market's view that a November hike widens NIM
faster than it lifts bad debts. Retail ran harder: TPW +33.42% on its AGM
strategy update (08:19, price sensitive), NCK +5.15%, WES +3.76%, HVN
+3.22%. Rallying the rate-sensitive consumer 11 days before a likely hike
is the market saying the hike is priced and the consumer is holding up
— EVT's "strong Q1" and ABB's trading update back that read.

ZIP -29.47% was the institutional casualty: its 1Q FY27 update (08:22, price
sensitive) disclosed a US fraud incident that hit volumes and new-customer
growth and cost $10.1m in cash opex (per ABC); Citi flagged onboarding
controls. A control failure is a multiple problem, not a one-quarter problem.

Breadth is split down the middle: the XJO logged 59 new 20day highs AND 59
new 20day lows, and 40 constituents fell more than 4% against just 3 that
rose 4%. That is a rotation market, not a risk-off market — but small caps
are paying for it. Next week: Q3 CPI (the RBA decider), Champion Iron
results 29/10, six trading halts to resolve, and post-close PMV annual
report plus IMM ESMO data as Monday catalysts.
