📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Monday 17 August was an earnings-driven large-cap sell-down: the XJO eased
−0.46% to 9,073.2 and the All Ords −0.37% to 9,279.0, but the damage was
concentrated in a handful of poorly-received full-year results rather than any
broad de-risking. The tell is the Small Ords finishing +0.49% — a clean cap
divergence that mirrored Friday's US session, where weak retail sales pushed
money into the Russell 2000 (+0.51%) over mega-caps.

JB Hi-Fi (JBH −12.31%) was the session. The FY26 numbers were fine; a cautious
outlook on discretionary spend did the damage, and it dragged the whole
Consumer Discretionary sector (XDJ −3.00%, worst on the board) with it — SUL
−6.64% and PMV −4.71% fell in sympathy ahead of their own results. Lendlease
(LLC −11.15%), IRESS (IRE −11.63%) and Aurizon (AZJ −10.34%) rounded out a
brutal large-cap results slate. AZJ fell despite winning a major Queensland
coal-haulage contract — the market focused on the earnings miss.

Financials (XFJ −1.76%) were the heaviest index drag by weight. NAB −4.62% on
a soft Q3 trading update set the tone and SUN −5.08% followed. With the banks
and discretionary both down, the large-cap benchmark had little chance of
holding green regardless of what resources did.

And resources did plenty. Materials (XMJ +1.76%) topped the board on a broad
gold-miner rally — EVN +5.15%, GMD +4.46%, CMM +4.30%, VAU +3.72% — with copper
firm (+1.82% LME). Gold jumped to Smart Money rank 2 (from 34 nine days ago)
and six of the ten ASX200/300 RS leaders are now gold names: this is a
precious-metals re-rating with real breadth. Healthcare's index was red
(−0.85%) but masked huge single-stock wins — ACL +14.89% and HLS +13.75% on
results, MSB +10.86% on a Phase 3 trial milestone.

Structurally the large-cap trend is intact: XJO +1.91% above its 50MA, T2108 at
66.5%, and 20day highs (22) still beating lows (17). Today reads as earnings
dispersion inside an uptrend, not the start of a reversal. The real test is
tomorrow — BHP, CSL, COH, Pro Medicus, Challenger and Sims all report tomorrow (18/8) -- the mega-cap results wave begins.
