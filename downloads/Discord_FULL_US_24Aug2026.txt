📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Monday opened the week with a chip-led decline that never became a risk-off event. The
S&P slipped -0.28% and the Nasdaq 100 -0.97%, but the Dow closed GREEN (+0.26%) and the median
S&P constituent advanced — 301 up versus 196 down. That divergence is the whole story:
capital did not leave equities, it rotated out of one crowded corner and into everything
else. The crowded corner was semiconductors, and the reason is two sessions away.

Nvidia reports Q2 FY27 after the close on Wednesday. Consensus is ~$91.9B revenue and
~$2.08 EPS against management's own $91.0B guide — a bar set high enough that the market
is unwilling to hold max semiconductor beta into it. XSD (semi equipment/design) was the
ONLY ETF beyond ±3% today at -3.34%; SOXX -2.67% confirmed the whole complex de-rated in
unison. The single-stock damage reads like a pre-earnings inventory purge: MU -5.83%,
WDC -5.24%, SNDK -6.45%, SMCI -5.56%, COHR -4.85%, AAOI -13.77%, CIEN -6.02%. Memory,
storage, optical and hardware — the highest-torque NVDA derivatives — took the most pain.
This is positioning risk being taken OFF, not a thesis being abandoned.

What absorbed the flow tells you the market's defensive reflex is intact and selective.
Consumer Staples (XLP +1.70%) and Financials (XLF +1.29%) led; Utilities (+1.05%) and
even Healthcare (+0.05%) held green. Payments were a standout — Visa +3.06% and Mastercard
+3.31% both cleared +3% on heavy turnover ($3.2B and $1.9B) — a clean rotation into
cash-flow compounders with zero AI-capex sensitivity. The consumer complex was broadly
bid: WMT +2.69%, TGT +2.69%, COST +2.50%, CMG +3.04%, DIS +2.63%, EXPE +5.44%, FIVE +4.99%.
This is textbook late-summer rotation: crowded growth funds a defensive-and-value barbell.

The one part of "risk" that did NOT sell was crypto — and that decoupling is the session's
most important tell. IBIT +2.20% and ETHA +2.30% rose straight through the chip selloff;
BitMine (BMNR) led the entire gainers board +5.74% on $1.6B turnover, MSTR +2.83%, and
BTCT posted +52.63% on the day inside the RS Top 20. MarketWatch flags Bitcoin closing on
$80,000, having beaten both stocks and gold over six months. Smart Money confirms it:
Blockchain & Cryptocurrency SURGED to rank 5 (from rank 116 nine days ago). When digital
assets rally on a day semis are dumped, the market is telling you this is a rotation
within risk, not a flight from it.

The geopolitical overlay is Iran. Bessent is readying a global anti-Iran sanctions push
(China not exempt), Iran is warning of Hormuz ship seizures, and global shipping rates
are soaring. Yet spot crude FADED — WTI -0.82% to $84.93, Brent -1.18% — the classic
"panic to pricing-in" fade the CNBC desk called out. The tradable signal isn't in oil,
it's in shipping: Marine Port Services rank 1 in Smart Money (ROC +123.24, up from rank
136 nine days ago) and Marine Freight & Logistics climbing to rank 9. Rates, not barrels,
are where the Hormuz premium is being expressed. Gold is the other beneficiary — GLD
+0.79%, a "massive" options trade flagged by CNBC, and Deutsche Bank telling clients to
buy gold on the Treasury-market intervention theme. Gold sits rank 7 in Smart Money.
