📝 SESSION SUMMARY
-----------------------------------------------------
Monday 6 July was a quiet-looking session that wasn't quiet underneath:
XJO slipped -0.15% to 8,831.0 and XAO -0.12% to 9,037.0, but decliners
outnumbered advancers 112 to 74 (0.66:1). A flat index built on negative
breadth is a distribution signal — index-level calm masking rotation.

The session's headline act was gold M&A. Genesis Minerals (GMD) lobbed an
unsolicited binding A$5.6bn bid for Vault Minerals (VAU) — 0.7629 GMD
scrip + 47.5c cash per share — which Vault's board declared a Superior
Proposal over the existing Regis Resources (RRL) merger. VAU surged
+11.62% to A$5.09 [H]; GMD fell -4.13% on scrip dilution; RRL rose on its
own strong June quarter and now has until 10 July to match. This is the
latest in an accelerating ASX gold consolidation wave (NST/De Grey,
Gold Fields/Gold Road) as record bullion prices fund production land-grabs.

Away from gold, the tape was defensive and US-shaped. Healthcare (XHJ
+1.50%) led on CU6 +19% (Clarity Pharma), plus CSL, COH and PME all
printing fresh 20day highs — a classic risk-management bid. IT (XIJ
+0.93%) was propped by WTC +7.31% rebounding off its recent A$30 sell-off,
even as Weebit Nano (WBT -5.84%) tracked the US chip slump. Materials
(-0.63%) and Utilities (-0.79%) lagged: iron ore has slid to ~US$98/t
(-5.3% over the month, sub-US$100), pressuring BHP/RIO/FMG, while bond-
proxy utilities keep de-rating (-4.05% over 5day) as the long end stays firm.

Macro backdrop: the RBA held at 4.35% in June after three hikes this year
and next meets 11 August — outside the report window, but the still-
restrictive setting is the structural weight on rate-sensitive REITs,
staples and utilities. AUD/USD eased to 0.6933 (-0.14%); a softer Aussie
is a modest tailwind for USD-earning miners, partly cushioning weak iron ore.
