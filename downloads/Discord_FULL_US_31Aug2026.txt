📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Two macro forces collided into the August close and both point the same way:
higher rates and higher oil. Fed Chair Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole tone has
markets now pricing better-than 80% odds of an actual September rate HIKE — not
a pause, a hike — while renewed Middle East attacks pushed WTI +1.42% to $86.30
and USO +4.09%. Rising crude feeding into a hawkish Fed is the textbook
stagflation-lite cocktail, and the tape traded exactly to that script:
rate-sensitive and oil-consuming sectors were sold, real assets and energy were
bought, and the long-duration growth complex was spared only at the very top.

Energy was the unambiguous winner. XLE +2.04% was the strongest sector by a
wide margin, backed by OIH +2.60% (oil services) and CRAK +2.03% (refiners) —
the whole crude value chain repricing higher together. SLB +4.83%, LNG +3.32%
and Cheniere-style LNG names led; XLE's 20day is now +8.79%, confirming this is
a multi-week structural bid, not a one-day headline pop. The mirror image was
the airlines and cruise lines: JETS -2.33%, RCL -3.82%, CCL -3.51% — every
oil-input consumer got repriced as a cost-of-fuel loser in the same move.

The day's dominant single-stock story was the California utility rout. Edison
International EIX -23.07% and PG&E PCG -20.06% collapsed on heavy turnover
($1.3B and $2.0B), and crucially both are down roughly 27% on the 5day — this
is an escalating wildfire-liability de-rating that accelerated Monday, not a
one-session shock. The pair dragged XLU -1.17% and reintroduced tail-risk
pricing into the whole regulated-utility complex. Treat it as an
idiosyncratic California liability event compounding the rate-driven selloff in
rate-sensitive defensives, not a broad utility fundamentals story.

Underneath the red tape, capital was rotating hard into crypto and real-asset
inflation hedges. Circle CRCL +9.65%, Coinbase COIN +5.31%, MSTR +4.42%, BMNR
+6.39% and IREN +4.70% led a broad digital-asset bid (IBIT +1.34%, ETHA
+1.67%), and this is corroborated three ways: Blockchain & Cryptocurrency is
the number-one 10day Hot Industry, Smart Money ranks it 4th (up from 9day rank
128), and Misc Fintech Infrastructure sits at Smart Money rank 1 (ROC +43.36).
Alongside crypto, Coal, Gold and Specialty Mining all climbed the Smart Money
board — the classic inflation-hedge rotation you would expect with oil bid and
a hawkish Fed. RS leadership, by contrast, is dominated by biotech (roughly
half the top 20), the defensive-growth corner that outperforms when the cycle
signal turns defensive.

Breadth confirmed rather than diverged: SPX advancers 136 vs decliners 359 on a
down day is internally consistent, but the magnitude is a warning — RUT ran
588 advancers against 1,322 decliners (better than 1:2 negative) and SPX new
20day lows (128) swamped new highs (22). T2108 at 46.2% and only 38.8% of SPX
names above their 20MA say participation has thinned to the point where the
index level is being propped by a shrinking cohort of mega-caps. There were no
ETF moves beyond ±3% in either direction — selling was broad but shallow, and
VIX at 14.92 (+3.47%) is rising off a low base without pricing panic.
