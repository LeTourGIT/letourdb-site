📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday was a bond-market story in an equity costume. Wednesday, Treasury Sec
Bessent announced he'd at least double long-dated buyback operations (cap $2B to
>=$4B, running Sept 9 to Nov 4); the 30Y fell ~10bps to ~5.18% and stocks rallied.
By Thursday the whole move unwound — 30Y back to ~5.25%, near a 19-year high, and
the Dow shed ~700pts. The market's verdict: the buybacks don't even begin until
Sept 9, they reshuffle the maturity schedule rather than pay down debt, and total
public debt just crossed $40 trillion. JPMorgan: the intervention 'belies the
underlying structural challenges.'

The rotation underneath was textbook fiscal-debasement positioning. Every clearly-
green pocket was a hard asset: Energy (XLE +0.27%) on WTI +2.3%, gold miners
(AngloGold +6.1%) with bullion at $4,575, and crypto in a class of its own —
Bitcoin (IBIT +6.24%) and Ether (ETHA +10.5%, EETH +10.6%) were the day's ONLY
ETFs above +3%, while Blockchain surged +117 ranks in the Smart Money model. When
investors doubt the state's grip on its own borrowing costs, they buy what can't
be printed. Fiscal fear -> yield spike -> hard-asset bid ran through every section.

The losers were the mirror image: anything priced for perfection at a high multiple.
Walmart beat EPS and raised guidance yet fell 9.2% — a ~40x retailer whose US comps
(+2.6%) missed, meeting a tape unwilling to pay premium multiples as the discount
rate rises. High-growth software (CrowdStrike -5.6%, MongoDB -4.6%, Cloudflare
-4.4%) and long-duration medtech (Intuitive -5.8%, Boston Scientific -5.1%) took the
same medicine. Defense was a separate, cleaner signal: ITA -3.5% and PPA -3.2% led
ETF decliners with AXON -4.9% and RKLB -3.8%, as Ukraine ceasefire diplomacy (Trump
envoys in Cairo, Zelenskyy facing wartime-election pressure) bled the war premium out.

Moderna was the spectacle — down 23.6% after Wednesday's record +177% on the first-
ever Phase 3 mRNA cancer-vaccine win (intismeran + Merck's Keytruda, melanoma). That
is a valuation reset and short-squeeze unwind (12.5% short float), not a broken
thesis, and it is fuelling a broader biotech bid: 9 of the top 20 RS names are now
biotech/healthcare — a genuine leadership rotation off the spring's semis-and-software
regime. Deere (+6.9%) gave the one constructive earnings note, management calling
2026 the bottom of the farm-equipment downcycle.
