📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The session was set by oil, not equities. Brent ripped +10.06% to $107.00 while WTI managed only +3.73% to $94.71 — the Brent-WTI spread roughly doubled in one session, from ~$5.90 to ~$12.30. That split is the key read: this is a seaborne, Gulf-supply shock, not a US-supply shock. The market is paying up for barrels that can reach Asia and Europe without transiting Hormuz, after reports of fresh disruption to Saudi Arabia's Hormuz-bypass export route. Bloomberg reports of a phased US-Iran deal to reopen the Strait capped it only briefly; Brent settled above $106.

The transmission channel into equities is rates. The Fed hiked eight days ago for the first time since 2023 (3.75-4.00%), and the 10-year Treasury crossed 5% on 14/09. An oil spike now feeds straight into inflation expectations and the bond market — CNBC's front page paired 'surging yields' with 'bond liquidation wrecks small caps'. Everything long-duration or capital-hungry was sold: XLU -0.98% (5day -5.59%), XLRE -0.45% (5day -3.00%), REM -2.07%, TAN -3.51%, ICLN -2.58%, URNM -2.94%. SPLV (low-volatility) is -6.29% over 20day — bond proxies offer no shelter when the bond itself is the problem.

The index print hides the damage. SPX closed -0.02% with 173 advancers vs 326 decliners, and RSP (equal-weight) fell -0.50% vs SPY -0.02%. META +4.50% on $26.9B of turnover — by far the largest in the Gainers/Losers universe — plus Alphabet strength did the cap-weighted heavy lifting. Underneath: only 25.4% of SPX members sit above their own 50MA while the index itself is +0.94% above its 50MA, T2108 is 22.4% (washout zone starts below 20%), and new 20day lows (155) outnumber highs (25) six to one. New lows have nearly doubled from 87 on 17/09.

Inside tech, the AI trade rotated layers rather than broke. Memory/storage and AI-infrastructure spend were sold — SNDK -3.47% on $14.3B turnover, WDC -4.94%, ORCL -3.47% after declaring force majeure over setbacks at a New Mexico data-centre project — while design IP, foundry and enterprise storage were bought: CDNS +4.18%, INTC +3.91% ($15.1B turnover, 5day +17.09%), and Everpure +11.15% after guiding FY28 revenue to $7.0-7.3B (+39-45%) vs a $6.37B consensus, days after its S&P 500 inclusion. SOXX flat (+0.06%) after a +9.05% 5day run is consolidation, not distribution.

Healthcare delivered the most coherent institutional bid of the session: ILMN +7.25%, TEM +7.38%, MRNA +6.98% (5day +23.25%), NTRA +4.61%, A +4.55%, RVMD +4.78%. HLTH +2.97% missed the +3% flag by three basis points; BBH +1.49%, XLV +0.63%. Ten of the RS Top 20 are healthcare, led by a genomics/precision-diagnostics cluster (TXG, TWST, CDNA, PSNL, with GRAL, ADPT, NTRA in rank 21-30). This is where growth capital hides when rates shut duration trades: domestic revenue, no oil sensitivity, self-generated catalysts.

Corporate news punished leverage. MGM -10.99% after People Inc withdrew its $18B ($48.30/share) proposal — the deal floor is gone. Gen Digital -12.05% (5day -23.63%) on an FT-reported all-cash approach for GoDaddy (~$12.2B; GDDY +4.60%) — with the 10-year near 5%, debt-funded acquirers get the penalty, not the premium. FDX -4.10% and UPS -3.93% absorbed record diesel directly into margins; IYT is -9.52% over 20day while DJI sits in a Bearish regime — a textbook Dow Theory non-confirmation of the NDX highs.
