📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday was a semiconductor rebound bolted onto a deteriorating tape. The S&P
closed +0.17% and the Nasdaq 100 +0.67% — but the average stock fell hard. SPX
advancers 155 vs 344 decliners (better than 1:2 negative), Russell
internals 688/1220, T2108 at just 23.6% and only 19.0% of SPX
names above their 20-day MA. This is a textbook cap-weight illusion: a handful of
mega-cap chips are mathematically holding the index green while breadth implodes.

The macro spine is the 16 September FOMC. The Fed hiked 25bp to 3.75-4.00% — its
first hike since 2023, a unanimous 12-0 vote, with the dot-plot signalling at
least one more this year. The 10-year yield pushed to ~5%, its highest since 2007.
Rising rates are repricing the entire value/rate-sensitive complex: utilities
-1.42%, materials -1.42%, homebuilders (LEN -4.10% on earnings), and
steel (NUE -6.32%, STLD -4.11%, NUCOR the Dow drag) all sold. That is why RUT and
DJI have flipped Bearish while NDX clings to Bullish — duration-insensitive
mega-cap tech is the only place hiding from the yield move.

The day's leadership was the memory/storage complex, and the catalyst is concrete,
not sentiment. SanDisk (+10.99%) led the entire market on $31.8B turnover — the
session's single largest — ahead of its S&P 100 inclusion effective 21 September
(alongside Dell, Palo Alto, Arista), a forced-buying event for index funds. Layered
on top: spot NAND prices up ~5% on the week as Nvidia's Vera-Rubin ramp tightens
supply, plus SanDisk's Investor Day 80% gross-margin / $93.9B contracted-backlog
thesis. WDC, STX, MU, MRVL and LITE rode the same tide in sympathy. Critically,
semiconductor EQUIPMENT joined hard — LRCX +6.98%, AMAT +6.51%, TER +5.19%, ONTO
+5.36% — but all with NEGATIVE 5-day numbers. This was a violent mean-reversion
bounce off an oversold semicap group, not fresh accumulation. QCOM -5.82% is the
tell: this was a memory/equipment/optical bid, not an all-chips bid.

Smart Money confirms the rotation was pre-positioned. Semiconductor Equipment
rocketed to rank 4 from 9-day rank 112 (+108), and Semiconductors to rank 8 from
101 (+93) — two of the most violent rank surges on the board. Institutions were
building the chip position for over a week; Friday's tape was the payoff. Yet the
structural RS leadership tells a different, quieter story: the RS Top 25 is
dominated by biotech/pharma and medical names (TEVA, MRNA, CYPH, IOVA, TWST, NEO)
— defensive growth is the multi-month leader, and today's chip movers are absent
from it. Two clocks running at once: a defensive structural core, a cyclical bounce.

Geopolitics cut the other way for energy. Iran halted strikes on Israel and Trump
signalled the war is 'nearing an end' — WTI crashed -5.63% to $95.47, Brent -4.96%.
The war-risk premium is bleeding out of crude even as the Fed hikes ON that same
energy-inflation. Crypto ripped on the risk-on tone (VIX -4.08% to 14.81): ETHA
+7.85%, IBIT +6.28%, MSTR +16.39%, COIN +11.66% — Blockchain jumped to Smart Money
rank 3. The bid is real but concentrated; the median stock did not participate.
