📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Tuesday 7 July saw the XJO ease −0.31% to 8,803.9 — a third
soft session in four as a resources rout overwhelmed a genuine
bid in Tech and Financials. Decliners beat advancers 122 to 69
on the XJO (0.57:1) — breadth was materially weaker than the
headline −0.31% suggests, a classic index-masking-internals tape.

Materials (XMJ −2.64%) was the wrecking ball. Liontown (LTR
[ASX200]) −7.55% led a broad lithium/gold/uranium liquidation —
Boss Energy −8.86%, Deep Yellow −6.87%, Galan −6.76%, Wildcat
−6.73%. The driver is macro, not stock-specific: a hawkish Fed
pivot under Kevin Warsh has repriced the USD sharply higher,
and a strong USD mechanically compresses every USD-denominated
commodity at once. Iron ore near US$97/t (Simandou supply +
soft China steel) removes the one offset the complex had.

The other side of the rotation was orderly and institutional.
Info Tech (XIJ +2.01%) led every sector — WiseTech [ASX50]
+5.65% (despite chair Richard White stepping down), NextDC
[ASX100] +3.6% and CAR Group [ASX50] +2.99% — the hyperscaler/
cloud-capex thesis intact. Financials (XFJ +1.25%) firmed with
Netwealth [ASX200] +6.73%; a higher-for-longer curve modestly
improves bank NIMs. This is textbook new-financial-year rotation
out of the resource complex into growth + quality.

Under the surface, Smart Money is decisively domestic-defensive:
Homebuilding surged to #1 (from #61) and Insurance Funds to #3
(from #77) — capital rotating toward rate-and-consumer plays
insulated from the USD/commodity axis. Healthcare (XHJ +5.2%
over 5day) remains the standout medium-term sector even after a
flat session. AUD at 0.6945 (−0.21%) near multi-month lows is
the tell: this is a USD-strength story, and Australia is on the
wrong side of it for its biggest export earners.
