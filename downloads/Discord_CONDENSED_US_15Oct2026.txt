📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
A broad rally sat on top of a flat index. 75% of SPX members advanced and RUT
went 1,467/470 a day after a 1:4.7 washout, yet NDX closed red as NVDA
(-1.84%, $27.5B), DELL (-6.80%) and HPE (-7.10%) were sold. Hot PPI raised
hike risk, but the 10-year yield dived on AI-credit fears (CoreWeave CDS at
distress). When bonds rally through hot inflation, they are pricing a growth
or credit scare.

Falling yields drove everything else: housing (ITB +4.03%, HD +4.31%),
application software (NOW +6.14%, INTU +6.36%), credit bureaus (TRU +10.73%)
and gold miners (GDX +4.34%). Bank prints (BLK +4.38%, BNY +3.23%) lifted the
Dow. Energy fell -1.58% despite WTI +2.33%: equities read higher crude as a
demand tax.

Inside tech the split was surgical: the AI capex layer (AMAT -3.72%, LRCX
-3.30%, ANET -5.04%, SMTC -10.01%) was sold while AI applications re-rated
higher (FICO +6.99%, APP +6.52%, ADSK +6.07%). Capital is leaving AI capex,
not AI. Smart Money confirms it: nine of the Top 16 industries are consumer,
and the AI build-out complex (Semi Equipment, Construction & Engineering) is
exiting in rank.
