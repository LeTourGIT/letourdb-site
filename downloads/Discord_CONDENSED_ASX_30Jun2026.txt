SESSION SUMMARY
The ASX200 closed FY2026 down 0.51% to 8,778.7 — a soft full stop on a year that still
delivered +2.8%. The session's character was set entirely by the gold complex: bullion
broke below US$4,000/oz overnight (first sub-4k print since November 2025) as sticky US
inflation drove the USD to multi-month highs. Because gold miners carry high fixed-cost
operating leverage, a ~6% AUD-gold move translated into double-digit equity drawdowns in
the higher-cost producers. Gold names were 11 of the 15 biggest ASX200 fallers — NST -5.82%
(ASX20), PRU -6.98%, RRL -6.94%, VAU -7.13%, RSG -8.25%, OBM -6.22%. Materials (XMJ -1.6%)
led the market lower and Real Estate (XRE -2.22%) was worst as firmer USD/yields hit long-
duration names.

Crucially this was a rotation, not a liquidation. Financials (XFJ +0.42%) led as the day's
refuge, absorbing capital exiting gold; Utilities (+0.39%), Energy (+0.38%) and IT (+0.35%,
on the Nasdaq +2.07% tailwind) also finished green. Megaport (MP1) +6.36% on an institutional
raise, Neuren (NEU) +6.93% and Electro Optic (EOS) +6.63% on defence demand show risk
appetite was alive — just not in resources. Breadth was negative (XJO 82/111) but the index
lost only half a percent: the rout was deep but narrow. The structural read into the new
financial year is the XTO-above-all-MAs / XSO-below-all-MAs split — large-caps intact, small-
caps (-6% vs 200MA) the weak link.
