📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Wednesday 5 August saw the XJO print a new intraday record high and close
+0.90% at 9,227.8 — a session imported almost wholesale from Wall Street, where
the S&P 500 (+1.79%) and Dow (above 54,000) set fresh records on an AI-led rebound.
Materials (XMJ +3.56%) and IT (XIJ +2.51%) led; Energy (XEJ -2.22%) was the drag.

The Materials leadership was gold, not iron ore. Gold +1.18% (silver +1.65%) and the
Diggers & Dealers forum in Kalgoorlie drove a clean sweep of gold miners up the movers
board — SBM +10.31%, MEK +14.13%, RXL +13.70%, SX2 +13.23%, SMI +11.76%. Iron ore,
by contrast, sits soft near US$94/t. NEU +16.28% was the standout single name on a
record DAYBUE quarter and CY26 royalty upgrade — a fundamental re-rating carrying
Health Care (XHJ +1.70%). AI1 +14.29% (graphene milestone) and SDV's data-centre wins
kept the IT theme alive alongside the Nasdaq's overnight surge.

Energy was the mirror image: US signalling the Strait of Hormuz is 'free and open' and
an Iran deal 'close' repriced the oil risk premium lower, softening Brent toward ~US$79
and dragging WDS -3.55%, STO -2.19%, KAR -2.87% and BPT -2.73% (results due tomorrow).
Utilities (XUJ -1.62%) fell with the bond-proxy unwind on the risk-on tape.

Breadth confirmed the advance rather than flagging it: XJO advancers led decliners
145/46 (3.15:1), 24 stocks rose +4% with none down -4%, and new 20day highs swamped
lows 76 to 1. The one caution sits under the surface — only 105 of 200 XJO names (53%)
are above their 200MA, and the Small Ords index is still Bearish. This is a record set
by the top of the market, with the RBA decision on 11 August the next real test.
