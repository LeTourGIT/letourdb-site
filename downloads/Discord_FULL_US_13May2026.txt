📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Wednesday's session was a paradox: SPX and NDX printed fresh all-time highs while being systematically sold beneath the surface. The catalyst pairing was extraordinary — a double-barrelled inflation shock (CPI +3.8% YoY Tuesday, PPI +6.0% YoY Wednesday — the latter its worst print since 2022) sent the 10Y Treasury to 4.47%, its highest since July 2025, and pushed the 20Y and 30Y back above 5%. In any other market environment, that macro backdrop strangles equity multiples. Instead, SPX rallied +0.58% and NDX +1.20%. The mechanism is not irrational — it is selective. Markets are front-running a geopolitical resolution narrative: President Trump arrived in Beijing for a state visit with President Xi, with NVDA CEO Jensen Huang joining the delegation. The Hormuz-plus-China dynamic means the semi supply chain constraint and the AI export restriction story are now simultaneously in motion. Markets are pricing the resolution premium, not the current inflation data.

The earnings side amplified the bifurcation. BABA delivered cloud intelligence revenue of $6.13 billion (+38% YoY) — the most critical metric in the China AI story — alongside a surprise $2.5 billion dividend and a commitment to exceed its $55 billion three-year AI investment plan. That combination (cloud beats + shareholder returns + AI capex confirmation) sent the stock up 6.5-8.7% during the session, providing an anchor for the broader market's advance through the inflation headwinds. Nebius Group added a complementary data point: AI infrastructure revenue at $399 million (eightfold year-over-year growth), a stunning acceleration validating the hyperscale demand thesis that NVDA is expected to confirm next Wednesday (May 20).

The Semi complex continued its institutional accumulation despite the inflation macro overhang. AAOI +18.49%, ATOM +18.44%, WOLF +16.53%, QUIK +14.59%, LWLG +14.34%, and TSEM +22.61% dominated the daily leaderboard. The 60day industry ROC for Semiconductors sits at +64.30% — the single most dominant structural ROC reading in the dashboard — confirming this is not a news-cycle trade but institutional repositioning in a multi-quarter capex cycle. The Trump-Jensen Huang China visit is a direct semi/AI export catalyst; the market is pricing a reduction of Semiconductor Export Control overhang as part of the diplomatic rapprochement.

The inflation data created real damage where it should: Financials (XLF -1.14%), Utilities (XLU -1.15%), and Real Estate (XLRE -0.83%) led declines as the rate-sensitive sectors repriced the "higher-for-longer" Fed stance. Kevin Warsh inherits the most divided FOMC in over three decades with rate cuts now priced out of 2026 entirely. The 10Y at 4.47% is not yet a structural threat to equity multiples at current earnings growth rates — but 5% 30Y bonds are a genuine alternative to equities for institutional allocators, and that transition risk is what the breadth divergence is signalling.
