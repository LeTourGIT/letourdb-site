If MSFT/GOOGL steady the leaders Wednesday, participation is there for a recovery.

Next week is the densest risk cluster of the quarter: durable goods and consumer confidence Tuesday, FOMC Wednesday 2pm ET with MSFT and GOOGL after the close, Q3 GDP and September core PCE Thursday, ECI Friday — then the 3 November midterms with strike reports already attached.
