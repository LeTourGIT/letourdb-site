The result was a textbook sell-the-news: CBA -0.69%, Financials (XFJ -0.59%) the heaviest large-cap weight, and only two of eleven sectors green. The regime label still reads Bullish, but the tape underneath was defensive.

Utilities (XUJ +2.64%) was the session's only genuine bid, carried by AGL +5.95% on its FY26 result and FY27 guidance (08:26, price sensitive). Everything else leaned red -- a rotation into the one sector with a fresh positive catalyst while the market digested CBA and a hawkish RBA.

SEK -14.31% (FY26 miss) and PMV -11.01% (soft Premier Retail update) anchored the losers, dragging Comm Services and a retail cohort (SUL -5.48%, TPW -5.99%) with them. Breadth flatly contradicted the Bullish label: XJO decliners led advancers 129 to 69 (0.5:1). New 20day highs (30) still beat lows (12) and 61% of XJO names hold their 200MA, so the damage is shallow -- but a sub-1:1 advance/decline line on a red day is the kind of non-confirmation worth logging. FleetPartners +5.94% on takeover offers, SEG's halt-then-MediaWorks acquisition, and Growthpoint's $268m asset sale kept M&A alive beneath the surface.
