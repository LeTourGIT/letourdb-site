📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Tuesday was the hangover from Monday's Jensen Huang reinstatement party.
Nvidia's board reinstated Huang as CEO on Monday 23/11 — less than two weeks
after he resigned — and NVDA ripped more than 30% in its best session since
2003, dragging SPX from 6,910 to 7,237 on 416 advancers. Today the market
asked the harder question: does a returning CEO change the AI hardware cycle?
The answer from the tape was no. NVDA gave back -5.86% on $60.0B of turnover
(the largest dollar-volume print in the 16d universe by a factor of 2.4x),
AMD -8.86%, MU -11.01%, AVGO -3.98%.

The trigger for the second leg was Analog Devices. ADI beat cleanly — Q4
EPS $4.06 vs $3.89 consensus on $4.37B revenue (+42% YoY) — and still fell
-17.37%. The market looked straight through the
quarter to fiscal 2027: Q1 guide implies a sequential slowdown, holiday fab
shutdowns pinch margins, and capex more than doubles for capacity that does
not open until 2028. That is the critical read for traders: when a record
beat gets punished 17%, positioning — not fundamentals — is setting price.
The contagion map was precise. Analog peers TXN -11.25%, MPWR -10.77%, SMTC
-16.97%; AI-connectivity names ALAB -13.88%, CRDO -13.07%, LITE -10.52%, CIEN
-9.62%; AI hardware HPE -16.31%, DELL -11.83% (into its print), SNDK -11.13%;
AI neocloud CRWV -11.85%. SOXX -8.27% and XSD -7.84% both flag ⚠ EXTREME.

The money did not leave the market — it changed address. SPX printed 344
advancers vs 155 decliners (2.2:1 POSITIVE) while the index fell -0.50%.
This is an INVERSE breadth divergence: cap-weight concentration in semis is
dragging the index while the median stock rallies. RSP (equal-weight) +0.54%
vs IVV -0.48% = a 1.02-point equal-weight beat; RPV (pure value) +0.79% vs
RPG (pure growth) -2.59% = a 3.38-point value-over-growth spread. Healthcare
was the receiving account: IBB +5.17%, HLTH +4.66%, BBH +4.46%, XLV +2.95%,
led by INSM +14.13% after Sanofi agreed to buy it in pharma's biggest deal in
three years. Retail earnings supplied the second leg — ANF +19.69%, KSS
+19.48%, DKS +14.59%, DLTR +11.18%, TGT +4.68% — confirming the consumer is
spending into the holidays even as the AI trade wobbles.

Macro layered a geopolitical premium onto energy and stripped it from
metals. Iran said three tankers struck mines in Hormuz despite a US escort,
Houthis claimed strikes on a Saudi refinery, and Treasury widened sanctions:
WTI +2.65% to $81.70, Brent +2.69% to $88.46. Yet gold fell -2.62% and silver
-4.37% — a liquidation signature, not a safe-haven bid. When oil rises on war
headlines and gold falls, the metals trade is being used as a funding source
for losses elsewhere. VIX eased to 26.36 (-0.98%) but its 5day +3.53% keeps
the volatility regime Elevated. Treasury yields were steady ahead of the key
inflation report.

The structural read: AI hardware has moved from a momentum trade to a
capex-credibility trade. Semiconductors fell from rank 13 to rank 118 in
three sessions — ↓↓ EXIT velocity, not profit-taking. Until SOXX stops
making lower lows, NDX rallies are supply, not demand.
