📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The first session of FY2027 opened on the back foot. The XJO shed 0.64% to
8,722.9 in a decliner-led tape (Adv/Dec 72/120), straight after FY2026 was
put to bed +2.8%. There was no offshore excuse — Wall St just delivered its
best quarter since 2020 and US futures were flat — so the fade was a purely
domestic story written by the index's heaviest defensive-cyclical blocks.

Financials (XFJ -1.67%) were the single largest drag, big-four banks off
0.6-1.7% as the RBA June minutes reminded the market inflation is still above
the 2-3% band with more tightening possible after three 2026 hikes. But the
red sector print masked a violent internal rotation: wealth & fund managers
RIPPED against their own sector — PPT +16.8%, MFG +12.0%, NWL +7.7%, PNI
firm. The bank de-rate and the asset-manager re-rate are two different trades
sharing one sector code.

Consumer Staples (XSJ -2.14%) were the worst sector (grocery/food dumped;
CBO -7.1%). The defensive complex otherwise did its job: Health Care (XHJ
+0.77%) LED on a broad medical-rotation bid — PME & NEU holding 20day highs,
biotech Imugene (IMU) +21.7% topping the board — with Utilities (+0.52%) and
Energy (+0.36%) firm. Energy's lift was a URANIUM REBOUND: BOE +13.8%, SLX
+7.7%, PDN +6.8% snapping back a day after the nuclear trade pulled back.

Materials (+0.35%) were a tug-of-war: S32 +9.7% on reported Mozal-asset interest vs a heavy
Alcoa (AAI) -7.2% and Sims (SGM) -5.7%, the bulk miners capped by iron ore
near 1-year lows ~US$100/t (record China port inventories, soft steel
demand) — a structural revenue headwind for BHP/RIO/FMG. The day's real
casualty was Objective Corp (OCL) -34.5%, an ASX300 govtech software name
whose move is squarely in profit-warning territory (catalyst not yet on wires).

Bottom line: a low-conviction, defensively-led down day that widened rather
than repaired the structure — breadth stayed weak (41% of XJO above 200MA),
small-caps kept lagging, and "Bullish" labels look out of step with a tape
under every MA. Watch whether the fund-manager/uranium/healthcare pockets can
broaden, or whether banks and staples keep steering the index.
