📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
A deceptive session. XJO fell only -0.26% yet EIGHT of eleven sectors closed
green. The drag was entirely cap-weight: Materials (XMJ -1.48%) and Financials
(XFJ -0.15%) are ~40% of the benchmark. Iron ore sits near a one-year low on
record ~160Mt Chinese port stocks, and the CMRG/Fortescue standoff over Super
Special Fines has become an open pricing war. S32 -3.54%.

The gold complex fell (PNR -10.18%, CYL -4.97%) while spot gold ROSE +1.10% —
producers being sold on cost/execution risk, not commodity risk. Energy
(XEJ +1.67%) led as Trump declared the Iran ceasefire over; crude spiked ~6%
overnight then eased (WTI $73.25 -1.39%). NHC +5.45%, TBN +6.82%.

US overnight was a geopolitical de-rating, not a growth one: hawkish June FOMC
minutes (some members argued for further HIKES) plus higher oil crushed
credit-sensitives — DJI -1.09% vs NDX +0.20% on chipmaker strength. That
transmitted straight to Sydney REITs: XRE -1.11%, SGP -3.67%, CHC -3.28%.
ES +0.18% / NQ +0.43% futures at ASX close say the panic is already fading.

Beneath it all, the domestic housing cyclical is the strongest thing on the
board — and it has nothing to do with the index. Record rents, a supply crunch,
Homebuilding at Smart Money #1 for four straight sessions.
