📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday 11 September was the ASX 200's fourth straight decline — XJO -0.89%
to 8,741.2 — and it was macro, not local. Brent crude's surge to a four-
month high near US$110 (Houthi strikes on Saudi facilities, Middle-East
shipping attacks) reignited inflation fears, driving global bond yields to
multi-year highs (US 10Y ~4.90%) and equities lower across Asia (Nikkei
-2.8%, Kospi -2.3%). A synchronised, yield-driven risk-off — not local news.

The rotation was textbook. Financials (XFJ +1.08%) was the only clearly
green sector, led by insurers — IAG +4.23%, SUN +3.65%, QBE +2.86% —
whose investment income improves as bond yields climb. Utilities and
Industrials held flat as defensive havens. Everything high-beta was sold:
Materials (XMJ -3.63%) was crushed as the resources complex de-risked —
uranium (DYL -9.87%, PDN -9.59%, BMN -8.24%), lithium (LTR -8.55%) and
copper (CSC -8.12%, despite LME copper near records). IT (XIJ -2.06%) fell
as the rising discount rate hit long-duration growth.

Two moves were genuinely stock-specific: Echo IQ (EIQ -9.65%) tumbled on an
FDA rejection (-60% over 5 days), and GQG Partners (-9.50%) fell on a soft
FUM update. Immutep (IMM +27.27%) was the standout gainer on an efti
development-strategy refresh; Corporate Travel (CTD +15.49%) rebounded on
flow with no same-day filing.

Breadth was ugly and one-sided: XJO advancers 41 vs 150 decliners (1:3.7),
16 stocks down >4% vs one up, only 37% above the 200MA, T2108 at 28%, and
70 new 20-day lows against 2 highs. RSI-15 near 30 flags oversold, but with
US CPI tonight and the RBA on 28-29 Sept, the oil/yield impulse is the tape.
