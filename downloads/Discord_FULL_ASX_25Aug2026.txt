📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Tuesday 25/08/2026 saw the XJO add +0.68% to 9164.6 on a
genuinely broad tape -- XJO advancers led decliners 134/53 (2.5:1), T2108 at
61% and 42 new 20day highs against 15 lows. But the index level buries the
real story: this was the sharpest single-day earnings dispersion of the FY26
season so far, and the winners and losers sat inside the SAME sectors.

EOS (Electro Optic Systems) +23.02% was the standout -- its half-year report
and revenue update (08:25, price-sensitive) re-rated the defence thesis in a
single session. ARB +13.87% and ANN +8.16% (a 5-year high) confirmed that the
market is paying up hard for genuine FY26 beats. Against them, Industrials
also produced MND -11.96% (Monadelphous, margin disappointment) -- so XNJ
closed +0.58% with a ~35-point internal spread between its best and worst name.

The precious-metals complex was the day's leadership engine. Gold's push to a
3-month high (spot ~US$4,651) on USD softness carried VAU, GMD [H] and EVN,
and lifted Smart Money Gold to rank 4 and Uranium to rank 3 (BOE +4.12%).
Lithium was the mirror image: LTR -8.68%, PLS -6.03% (Bell Potter flagged the
stock 'may have peaked' after its result), ELV -5.82% -- a clean rotation out
of lithium and into gold/uranium within Materials, which netted to XMJ +0.30%.

Energy (XEJ -0.78%) was the only red major sector as Brent (~-1.35% to ~US$93)
and WTI (-1.6%) gave back gains on profit-taking ahead of the US Iran-sanctions
plan. With XJO at 9,164.6 -- 2.45% above its 50MA and 4.05% above its 200MA --
the structural backdrop is firmly constructive; the risk is idiosyncratic
(single-name earnings misses), not macro, while the calendar stays this heavy.
