📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Tuesday 30/06/2026 closed the 2026 financial year with the ASX200 down 0.51% to 8,778.7 —
a soft full stop on a year that still delivered +2.8%. The session's character was set
entirely by the gold complex: bullion broke below US$4,000/oz overnight (its first sub-4k
print since November 2025) as sticky US inflation drove the USD to multi-month highs. With
gold miners carrying high fixed-cost operating leverage, the equity reaction was amplified.

The damage was concentrated and severe. Gold names accounted for 11 of the 15 biggest
ASX200 fallers: NST -5.82% (ASX20), PRU -6.98%, RRL -6.94%, VAU -7.13%, RSG -8.25%, OBM
-6.22%. This is operating leverage working in reverse — a ~6% AUD-gold move translating to
double-digit equity drawdowns in the higher-cost producers. Materials (XMJ -1.6%) led the
market lower and Real Estate (XRE -2.22%) was worst as firmer USD/yields hit long-duration.

Crucially, this was a rotation, not a liquidation. Financials (XFJ +0.42%) led — the Big 4
banks were the day's refuge, absorbing capital exiting gold. Utilities (+0.39%), Energy
(+0.38%) and IT (+0.35%, riding Nasdaq's +2.07% overnight) also finished green. Megaport
(MP1) +6.36% on an institutional raise, Neuren (NEU) +6.93% and Electro Optic (EOS) +6.63%
on defence demand show risk appetite was alive — just not in the resource complex.

Breadth tells the dispersion story: XJO advancers trailed decliners 82/111 (0.74:1) even as
the index lost only half a percent — the gold rout was deep but narrow. XJO 20day Highs (34)
still outnumbered Lows (12), and T2108 held at 51.5%. The structural read remains the
XTO-above-all-MAs / XSO-below-all-MAs split: large-caps intact, small-caps (-6% vs 200MA)
the weak link heading into the new financial year.
