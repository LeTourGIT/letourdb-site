📝 SESSION SUMMARY

broke to 2011 levels. Higher oil + higher yields = a long-duration equity headwind.

event, not demand destruction. The only real bid was gold equities on stock catalysts:
SBM +16.44% (Simberi sale), FML +9.17%, OBM +4.93%. Defensives (Utilities, Telcos)
held best, IT worst — and Smart Money rotated hard into utilities, energy and REITs.
