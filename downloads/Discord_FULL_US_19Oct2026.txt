📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Monday's AI rebound came against a hostile backdrop. Houthi forces claimed
strikes on Saudi Aramco's Yanbu facility and the Shaybah oilfield, Hormuz
crude flows fell to about 8.5 mb/d, and November WTI settled +2.22% at
$103.66, its highest since 15/09. The 10yr rose 6bp to 5.15%. Equities still
bought the AI dip: NDX +1.17%, and the SOX +1.61% ended an 8-session losing
streak, its longest since 2008. Buying duration-sensitive tech as oil and
yields rise is shorts covering into earnings week, not a macro all-clear.

The leaders were the most damaged charts. 12 of the 15 biggest $500M+
gainers have negative 5day returns: WDC +6.09% (5day -8.98%), MRVL +4.06%
(-10.82%), AAOI +3.48% (-22.33%), CIEN +3.45% (-15.01%). The RS Top 20 has
no semiconductor or hardware name, which confirms relief in a downtrend
rather than new accumulation. AMZN +4.98% and TSLA +3.76% account for most
of XLY's +1.78%.

It was a cap-weight rally. SPX gained 0.41% with 173 advancers against 324
decliners, RSP fell 0.32% while IVV rose 0.44%, and SPX new 20day highs fell
from 171 to 41. RUT is worse: T2108 25.78% and 352 new lows against 84
highs. A handful of mega-caps are holding up indices whose members are still
in correction.

Rates did the selling: XLU -2.01% was the worst sector, XLRE -1.16% (HPP
-12.43%), and REM -3.18% was the only ETF beyond ±3%. Banks fell despite
regional EPS beats. NAHB sentiment hit 29, the lowest since 2012, yet Home
Improvement Retailers hold Smart Money rank 1: with turnover frozen, owners
renovate instead of moving.

Smart Money is not positioned for the AI bounce. Oil & Gas E&P rose from
rank 23 to 5 in two sessions and Integrated Oil & Gas from 26 to 7, and the
60day leaders are Integrated Oil (+14.26%) and Gold (+13.70%): a war-premium
regime. TSLA, META, IBM and TXN report Wednesday and decide whether this is
a turn or a bear-market rally. Until breadth confirms, use strength to
reduce weak AI-hardware positions, not to add.
