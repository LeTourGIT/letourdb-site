📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday was a textbook relief rally. The catalyst chain ran geopolitics →
volatility → tech: Iran halted strikes on Israel, draining the acute war-risk
premium, VIX collapsed -11.21% to 15.84, and the "chips staged a comeback"
that had failed the prior session finally held. Every major index closed green,
but the internals say relief, not re-acceleration.

The leadership was unambiguous: AI-infrastructure HARDWARE. Servers and storage
led the entire market — HPE +12.44%, DELL +11.98% (RBC initiation; +356% over
1year), HPQ +8.40%, SMCI +7.28%, NTAP +8.54% — with the semiconductor complex
right behind (ON +8.51%, SWKS +5.14%, ADI +4.85%) and networking confirming
(ANET +5.61%, CIEN +4.48%, CSCO +4.37%). Equal-weight semis (XSD +3.08%)
out-ran cap-weight (SOXX +1.86%), telling you the rebound was broad across the
chip supply chain rather than a mega-cap-only bid. Datacenter POWER rounded out
the theme — Bloom Energy BE +6.68%, Quanta PWR +5.15%, Comfort Systems FIX
+6.29% — the "electrons for AI" trade that Smart Money is also flagging (Heavy
Electrical Equipment rotated to rank 5 from rank 129 nine days ago).

The oil tape is the session's most important dislocation. WTI fell -3.13% to
$99.99 (first close below $100) and Brent -3.77% — EVEN AS the supply news
turned materially worse: Saudi Arabia shut its East-West crude pipeline after
drone attacks staged from Iraq, the Houthis are advancing on another shipping
route, and the IEA warned of a widening supply gap with refining "stretched to
the limit." Price fell anyway because Iran's de-escalation drained the war
premium faster than the new supply threats could re-price it. But under the
surface, capital is NOT buying the all-clear: five energy sub-industries sit in
the Smart Money top 14 (Refining rank 8, Renewable Fuels 10, E&P 12, Integrated
Oil 13, Oil Transportation 14), and both the 10day and 60day hot lists are
energy/refiner dominated. Spot price down, positioning up = accumulation into
a supply risk the tape is currently ignoring.

Beneath the green screen, breadth is the caution flag. SPX advancers led 335 to
165 (healthy 2:1) with ZERO stocks down 4%+, but only 24.8% of SPX names sit
above their 20MA, 38.6% above the 50MA, and new 20day lows (81) still crushed
new highs (19). Small caps are worse: RUT logged 347 new 20day lows against 95
highs and stayed in Neutral Bear. T2108 sits at 33.6% (SPX) / 34.3% (RUT) —
below the neutral 50 line. Translation: the bounce is real and daily
participation was positive, but the structural repair is not yet underway.

The overhang is the Fed. Headlines now treat a rate HIKE next week as a "done
deal" on hot inflation data — Jefferies' Fisher says the Fed has "no choice,"
and Cramer is drawing 2018 parallels. That is the binary the market must clear.
