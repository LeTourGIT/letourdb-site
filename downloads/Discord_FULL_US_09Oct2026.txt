📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday was a sector-rotation day dressed up as a quiet index gain. The SPX
added +0.59% to 7,811.54, 0.09% shy of its record. Underneath, a single
corporate announcement repriced an entire industry. SpaceX agreed to buy a
nationwide 800 MHz low-band spectrum portfolio from Grain Management (about
$8B, pending FCC approval). SpaceX said the buy paves the way for Starlink
Mobile to become "a major mobile carrier in the U.S." Low-band 800 MHz
travels far and penetrates buildings: it is the band that turns
direct-to-cell from a remote-area patch into a real terrestrial competitor.

The market voted immediately. VZ -10.14% (worst day since 2002), TMUS -13.27%,
T -10.82%, CHTR -5.74%, CMCSA -2.59%. Combined carrier/cable turnover in sheet
16d was $14.1B, so this was institutional de-risking, not a retail panic.
ASTS -10.48% is the subtle tell: the satellite challenger lost its moat to a
better-funded satellite challenger. The mirror trade was tower REITs.
CCI +15.60% and AMT +9.30% (XLRE +1.86%, best sector) on the logic that a
fourth national carrier must lease towers. Carriers deny satellite can
compete; AT&T's Stankey called it "solving the last 2%". That is the same
thing incumbents always say. The tape is pricing a four-player market and
lower wireless pricing power for years.

The second theme was a clean rotation inside tech, out of chips and into
software/security. In sheet 16d, ZS +7.80%, SNOW +7.42%, DDOG +7.11%,
NET +5.57%, OKTA +5.41%, PLTR +5.17% and PANW +5.09% produced $16.5B of
turnover. CIBR +3.58% and IGV +2.81% (20day +10.98%) confirm it at ETF
level. Twelve software names sit on the 20day-high sheet (DDOG, ZS, SNOW,
OKTA, FTNT, TEAM, TENB, VRNS, RPD, GTLB, FROG, PD). Meanwhile
Semiconductors collapsed to Smart Money rank 88 from rank 10 three days
ago, a full institutional exit. INTC -2.22% (5day -12.26%), SWKS -5.45%,
ON -2.37%, SOXX 5day -5.01%. CNBC flagged two block trades in MU and NVDA
alongside a "memory malaise". Capital is taking profits on the hardware
layer and redeploying into the application/security layer above it. That
pattern usually marks a late-stage AI capex cycle, not a broken one.

Healthcare was the third leg. MRNA +14.21% to an ATH at $225 on its first day
in the Nasdaq-100 (replacing WBD). That was forced index buying on top of a
stock already up sixfold in 2026; Citi sits at Sell with an $80 target. HUM
+11.56% emerged as the top beneficiary of the 2027 Medicare Star Ratings,
lifting Managed Healthcare to Smart Money rank 11 from rank 135 nine days
ago. Not every insurer won: ALHC -13.38% shows the ratings sorted winners
from losers inside the group. IBB +3.00% and 14 of the RS Top 20 being
healthcare make this the most concentrated leadership group in the market.

Geopolitics stayed bid in energy. Trump says an Iran deal "isn't what he
wants" with renewed strikes readied, and the Saudi-led coalition vowed a
"firm" response to Houthi attacks. Brent rose +1.47% to $104.43. Yet XLE
-0.25%, XOP -1.02% and refiners (CRAK -1.56%, VLO -2.26%) all fell. The
Trump-Putin deal to supply Russian diesel to US and global markets adds
distillate supply that compresses crack spreads, so refiners sold even as
crude rose. Smart Money still shows Drilling (SURGE to rank 15) and E&P
(rank 16 from 131 nine days ago) accumulating on 10day ROC. Buyers are
picking up oil exposure on red days.

Breadth was positive at the headline but weak underneath. SPX Adv/Dec
was 317/183 and new 20day highs flipped to 52 vs 20 lows (from 26/63). Yet
only 35.0% of SPX stocks are above their 50MA and 47.2% above their 200MA,
with the index 0.09% from a record. RUT closed +0.46% on 895 advancers vs
1,038 decliners. Small caps were lifted by a few names, not broad
participation.
