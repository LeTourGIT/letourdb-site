📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Monday was a textbook leadership-rotation session. The Dow closed +0.51% and
small caps +0.62% while the Nasdaq 100 fell -0.32% — the mirror image of the
tape traders have been conditioned to expect all cycle. The catalyst was a
weekend Wall Street Journal report that NVIDIA is in talks to provide a ~$250B
financing BACKSTOP for OpenAI's 10-gigawatt Ohio data-center project (total
cost >$500B). Markets read the structure as 'circular financing' — the chip
vendor guaranteeing its own customer's debt — and re-rated the entire AI-
infrastructure complex. NVDA -4.99%, AMD -5.17%, and the semiconductor ETF
SOXX -2.05% led the decline. Michael Burry publicly expanded shorts against
NVDA, MU, SOXX and CAT into the news, amplifying the narrative.

The second semi blow was idiosyncratic but thematically aligned: SanDisk (SNDK)
cratered -11.02% on the LARGEST single-name turnover of the day ($26.8B) after
China's ChangXin (CXMT) completed an $8.6B memory-chip IPO that surged 466% on
debut — a direct competitive-share threat to Western memory. WDC -4.21% and the
semi-equipment cohort (LRCX -4.46%, AMAT -3.61%, TER -4.33%, AMKR -6.54%) and
optical (LITE -6.69%, COHR -3.92%) all fell in sympathy. When the market's most
crowded long is the funding source AND the geopolitical-loser simultaneously,
capital does not leave the market — it rotates. And it did, decisively.

Where it rotated is the real signal. Enterprise SOFTWARE was the primary
beneficiary: IGV +3.33% was one of only seven ETFs above +3%, with WDAY +9.01%,
ADSK +7.70%, NOW +6.86%, CRM +6.07%, PLTR +7.00%, PATH +7.20% and TEAM +10.35%
all ripping. The read: software is being repriced as the ASSET-LIGHT way to own
AI — it monetises the capability without carrying the capex or the circular-
financing risk now attached to the hardware layer. Cadence (CDNS) reinforced it
after hours, +4% on a beat. Consumer discretionary joined — XLY +1.31%, retail
XRT +2.91%, online retail IBUY +4.28%, travel AWAY +3.88%, gaming ESPO +3.57%,
with DASH +6.63%, CVNA +5.92%, RDDT +6.22% and CPRT +6.62% leading single names.

Digital assets were the day's cleanest risk-on tell: Ethereum ETHA +4.77% and
EETH +4.67% topped the ETF board, crypto-proxy BMNR +13.49% and MSTR +7.61%
surged. CNBC framed it precisely — 'crypto stocks rally on rotation FROM AI
infrastructure.' With VIX up a trivial +0.54% to 18.67, this was orderly
reallocation, not a fear event. The bid even reached defensives (staples XLP
+1.46% led all sectors), which alongside the crypto/consumer strength paints a
barbell: investors rotating simultaneously toward safety AND high-beta non-AI risk.

Underpinning everything, oil COLLAPSED. WTI -3.90% to $81.89 and Brent -5.64%
(USO -8.70%) as Iran reportedly agreed to suspend attacks while the US pause
holds — evaporating the Strait of Hormuz risk premium that had driven crude up
~40% earlier this quarter. Energy was the worst sector (XLE -2.11%), the oil
complex (XOP -2.86%, CRAK -2.61%, IEO -2.54%) sat just above the -3% flag, and
COP -3.89%, WMB -4.34%, LNG -5.13% led single-name damage. Falling energy costs
are a disinflationary tailwind into this week's Fed meeting (hold expected) —
quietly the most market-friendly macro development of the session.
