📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Black Friday's half-session delivered SPX +1.60% and NDX +2.60%, capping
a +5.6% week, the S&P's best since April 2025, and the Dow's best since
October 2022. The week started with Jensen Huang's reinstatement at
Nvidia (NVDA +31% Monday), gave half back midweek, and resumed Friday.
The critical detail: yields rose 8-9bp across the curve while stocks rose.
The 10yr is near 4.94% and a 25bp Fed hike on 09/12 is all but fully
priced. Equities rallied against tighter financial conditions. That is
short-covering and re-risking after the 20/11 crash, not a liquidity tailwind.

Leadership was AI hardware's most-sold names: memory/storage (SNDK +15.96%,
MU +7.60%, WDC +7.22%), optical (COHR +14.07%, LITE +7.98%), connectivity
(ALAB +8.67%, CRDO +8.22%, MRVL +7.34%, ANET +6.55%) and servers (DELL
+6.70%). XSD +5.04% and SOXX +4.76% were both ⚠ EXTREME or close to it.
But the Excel shows what this is NOT. Semiconductors rank 124 of 137 in
Smart Money (10day ROC -13.73%), and Semi Equipment ranks 123. There is
not one semiconductor in the RS Top 20. And 14 of the 15 top gainers still
carry negative 10day returns (SNDK -27.16%, MU -18.97%). This is a reflex
bounce from the bottom of the rotation board. Re-accumulation is only
confirmed when semis climb that board, not when they print one green day.

Breadth is the genuinely constructive signal. SPX 405/94 (4.3:1), NDX
93/7, RUT 1,642/268. This is the third session in four at ≥2.9:1. SPX
T2108 has gone from 39.0% (20/11) to 62.8%, +23.8 points in four sessions.
The % above the 50MA went from 42.8% to 63.4%, and new 20day lows
collapsed from 173 to 2. That is a breadth-thrust profile. Breadth thrusts
historically mark durable lows more often than price does.

The structural tell is equal-weight vs cap-weight. RSP 20day -0.75% vs
IVV -5.31%. The "S&P correction" is a cap-weighted megacap-AI event:
63.4% of S&P stocks sit above their 50MA while the index sits 4.09% below
its own. The median stock never broke. NDX is where damage lives (47%
above 50MA, T2108 46%), and RUT is weakest structurally (T2108 40.4%,
652 stocks -25% QTR vs 416 +25%).

Cross-asset: gold +2.35% spot and miners +7% (SIL +7.44%, GDX +7.16%)
rose alongside higher yields. That is a geopolitical/fiscal hedge bid,
after a drone strike in Kuwait killed two Italian contractors and two
ships were attacked in Hormuz. Oil fell anyway (WTI -1.59% to $83.08,
-21% over 20 days) ahead of Sunday's OPEC+ meeting. Crypto did not join
in (IBIT -0.62%, Blockchain rank 136). Today's risk-on was selective, not
the speculative-liquidity kind.
