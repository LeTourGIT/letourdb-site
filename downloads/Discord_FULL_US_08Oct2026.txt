📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday's session was about the AI trade's revenue denominator. The FT and CNBC
reported that OpenAI's annualised revenue at the end of September was about $50B,
around $20B below the ~$68B figure the market had been using. OpenAI told investors
the number was "lower than widely reported". That matters well beyond one private
company. OpenAI is the anchor tenant underneath Oracle's backlog, CoreWeave's
contracts, and a large share of the GPU, optical and power orders those contracts
pay for. The market reacted to a smaller revenue base by repricing the whole chain.

The selling went through every layer of the AI build-out at once. Compute and
silicon: INTC -5.34% on $12.7B turnover, ALAB -9.21%, SOXX -3.35%, XSD -3.68%.
Optical: AAOI -13.58%, COHR -9.63%, GLW -6.38%, LITE -5.62%. Neocloud and hosting:
CRWV -7.77%, IREN -7.70%, WULF -5.21%, ORCL -5.48%. Power: VST -6.35%, BE -6.34%,
OKLO -6.11%, with uranium ETFs URA -3.43% and URNM -3.72%. When optical, power and
nuclear fall together on one revenue headline, it tells you all of them were priced
off the same demand assumption. Concentration risk shows up on days like this.

The critical read is that capital rotated rather than left. SPX fell 0.47% but
advancers beat decliners 337 to 162 (2.08:1), a full reversal of Wednesday's 136/361.
Equal-weight RSP gained 0.60% while SPY lost 0.43%, a 1.03-point cap-weight drag.
Pure Value RPV +1.33% vs Pure Growth RPG -1.45% is a 2.78-point style spread. That
is an inverse breadth divergence: the index is down while the typical stock is up.
It is the opposite of the narrow-ATH warning. Money is reallocating, not de-risking.

Where did it go? Energy and defensives. Trump said the Iran deal "isn't what he
wants" while the U.S. prepares renewed strikes, and Brent rose 1.47% to $103.85. At
$12.71, the Brent-WTI spread shows the Hormuz premium sits in the seaborne barrel.
That is why refiners (MPC +4.77%, VLO +4.65%, PSX +3.67%) and tankers (Oil & Gas
Transport at Smart Money rank 5) lead. Staples (XLP +2.11%) took the defensive bid
as PEP rallied on a beat with a trimmed guide, joined by STZ, PM and KR.

Rates make the rotation sticky. With the 10yr near 5.3% and a Fed still hiking, AI
equities lose the multiple support that justified buying them ahead of revenue.
