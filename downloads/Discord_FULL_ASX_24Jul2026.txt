📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday 24 July was a breadth failure dressed as a modest index decline.
The XJO fell just 0.75% to 8,772.3, but underneath that: 38 advancers
against 149 decliners (0.26:1), 21 stocks down more than 4% against a
single stock up more than 4%, and 38 new 20day lows against 13 new highs.
In the broader XAO the ratio was 86/353. T2108 fell to 41%. A 0.75% index
print with those internals means the losses were concentrated in exactly
the places that do not carry index weight — and that the top 100 (XTO,
still +0.65% above its 50MA) is doing all the load-bearing.

Two forces did the damage, and they arrived from opposite directions.
Overnight, Alphabet fell about 6.9% and Tesla about 14% after guiding
capex toward US$205bn, taking the Nasdaq Composite -2.15%. Alphabet BEAT
— it was punished for spending, not for missing. That is a discount-rate
judgement, not an earnings judgement, and it transmitted directly into
Info Tech XIJ -3.98% (the worst sector by a clear margin), Comm Services
XTJ -1.52%, and Consumer Discretionary XDJ -1.06%. Appen -8.38% and
Brainchip -7.69% were the sharpest local expressions.

The second force was domestic and arguably more important. Thursday's
76,000-job employment print against a 15,000 expectation pushed AU 10year
yields toward 5% and repriced August RBA hike odds from near-zero to
roughly 36%. The RBA does not meet until 10-11 August, so this is pure
anticipation risk with 12 sessions to run. The rate-sensitive complex wore
it: Real Estate XRE -1.22% with Centuria Capital [ASX200] -8.49% the worst
large-cap loser in the sector, and ALS [ASX50] -7.59% — no Section 2b
announcement, so a pure multiple de-rating on a long-duration mining-
services earnings stream, not a company event.

Where money actually went is the constructive part of the tape. Energy
XEJ +0.93% led on Brent above US$100 after Houthi-claimed strikes on two
Saudi tankers, with Karoon Energy [ASX200] +10.84% the standout — and no
company announcement behind it, so that is a clean commodity beta move.
Financials XFJ +0.88% rose on the same yield curve that hurt REITs, with
WBC, NAB and BEN all making new 20day highs. Utilities +0.80% and Staples
+0.40% completed a textbook defensive rotation. Four green sectors, and
every one of them is either a yield beneficiary or a defensive.

Materials XMJ -2.84% deserves separate treatment because both legs broke
at once: gold fell 2.4% overnight to about US$4,048/oz as US 10year yields
hit a 52-week high of 4.67%, and iron ore is pinned near US$98/t with
Dalian futures under 1,000 yuan on Chinese steel production controls.
Turaco Gold -8.80%, Dateline -10.71% and Chalice -8.47% carried the
precious/critical-minerals damage; Liontown [ASX100] -7.42% extended the
lithium unwind to -10.90% over five sessions on a day it held a quarterly
webcast (14:43) — the webcast is context, not catalyst.
