📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Tuesday delivered a third consecutive losing session as two macro forces crushed momentum:
surging Treasury yields and the Seagate-triggered memory chip correction extending from Monday.
The 30-year Treasury yield touched 5.12% intraday — its highest since 2007 — as bond vigilantes
pushed back against incoming Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's perceived dovish leanings. The 10-year
settled near 4.66%, with the 2-year at 4.11%. This yield spike is a direct Warsh confirmation tax:
the bond market is demanding he establish inflation-fighting credibility before it will let yields
relax. Ed Yardeni, who coined "bond vigilantes," called it plainly — the Fed must now pursue a
hawkish tightening bias to regain control. Markets now price a 42% chance of a rate hike by year-end.

The Seagate-Micron contagion from Monday continued Tuesday. Seagate's CEO comment at JPMorgan —
that building new factories "would just take too long" — exposed a critical supply-constraint
narrative for AI storage. This is not a demand problem (nearline HDD capacity is sold through 2027
into 2028) — it is a capacity ceiling problem. That distinction matters: Seagate's fundamentals
(Q3 revenue +44% YoY, record margins) remain intact, but the market is pricing the gap between
AI storage demand and available supply as a ceiling on near-term earnings elasticity. SNDK -3%+
premarket extension confirms the tape read is supply-worry, not sector exit.

Home Depot delivered a clean beat — $41.77B revenue vs $41.63B est, EPS $3.43 vs $3.41 est —
and reaffirmed FY2026 guidance. CEO Ted Decker noted core consumer resilience despite higher gas
prices and plummeting confidence. Free cash flow margin expanded from 8.8% to 12.4% — the most
important number in the report. The stock's +0.77% response was muted: the street got what it
expected and nothing more. Comps +0.6% vs 0.9% estimate underscores housing market drag.

The NextEra ($67B Dominion acquisition, confirmed Monday) reverberated Tuesday. Dominion +9.44%
Monday extended, while NextEra digested the deal premium. The strategic logic is undeniable:
Dominion's Northern Virginia footprint is "Data Center Alley" — the world's largest data center
concentration — giving NextEra direct leverage on AI-driven electricity demand. PJM forecasts 94%
of its load growth through 2030 attributable to data centers. This is the power sector's AI trade.

Iran continues to send amended peace terms via Pakistani mediators. Trump's response remains
hostile ("TOTALLY UNACCEPTABLE"). WTI held near $104/bbl — elevated but not accelerating.
IEA chief warned commercial oil inventories "depleting rapidly" with only weeks of supply left,
strategic reserves supplementing at 2.5M bbl/day but "not endless." Oil price trajectory remains
the linchpin variable: every $5/bbl sustained adds ~0.2% to CPI, tightening the Fed's options.
