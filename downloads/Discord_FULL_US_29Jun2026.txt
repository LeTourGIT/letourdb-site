📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Monday was a risk-on relief rally built on one macro pivot: the US-Iran
ceasefire held through the weekend despite retaliatory US strikes on Iranian
missile/drone sites, and both sides agreed to keep the Strait of Hormuz open
to commercial traffic. WTI's settle below $70 on Friday — the first sub-$70
print since 27 February, the day before the war began — is the single most
important number on the tape. It tells you the market is pricing the war
premium OUT of crude, and that repricing cascaded straight into equities:
offensive sectors bid, defensives and energy sold, and the volatility complex
compressed (VIX -4.13% to 17.65). Fresh US-Iran talks in Qatar on Tuesday are
the next catalyst; any breakdown there reverses today's entire setup.

The session's dominant institutional story was a semiconductor squeeze. SOXX
+4.14% and XSD +3.62% reversed a brutal -6% to -8.5% five-day drawdown, with
the heaviest beta in the equipment and connectivity layer: KLAC +11.97%,
AMAT +10.82%, WDC +11.16%, ALAB +16.39%. This is a mean-reversion squeeze
after last week's de-risking, not fresh accumulation — SOXX is still -6.21%
on 5day. The MarketWatch divergence flag is the key nuance: semis are
surging while the Mag-7 'struggles' (Alphabet aside), which historically
signals late-cycle rotation INTO cyclical chip beta and OUT of mega-cap
crowding. Watch whether the chip bid holds without the generals leading.

Index mechanics mattered today. Alphabet joined the Dow and popped ~5% on its
debut, mechanically lifting the price-weighted DJI to its first close above
52,000. Comcast +4.4% on its NBCUniversal spinoff plan and Charter +9.4% on
deal speculation lit up Communication Services (XLC +1.60%). But beneath the
headline strength, SPX advancers LAGGED decliners 228 to 271 — a negative
breadth reading on a +1.18% day. The index gain is concentrated in Alphabet,
semis, and a handful of mega-caps; the median S&P stock fell. Russell 2000
finished dead flat (+0.01%) with 865 advancers vs 1,001 decliners, confirming
the rebound never reached the broad market. This is a narrow-leadership tape.

Space and satellite communications was the cleanest pure-momentum theme:
IRDM +25.44%, ASTS +21.44%, RKLB +15.93%, with AVAV +18% in the after-hours
on a defense beat. This complex has decoupled from the macro narrative
entirely — it is trading on the SpaceX-debut halo and a structural defense/space
capex cycle. Honeywell's headline -50.95% is NOT a real loss: it is the
mechanical effect of a 1-for-2 reverse split tied to the completed Aerospace
spinoff (one HONA share per two HON). Screen it out of any laggard read.
