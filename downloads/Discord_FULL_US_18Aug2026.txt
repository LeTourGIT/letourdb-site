📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Tuesday was a rotation, not a rout. The headline indices masked a violent repricing beneath the
surface: the AI-infrastructure complex — optical, memory, semis, AI power — was sold wholesale, while
capital rotated into pharma, energy and staples. The SOX semiconductor gauge fell ~5.5% and the NDX
-1.68% dragged its regime down to Neutral Bull, yet the equal-weight tape barely flinched: SPX only
-0.69%, Dow -0.22%. That gap IS the story.

The proximate catalyst was Fabrinet. FN reported a record quarter — Q4 revenue $1.316B (+45% YoY),
non-GAAP EPS $4.10 (an all-time high, a clean beat) — and fell 19.4%. The problem was positioning, not
results: FN had run +25% into the print, Q1 guidance implied a far more modest sequential step-up than
the 45% just delivered, and negative free cash flow plus a securities loss turned beat-and-raise into
'good but decelerating.' As the contract manufacturer behind much of the industry's 800G optics, FN's
tape set the tone: AAOI -15.2%, COHR -12.8%, LITE -9.9%, CIEN -8.9%. Fabrinet lit the fuse; macro caught it.

The macro was the bond market. Long-end Treasury yields extended a surge to multi-year highs — the
30-year pushing to roughly its highest since 2007 — on persistent inflation risk, heavy government
borrowing, and, pointedly, the debt issuance funding the AI capex boom itself. That last link is what
makes this rotation coherent: the same force (a flood of AI-related debt) that raises term premia is
the force repricing the AI-infrastructure equities. A weekend data point — Anthropic disclosing ARR of
~$65B, below the $80B+ some bulls had floated — added a fundamental question mark just as NVDA looms next week.

The unwind was broad within its lane: memory (SNDK -9.0%, echoing 'Micron peers falling'), AI power
(BE -10.0%, TLN -11.0%, APLD -8.6%), AI cloud (CRWV -12.1%), semi equipment (TER -8.8%). Eleven ETFs
breached -3% and every one was semis, metals-miners, uranium or construction — zero breached +3%. The
only extreme-move flag was XSD -5.75%. This is the cleanest single-day de-rating of the AI-infra trade of the cycle.

Where did the money go? Straight into defensives and real assets. Pharma led the S&P gainers wholesale
— LLY +3.6%, JNJ +3.3%, ABBV +3.4%, GILD +3.3% — the classic bid when growth-multiple risk rises. Energy
(XLE +1.76%) was bid on a 20-year TRGP–ExxonMobil midstream deal (TRGP +7.1% to a record) plus the
prolonged-Hormuz-blockade premium, even though crude itself slipped (WTI -0.94% to $84.45). Staples (MNST
+4.1%) and defensive software (INTU +4.4%, ADBE +3.6%) rounded out the rotation. Notably, gold and silver
did NOT play defence today: rising real yields sent Gold -1.4%, Silver -3.1%, GDX -3.2% — a tell that the
dominant driver was the yield surge, not a classic fear bid.
