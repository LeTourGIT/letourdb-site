📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Tuesday 11 August 2026 was an RBA decision day that resolved exactly as priced —
a hold at 4.35% — leaving the tape free to trade the two things that
actually moved: oil and earnings. XJO added +0.19% to 9,250.6, holding
record territory, but the modest headline masks sharp single-stock dispersion.

Energy (XEJ +3.91%) led every sector as the Hormuz risk premium kept Brent
near US$88 and Santos loaded its first Alaskan Pikka cargo. This is the same
force the RBA named as its chief upside-inflation risk — the macro and the
sector leadership are one story. Health Care (+2.15%) took the defensive-plus-
biotech bid; Materials (+0.51%) stayed green on lithium and gold rather than
the iron-ore majors, with the bulk price soft near US$94 into BHP's 18 Aug result.

FY26 reporting season set the losers' board: Life360 (360 -19.44%) posted
RECORD Q2 numbers and was still dumped — a textbook high-multiple 'sell-the-
news' — while SGH -10.27% and Amotiv -13.50% (the latter with a CEO succession)
show zero tolerance for a large-cap miss. The gainers rewarded genuine beats:
Austal (ASB +17.45%, halt exit) and Chrysos (C79 +17.21%, FY26 beat).

Beneath the large caps, the spec book is a one-way critical-minerals bet.
Lake (LKE +26.67%) and Core Lithium (CXO +11.48%, on a real BP33 drill hit)
confirm the lithium re-rating has broadened; gold and silver spec names
(SVL, MM1, RML) rode the same safe-haven-plus-scarcity theme.
