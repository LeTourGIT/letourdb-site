📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The XJO added just +0.10% to 9,029.7, but the flat print hides the session's real story: a 4.2-point gap between the best and worst sectors. Materials (XMJ +2.88%, 5day +6.12%) led on a broad resources bid. Gold miners rallied as spot gold rose 1.4% to ~US$4,121: ALK +7.3%, GMD +6.4%, OBM +6.3%, RMS and VAU +6.0%, RSG +5.3%, WGX +5.2%, EVN +4.8%. Regis (RRL +3.9%) rose despite a fatal incident at Duketon South. BHP +2.4% and IGO +4.8% (Nova mining concluded ahead of production and cost guidance) gave the move institutional weight, and Brazilian Rare Earths (BRE +12.7%) carried the critical-minerals end.

Uranium was the second engine. Silex (SLX +12.1%) was the top ASX 200 stock, alongside Paladin (PDN +10.9%), Boss (BOE +10.7%), Deep Yellow (DYL +10.6%) and Bannerman (BMN +5.7%). None of them lodged an announcement today, and Paladin's quarterly landed on 05/11, so this was sector flow, not news. Uranium is now third in Smart Money (from rank 13 nine days ago). The AI-power thesis is being expressed through fuel just as AI equity multiples crack in the US.

The funding side was rate-sensitive Australia. Financials (XFJ -1.11%) and Real Estate (XRE -1.32%, the worst sector) absorbed the selling. ANZ (-3.2%) reversed early gains: cash profit rose 31% to $7.59b, but a flat 83c dividend, a ~$800m rise in non-performing loans and a weaker capital position spoiled it. Scentre (SCG -4.4%), GPT and Stockland (-3.5%) and Lendlease (LLC -5.4%, MSG North sale terminated) led the REIT selloff. With the cash rate at 4.85% after last week's 6-3 hike and the banks passing it on, the market is selling duration to buy hard assets.

Results day punished cash-light stories. Ventia (VNT -16.2%) hit a 52-week low after its strategy reset under the new CEO flagged lower 2H revenue. Dyno Nobel (DNL -11.0%) posted record explosives EBIT (+12% to $485m) but cut its dividend to 9.6c, with cash conversion at 35.5% and leverage at 2.1x. → The market is paying for cash flow, not EBIT. Service Stream (SSM) halted pre-open for its Programmed acquisition and $475m raise, so expect discount-driven pricing on resumption.

Breadth warns that the headline overstates the tape. XJO advancers/decliners were 92/101, so the index rose with more stocks down than up. The broader XAO was healthier (255/200) and the Small Ords stronger still (88/58). The regime upgrades (XAO → Bullish, XSO → Neutral Bear) are real, but XAO participation is only 40% above the 200MA. Into Tuesday: ES/NQ are slightly soft, US CPI is the week's event risk, and Thursday brings ALL, XRO, ORI and GNC results. The RBA's next meeting is December, outside the 7-day window.
