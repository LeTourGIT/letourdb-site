📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The S&P 500 closed above 7,800 for the first time, clearing its 13/08/2026
high, and the Nasdaq 100 made it six straight gains. The 10-year yield eased
about 4bps to 5.27%, bond proxies bounced, and the VIX slipped to 15.01.

Power was the defining trade. Google signed a 20-year agreement with
Constellation Energy for 890 MW of new nuclear capacity: CEG +12.25%, TLN
+12.43%, VST +10.77%. A hyperscaler PPA turns merchant power-price exposure
into contracted revenue, a re-rating from commodity generator to
infrastructure annuity. XLU is still -5.27% over 20day, so this is a
reversal from oversold, not yet a trend.

Inside technology, Marvell's analyst day sized AI interconnect at roughly
$65B by 2030 and optics ran (CIEN +13.85%, MRVL +5.81%) while semi equipment
and storage were sold (KLAC -4.54%, WDC -6.93%). Capital is moving from
layers adding supply to layers still bottlenecked. Healthcare paid for it:
genomics and AI-diagnostics leaders were flushed with no single catalyst,
and 14 of the RS Top 20 are Healthcare. The most crowded momentum cohort
being sold on a record day is distribution funding the next leadership, and
the 20day High sheet shows where it went: cybersecurity and AI networking.

The record is narrow. Only 29.4% of S&P 500 members are above their 50MA;
across 275 record closes since 2018 the prior low was 43.4%. QQQ is +5.75%
over 20day against RSP -2.03%. T2108 has risen every day from 19.0% four
sessions ago, so breadth is turning up from a washed-out base, but there is
no cushion if FOMC minutes or CPI revive hike pricing.
