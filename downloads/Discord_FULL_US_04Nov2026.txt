📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Tuesday's midterms delivered a Democratic sweep of both chambers. The AP called the Senate at
11:40pm ET and the House at 1:27am ET. The market read it through three lenses at once:
gridlock (policy-risk relief), an earlier end to the Iran war, and a tougher road for AI data
centers. VP Vance blamed the losses on the war and Senate Democrats signalled they could use the
power of the purse. WTI collapsed -8.73% to $87.44 as the war premium came out.
The Dow added 1,579 points (+3.05%) for its best day since April 2025. Equal-weight S&P closed at
a record.

Cheaper oil is a consumer tax cut, and capital went straight to the consumer. XLY +4.26% led all
sectors. Homebuilders (ITB +7.25%, XHB +5.43%), airlines (JETS +6.70%), retail (XRT +5.54%) and
travel (AWAY +5.51%) all printed EXTREME moves. This was not a new trade. Smart Money had already
moved Appliances from rank 83 to 2 and Homebuilding from rank 31 to 3 over nine days. Today was
the confirmation print for a rotation institutions began building in late October.

The AI trade split in two, and that split is the most important signal of the session. Companies
that SPEND on AI were bought: MSFT +6.68%, AMZN +6.12%, AAPL +5.31%, GOOGL +3.18%. Companies that
SELL to the build-out were liquidated. In power, VST fell -13.83% and NRG -13.52%. In data-center
construction and equipment, STRL fell -21.46%, MOD -21.43%, FIX -15.08%, VRT -12.89% and PWR
-11.65%. In semis, MU fell -7.00%, MRVL -7.08% and SNDK -7.91%. The logic: a Democratic Congress
may challenge 100% expensing for data centers and state permitting may tighten. Slower capex is a
cost saving for the hyperscaler and a revenue hit for the supplier. XLK at -0.01% hides a
massive internal rotation.

Breadth confirms this was a broad thrust. SPX printed 373 advancers vs 127 decliners (2.9:1). 140
stocks rose +4% vs 67 that fell -4%, and new 20day highs jumped from 54 to 257. RUT was even
stronger at 1,454 vs 478, with 456 names up +4%. Yet the cap-weighted indices barely reflect it.
SPX regime is still Neutral Bear and NDX Bearish, because the old leaders (semis) were the
session's funding source. This is an inverse divergence: breadth far stronger than the index.
Historically that is the healthier kind, because it means participation is widening, not narrowing.

Precious metals added a second, less obvious signal. Gold +4.19% to $4,132.90, silver +6.30%,
SIL +9.60% and GDX +9.01% all rose on the same day the war premium left oil. That is not a
safe-haven trade. It reads as a weaker-dollar and policy-credibility trade: a divided government,
a delayed Lisa Cook hearing (now 14/12) and euro zone inflation at 4% point to real-rate pressure
on the dollar. AUD/USD +0.55% to 0.7004 agrees.
