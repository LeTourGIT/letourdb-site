📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
A cost-of-capital session. September core CPI ran hot (0.3% MoM, 2.6%
YoY), the 10-year spiked to ~5.33% intraday, and October hike odds jumped
from ~21% to above 50% before settling near 37%. The market sold the asset
most exposed to a higher discount rate: debt-funded AI infrastructure.

All 15 of the session's largest $500M-turnover losers are Technology, and
they trace the AI build-out layer by layer — compute rental (CRWV -15.46%,
DOCN -14.76%, IREN -12.18%), optical fabric (AAOI -11.82%, CIEN -10.19%,
LITE -9.28%) and servers (DELL -11.27%). CoreWeave's bonds fell with its
equity: for the first time the financing chain, not the demand chain,
was the catalyst.

The capital went to cash-flow certainty. 10 of 15 top gainers are
healthcare (UNH +4.73%, MRNA, BMY, LLY, MRK, ABBV), plus WMT, KR and PM.
Value beat growth by 3.7pts (RPV +0.24% vs RPG -3.46%). Iran threats and
an Aramco attack kept Brent near $105 — high enough to keep headline CPI
sticky, which makes the energy shock and the rate shock one trade.

The regime read matters more than the 1day print. SPX and NDX remain
Bullish by classification, but momentum reset fast: SPX RSI-15 fell from
58.85 to 49.87 and both indices now hug the 50MA. The Russell 2000 —
the purest rate-sensitive cohort — is already Bearish and oversold
(RSI-15 28.37). The Smart Money sheet saw this coming: Semiconductor
Equipment collapsed 100 ranks over three sessions before today's XSD
-5.31%. Thursday's TSM print and Friday's follow-through decide whether
the AI complex is being de-rated for its cost of capital or re-rated on
weaker demand — the first is a buyable correction, the second is not.
