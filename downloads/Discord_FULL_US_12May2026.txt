📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Tuesday delivered the macro shock the market had been bracing for: April CPI
printed 3.8% headline (3yr high, beat 3.7% consensus), driven by Iran-war energy
costs that accounted for over 40% of the monthly increase. Gasoline +28.4% YoY.
Core CPI +2.8% (+0.4% MoM), also above the 2.7% estimate. Real wages fell
0.5% in April — the first month wages have failed to outpace inflation in three
years. The yield on the 10yr Treasury climbed to ~4.42% (week high), and CME
FedWatch now prices roughly 27-30% probability of a rate hike by December,
up from effectively zero six weeks ago. The Fed's "higher for longer" framework
is no longer a constraint — it is the base case.

Simultaneously, President Trump declared the US-Iran ceasefire "on massive life
support," rejecting Tehran's counter-proposal (sanctions relief, reparations,
Hormuz authority). Reports emerged that Trump is meeting his national security
team to weigh resumed military operations. WTI rose +3.4% toward $101.63 intraday.
Saudi Aramco CEO warned the market is losing ~100M barrels of supply weekly.
Two independent macro negatives — energy-driven CPI and geopolitical re-escalation
— landed on the same session. The market's resilience (SPX only -0.16%) is itself
a signal: institutions are not capitulating. They are rotating.

The rotation story was the session's structural insight. Healthcare led the day:
XLV +1.96%, IHF (Healthcare Providers) +2.64%, IHI (Medical Devices) +2.42%,
BBH +1.76%. This is not defensive repositioning — it is capital moving from a
high-RSI, CPI-sensitive semiconductor complex toward an inflation-resistant,
earnings-season-driven healthcare thesis. Consumer Staples XLP +1.28% and
Financials XLF +0.78% added texture. Meanwhile, XLK -1.51% with semi ETFs
SOXX -3.15%, XSD -4.26%, URNM -3.94%, URA -5.03% continued the rotation
away from the semi/nuclear complex that began last Thursday.

Two breadth readings demand attention. SPX Adv/Dec was 259/240 — essentially
neutral on a negative-index day, which is constructive. But RUT breadth is
severely degraded: 648 advancers vs 1,230 decliners. Small caps are absorbing
the CPI shock disproportionately. The inflation tax on small businesses and
rate-sensitive balance sheets hits them hardest. RSP (equal-weight SPX) at -0.09%
vs SPX -0.16% also suggests the cap-weight distortion is minimal today — this is
broader-based softness, not a mega-cap-only story.

Gold tells the smartest story of the session. GLD -0.40% (spot gold pressured by
real yield spike on hot CPI) while silver SLV +0.71% and copper COPX +3.63% surged.
Industrial metals decoupling from monetary metals = real-economy demand signal.
Copper's 5day +16% on infrastructure/AI data center construction demand is the
commodity market pricing in AI capex duration, not inflation risk. The Smart Money
top remains Semiconductors at #1 (10day ROC +25%), but today's session shows the
next rotation candidates assembling: Gold #8 (↑↑ SURGE), Integrated Mining #9,
and Precious Metals #10 all surged 60-130 ranks in the past 9 days.
