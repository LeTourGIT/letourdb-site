📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday's session rewrote the AI trade's short-term narrative in a single session.
Broadcom's technically-stellar quarter — AI revenue $10.8B (+143% YoY), EPS beat, Q3 guidance
$29.4B (+84% YoY) — was punished -13% because the market had priced something even larger.
CEO Hock Tan reaffirmed but did not raise the $100 billion FY2027 AI semiconductor target.
After a 40% YTD run into earnings, that reaffirmation was read as a ceiling, not a floor.
The result: $250 billion in AVGO market cap evaporated in one session, dragging SOXX -2.10%,
XSD -0.83%, and the Nasdaq Composite -0.09% while unlocking capital for the rest of the market.

The AVGO washout catalysed a broad, healthy rotation. Healthcare led at +3.07% — XLV's
strongest session in months — with UnitedHealth +5%, LLY +4%, ABBV +3.6%, and WFC/JPM/BAC
adding 3-4% as financials surged +2.59%. This is not defensive positioning; it is institutional
rebalancing from over-indexed AI semis into underweighted sectors. The breadth confirmation is
striking: SPX Advancers 359 vs Decliners 140, a 2.6:1 positive ratio on the same day the
index hit an ATH. This is the constructive breadth signature the market has been missing.

The Israel-Lebanon conditional ceasefire, announced Wednesday after US-brokered talks in
Washington, provided a secondary geopolitical tailwind. The deal is contingent on Hezbollah
ceasing fire and withdrawing from south of the Litani River — Hezbollah has not agreed. But
the diplomatic framework reduces near-term escalation risk, supporting the VIX decline to
15.40 (-4.11%) and marginal strength in airlines (JETS +0.61%) and cruise stocks.

Quantinuum's (QNT) IPO debut was the market event narrative anchor. Priced at $60, opened
at $68, hit $71.35, and closed approximately flat. The $1.68B raise at a $14-15B valuation
on $30.9M in 2025 revenue (450x price/sales) captures the quantum sector's speculative
premium — and the institutional pragmatism that priced it right back down by close.

CIENA's massive beat (EPS $1.64 vs $1.49 est, Rev $1.57B vs $1.53B est, +40% YoY) was
treated counterintuitively: stock fell ~13% AH on elevated expectations. Optical networking
backlog $7.7B, raised FY guidance to $6.3B. The lesson: in a year where CIEN is up 150%,
"strong" is not enough — you must beat the priced-in beat.
