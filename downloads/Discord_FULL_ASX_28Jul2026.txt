price-sensitive announcement of material contracts and a product release - a textbook sell-the-news
after a large run (5day -16.8%), with EOS -7.52% sympathising. Materials was dragged by a gold and
junior-mining pullback (SRL -9.20%, LIN -8.88% after an ASX price query, MTM, MEI, VMM) with iron

signal. Forward-looking, the Smart Money table is the tell: Homebuilding surged to #1 (from #67 nine
days ago), Insurance Funds to #2 - rate-cut-sensitive domestic cyclicals leading capital rotation
into the 10-11 August RBA meeting.
