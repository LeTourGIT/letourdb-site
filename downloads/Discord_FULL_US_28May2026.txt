Thursday delivered the session the software bulls had been building toward.
Snowflake's +36% explosion — its best single session ever — was not just a
stock event; it was a thesis event. Revenue +33% YoY, product revenue +34%,
a $6B five-year AWS partnership anchored to Graviton AI silicon, and an

YoY — the highest reading since May 2023 — as the Strait of Hormuz energy
shock continued feeding into the broader consumer basket. Core PCE came in
at 3.3% YoY, slightly below the 0.3% MoM consensus at +0.2% on the month.
The market's reaction was decisive: the Fed is not cutting. Yet markets made
new ATHs anyway. Why? Because the data was "less bad" than feared (MoM
deceleration in both headline and core), and AI earnings momentum is
overriding the macro drag. When SPX and NDX print ATHs on 3.8% PCE, the
market is telling you the earnings cycle is doing the heavy lifting.

Iran geopolitics remained the session's live wire. The US and Iranian
negotiators drafted a 60-day MOU to extend the ceasefire and commence
nuclear talks, but President Trump's approval is still pending. Early
session saw US strikes on an Iranian missile site, which pushed WTI toward
$97 before the MOU draft reversed losses. Brent settled near $97, WTI in
the low $90s range — volatile but not panicking. The structural message
is that both sides want the deal more than markets are pricing in.

SNOW-driven leadership is distorting headline numbers. XSD's +1.47% and
