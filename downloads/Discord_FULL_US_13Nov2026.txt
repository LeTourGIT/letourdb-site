---------------------------------------------------
The Nvidia governance story graduated from overhang to event. CNBC headlines
confirm Jensen Huang has departed; the board told CNBC it has not yet hired a
search firm, and HSBC downgraded the stock, saying it will struggle until a
new CEO is named. NVDA fell 2.01% to $162.85 on $59.6B of turnover — more than
double any other name on the tape — and MarketWatch tallies $657B of market
cap shed this week in a losing streak not seen in 14 years.

The insight is that NVDA fell LESS than its ecosystem. AMD -8.75% on $26.4B
turnover, AVGO -4.76%, MRVL -4.68%, CRDO -5.94%, COHR -6.00%, TER -5.38%,
SNDK -5.59%, WDC -5.00%, DELL -7.18%. All of those names were up +5% to +19%
on the week coming in (AMD 5day +18.93%, AVGO +18.16%). The market is not
selling Nvidia the company; it is repricing the AI capex flywheel that Huang
personally sold to hyperscalers, sovereigns and Washington. The highest-beta
expressions of "capex keeps compounding" were liquidated first. SOXX -3.67%
and XSD -3.20% were two of only five ETFs to breach -3% — and none breached +3%.

Earnings piled onto the hardware stack. CSCO -6.24% after its fiscal Q1 print
— the third straight report the market has sold, as hardware-heavy AI mix keeps
pressuring margins. AMAT -2.74% after its fiscal Q4 and another -2.09% AH,
with LRCX -3.36% and KLAC -3.17% in sympathy and ONTO -3.24% / ACMR -3.28% AH.
Equipment, networking, memory and custom silicon all red together = the whole
AI hardware chain de-rated as one trade. Neoclouds took the hardest hit: NBIS
-9.92% post-earnings, CRWV -6.13%, IREN -6.72%. That is where the leverage
sits — CNBC notes AI borrowers now drive nearly half of long-dated US IG
issuance — so a confidence shock at the top of the chain lands hardest there.

Underneath, the tape was healthier than the index. SPX advancers 313 vs 184
decliners (1.70:1) on a -0.40% close — yesterday was 123/375. This is an
INVERSE cap-weight divergence: the median stock rose while the AI mega-caps
dragged the index. RSP +0.20% vs VOO -0.38% (+58bp equal-weight edge) and
RPV +0.65% vs RPG -1.47% (+212bp value-over-growth). Staples led (XLP +1.42%:
PEP +2.24%, KDP +2.60%, KMB +2.09%) and Smart Money confirms the move is
structural: Non-Alcoholic Beverages rank 2, Personal Products rank 3 and
Household Products SURGING to rank 10 from rank 91 three days ago.

Within tech the money rotated rather than left. Software and platforms held:
IGV -0.26% vs XLK -2.01%, APP +3.92%, U +5.24%, ADBE +1.90%, META +2.90%.
Cerebras +11.22% post-earnings is the cleanest "AI without Nvidia" hedge on
the tape. The read: AI demand is not being questioned — the pricing power and
roadmap credibility of the incumbent supply chain is. VIX +3.21% to 19.30 —
still below 20 — confirms this is rotation, not liquidation. Next week is the
binary: NVDA reports Tuesday 17/11 — its first print without Huang —
and the week is capped by the Anthropic IPO.
