📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday was a textbook rotation session dressed as a quiet up-day. The headline
prints were benign — SPX +0.42% to 7,575, NDX +0.33%, DJI +0.29% — and VIX
collapsed 5.1% to 15.03. But beneath the surface, three simultaneous capital
rotations reshaped leadership, and the Russell's -0.49% was the tell that this
was a redistribution of risk, not a broad advance.

Rotation one, WITHIN technology. Megacap and semis ripped — META +5.97% on
cloud/AI-capex news, NVDA +4.03%, AMD +2.04%, CSCO +2.54%, AMAT +2.35%, LITE
+2.07% — while high-multiple software and cybersecurity were dumped: CRWD
-5.66%, ZS -5.34%, PANW -3.67%, FTNT -3.80%, DDOG -4.26%. That split is why XLK
closed up only +0.23% despite housing the day's biggest winners — software (IGV
-1.57%) and cyber (CIBR -2.52%) offset the megacap engine. This is a quality-
and-liquidity preference: capital is paying up for tangible AI infrastructure and
memory (SNDK +3.10%, [H]) and selling the richest valuation-multiple corner.

Rotation two, INTO financials. The Smart Money board is unambiguous: Fintech #3,
Reinsurance #4, Diversified Investment Services #5, Financial & Commodity Market
Operations #7 (+25 ranks in 3 days), Property & Casualty Insurance #14 — five
financial sub-industries in the top 14. Circle (CRCL) +4.97% and fintech's slot
as a 10day-hot industry (PAY, DAVE, ACIW, HOOD) confirm real institutional
accumulation. With Chair Warsh's new-look Fed keeping bond vol elevated (Man
Group's Hooper flagged exactly this), capital is positioning in rate-sensitive
financials and market-structure names that monetise volatility itself.

Rotation three, OUT of biotech and pharma. Healthcare (XLV -0.82%) was the SOLE
red sector, and it was ugly: MRNA -10.83%, IONS -9.37% (5day -28.79%), ALNY
-4.49%, BBIO -4.75%, GILD -3.72%, with IBB -2.68%. This is the mirror image of
the crowded-momentum unwind visible in the RS list, where biotech leaders QURE,
ABSI (-7.59%) and SLS (-5.55%) sold hard. Only 2 of the RS Top 20 printed new
20day highs today — the momentum book is digesting, not breaking.

The geopolitical backdrop turned sharply worse on paper yet the tape shrugged.
Trump said Iran phoned to make a deal after US strikes, then warned it is unclear
whether war is 'back on'; the ceasefire has collapsed, tankers were hit in the
Strait of Hormuz, and Washington revoked Iran's sale authorization. And yet WTI
FELL -1.22% to $71.51. CNBC framed it precisely — markets have moved 'from panic
to pricing in,' past peak fear. The nuance for positioning: spot crude fades, but
the toll-risk trade lives on the water — oil services OIH +5.74% (5day) and
shipping BOAT +4.09% (5day) are where the Hormuz premium is now expressed.
