📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
September opened the way August closed — under the twin weight of a hawkish Fed and an
oil shock. Fed Chair Warsh's Jackson Hole message ("we have work to do" on inflation)
has repriced the September meeting to a ~65% chance of a rate HIKE, up from ~40% a week
ago. The 10year yield sits near 4.8% (highest since 2023) and the 30year near 5.28%
(levels last seen in 2006-07), with the selloff global. This is the master variable for
every sector rotation below: when the discount rate is rising and energy is reigniting
inflation, long-duration equity (high-multiple software) de-rates and hard assets bid.

That is exactly what printed. The indices closed green, but the leadership was Materials
(XLB +1.69%) and the precious-metals miners — SIL +4.67%, XME +3.19%, GDX +3.13% were
the ONLY three ETFs above +3% on the day, driven by gold +2.0% and silver +2.7%. Gold
is caught in a tug-of-war (rising real yields are a headwind; geopolitical + inflation
premium a tailwind) and today the premium won. Technology (XLK -0.02%) was flat only
because a single name masked a rout underneath it.

The tech story was a violent hardware-over-software rotation. DELL +15.81% (AI-server
demand beat) was the day's top large-cap gainer and effectively held XLK flat single
handedly, while the software complex was liquidated: PANW -9.28%, CRDO -20.04%, MDB
-13.54%, DDOG -6.53%, PLTR -5.81%, CRWD -5.42%, NOW -4.32%, FTNT -4.52%, NET -4.47%,
SNOW -4.37%. In a rising-rate regime the market is paying for AI capex hardware (DELL,
and by extension the server/compute layer) and selling the high-multiple application
and security software that had led all year. This is a duration trade wearing a sector
costume — the RS and Smart Money tables below confirm the money did not leave the market,
it rotated to shorter-duration and hard-asset cash flows.

Breadth is the warning. The SPX advanced 298/201 on the session, but the quarter-to-date
internals are rolling over: only 37.2% of SPX names are above their 20day MA, T2108 sits
at 45.4% (sub-50), and — the single most important tell — the SPX printed 106 new 20day
LOWS against just 39 new 20day highs on a green day. Russell 2000, despite leading the
tape at +1.13%, is already in a Neutral Bear regime with 233 new lows vs 148 highs. A
green index built on more new lows than new highs is a market being carried by a narrow,
rotating cohort while the median stock deteriorates. Respect it.

After the close the bifurcation continued. SNOW ripped +20.4% AH on strong results and AI
coding momentum, fully reversing its -4.37% regular-session loss — a reminder that inside
the software wreckage, execution is still being rewarded selectively. AVGO fell -3.31% AH
as soft guidance overshadowed an earnings beat; NTAP -8.75% AH; and Ultragenyx (RARE)
collapsed -44.53% AH on a clinical/guidance shock. Netskope +12.44% AH and Argan +9.93%
AH were the other notable movers. Watch AVGO Wednesday as the tell for whether the
semiconductor complex follows CRDO's -20% lower or DELL's hardware bid holds.
