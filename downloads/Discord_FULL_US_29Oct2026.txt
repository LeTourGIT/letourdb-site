📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday was the session the AI trade changed its exam. Alphabet's Q3, released
Wednesday night, showed Google Cloud revenue doubling to $30.7B — and the stock
still suffered its worst day ever (-12.64%, ~$500B of value), because free cash flow
was -$9.1B for a second straight quarter and 2027 capex is heading toward ~$300B.
The market is no longer paying for AI revenue growth on its own; it is demanding cash
conversion. Amazon confirmed the pattern after the bell: AWS +39%, record margin,
and TTM FCF of -$42.4B (AH -6.6%).

Software took the second blow. ServiceNow -26.95% (worst day since its 2012 IPO) and
Fortinet -20.09% turned a two-cent EPS miss and a service-revenue guide trim into a
factor de-rating. IGV -3.52%, CIBR -2.71% and TEAM -7.28% confirm the market is
pricing AI as a disruptor of seat-based software economics rather than a tailwind.
MRVL -13.24% on reported all-stock talks to buy Lumentum added dilution fear to the
semi complex (SOXX -1.55%, MU -4.98%, SNDK -5.32%).

Underneath, the tape was strong — and that is the gold nugget. SPX fell -0.30% yet
advancers beat decliners 404 to 96 (4.2:1), stocks above the 20MA jumped from 69.4% to
75.4%, T2108 rose to 70.2% and new 20day highs nearly doubled to 156 (from 80).
That is not a sell-off; it is a rotation out of a handful of cap-heavy AI names into
everything else. The leadership tells you where it went: low-volatility SPLV +1.67%,
healthcare XLV +2.40% (LLY +6.63% beat-and-raise), real estate XLRE +2.21%, staples
and utilities +1.5%, homebuilders ITB +3.49% and defence ITA +3.16%.

Macro reinforces it: GDP +3.4% and core PCE 3.1%, a day after two FOMC hike dissents.
A Fed that cannot ease and a 10yr near 5% de-rates cash-burning growth and rewards
cash generation today. WTI near $103 on Iran brinkmanship keeps inflation alive.

Positioning: index risk is to the downside into Friday's AMZN/INTC gap, but breadth
this positive under a falling index reads as rotation, not distribution. Cautious on
megacap AI and software; constructive on the broadening layer Smart Money is buying.
