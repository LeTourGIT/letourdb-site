📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday was a textbook "negative risk trinity" — crude spiking, Treasury
yields surging to multiyear highs, equities sliding together. WTI ripped
+8.16% to $103.94 (Brent +7.90%) as OPEC+ held October output unchanged and
Trump warned Iran "not to get cute" over its Pickaxe Mountain nuclear site —
the Hormuz premium reasserting itself just as the market had begun to fade it.
A hotter wholesale (PPI) print simultaneously drove the 10year yield toward 5%
and a Treasury buyback failed to calm the long end. Oil-push inflation plus a
hawkish yield backup is the exact cocktail that compresses equity multiples.

The cleanest expression was in commodities, which split violently. Six oil-linked
sub-industries surged up the Smart Money ranks while the ENTIRE metals/miners
complex was liquidated: every one of the nine ETFs below -3% was a metal or
miner (COPX -6.99%, SLV -5.30%, REMX -5.13%, uranium -4%, silver miners -3.9%,
gold miners -3.5%). Silver futures -5.69%, gold -2.11% as the real-yield spike
knocked out the precious-metals trade that had been a 2026 workhorse. Capital
did not leave resources — it rotated hard from metals into hydrocarbons.

Within equities, selling concentrated in the AI-hardware/semiconductor complex —
the cycle's leadership. INTC -5.57% ($9.6bn turnover), MU -4.90% ($25.0bn, the
day's heaviest tape), LRCX -5.65%, ALAB -5.33%, plus AI-infra CRWV -6.13%,
VRT -5.61%, DELL -5.35%, HPE -6.25%, LITE -5.39%. CNBC's "AI is losing its
stranglehold" and "chip rebound fails" capture the psychology: a yield shock
forces a re-rate of the most richly-valued, longest-duration cohort first.

The offsetting bid was defensive and idiosyncratic, not real risk appetite:
staples/tobacco (PM +2.19%) held, managed care firmed (ELV +4.95%), autos
rallied on own catalysts (F +3.20%, GM +2.82%), and Apple stood out at +3.56%
on $22.8bn turnover — Phones & Handheld Devices vaulting to Smart Money rank 9
from rank 136 nine days ago, an institutional flight into the one megacap seen
as insulated from capex-cost anxiety. SWKS +9.79% (Apple supplier) led gainers.
But breadth told the real story — and it was ugly.
