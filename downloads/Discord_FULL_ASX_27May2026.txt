Wednesday 27 May delivered a clean 0.69% advance for the XJO to 8,717.7,
with the session entirely reframed by the April CPI data released before
market open. Headline inflation printed 4.2% — 20bp below the 4.4% consensus
— and immediately repriced rate-sensitive sectors higher. Consumer Discretionary
(+1.81%), IT (+1.78%), and Utilities (+1.70%) led the board, a textbook rotation

since late 2024, and the headline beat was substantially engineered by a

the headline; the RBA will read the underlying. This is relief, not resolution.

Micron's $1 trillion market cap milestone overnight — the global memory and
semiconductor complex is pricing a structural AI demand upcycle, and WBT's
ReRAM IP positions it directly in that narrative. Megaport (MP1) +8.63% on
separate catalysts, pressing new 20-day highs with an RS rank of #8 on the
All-ASX board. Both represent the ASX's domestically-investable expression
of the US tech ATH story.

Coal stocks NHC (+2.65%) and WHC (+2.22%) held Smart Money rank at #11 on
the rotation table — their trajectory from the bottom of the rankings over
8 weeks has been the stealth chart of the cycle. S32 (+3.46%) confirms the

Communication Services (XTJ −0.25%) — is a micro-narrative: the sector's
domestic media heavyweights and telcos have no rate-relief tailwind and face
structural headwinds irrespective of monetary policy.
