📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Melbourne Cup day delivered the RBA's fifth hike of 2026: +25bp to 4.85%, the highest cash
rate since November 2008. The XJO still closed +0.34% at 8,886.8, recovering Monday's -0.58%.
The market opened ~0.6% higher and gave it all back into 2:30pm. It briefly dipped red on the
decision, then rallied once the details landed: a 6-3 split, a statement with no tightening
bias, and a Governor emphasising lags. That is a peak-rate signal, and the tape priced it.

The tell is which sectors led. Real Estate (XRE +2.88%) was the best sector, with LLC +5.5%,
SGP +4.0%, SCG +3.7% and GPT +3.6%. Consumer Discretionary (+1.87%) followed through on
Monday's pre-positioning, with TPW +7.0%, HVN +5.8% and NCK +5.6%. Sectors that should suffer
from higher rates outperformed the ones that shouldn't. When the most rate-sensitive cohort
rallies on a hike, the bond market is saying "last one".

Energy (XEJ -3.45%) was the session's casualty. WTI settled -7.36% overnight on US-Iran talks
hopes. WDS fell -3.5%, ALD -5.0% and VEA -6.6%, and the coal names went too (YAL -6.4%,
NHC -5.4%). Uranium (NXG, DYL) was sold with the complex. The mirror image was the airlines:
VGN +8.9% and QAN +6.4% (a 20day high), as jet fuel is their largest variable cost.

Breadth confirmed the large-cap tape but not the small-cap one. XJO advancers beat decliners
123/71 (1.7:1). New 20day highs rose to 48 from 38 and lows fell to 12 from 20. XJO stocks
above their 200MA improved to 41.5% from 37.0%, which is better but still a minority. The
XAO is at 34.1% and the XSO at 32.9%: two-thirds of the broad market remain in long-term
downtrends.

The forward look is crowded. US midterms run tonight and the VIX is 21. NAB reports Thursday
and MQG Friday. Avenira (AEV) entered a late-session trading halt at 15:25. Oil remains the
master variable: fresh US strike reports on Iranian targets cut the intraday rally, and
WTI is only +0.33% at report time.
