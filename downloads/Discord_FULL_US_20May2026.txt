📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Wednesday delivered a structural macro break. Trump announcing the US-Iran
negotiations were in "final stages" sent WTI crude -5.66% to $98.26 — the
first sub-$100 close since the war escalation — unwinding the energy premium
that has dominated the macro tape since February. Treasury yields cooled on
the back of the same signal (30-year had touched 5.19% on Tuesday), giving
rate-sensitive tech and growth stocks their largest relief day in weeks. The
Dow Jones crossed 50,000 for its first clean close — a round-number milestone
that arrived on the back of Iran hopes, not NVDA (which is not in the Dow).

Semiconductor stocks led the index recovery. XSD +5.14% and SOXX +4.74%
reflect the NVDA halo — institutions pre-positioned ahead of what consensus
expected to be a clean beat. NVDA closed +1.22% on the session before
reporting Q1 FY27 results AH: revenue $81.62B (+85% YoY, vs est $79.2B),
EPS $1.87 (vs est $1.76), Data Center revenue $75.2B (+92% YoY). Q2 revenue
guide of $91B demolished the $87B consensus. The market's response was the
defining story of the night: little changed AH (~flat/slight dip), confirming
that even a historic beat against a $91B guide next quarter cannot overcome
a whisper number that had already priced perfection. NVDA +85% rev growth,
$20B shareholder returns, 25x dividend increase — and the stock went nowhere.

Airlines (JETS +6.57%) are the session's most underappreciated signal. The
directional move is a direct Iran peace trade: lower fuel costs = margin
expansion, lower yield fear = travel demand resilience. JETS leading the day
at +6.57% over XSD's +5.14% means the broadest beneficiary of an Iran
resolution just beat the AI chip complex in a single session. Homebuilders
(ITB +4.53%, XHB +4.28%) are the second-order play — Treasury yield relief
translating directly into mortgage rate expectations, the key constraint on
housing since 2024. When JETS and ITB outperform, the market is pricing
geopolitical resolution, not AI continuation.

The RUT -1.01% divergence from large-cap gains is the session's structural
warning. Small-caps are disproportionately leveraged to domestic economic
conditions — higher rates, tighter credit — and have not participated in
the large-cap recovery since the April ATH cycle. RUT 5day and 20day trends
are BOTH DOWN, a regime divergence from SPX/NDX/DJI. This is not a rotation
within small-caps — it is the entire cohort failing to respond to the same
Iran peace signal that lifted homebuilders and airlines. Watch RUT vs SPX
ratio as a leading indicator of breadth normalization.
