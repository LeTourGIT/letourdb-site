📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Wednesday was a rates day wearing a geopolitics costume. The 10-year
Treasury yield jumped roughly 11bp to 5.08% — its highest since 2007 — after
September business activity expanded at its fastest pace in five years. That
print came one week after the Fed's first quarter-point hike in more than
three years, and it pushed traders to materially lift the odds of a second
hike at the October meeting.

Oil added the second leg. WTI surged 3.81% to $92.75 after Iranian President
Pezeshkian used his UN speech to say Tehran will never surrender to U.S.
pressure — a day after Trump threatened to 'annihilate' Iran.

The third force was structural, not macro: the AI-disruption trade went into
overdrive. Meta's Muse personal agent — now the most-downloaded app on both
major stores — can research, compare and complete a booking directly with
the airline or hotel. The market repriced every business model that depends
on a shopper arriving at a toll booth: Expedia -7.72%, Airbnb -7.56%,
Booking -5.07%, AppLovin -4.10%, and Alphabet -3.5% as search-ad economics
come into question.

The mirror image was cybersecurity. Palo Alto +5.00%, CrowdStrike +4.97%,
Okta +4.45% and Fortinet +2.57% rallied as investors positioned for the AI-
safety spend that autonomous agents make unavoidable — every agent that can
transact is an attack surface.

Breadth is now the dominant risk. SPX closed with 170 advancers vs 329
decliners, only 25.8% of members above their 20MA, 28.8% above their 50MA,
T2108 at 24.6% and new 20day lows outnumbering highs 143 to 38 — yet the
index sits just +1.00% above its 50MA and +6.61% above its 200MA, with fewer
than half (47.2%) of its members above theirs.

Setup into Thursday: the Trump-Xi summit (AI policy, trade truce, Taiwan
arms) is the binary, alongside a 7-year Treasury auction that will test
whether buyers really step in around 5%. With VIX only 15.18 (+6.83%) the
options market is not paying for the breadth damage — complacency at the
index level while the median stock is already in a correction is the
definition of an asymmetric setup.

members above their 20MA (T2108 24.6%). Over 20 days IVV +0.21% vs RSP
