📰 KEY HEADLINES
---------------------------------------------------
⚠️ Unemployment surges to 4.9% -- recession warnings mount
   Australia lost 43,500 jobs in October against expectations of a gain, and
   the ABC ran three separate recession-warning stories off the print. With
   the RBA at 4.85% after five hikes this year, the market faces the worst mix
   for equities: a weakening economy that the central bank cannot yet cut
   into. Consumer credit (ZIP) and discretionary are first in line.
🏢 Anthropic lists in record IPO -- and the AI trade falls anyway
   The most duplicated story in the feed (ABC Business, ABC World, CNBC,
   MarketWatch): ANT priced at US$83, opened at US$111.50 and closed +17%, yet
   the Dow fell ~1,100 points. Australians largely missed the allocation.
   Locally the listing worked as a funding drain on AI-adjacent stocks -- NXT,
   MP1 and MAQ were sold to make room.
🇺🇸 Nvidia has shed US$611bn in six sessions; SoftBank -16%
   Nvidia's slide, California's subpoena over China chip diversion and AMD's
   short-report drop put semiconductors at the centre of the sell-off, and
   SoftBank's 16% plunge carried it through Asia. ASX semis (WBT -8.02%, SLX
   -7.89%) followed.
🏢 Xi declares 'complete turnaround' in China-Australia relations
   After the APEC meeting (Trump, Xi and Putin issued a joint declaration),
   Xi's comments are a medium-term positive for Australian exporters, but iron
   ore near US$91/t and falling Chinese steel output say demand has not yet
   followed the diplomacy.
🏢 Rio Tinto pledges $370m to Bougainville landowners
   A step toward resolving the Panguna legacy, which matters for Rio's licence
   to operate in the region. RIO closed flat (+0.04%) while Materials fell
