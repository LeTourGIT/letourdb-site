📝 SESSION SUMMARY
-----------------------------------------------------
Monday was a textbook "index says nothing, internals say plenty" session. XJO
eased 0.14% to 8,816.1, XAO -0.18% to 9,031.2 — cosmetic declines masking a
decisive rotation despite a strong US lead.

THE event: WiseTech (WTC -18.4% to ~A$30.08) cratered to multi-year lows after
reports the AFP is investigating Exec Chairman/founder Richard White over serious
allegations. For a stock already carrying the ASX's biggest governance discount,
this is pure key-person repricing. WTC alone drove IT (XIJ) -4.20%, the worst
sector by far. CargoWise franchise (1H NPAT A$114.5m, guidance reaffirmed) is
intact — a trust event, not an earnings event.

Materials (XMJ -0.61%, -4.93% over 5 days) was the second anchor: iron ore stuck
near US$101/t (-8% MoM), lithium range-bound as Aussie mine restarts add supply.
A sharp regime change from early-May's lithium-led resources rally — resources
are now a DRAG, not a leader. The AUD slide to ~0.70 (from 0.72 six weeks ago)
partly cushions USD miner revenues.

Where money went: Consumer Disc (+0.56%), Financials (+0.50%), Industrials
(+0.48%), Utilities (+0.32%) — the domestic, rate-sensitive, cyclical end. Gold
miners led the gainer board (BNZ +11%, OBM +6.6%, KCN +4.6%) on firm gold ~$4,216.
Construction names (SXE +5.0%, FBU +4.9%, PRN, MI6) confirmed the infra/housing
theme that dominates the Smart Money table.

The enabler: RBA's 16 June hold at 4.35% (first pause after 3 hikes), NAB flagging
the next move is likely DOWN, and the US-Iran roadmap reopening Hormuz and easing
the oil-inflation impulse. A market that believes rates have peaked rotates exactly
this way. XSO's Bullish regime is that thesis in price.
