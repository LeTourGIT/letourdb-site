📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday delivered one of the widest single-session dispersions of the year. The
cap-weighted tape printed green — SPX +0.70%, NDX +0.60%, DJI +0.53% — but the
gains were manufactured almost entirely by a handful of mega-cap earnings winners.
Amazon +15.3% (AWS reacceleration, margin beat, Jassy defusing the AI-capex
anxiety) and Alphabet +6.7% did the heavy lifting, with Microsoft +3.0% and Meta
+3.3% adding ballast. Strip those out and the market was heavy: SPX advancers
trailed decliners 220 to 279, Russell 2000 fell -0.50% on 820/1096 breadth, and
SPX new 20day lows (45) outnumbered new highs (21) on an up day.

The offsetting shock was Apple -7.35% — an iPhone and guidance miss that handed
the most-valuable-company crown back to Nvidia and single-handedly pinned XLK to
-0.22% despite genuine strength beneath it (MPWR +8.4%, ANET +5.5%, SNPS +4.4%,
COHR +5.6%). The real damage was in the momentum and single-name earnings cohort:
RBLX -26.9%, RDDT -21.0%, MTZ -18.9%, GDDY -16.7%, CTVA -11.9%, COIN -10.6% and
NBIX -10.1% were all earnings-driven air-pockets. This is dispersion, not
contagion — but with NDX already Bearish, the market has no cushion for misses.

Geopolitics re-entered hard. Iran escalated — striking US assets in Kuwait and
a first drone attack on Egypt — sending WTI +5.39% to $86.80 and Brent +2.35%.
Crucially, energy EQUITIES lagged the futures: XLE +1.0%, USO +0.91%, XOP +1.45%.
The market is pricing this as a geopolitical risk premium, not a demand signal —
corroborated by VIX collapsing -6.44% to 15.99. When crude spikes 5% and the fear
gauge falls 6%, traders are treating the oil move as a headline, not a regime.

Beneath the surface, leadership has rotated defensive. The RS Top 20 is now
dominated by healthcare and biotech (AGL, FBRX, CORT, CRNX, AGEN, CDNA, QURE) —
a stark contrast to the semiconductor-led leaderboards of the spring. Smart Money
industry rankings are reshuffling violently (Construction & Engineering to #1 from
#130, Renewable Energy #12 from #127), the signature of a market repricing
leadership rather than trending. Gold miners were dumped (GDX -3.49%, SIL -3.20%)
as long-end yields backed up on the Warsh credibility debate — a rare risk-off
session where both crypto AND precious metals fell together.
