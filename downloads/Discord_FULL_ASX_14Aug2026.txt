📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday 14/08/2026 saw the XJO give back -0.80% to 9,115.2 and the XAO
-0.73% to 9,313.2 — a shallow, rotation-driven pullback rather than a
risk-off event. The split was stark: Info Technology (XIJ +2.83%) topped the
board on the back of Wall Street's record close, while Materials (XMJ -2.58%)
single-handedly anchored the index lower. This was a resources-led decline that
is the mirror image of a resources-led rally — the same sector, opposite sign.

The materials drag is fundamentally sourced. Iron ore is pinned near 14-month
lows around US$95/t (62% Fe), with the Dalian CNY contract approaching CNY 700/t
on soft Chinese steel margins; the one offset — a widening strike at BHP's Port
Hedland export hub — is a supply, not demand, story and hasn't lifted equities.
Integrated miners (DVP -6.71%, SRL -5.62%, MI6 -5.13%) and gold spec names (WIA,
MEK) led the sector lower. SRL's fall still leaves it +10.67% on the week — this
is profit-taking in strong names, not capitulation.

The offset was reporting season and offshore tech. IPG +13.10% (Appendix 4E +
FY26 results, lodged 08:24-08:35, price-sensitive) was the clean earnings winner;
SEK +9.13% and CAR +5.47% extended post-result runs. Large-cap software rode the
US Nasdaq record: WTC +5.55%, XRO +5.54%, XYZ (Block) +6.07%. On the debit side,
SKG -8.30% and QBE -1.62% show the market is not paying up indiscriminately for
results — the reaction is being earned line by line.

Breadth confirms the pullback rather than contradicting it: XJO advancers trailed
decliners 67/127 (0.53:1), yet 67% of the 200 remain above their 20day MA and
T2108 sits at a healthy 71%. This is a market thinning at the surface while its
internals stay firm. With the RBA out of the way until late September, the real
test is whether iron ore stabilises and whether reporting-season beats can keep
offsetting the resource complex. Watch NextDC — its FY results webcast invite
(17:17, post-close) flags results imminently.
