📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Friday was a reflex bounce inside a broken week, not a reversal. Thursday's AI-led liquidation
(S&P -4.36%, Nasdaq Composite -5.52%, the Nasdaq 100's worst day since 2020) was the market
pricing the Democratic sweep as a regulatory tax on the AI data-centre buildout, on top of
AI-credit stress. Friday got NDX back +1.28% and SPX +0.27%, but the week still closed
SPX -2.7%, Nasdaq -3.4% (fourth straight weekly loss, in correction) and Dow -0.9%.

The macro catalyst cut both ways. Payrolls +165K vs ~60K, unemployment 4.1% and +84K of
revisions killed the growth scare, so chips could bounce. But December hike odds rose to ~78%
and the Fed's Daly warned of more hikes. Strong data in a hiking cycle = a higher discount rate
for long-duration growth, which is why the bounce faded and most stocks closed red.

The breadth gives it away: SPX +0.27% on 183 adv / 316 dec with RSP -0.38% (a 65bp cap-weight
gap); NDX +1.28% on 46/54. This was a cap-weighted short-cover in AI semis (XSD +5.79% ⚠ EXTREME,
AMD +11.04%, SITM +17.44%) and AI power/hosting (OKLO +22.66%, IREN +20.02%). SOXX is still
-13.13% over 5days; the most-damaged names bouncing hardest is short-covering, not accumulation.

The structural insight: SPX is 4.10% below its 50MA, yet 61.4% of its members are above their
own 50MA and T2108 is 65%. The damage sits in mega-cap AI weights; the average stock is intact.
This is rotation, not a bear market in stocks: XLK -8.23% over 5days vs XLC +4.89%, XLRE +1.92%,
XLY +1.91%. Smart Money shows the destination: consumer travel (Airlines rank 4, Hotels rank 5)
and hard assets (Gold SURGE to rank 7) — while AI-capex plumbing is exited (Electrical
Components to rank 70, Construction & Engineering to rank 84).

Energy is the other unwind: XLE -12.00% over 5days, WTI $90.34, Brent $97.76. Cheaper fuel
feeds the travel bid. Oil & Gas Drilling fell from Smart Money rank 8 to rank 129 in nine days.

Until NDX reclaims its 200MA (-3.68%), treat AI rallies as supply; the long side lives in the
RS list's healthcare leg and the Smart Money travel/metals rotation. CoreWeave and Nebius
earnings next week are the direct test of AI credit.
