📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Monday was a chip-led relief rally that reversed the prior week's AI-complex unwind.
CNBC's session headline said it plainly — the S&P closed higher as chips staged a
comeback while Iran halted strikes on Israel. The semiconductor and AI-hardware
complex, epicentre of the recent drawdown (South Korea's tech-heavy KOSPI had shed
5%+ following Wall Street's AI names lower), was the epicentre of the recovery: AMD
+6.61%, QCOM +5.80%, ANET +8.31%, CRDO +9.77%, WDC +7.14%, ALAB +6.48%, MRVL, DELL
+4.43% and NTAP +6.08% all ripped. This is a V-shaped snapback in the exact names
that led the sell-off — classic wash-out mechanics, not fresh accumulation.

The macro backdrop de-risked in tandem. OPEC+ approved a further output increase just
as Hormuz exports begin recovering, and Brent has now posted its biggest monthly
decline since 2020 — WTI sits at $68.68, a world away from the $100+ war premium of
the spring. Falling oil + a Fed-pause narrative (weak jobs growth, easing crude, FOMC
minutes on deck) is the goldilocks cocktail risk assets want. VIX collapsed -3.59% to
15.57. The unwind of the geopolitical fear bid is why energy (XLE -0.17%) sat out the
rally entirely while every growth-cyclical sector caught a bid.

The risk-on signal was loudest in the highest-beta corners. Crypto proxies surged —
ETHA +5.37%, IBIT +3.58%, with Ether ETFs the single best-performing equity ETF group
on the board (+13.9% over 5day). Crypto-miners and treasuries followed: IREN +13.11%,
BitMine (BMNR) +8.29%. Power/electrification names joined — Bloom Energy +8.92%, FCEL
+5.76%, VRT +5.97% — the "AI needs electrons" trade reasserting alongside the silicon.
Autos ran too (RIVN +8.11%, TSLA +6.69%, CARZ ETF +3.38%). When crypto, chips, power
and autos all lead the same session, that is beta being re-embraced wholesale.

Not everything participated, and the laggards tell the rotation story. The defensive
and rate-sensitive complex was sold to fund the beta chase: Staples XLP -1.05%,
Healthcare XLV -1.09%, Utilities XLU -1.01%, Real Estate XLRE -0.87% all red on a
green tape. Auto-parts retailers were hammered on their own idiosyncratic thread —
ORLY -6.66%, AZO -6.38% — while space/sat names (RKLB -7.34%, ASTS -5.27%) and
Solstice Advanced Materials (SOLS -15.14%) took stock-specific hits. Microsoft's
4,800-job cut and plan to spin off four Xbox gaming studios is a structural cost-out
signal that megacap capex discipline is arriving even as AI hardware re-rates.

Breadth was healthy but not euphoric. SPX advancers trailed decliners 219 to 278 —
a green index on 2:3 negative breadth, the cap-weight fingerprint of a few large chip
names doing the heavy lifting. But the deeper internals are constructive: T2108 at
67.6%, 67.2% of SPX above both 20MA and 50MA, and 98 new 20day highs vs only 17 new
lows. Russell breadth was near-even (991/982) with 347 new 20day highs — small-caps
quietly broadening. This looks like a durable base-building bounce with room to run,
provided the chip leadership doesn't fade into FOMC minutes.
