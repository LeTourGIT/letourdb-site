Twilio -3.65%, plus Meta -3.38% and Uber -4.05%. This is a clean intra-AI rotation
— OUT of the application/software layer, INTO infrastructure/hardware. A positioning
shift, not a growth scare: RUT +0.61% with 1,151 advancers.

transit, Houthi Red Sea attacks continuing — yet WTI fell 1.96% to $82.60. Closed
strait, falling crude = the market pricing in the war rather than re-fearing it,
which is why gasoline eased and helped CPI. Energy sat it out (XLE +0.16%).
