📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
A quiet +0.59% index day hid one of the sharpest industry repricings of
the year. SpaceX bought a nationwide 800 MHz low-band spectrum portfolio
from Grain Management (~$8B, FCC approval pending) to turn Starlink Mobile
into "a major mobile carrier". Low-band reaches indoors, which is what makes
satellite a real terrestrial threat. VZ had its worst day since 2002
(-10.14%), TMUS fell -13.27% and T -10.82%, on $14.1B of carrier/cable
turnover. ASTS -10.48% shows a better-funded rival has taken the satellite
challenger's moat. Tower REITs were the mirror trade: CCI +15.60% and AMT
+9.30% on the logic that a fourth carrier must rent steel.

Inside tech, money left chips for software and security. ZS +7.80%, SNOW
+7.42%, DDOG +7.11%, PLTR +5.17% and PANW +5.09% traded $16.5B. Twelve
software names printed 20day highs. Semiconductors fell to Smart Money rank
88 from rank 10 three days ago (INTC 5day -12.26%). Profits are being taken
on the hardware layer and redeployed into the application layer above it,
a late-cycle AI rotation rather than a broken trend.

Healthcare rounded it out. MRNA hit an ATH on forced NDX-100 buying, and HUM
topped the 2027 Star Ratings while ALHC sank -13.38%. Energy split: Brent
+1.47% on Iran strike risk, but refiners fell (CRAK -1.56%) as the
Trump-Putin Russian diesel deal threatens crack spreads.
