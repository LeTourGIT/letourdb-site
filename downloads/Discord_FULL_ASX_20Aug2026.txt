📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday 20/08/2026 was a two-engine session: a broad gold-miner surge and August full-year
results. Materials (XMJ +3.52%) led every sector, powered by gold names making fresh 20day
highs — Ora Banda +14.03%, Genesis Minerals +10.33%, Regis +10.92%, Capricorn +10.28%. Gold
holding near record levels (~US$4,545/oz) plus concrete earnings (GMD's EBITDA more than
doubled to A$952m) means this is a re-rating with cash behind it, not just a bullion-price chase.

Reporting season supplied the individual fireworks. On the beat side: Zip +18.22% and Super
Retail +15.05% on FY26 results, MA Financial +18.14% on a 1H26 beat, Universal Store +17.73%,
and Codan +12.42% — the last confirming why Electronic Equipment sits atop Smart Money. On
the miss side, IDP Education cratered -20.74% on a guidance shock, Downer EDI -10.34% on a
weak Preliminary Final, and Sonic Healthcare -9.25%. Results, not macro, set the dispersion.

Financials (XFJ -1.93%) was the clear laggard, but the driver was specific, not systemic:
insurers derated hard (Helia -11.09%, Medibank -6.36%, NIB -5.34%, IAG -4.18%) on results and
sympathy selling. This is an insurance-sector event inside Financials, not a banks-led de-rate.

AUD/USD at 0.7124 (-0.06%) was a non-event — no revenue-compression drag on the miners today,
which let the gold and iron-ore names run cleanly. Iron ore is firm (~US$105/t) on Chinese
restocking, with July ore imports +6% YoY even as crude-steel output fell 3.1% — a supportive
backdrop for BHP/RIO/FMG ahead of Fortescue's result due today.
