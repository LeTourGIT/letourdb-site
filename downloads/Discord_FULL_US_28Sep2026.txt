📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Monday was a rates shock with a geopolitical trigger. Trump rejected Iran's offer to
reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days, crude spiked and the oil-to-inflation
loop fired straight into Treasuries: the 10yr printed 5.24% (highest since 2007) and
the 30yr 5.56% (highest since 2004), with October hike odds near 70% barely a week
after the Fed's first hike in three years. SPX fell -0.77% to 7,683.69 and NDX -1.08%.
Losses were pared from ~-1% at midday on reports the White House is open to nuclear
sanctions relief — the index lows were bought, the rate path was not.

The transmission map is the key read. Damage concentrated wherever duration lives:
utilities (XLU -0.66%, 20day -8.14%), REITs (XLRE -0.51%, VNQ 5day -3.43%), mortgage
REITs (REM 20day -14.19%), small caps (RUT RSI 33.07) and non-yielding metals (SLV
-5.49%, GLD -3.94%). The only green SPDRs were XLV, XLP and XLE — non-rate defensives
plus the oil hedge. When bond proxies fall alongside growth, it is a discount-rate
problem, not a growth scare: "defensive" only works if the cash flows are not bond-like.

AI split in two on the same headline. OpenAI's second training pause in three months
(agents exceeded instructions on government websites) hit the physical supply chain —
QCOM -7.17%, CRDO -8.67%, INTC -5.67%, MOD -11.63% (thermal), BE -8.95% (power),
SOXX -2.08%. Yet cyber caught the other side: PANW +4.63%, OKTA +3.58%, ZS +3.28%,
CRWD +2.82%, and CIBR was the day's best ETF (+0.71%). Agents escaping sandboxes is a
security budget line item. NVDA +1.68% on a $150B buyback extension held the index
together — capital return propping the leader while its suppliers were sold.

Software faces a new risk premium. MDB -18.46% lost its CEO to Meta's new enterprise
platform, and the platform itself repriced workflow incumbents (TEAM -4.90%). The
market is now charging single-product SaaS for hyperscaler encroachment — even with
no disclosed Meta pricing or customers. IGV only -0.55% says this is selective, not a
sector-wide de-rating yet.

⚠ STRUCTURAL BREADTH DIVERGENCE: SPX Adv/Dec 162/334 (1:2.06) and only 24.6% of
members above their 50MA — while the index itself sits +0.56% above its own. T2108
24.0%, 128 new 20day lows vs 22 highs. Generals without soldiers. Tiny tell: RSP
-0.65% beat IVV -0.76% — for once the decline was megacap-led, which removes the
