A flat tape hid three colliding flows. SPX closed at 7,805.45 (-0.02%) as a
sixth straight session of semiconductor liquidation (chips' worst stretch in
16 months per MarketWatch, framed as 'AI fatigue') met a bank-earnings kick-
off the market sold and a refiner-led energy bid as WTI rose +2.81% to
$95.24. The Dow snapped a three-session win streak (-0.35%), and RUT
(-0.40%) now sits 0.29% below its 200day MA.

Chips: the liquidation has migrated to the capex layer. AMAT -6.69% (5day
-12.45%) led equipment lower while analog and compute bounced (TXN +2.17%,
ADI +2.08%, AMD +1.90%), leaving SOXX -0.17% and XSD +0.12% on the day.
Equipment is the second derivative of AI spend, so it reprices first when
capex pacing is questioned. Analog is levered to an industrial recovery, not
AI. This is an AI-pacing repricing, not a semi bear market: SOXX is still
+9.03% over 20day.

Banks: C -5.60%, WFC -3.10%, JPM -0.86% and GS -0.23% on Q3 prints, with KKR
-4.75% and BX -4.18% joining. The tell is the capital structure: JPM and WFC
preferreds rallied +1% to +2% as the 10-year yield eased, while the commons
fell. Credit is comfortable; equity is de-rating the earnings path. The
group had already sold off on yields into the prints and still could not
clear a low bar. (Line-item results were not sourced this session; this read
comes from price action.)

Energy: growing Republican opposition to Trump's Russia diesel deal ('dead
on arrival' after the midterms, per one expert) removed hoped-for distillate
relief, and refiners led: VLO +4.69% (0.2% from its 52-week high), PSX
+4.00% and MPC +3.96% are three of the top four large-cap gainers, and CRAK
+3.24% was the top ETF. Iran's reported nuclear-site fortification and
continued attacks on Saudi Arabia rebuilt the risk premium. The Brent-WTI
spread narrowed from $12.80 to $11.66, which points to US-centric tightness.

Healthcare and breadth: JNJ -5.73% on earnings (2027 patent-cliff overhang)
sank XLV -1.35%, the worst sector. SPX breadth was positive (263/236) with
20day highs up to 94, and RPV +0.65% beat RPG -0.50%, so the average large
cap beat the index. Small caps are in distribution: RUT Adv/Dec 792/1,134,
