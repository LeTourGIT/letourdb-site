📝  SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday 17 Sep printed a green headline — XJO +0.41% to 8,732.4, a
second straight up-day off Monday's 8,672 low — but the internals told a
different story. Advancers (94) trailed decliners (97): the gain was purely
cap-weighted, carried by the banks, while the median ASX 200 stock fell.
This is a narrow, low-quality bounce inside an intact Bearish downtrend, not
a reversal. The 15day ROC is still -3.38% and RSI-15 sits at 34.1.

The driver was offshore and structural: the US Fed's first rate hike in three
years to ~4% forced a global re-pricing higher in yields. On the ASX that
expressed as a defensive, rate-winner rotation. Financials (XFJ +1.42%) led
every sector — banks and insurers are the classic higher-for-longer NIM
beneficiaries — with Healthcare (XHJ +0.98%) and Real Estate (XRE +0.66%)
the other green pockets. Materials, IT and Energy all fell.

The session's sharpest move was in the gold miners — but to the downside.
WAF -7.37%, PNR -7.45%, PDI -6.02%, RMS -4.14% and CMM -3.42% dragged XMJ to
a 5day loss of -5.45%, the worst weekly damage on the board. None carried a
same-day announcement: this was crowded-trade profit-taking. The irony is
that gold names remain the 3month RS leaders (SBM [H], TCG, ALK, RSG) — the
structural uptrend is intact; today was positioning unwinding into the Fed.

At the other end, defence was the standout theme: EOS +7.99% and DRO +6.77%
directly confirm Smart Money's top-ranked Aerospace & Defense surge. Uranium
(BOE +7.54%, VR8 topping the RS table) and med-tech (EIQ +14.56% on an FDA
filing update, AYA +10.98%, 4DX +7.34%) rounded out the leadership — a
barbell of hard-asset and idiosyncratic-catalyst names against a soft tape.
