📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Monday's headline damage was invisible at the index level and total beneath it.
Iran hardened its stance on the Strait of Hormuz — denying direct talks with the
US and setting conditions for reopening — and the market repriced the entire energy
complex in one session. WTI +4.40% to $82.28, Brent +4.04%, USO +7.16%. This is the
exact mirror image of the Iran peace-signal sessions earlier in the cycle: the same
Hormuz variable, now running the other direction. The US SPR falling below 300M
barrels (lowest since 1983) removes the release valve that capped prior spikes —
structurally, the policy buffer against an oil shock is thinner than at any point
in four decades.

The rotation was not confined to oil. The dominant institutional story is a wholesale
move into hard assets. Silver ripped +3.32% (SLV 5day +13.25%), Gold +1.39%, and the
Smart Money rotation board is now almost entirely mining and metals: Mining Support
Services vaulted to rank 1 (from rank 134 nine days ago), with Precious Metals rank 2,
Gold rank 3, Specialty Mining rank 4, Integrated Mining rank 5, Uranium rank 6 and
Aluminum rank 9. The 10day hot-sector leaders confirm it — Alcoa, Century Aluminum,
Kaiser Aluminum, Aura Minerals, Endeavour Silver, USA Rare Earth. MP Materials +6.95%
tied to the DoD's rare-earth miner investment. When oil, precious metals, industrial
metals AND rare earths surge together while the SPR empties, the tape is pricing a
hard-asset / inflation-hedge regime, not a one-off oil headline.

The funding source for that rotation was the semiconductor and optical complex. CNBC's
read — "chip stock rebound fails" — showed up in the tape: XSD (semis) -3.54% and JETS
(airlines, oil victims) -3.38% were the only two ETFs below -3%. Coherent Corp cratered
-14.24%, Lumentum -8.61%, Ciena -5.99%, Corning -4.78% — the entire AI-optical
interconnect layer sold; Astera Labs -5.07% and Marvell -4.65% carried the semis. This
is what flipped NDX to Bearish. Notably it was NOT all tech: software and security held
up (DDOG +11.48%, RBRK +8.70%, PANW +5.82%, CRWD +5.01%) — capital rotated within tech
from the AI-hardware layer toward software, at the same time it rotated out of tech
into materials. Two rotations stacked on one flat tape.

Rate-sensitives were the collateral damage: Utilities -1.10% and Real Estate -1.29%
were the worst SPDRs, tracking the "rates higher for longer" narrative and a 10year
yield rising into this week's CPI print. Nvidia's $500B financing push with Wall Street
asset managers (Jensen Huang: chips are an "investable asset") is the one megacap AI
headline holding the structural bull thesis together — but it did not stop the semi tape
from breaking today.

Breadth ratified the caution. SPX advancers/decliners 228/326 negative, and Russell
2000 was brutal at 627/1296 — a 1:2 decliner ratio beneath a -0.56% headline. When the
broadest index is 2:1 negative, the "flat day" framing collapses: this was a distribution
session dressed as a quiet one.
