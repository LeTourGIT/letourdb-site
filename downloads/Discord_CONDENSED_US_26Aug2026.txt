📝 SESSION SUMMARY

Nvidia delivered a blowout — Q2 revenue $96.2B (+106% YoY), EPS $2.22, ahead of
the ~$92B Street — and the stock STILL fell -1.59% (and -1.07% AH). When you ARE
the trade, execution stops being a catalyst and becomes a prerequisite. The
market has priced perfection in AI-infrastructure and moved on to the next
question. That question got answered after the bell, and it wasn't about chips.

The after-hours tape was a software and cybersecurity blowout: Okta +19%,
Salesforce +13% (AI growth plus a mark-up on its Anthropic stake), CrowdStrike
+11%, Nutanix +9%, Veeva +8%, SailPoint +7%, SentinelOne +5%, Tenable +5%, Palo
Alto +5%, ServiceNow +4%. Capital is rotating from the AI-INFRASTRUCTURE layer
(NVDA) to the AI-APPLICATION and SECURITY layer, where monetization is now a
real revenue line in enterprise P&Ls, not a capex promise. HPQ -9% AH (soft
guide) is the clean counter: the market pays for AI that prints, not hardware.

Intraday confirmed the same message. "Chip rebound fails" — the speculative
AI-power/quantum complex was dumped (IREN -6%, OKLO -6%, IONQ -5%, RDDT -4%),
while cyclicals and value led: Industrials +1.09%, Energy +0.60%, Utilities
+0.46%. Long-duration and defensives sagged: Healthcare -1.00% (worst), Cons
Disc -0.67%, Real Estate -0.60%. This is a rates-regime trade: July PCE hot at
3.7% headline (core 3.3%, 65th month above target), a hawkish FOMC hold with
three dissents FOR a hike, and the 30year yield near 5.17% — a 2007 high — into
Fed Chair Warsh's Jackson Hole keynote Friday. Sticky inflation + hawkish Fed +
record long yields is exactly why growth stalled and cyclicals led.

The third force is Iran/Hormuz. Sixth month of conflict, ceasefire window
missed; Bessent threatens an "economic D-Day" of sanctions while Iran threatens
vessel seizures and tolls in the strait that carries ~25% of seaborne crude.
Tanker rates and war-risk insurance are spiking — which is why Smart Money is
pouring into shipping and transport. WTI finished +1.94% at $81.83 on a
two-sided tape (sanctions "less severe than feared"; Iran-Oman transit talks).
