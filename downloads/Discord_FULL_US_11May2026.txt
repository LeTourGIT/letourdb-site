📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Monday's session delivered the paradox of the current bull market: new ATH
closes alongside a VIX spike, negative breadth, and oil rising on geopolitical
deterioration. SPX added 0.19% to 7,412.84 — its third consecutive ATH close —
while VIX jumped 6.92% to 18.38. These two facts co-existing encodes the market's
current message: tech momentum is strong enough to overwhelm Iran risk, but the
risk is not going away.

The catalyst architecture was semiconductor-led, not news-driven. XSD +3.85% and
SOXX +2.39% were the session's only ±3% ETF flags. Smart Money rank #1 Semiconductors
with 10day ROC of +22.43% — the industry group with the single highest rate of
institutional capital accumulation in the market. This is a capex-cycle call. Memory
(MRAM +47.68% on $40M DoD subcontract + earnings), compute (MU +6.5%, AMD +0.79%,
INTC +3.62%), and AI silicon all participated. The Pentagon continues to serve as
the structural demand backstop: DoD money flowing to MRAM validates the same
government AI thesis that drove NVDA/MSFT/AMZN/Reflection AI last Friday.

Monday.com's +21% session provided the software counterpart. MNDY's Q1 print
(revenue $351M +24% YoY, EPS $1.15 vs $0.93 est, AI driving 10% of net new ARR)
is the most important enterprise software datapoint of the day — not because of
MNDY's own size, but because it directly contradicts the AI-disruption thesis for
the SaaS layer. AI is compounding their ARR, not cannibalising it. BAND, RXT, and
INOD all moved in sympathy. The Five9 thesis from last Friday is now a confirmed
pattern, not an outlier.

The geopolitical backdrop darkened. Trump declared the ceasefire "on life support"
and Iran's counter-proposal "TOTALLY UNACCEPTABLE," sending WTI higher. Yet equities
absorbed oil's rise without breaking — a sign of institutional conviction in the
tech thesis's independence from energy resolution. JETS -3.17% and XRT -3.61% are
the exceptions: airlines and retail face direct margin compression from sustained
high oil and cautious consumer (DG -5.8% guidance miss). These two sectors are the
honest read of Main Street risk embedded in Wall Street's ATH.

The breadth structure remains the single most important warning in this market.
SPX Adv/Dec: 214/286, negative for the fourth session in five. % above 20MA at
only 41.8%. At 7,412 SPX, only 41.8% of S&P 500 members are above their own
20-day moving average. This is a cap-weight ATH, not a broad-market ATH. A handful
of semiconductor and AI-platform names are mathematically distorting the index.
