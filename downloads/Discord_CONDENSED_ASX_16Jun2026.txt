📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
A near-dead-flat headline session hid a violent rotation underneath.
Trump's weekend US-Iran peace deal (Hormuz reopening) sent Wall
Street higher overnight (Nasdaq +3.07%, best day since March) and
oil into a multi-day collapse, while the RBA's 2:30pm hold at 4.35%
was a non-event — every major bank had it priced. ASX 200 futures
opened down 1.10%, the cash index fell as much as -0.90% intraday,
then fully recovered to close +0.04%. Underneath, capital rotated
hard out of defense/coal/critical-minerals and into insurance,
consumer retail and construction supplies — a textbook "peace
dividend" trade. SXE (+19.90%) was the standout on a NEXTDC data
centre contract; the critical-minerals complex (LTR, CXO, IPX) and
oil-linked KAR (-11.56%) wore the other side of the trade. All Ords
breadth was actually negative (203 advancers vs 233 decliners)
despite the index closing positive — a cap-weighting divergence.

🏦 RBA WATCH
---------------------------------------------------
HELD at 4.35% (unanimous), 3rd straight hold-after-hike check since
the May rise. Statement: oil eased recently but related prices still
above pre-conflict levels; inflation "still too high"; Board watching
for the oil impulse to NOT become embedded. CBA/NAB/ANZ tip an August
CUT; Westpac tips hikes in both August AND September — a rare 3-way
split. Implication: AUD's -0.20% drift is a non-event drift, not a
shock — the real swing meeting is now August, contingent on whether
oil's collapse holds through the 24 June CPI print.
