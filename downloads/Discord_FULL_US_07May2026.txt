📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday delivered the market's first meaningful cooling after an extraordinary seven-day
run that saw SPX and NDX print successive ATHs. The session was defined by a three-way
collision: semiconductor profit-taking after weeks of vertical accumulation, Iran's
declaration that the U.S. had violated the ceasefire by striking Qeshm Port, and a
$2.2 billion earnings burst from Datadog (+30% AH) and Cloudflare (+15% AH) that is
poised to reignite the software/cloud AI thesis at Friday's open.

The semi pullback was entirely predictable given the sector's technical condition. Micron,
Broadcom, and AMD (the latter up 19% on Wednesday) each gave back session gains as NDX
RSI-15 hit 78.16 — the first extreme overbought reading of the 2026 cycle. XSD -1.53%
was the tell: capital was rotating out of semi equipment and fabless leaders into
cybersecurity (CIBR +4.23%), cloud software (WCLD +3.03%), and select reopening names
(JETS +1.28%). This is the same accumulation-layer rotation visible in the RS table —
equipment sliding, software ascending.

The Iran ceasefire violation is the session's geopolitical wildcard. Tehran claimed U.S.
strikes on Qeshm Port (and "cooperation of regional countries") broke the month-long
truce. WTI initially plunged below $92 intraday on peace deal optimism before recovering
sharply to close $94.81 (-0.28%), dragging energy from the largest sector laggard to a
merely modest loser. The whipsaw in crude is the market telling you it does not yet
believe this is a binary peace-or-war moment — Goldman's 4-6 month normalisation timeline
is still the base case. Critically, the U.S. Trade Court ruling striking down Trump's 10%
global tariff is a structural positive that received almost no session airtime — watch
for that to surface in Monday pricing once traders process its second-order implications.

SPX breadth held slightly better than the prior ATH session but was still negative:
172 advancers vs 327 decliners. The index closed down only -0.38% because mega-cap tech
still provided a cushion (AAPL touching an ATH at $290.33 intraday, Intel hitting
all-time highs back to its 1971 IPO). Dow -0.63% vs NDX -0.13% confirms the growth
rotation remains structurally intact even in down sessions. The RUT at -1.74% is the
canary: small-caps have the highest energy and industrial weighting, and those sectors
are pricing in renewed geopolitical premium rather than imminent resolution.

After-hours: Datadog +30%, Cloudflare +15% (revenue $639.8M, +34% YoY, announced
20% workforce AI-restructuring), and Fortinet's +24% session (reported Q1 beat Wednesday
AH, surged Thursday) have collectively re-rated the cloud/cybersecurity thesis in one
session. The software layer — which was the RS #6 Smart Money entrant today — is now
receiving institutional flow directly. WCLD's 5day return of +9.7% vs XSD's +7.3%
confirms the rotation is already underway in cumulative terms.
