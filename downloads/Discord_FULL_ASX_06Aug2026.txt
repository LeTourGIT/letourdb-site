📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday 06/08/2026 delivered a second consecutive ASX 200 record close (9,271.6,
+0.47%), capping a four-session advance. Two engines drove it: a surging gold
complex and a broad miner rally on hopes of a US-Iran peace deal reopening the
Strait of Hormuz — which pushed oil and bond yields lower and lifted risk appetite.

Materials (XMJ +1.11%) led, with RIO/BHP/FMG +1-1.4% as copper hit a 3-month high
and iron ore futures snapped a declining streak. But the day's real signature was
gold: spot's surge lifted the ASX gold sub-index >11% in two sessions, and the
gainer board is a sea of gold small-caps — plus ASX200 producers PNR +9.95% and
MI6 +10.61%. Gold vaulted 45 ranks in the Smart Money table to #14.

Beneath the resources story sits a quieter, more important rotation. The Smart
Money top 10 is dominated by CONSUMER CYCLICALS — Airlines #1, Appliances #2,
Leisure #3, Home Furnishings #4, Restaurants #5, Apparel #6. This is capital
positioning for an RBA that has pivoted from feared-hiker to near-certain holder
after soft June CPI (3.6% trimmed mean). Peak-rates expectations are being
expressed through household-spending names — the cleanest macro tell of the day.

The one dissent is Real Estate (XRE -0.90%), the sole materially red sector —
rate-sensitive profit-taking into decision week, not fear. Financials (+0.45%)
held firm ahead of CBA's 12 Aug result; the banks are already +10% since July.
Reporting season is live: REA +3.4% and AMP +5.96% on results, AMP printing a
fresh 20-day high with RS 97.

The structural caveat: the large-cap complex is overbought (RSI-15 73-76) at
records, and XSO remains Neutral Bear below its 200MA. The record is real and
broad (XJO adv/dec 135/55, 82 new 20-day highs vs 2 lows) but late-stage.
Bullock's Tuesday statement is the next test.
