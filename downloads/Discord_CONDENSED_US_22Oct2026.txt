📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
A rotation, not a liquidation. SPX had its worst day since July yet two of
every three constituents rose. Meta creating a non-voting share class to
fund AI, Lam's slightly-light guide and CoreWeave's debt worries (-11%) told
the market the AI build is now funded by equity issuance and leverage — so
the supply chain was repriced on cost of capital. Equipment, optical,
connectivity, PCB and power names fell 10-28%; SOX lost roughly $616B and
hit its lowest close since 29/07. NVDA (-4.3%) was the heaviest S&P drag.

The money stayed in equities. The 10-year's 17bp collapse (biggest drop in
2+ years) with jobless claims at a three-month low is a flight-to-quality
bid, not a growth scare — and it poured into housing (ITB +5.39%, LEN
+8.69%), regional banks (KRE +2.53%), REITs and staples (XLP +3.31%,
mirroring XLK -3.29%). Software held inside a crashing tech sector: ROP
+9.73%, ADBE +6.07%, ACN +7.69% — AI adopters bought with the proceeds of
selling AI builders.

Brent hit $112.80, a five-month high, on attacks on Saudi infrastructure
and reports of renewed Iran strike plans — yet XLE fell -0.87%. The Defense
Production Act puts a policy ceiling on refining margins. Gold also fell
-2.8% on a day yields plunged: that is liquidity selling across every
crowded long (AI, metals, crypto), and it may not be finished.
