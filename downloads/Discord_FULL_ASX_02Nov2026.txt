📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
The ASX 200 shed 0.58% to 8,856.5, giving back Friday's gain and snapping a two-session run that had produced the index's best week since August. The selling was positioning, not panic: only 7 XJO stocks fell more than 4% and volatility stayed 'Normal' on every index - this was a market de-risking into Tuesday's 2:30pm RBA decision, where a fifth 2026 hike to 4.85% is ~80% priced.

Gold was the session's damage channel. Friday's -3.6% bullion slump to a one-year low (US$3,875) hit the open: NST -4.75%, CYL -7.40% (bottom ASX 200 name), TCG -9.8%, SBM -13.94%. St Barbara is the instructive one - its 08:20 'Touquoy restart approved' headline carried a sting (IAAC complicates permitting), and the market sold the permitting risk, not the 0.5Moz Old Austen resource or the buy-back. Add BHP -1.42% and RIO -1.17% on weaker iron ore (~US$90.80) and copper, and Materials (-1.35%) explained most of the index loss.

Financials (-0.63%) were a results-day split. WBC +1.00% on a $7.0b cash profit that edged consensus and a $1.54 dividend, but its NIM fell 7bp to 1.87% - and CBA (-1.0%) and NAB (-1.45%, reports Thursday) were sold as the market read that margin squeeze across the sector. AMP -7.48% (13:59 price-query response) and HLI -7.93% (mortgage insurer into a 'largest property downturn in 40 years' headline) widened the drag.

Defensives did not defend. Consumer Staples was the worst sector (XSJ -1.39%; WOW -1.89%, COL -1.75%, COL now -12% over 5 days) and Health Care fell 0.76% as ResMed (-5.77%) sold off after its 08:13 10-Q. The money that left went to Industrials (XNJ +0.76%, REH +5.97%, SGH +3.02%) and Consumer Discretionary (+0.65%) - the same transport/leisure/REIT rotation Smart Money has been tracking for two weeks.

Breadth confirms the caution: XJO 86 advancers vs 102 decliners, XAO 181/262, new 20day lows on the XAO jumped from 39 to 59. The forward calendar is dense: RBA + SMP (Tue), US midterms (Wed AEDT), NAB result (Thu), MQG (Fri), ANZ (09/11). Nine trading halts in the speculative resource tier flag a capital-raising wave to resolve this week.
