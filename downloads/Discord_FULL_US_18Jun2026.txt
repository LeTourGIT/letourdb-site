📝 SESSION SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------
Thursday was the relief rebound after Wednesday's Warsh-Fed sell-off, and the tape split
along a single fault line: the silicon layer ripped while the human-services layer broke.
Semiconductors fuelled the entire comeback — XSD +7.26% and SOXX +6.62% (both EXTREME >5%)
led nine ETFs above +3% against zero below -3%. INTC +10.64% on reports Apple agreed to
build chips with Intel in the US; SNDK +11.54%, MU +8.70%, ALAB +11.31%, GLW +11.13%,
KLAC +8.73% and ENTG +13.62% completed a near-pure chip sweep of the leaderboard.

The mirror image was Accenture -17.97% — its worst single session on record — after it cut
FY26 revenue-growth guidance to 3-4% (from 3-5%), missed on revenue ($18.7B) and printed
Q3 bookings down ~2% YoY. The read-through hammered the whole IT-services consulting complex:
CTSH -10.49%, IBM -5.05%, Gartner (IT) -4.56%, and RXT -4.12% — the #1 RS name. With Cognizant
set to exit the Nasdaq-100 on 22 June, the market is repricing labour-arbitrage consulting as
the loser of the AI build-out: capital is paying for the picks-and-shovels, not the headcount.

Overhead, two macro locks turned at once. The US and Iran signed an interim peace deal (MOU),
ending the 3-month war, reopening the Strait of Hormuz and waiving sanctions on Iranian oil.
WTI fell to a 3.5-month low at $75.46 — essentially prewar pricing — and the Navy ended its
blockade. That peace dividend is the second engine under the rotation: Smart Money's #1 industry
is Marine Port Services (Hormuz reopening), with Airlines surging #32→#14 and Hotels #21→#11 on
cheaper fuel and reopened trade lanes. Defense was the casualty — NOC -5.21%, LHX -5.86%.

Against that, new Chair Kevin Warsh delivered a hawkish FOMC debut: rates held at 3.50-3.75%
but 9 of 19 officials now pencil a 2026 hike to quell energy-driven inflation, and the easing
bias was stripped from the statement. That is why gold fell -2.55% and silver -5.04% even as
the geopolitical premium faded — higher-for-longer rates, not just peace, are pressuring metals.
Breadth confirmed the rebound was real: SPX Adv/Dec 262/237 positive, RUT a powerful 1403/460,
only DJI negative (14/16). No cap-weight distortion here — this was a broad, small-cap-led bounce.
