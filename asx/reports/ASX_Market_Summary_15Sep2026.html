📝 Session Summary

Tuesday dragged the ASX 200 down −0.88% to 8,672.5, a three-month low, as one macro force overwhelmed the tape: a Middle-East oil shock (Brent ~$107; WTI +10.5% over five sessions) feeding directly into inflation fear, with US 10-year yields at ~5% — the highest since 2007 — into Wednesday's Fed decision. This was imported, not domestic.

Materials (XMJ −2.21%) was the worst sector, and the mechanism was specific. US real yields at multi-decade highs cracked the gold price (gold −0.61%), and the ASX gold-miner complex — the market's structural RS leadership — unwound hard: RMS −4.76%, CYL −4.73%, OBM −5.26%, KCN −6.57%, SMI −8.04%, MEK −7.69%. A leadership-group correction is the most damaging kind of down day, quite different from a rotation into fresh names.

The mirror image was defensive. Health Care (XHJ +1.50%) led every sector as capital hid from duration and cyclicality — 4DX +8.72% and TLX +8.63% (both ASX200) plus HLS +7.06% drove it, with Staples (+0.88%) and Discretionary (+0.87%, Life360 +5.02%) filling out the risk-off leaderboard. Financials (XFJ −1.08%) led the index lower, banks caught between a higher-for-longer NIM tailwind and rising bad-debt risk.

Energy (XEJ −1.62%) falling on a day oil surged is the session's sharpest tell: broad de-risking and profit-taking after a huge multi-day crude run, not a rejection of the energy thesis — Smart Money is still rotating into the oil complex beneath the surface. EQR +11.69% (a binding US tungsten-plant JV) was the clean idiosyncratic winner against the tide.

Breadth confirms damage rather than a capitulation-and-turn: XJO Adv/Dec 78/116, only 35% of XJO names above their 200MA, T2108 at 28%, and 52 new 20day lows against just 7 new highs. The RBA (29 Sep, cash rate 4.35%) now meets into a fresh imported-inflation impulse — the local circuit-breaker is two weeks out, so oil and US yields, not the RBA, are the near-term drivers.
