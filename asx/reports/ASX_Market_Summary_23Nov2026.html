📝 Session Summary

Resources carried the tape but could not save it. Materials (XMJ +1.82%) was the only sector to clear +0.3%, a short-covering bounce after Friday's US miner washout (XME -5.95%, GDX -5.00%, COPX -4.98% on sheet 7b). BHP (+2.18%) did the heavy lifting off its 08:24 Copper South Australia site tour, a plan to double SA copper output by 2040 with US$14.2-18.1bn of investment and a final decision due in the first half of next year. RIO +1.37%, S32 +2.49%, EVN +2.61%, NEM +2.34% and the battery-metals pair IGO +4.67% / PLS +4.58% joined in. Sims (SGM +9.69%) topped the ASX 200 on broker crosscurrents with no ASX filing.

The banks were the anchor. Financials (XFJ -1.20%) finished last: CBA -2.22%, WBC -1.89%, ANZ -1.66%, NAB -0.58%. October unemployment is at a five-year-high 4.9%, the November flash composite PMI fell to 48.7, and the cash rate is 4.85% after five hikes. The market is moving credit-cycle risk into the lenders and out of the miners. Real Estate (XRE -1.03%) and Consumer Discretionary (XDJ -1.00%) filled out the rate-sensitive bloc at the bottom, while MQG (+2.23%) was the only large financial to rally.

The XJO closed at 8,570.6 (-0.26%), its eighth straight decline, after opening 0.6% lower and grinding back as US futures rallied. ES finished about 2.1% above Friday's 6,935 settle (sheet 7b), which matches ABC's +2.2% S&P futures read. That is the uncomfortable detail: a red close on a day US futures recovered 2% says local sellers are not done. All three large-cap regimes slipped from Bullish to Neutral Bull, and the XSO fell to Bearish.

Breadth improved but did not confirm. XJO advancers/decliners was 71/119 against Friday's capitulation-grade 19/176. But only 21.5% of the index is above its 20MA, T2108 is 39%, and new 20day lows (73) outnumber highs (2) by 36 to 1. That is an oversold-bounce setup, not a turn. The first higher close after an eight-session streak is the signal to wait for, and Wednesday's October CPI (consensus annual headline 4.3% to 4.8%) is the event that decides it.

Into Tuesday: US futures point to a firmer open. Two price-sensitive items landed after the close: CCM's response to an ASX aware letter (16:35) and EGR's German Government grant for Epanko (17:17). Twelve trading halts are still pending, including Maas Group (MGH), Tasmea (TEA) and uranium developer Peninsula (PEN). Results week continues: SHV on Tuesday, RYM and WEB on Wednesday, FPH and TWR on Thursday. WEB has already fallen 10.44% into its print.
