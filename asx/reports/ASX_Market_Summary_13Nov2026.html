Session Summary

The ASX 200 shed 64.6 points (-0.71%) to 9,082.2, a second straight decline after Wednesday's 9,153.2, but still banked +0.68% for the week. The overnight hand-off looked like an Nvidia story -- Jensen Huang's resignation -- yet Wall Street absorbed that with the S&P 500 down only 0.35%. The real transmission was rates: US yields posted their biggest surge since 2022 on Fed-hike talk, and the ASX sold exactly the duration- and funding-sensitive segments: Real Estate (XRE -1.34%), Small Ords (XSO -1.75%) and gold.

Materials (XMJ -1.61%) was the largest drag, and it was not an iron ore day (US$91.60, -0.6%, still near two-year lows). The selling came from gold miners after bullion's -2.85% overnight slide (NST -2.83%, EMR -2.27%, WGX -1.72%), Capstone Copper -5.43% on its 08:21 operations update (a production downgrade), and the critical-minerals spec complex (IPX -6.99%, A4N -7.23%, LIN -5.88%). A weaker AUD (0.6802, down ~2 cents in a fortnight) is a revenue cushion for miners, but it did not stop the selling.

Breadth is the warning: 32 advancers versus 159 decliners in the ASX 200 (0.20:1) -- the weakest of the six sessions in the sheet -- with zero members up 4% and nine down 4%. The index lost only 0.71% because heavyweight quality held: Aristocrat +2.03% and Xero +2.34% (both post-result follow-through), Computershare +2.10%, ResMed +1.42% and APA +1.61% leading Utilities (XUJ +1.28%), the only sector up more than 0.2%. That is rotation into defensive cash flows, not a liquidation -- but a market held up by five stocks is fragile.

Stock-specific, the tape punished capital-structure events. Paladin fell 17.59% to $7.17 after completing its A$525M raise at $7.80 (a 10.3% discount, per Market Index) -- closing 8% below the issue price leaves day-one placees underwater and creates an overhang. Lendlease dropped 8.43% on a ~$1.22bn bond redemption costing ~$57m, read as expensive liability management. Big River jumped 40.54% on a Scheme Implementation Deed with CTL. NAB's Andrew Irvine and CBA's Matt Comyn fronted a parliamentary committee, with all big four trading lower through the afternoon (XFJ -0.44%).

Forward look: XJO's Bullish regime is intact (+2.88% above its 50MA), but XSO lost its 50MA and flipped its 20day trend Down, widening the large/small divergence. Known catalysts: Microba's 16:16 trading halt (post-close, not in today's price), outstanding halts at ACW and WGR, Elders and FleetPartners results Monday, TechnologyOne and Catapult Tuesday, Infratil Wednesday, and Nvidia's Q3 on Wednesday AEDT -- the first read on whether the AI capex story survives its founder.
