Session Summary

The ASX 200 eased -0.27% to 9,058.9 — a quiet index number masking a loud, two-sided reporting-season tape. Decliners beat advancers 104:87 on the XJO even as the benchmark barely moved, which tells you the damage was concentrated in a handful of heavily-sold results while the median stock held firm. This was a stock-pickers' session, not a macro one.

Gold is the through-line. Bullion sits near US$4,620 and the ASX200/300 relative-strength table is now eight-of-ten gold miners — VAU, GMD, RRL, EVN, CMM, OBM, SBM. Regis (RRL +3.28%) delivered a record profit and a record 20cps final dividend; the complex is being re-rated on cash returns, not merely the metal price. Materials (XMJ +0.17% today but +13.8% over 20 days) is doing the index's heavy lifting even with iron ore flat at ~US$95.

The winners were catalyst-driven: PWH +15.49% and GYG +11.39% on FY26 results, EQT +9.30% on a BGH Capital takeover proposal, plus a cluster of gold/silver explorers (MEK +13.04% on a visible-gold intercept, MKR +12.50% on restarting Wonawinta production). The losers were equally result-driven: ZIP -15.74% and TLX -10.13% extended post-result selldowns, DGT -9.32% paired a soft FY26 with a CEO change, and ING -7.21%, SUL -7.61% and CHC -6.33% completed the misses.

The consumer is the quiet warning. Walmart's -9% guidance shock overnight, Super Retail -7.6% here, and Consumer Discretionary -1.82% (-6.55% over five sessions) all point the same way. With unemployment at 4.5% (a post-COVID high) and the RBA on hold at 4.35% into late September, softening labour demand is now the dominant domestic signal — dovish for rates but cautionary for retail earnings.

Forward look: Steadfast (SDF) went into a trading halt pending an announcement — a resolution that is tomorrow's catalyst. Post-close, Energy Resources of Australia (ERA) lodged its half-year report (16:52), not in today's price. Next week brings a heavy slate — Woodside, Wesfarmers, Woolworths, Coles, PLS and Lynas — that will test the consumer and resources narratives directly.
