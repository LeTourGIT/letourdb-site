📝 Session Summary

The session split cleanly along an energy-security fault line. Uranium was the standout — six uranium names (AEU, LOT, BOE, DYL, BMN, PDN) inside the top 15 gainers, PDN the only large-cap of the group at +10.6%. Earnings season did the rest: results drove ANN +9.6% (buyback), DTL +17.9% and Nuix +34.4% higher, and punished NHF -9.1%, KGN -18.6%, CNU -5.7% and ABB -6.5%. DXN +41.8% on an AI HPC contract sits in the speculative tier but is the clearest single-stock expression of the AI-compute build-out. RWC -4.8% is a profit-take, not a downgrade — it is still +16.3% over five days.

The macro spine is the Iran sanctions "economic D-Day": Treasury Secretary Bessent unveiled the toughest-ever US sanctions package on Monday, with Iran threatening Strait of Hormuz passage. That backdrop is powering an energy-security bid that ran straight through the uranium complex and into the Smart Money rotation — coal, oil & gas and utilities all surging together. Materials +2.43% cleared the 1.5% lead threshold; Financials -0.72% and Consumer Staples -0.95% were the drag.

With FY26 full-year reporting season live, result quality set the biggest single-stock moves — a stock-picker's tape underneath a resources-led index. AUD/USD 0.7168 was flat, so no miner-revenue translation effect today. Breadth confirmed the advance: XJO advancers 107 vs 88 decliners, 46 new 20day highs against 33 lows, and 57% of XJO names above their 40day MA.
