📝 Session Summary

A resources-led rebound lifted the ASX 200 +0.17% to 8,809.9 after Friday's liquidation session, when 41 XJO stocks fell 4% or more. Materials (XMJ +1.71%) did all the heavy lifting on a two-pronged rotation: lithium (PLS +8.40%, VUL +10.63%, MI6 +7.58%, LTR +6.25%, ELV +4.12%) and gold miners (PDI +7.34%, GGP +7.03%, OBM +6.76%, NST +2.77%). None of the lithium majors lodged news — this is sector flow and short covering after XMJ lost 6.22% over 5day, not a fundamental re-rating.

BlueScope (BSL +2.94%, new 20day high) upgraded its 1H FY27 earnings guidance at 08:29 — a clean steel-margin beat in a tape that needed one. FMG +2.17% and BHP +1.04% added institutional weight, but iron ore at $90.83 (-6.4% over 20day) caps how far the big miners can run. The Materials bid was lithium and gold, not ore.

Underneath, the rate trade dominated. RBA Assistant Governor Sarah Hunter said rates may need to rise further and November hike odds cleared 60%: Financials, Utilities, Discretionary and Staples (XSJ -1.54%, the weakest sector) all closed red. Stock-specific damage was brutal where regulators or reviews hit — TUA -30.49% on a Singapore IMDA show-cause notice and OFX -24.51% on its strategic review and trading update.

This is peak quarterly season: 81 Appendix 4C/5B filings from 70 companies, 46 of them Materials. EIQ +17.05% (4C), LOT +12.12% (Kayelekera first export) and TCG +7.87% (high-grade extension) were the filings the market rewarded. Eleven trading halts remain open — each is a pending catalyst.

Structurally nothing is fixed. XJO breadth was only 111/84 (1.3:1), new 20day lows (28) still outnumber highs (15), and just 33.5% of the XJO sits above its 200MA. The test is the 8,830–8,845 zone (200MA/50MA) into this week's September inflation data and the 03/11 RBA decision.
