📝 Session Summary

The ASX 200 rose 1.00% to 8,806.7, its biggest one-day gain since 04/08/2026 and its highest close since 10/09/2026, a five-week high. Resources led: Materials (XMJ) +3.18%. BHP +3.50% was the single biggest index contributor, with RIO +1.46% and FMG +1.13% trailing, and gold miners were bid across the tier (NST +3.25%, EVN +3.03%, GMD +4.92%, PDI +9.77%). The key signal is that iron ore only rose 0.7% to US$90.90/t. BHP's move outran its main commodity, so this was positioning rotating back into beaten-down resources (XMJ is still -2.31% on the week and -4.52% over 20 days), not a fresh China demand impulse.

Lithium delivered the day's clearest message. PLS approved the P2000 FID (~2Mtpa) and upgraded its Pilgangoora reserve 31% at 08:17, yet closed just +0.34% after a -14.86% week. A producer adding supply into a weak price is not what lithium bulls want to hear. Peers outperformed the company making the news (LTR +4.41%, IGO +0.93%), which suggests the market treats PLS's expansion as a supply-side headwind for the sector rather than a vote of confidence.

Tech was the other engine: XIJ +5.62%. WiseTech +9.01% after ASIC closed its governance and share-trading investigations on Thursday, removing the overhang that had capped the stock all year. Xero +7.05%, Pro Medicus +7.43% and Netwealth +7.46% followed. Weebit Nano jumped +17.07% on its Q1 4C (08:56, price sensitive). Context matters: XIJ is still -1.28% over five days and -41% over a year. This was short-covering and rotation into the most-sold sector of 2026, mirroring the US move from AI hardware into earnings-backed names.

The weak point was the banks. Thursday's soft September jobs data sent the AUD to a 3.5-month low and cut RBA hike odds, which would normally favour rate-sensitive financials. Instead Financials fell 0.52% (CBA -1.13%, WBC -1.57%, NAB -0.98%) and Staples fell 0.53% (COL -1.52%). Funds sold the year's crowded defensive winners, after a +3-4% bank week, to pay for the tech and miner rebound. Breadth was strong (XJO 141 up / 52 down, 14 stocks +4%, none -4%), but it was built on that rotation rather than fresh money.

Next week: XJO's 50-day sits ~0.9% overhead and only 33.5% of its constituents are above their own 50-day. The rally needs a second day to confirm, and Monday opens with ES/NQ futures slightly lower. Stock catalysts on the calendar: the Cleanaway/EQT exclusivity deadline (Monday), unresolved halts in SPL and AEI, the Appendix 4C/5B quarterly wave building through late October, Alkane results (23/10), and RBA Deputy Governor Hauser speaking on Wednesday.
