📝 Session Summary
The ASX 200 slipped -0.40% to 9,127.8, but the headline number badly undersells the day. This was August reporting season at full velocity — the index barely moved while individual names swung 10–20%. Decliners beat advancers 123 to 73 on the XJO, yet 20-day highs (46) still outnumbered lows (14): a rotational shake-out inside an uptrend, not distribution.
The damage was concentrated in tech and growth. WiseTech (WTC -10.07%) was the session's paradox — it printed record revenue up 79% and beat on EBITDA, then was sold hard on valuation, dragging Info Tech (XIJ -3.47%) to worst sector and pulling Xero (XRO -5.67%) and SiteMinder (SDR -8.53%) down in sympathy. Worley (WOR -10.81%) and Neuren (NEU -10.56%) deepened the reporting-season wreckage.
Against that, defensives and miners held the line. Woolworths (WOW +3.42%) delivered a bumper FY profit and carried Consumer Staples (XSJ +1.83%) to the only strong sector — though the fact the index still fell on a good WOW is itself the story ABC led with. Lovisa (LOV +12.73%) and HMC Capital (HMC +11.26%) were the results winners; gold names — Perseus (PRU +9.46% on record shareholder returns plus a buy-back) and a swarm of drillers led by Torque (TOR +20.45% on multi-ounce intercepts) — kept Materials flat despite the risk-off.
Macro sat underneath it all. A hot Australian CPI print (ABC: 'an unfortunate kick in the guts') has re-loaded RBA hike risk into the 28–29 September meeting (cash rate 4.35%), pressuring Financials (XFJ -0.58%) and capping rate-sensitives. Offsetting at the margin, oil fell again — Brent -2.67% to ~$89 on Iran-Hormuz de-escalation — which trims the imported-fuel leg of the CPI problem even as domestic services inflation stays hot.
The forward look points offshore and post-close. Nvidia reports after the US close tonight and, with US July PCE landing alongside it, sets Thursday's tone for the local tech complex. Domestically, Webjet upgraded 1H27 guidance after the bell and Macquarie Technology and BrainChip reported post-close — none is in today's tape, so all three are tomorrow's catalysts.
