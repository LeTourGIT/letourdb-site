📝 SESSION SUMMARY — 18/05/2026

Monday 18 May delivered a broad risk-off session on the ASX as the dual weight of a Friday Wall Street selloff (US CPI 3.8%, 30yr Treasury yield breaking 5%) and resurgent Iran blockade oil risks reset the macro backdrop. The XJO closed at 8,505.3 — extending losses from the prior week's budget-driven volatility and printing XJO's most oversold RSI-15 reading (35.47) since the late March correction. XSO was the most damaged, now sitting 8.21% below its 200MA — the clearest gauge of how extensively institutional capital has rotated out of domestic growth and small-cap risk.

The session's defining character was a stark divergence between Energy (XEJ +2.00%) — the single green sector — and everything else red, with Industrials (XNJ -4.02%) bearing the heaviest selling. Energy's outperformance was entirely Iran-driven: WTI back above US$109 (Brent) on Trump's renewed blockade warnings means Santos (STO) hit a fresh 20day high (+2.66%), as did QBE Insurance (QBE +0.95%) and SSM Service Stream (+4.21%). Six ASX200/300 stocks made 20day highs — all in oil & gas, insurance, or construction engineering — a cluster that maps precisely onto the Smart Money rotation: Construction & Engineering #3, Insurance Funds #2, Corporate Financial Services #4.

Industrials' -4.02% collapse was the sharpest sector decline since March, but it masks an important internal divergence: the Construction & Engineering sub-sector (Smart Money #3, 10day ROC +5.01%) is holding institutional bid while broader Industrials are sold. NWH (NRW Holdings) shed 5.51% on the day despite being the 10day sector leader — typical of institutional accumulation phases where short-term selling creates entry for longer-duration positioning. Healthcare (XHJ -1.48%) remains in structural decline, the combination of CSL's guidance cut and PE compression from higher discount rates making it the most persistently weak large-cap sector in 2026.

The week's critical event is Nvidia earnings Wednesday night (AEST). Smart Money Semiconductors at #1 (10day ROC +14.82%, rising from rank 41 nine days ago) is not a coincidence — institutional capital globally is front-running the NVDA print. WBT (Weebit Nano, ASX300) is the clearest ASX proxy: semiconductors, 10day ROC +45.5%, RS 96.55. A strong NVDA beat with positive hyperscaler capex guidance would directly flow into WBT, and the broader IT Services cluster (MP1 at RS 95.07 making a 20day high). A miss would unwind the #1 Smart Money position aggressively.
