Session Summary

Monday 6 July looked quiet on the tape and wasn't underneath. The XJO slipped -0.15% to 8,831.0 and the All Ords -0.12% to 9,037.0, yet decliners outnumbered advancers 112 to 74 (0.66:1). A flat index built on negative breadth is a distribution signal — index-level calm masking rotation beneath.

The session's headline act was gold M&A. Genesis Minerals (GMD) lobbed an unsolicited binding A$5.6bn bid for Vault Minerals (VAU) — 0.7629 GMD scrip plus 47.5c cash per share, a 14.5% premium the Vault board immediately declared a Superior Proposal over the standing Regis Resources (RRL) merger. VAU surged +11.62% to A$5.09 (new 20day high); GMD fell -4.13% on scrip dilution; Regis rose on a strong June quarter and now has until 10 July to match. It is the latest leg of an accelerating ASX gold-consolidation wave (Northern Star/De Grey, Gold Fields/Gold Road) as record bullion prices fund production land-grabs.

Away from gold, the tape was defensive and US-shaped. Healthcare (XHJ +1.50%) led on CU6 +19% (Clarity Pharma), with CSL, COH and PME all printing fresh 20day highs — a classic risk-management bid. IT (XIJ +0.93%) leaned on WTC +7.31% rebounding off its recent A$30 sell-off, even as Weebit Nano (WBT -5.84%) tracked the US chip slump. Materials (-0.63%) and Utilities (-0.79%) lagged: iron ore has slid to ~US$98/t (sub-US$100, -5.3% over the month), pressuring BHP/RIO/FMG, while bond-proxy utilities keep de-rating (-4.05% over 5day) as the long end stays firm.

On the macro backdrop, the RBA held at 4.35% in June after three hikes this year and next meets 11 August — outside the report window, but the still-restrictive setting is the structural weight on rate-sensitive REITs, staples and utilities. AUD/USD eased to 0.6933 (-0.14%); a softer Aussie is a modest tailwind for USD-earning miners, partly cushioning the weak iron ore print.
