📝 Session Summary

Thursday delivered a shallow −0.23% dip in the ASX 200 to 9,188.5 — a pause at record-high territory rather than a reversal. With the trend structure fully Bullish and RSI at 64.7, the tape is consolidating a strong run while the market works through the busiest week of FY26 reporting season.

The session's character was two heavyweight results dragging the index while the broad market held. Telstra's FY26 print sank Communication Services (XTJ −2.30%, the day's worst sector) and CBA fell −2.15% the day after its own result, with home-loan applications reported down 15% since May — a concrete read that mortgage demand is cooling. Yet Financials (XFJ) still closed +0.08% as Suncorp (+3.28%) and insurers absorbed the CBA drag: the bank weakness was name-specific, not sectoral.

Reporting-season dispersion was the real story. ASX Ltd (+9.03%), Origin (+5.33%, leading Utilities) and Treasury Wine (+4.91%) were rewarded; IAG (−5.10%) and HomeCo Daily Needs (−5.49%) were punished. This is a stock-pickers' tape — index beta is flat while single names run 5–15%. Cleanaway (+15.19%) topped the board on a genuine corporate catalyst: a Process Deed with EQT following a takeover approach.

Beneath the surface, the Smart Money rotation is emphatically risk-on: Renewable Energy Equipment, Aerospace & Defence, Uranium, Semiconductors and Biotech all surged 50–80 rank positions in nine sessions, with Gold entrenched near the top. That breadth of capital rotation — resources, defence, growth and clean energy all bid at once — is what underpins the record-high tape despite a soft index print.
