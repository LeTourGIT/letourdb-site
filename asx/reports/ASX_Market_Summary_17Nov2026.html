Session Summary

Resources led the market lower -- and hard. The ASX 200 fell 161.5 points (-1.79%) to 8,883.2, its fourth straight loss, with Materials (XMJ -3.86%) the worst sector by a wide margin and now -8.69% over five days. This was not iron ore (spot -0.62% on 16/11): it was the AI-adjacent commodity complex. Lithium (PLS -8.65%, MIN -8.45%, PMT -8.20%), rare earths (LYC -8.24%, LIN -10.00%), scandium (SRL -11.00%) and copper (FFM -8.09%) followed their US-listed peers (REMX -5.9%, COPX -5.4% overnight, per Market Index). The market is treating critical minerals as a leveraged AI-capex trade -- when Nvidia sneezes, Lynas catches a cold.

Breadth was the real damage. Only 21 XJO stocks advanced against 174 decliners (0.12:1), 32 fell more than 4% while just one rose 4%, and T2108 dropped from 63.5% to 55.5% in a single session. The XAO was worse: 65/388 with 65 stocks down 4%+. New 20day lows (23) overtook new highs (14) on the XJO. This is liquidation, not rotation -- although the few places money did go are telling.

Where it went: Utilities (XUJ +1.52%; APA +1.78%, ORG +1.62% at a 20day high) and refiner Ampol (+1.65%) -- exactly the defensive pocket that held up on Wall Street overnight. Gold miners were relatively firm (RRL +1.52%, BGL +2.92% on its hedge-free refinancing) with bullion steady at US$3,878 after a -6.9% five-day slide, and gold names dominate the ASX 200/300 RS leaderboard.

Stock-specific stories cut both ways. Mayne Pharma (MYX +87.23%) signed a Scheme Implementation Deed with Gedeon Richter (09:13, price sensitive). TechnologyOne (TNE +8.86%) delivered FY26 ARR +18% and PBT +20% at the top of guidance -- the only large-cap software name rewarded on an AI narrative today. Against that, Catapult (CAT -16.57%) was punished on its 1H FY27 result and IPH (-13.42%) on its FY27 trading update. Six trading halts and two suspensions point to more capital-raising news in the explorer tier.

The forward read: XJO is still +0.71% above its 50MA and +0.51% above its 200MA, so the long-term structure survives -- but only just, and the XAO has already lost its 200MA. The 8,834 close of 04/11 is the line to watch. Nvidia's result is the catalyst; post-close, MA1 (17:02) lodged its buy-back booklet and Infratil (IFT) reports tomorrow, Nufarm/AAC on 19/11.
