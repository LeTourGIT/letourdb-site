📝 Session Summary

Wednesday was a two-act session. Act one: miners and tech carried the ASX 200 up about 0.9% into the CPI print. Act two: September CPI landed at 4.3% y/y and the Q3 trimmed mean rose 1.0% q/q — a full 20bp above the RBA's August forecast — and market odds of a hike on Tuesday leapt from ~60% to ~85-90%. The index bled back to close -0.03% at 8,847.3.

The rotation underneath was textbook rate-repricing. Technology (XIJ +2.36%) led on stock-specific news: NXT +9.39% after confirming covenant headroom (interest cover 5.9x vs a 1.5x minimum) answered the balance-sheet fear behind its ~40% drawdown; SDR +18.12% on its AGM address; DTL +7.78% on guidance. Materials (XMJ +1.07%) rode the quarterly window — ILU +8.17% and PLS +3.82% on their quarterlies, and gold equities (PRU +8.67%) bid even as bullion slipped 0.44%.

The losers map the hike. REH -8.81% was the largest ASX 200 decline with no filing — housing was the single biggest CPI contributor (+5.2% y/y), and Reece is the purest listed play on housing activity. Residential developers LLC -4.58%, PPC -5.10% and CWP -5.07% followed; Consumer Discretionary fell 1.53% and Utilities 1.24%. Energy (XEJ -2.45%) was the worst sector: WDS -3.16% on its Timor-Leste exit and coal names down 3-4%, even as Brent recovered ~1.2% intraday.

Breadth was thin at the top: XJO Adv/Dec 98/91 (1.08:1), only 34.5% of the 200 above their 200MA, T2108 at 47%. But XJO 20day highs (18) tripled 20day lows (6), and the All Ords ran 280/172 — the broader tape is healthier than the flat index suggests.

Forward look: Champion Iron reports Thursday, Westpac's full-year result lands 02/11, and the RBA decides on 03/11. AX1 (response to Frasers' best-and-final) and ELS (4C) lodged after the close and are not in today's prices.
