📝 Session Summary

Thursday 20/08/2026 ran on two engines: a broad gold-miner surge and August full-year results. Materials (XMJ +3.52%) led every sector, powered by gold names printing fresh 20day highs — Ora Banda +14.03%, Genesis Minerals +10.33%, Regis +10.92%, Capricorn +10.28%. With bullion near record levels (~US$4,545/oz) and Genesis's EBITDA more than doubling to A$952m, this is a re-rating with cash flow behind it, not a bullion-price chase.

Reporting season supplied the dispersion. Beats were rewarded hard: Zip +18.22%, Super Retail +15.05%, MA Financial +18.14% (1H26), Universal Store +17.73%, and Codan +12.42% — the last confirming why Electronic Equipment tops the Smart Money board. Misses were punished just as hard: IDP Education -20.74% on a guidance shock, Downer EDI -10.34% on a weak Preliminary Final, Sonic Healthcare -9.25%. Results, not macro, set the day's spread.

Financials (XFJ -1.93%) was the clear laggard, but the driver was specific: insurers derated together (Helia -11.09%, Medibank -6.36%, NIB -5.34%, IAG -4.18%) on results and sympathy selling. This is an insurance-sector event inside Financials, not a banks-led de-rate — an important distinction for anyone reading the red as systemic.

AUD/USD at 0.7124 (-0.06%) was a non-event, so there was no revenue-compression drag on the miners — letting the gold and iron-ore complex run cleanly. Iron ore is firm (~US$105/t 62% Fe) on Chinese restocking: July ore imports rose 6% YoY even as crude-steel output fell 3.1%, a supportive setup for BHP/RIO/FMG ahead of Fortescue's result due today. Macro backdrop: unemployment hit 4.5% — a post-COVID high that lands exactly on the RBA's own Q4 forecast, keeping the Bank on hold at 4.35% (next decision 29 Sep) and nudging the debate toward eventual cuts. The one thing that can delay that is oil: Brent's Hormuz-driven spike to two-week highs is the live upside-inflation risk.
