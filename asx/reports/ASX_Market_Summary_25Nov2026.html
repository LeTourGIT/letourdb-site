Session Summary

The ASX 200 gave back -0.39% to 8,692.4, surrendering a fifth of Tuesday's +1.8% relief rally that had snapped an eight-session losing streak. Two hits landed in sequence: an overnight ~5% slump in LME copper to a 13-month low hammered the resource heavyweights at the open, then the 11:30 October CPI print -- 4.9% headline (vs 4.8% expected, up from 4.3%) with trimmed mean lifting to 3.8% from 3.6% -- flipped Financials from +0.47% to red. By midday the index was -0.81% at 8,655; the recovery into the close came from defensives, not from risk appetite.

Materials (XMJ -2.08%) was the anchor. BHP -1.90%, RIO -2.04% and FMG -2.56% fell even with iron ore flat, which tells you this was a copper and growth-scare trade, not a China steel trade. Lithium was routed in parallel -- PLS -5.88%, IGO -5.25%, LTR -8.62%, ELV -9.52%, MIN -6.24% -- with no stock-specific news on any of them, and CXO's first post-restart spodumene shipment (-2.13%) could not buck the tape. XMJ is now -9.66% over 20days.

IT (XIJ -2.22%) mirrored Nasdaq's -1.48% AI unwind: MP1 -7.18%, SKS -7.58%, TUA -9.44% (after answering an ASX price query). Against that, Healthcare (XHJ +1.23%) led all sectors on Pro Medicus' FDA clearance for Visage7 AI mammography (+3.64%) and CSL +1.75% printing a 20day high. Energy (+0.70%) caught a Hormuz tanker-attack bid intraday, but WTI futures were -1.55% by report time -- that support may not carry into Thursday.

The read for rates: a hot CPI that did NOT crush REITs (XRE +0.48%) or banks (XFJ -0.15%) means the market believes the RBA's hands are tied by 4.9% unemployment -- a December hold with a hawkish tone is the consensus. AUD/USD fell -0.65% to 0.6592 on the same logic: hot inflation without a hike response is currency-negative. That softer AUD is a quiet cushion for USD-revenue miners once copper stabilises.

Stock-level, it was an announcement-driven tape: TYR +16.67% on an on-market buy-back plus reaffirmed FY27 guidance, IGL +9.26% after its AGM, and WEB -23.53% as its 1H27 result (Spanish tax issue in focus) was the session's biggest institutional loss. Breadth was poor (XJO 76 up / 119 down, XAO 164/281) and four trading halts plus three suspensions (PEN, MGH, MAP into administration) kept the event-risk premium high.
