📝 Session Summary

Monday was M&A Monday. The ASX 200 rose +0.44% to 8,845.7 and closed back above its 200day moving average, but the gain was manufactured by takeover premiums rather than broad buying. Cleanaway (CWY +20.24%) agreed a binding A$3.095 cash scheme with EQT Infrastructure (~A$9.3bn including debt), Challenger (CGF +19.29%) received an A$11.80 indicative proposal from 20.4% holder Daiichi Life, FleetPartners (FPR +7.13%) signed with SG Fleet at A$5.30, RAS Technology (RTH +85.19%) signed with The PA, and New Hope (NHC +5.82%) announced an all-scrip combination with Malabar. When private capital and strategics pay 20% premiums for ASX 200 franchises while the index sits below its 50day, they are telling you public-market multiples are too low.

The breadth beneath it disagrees with the headline. XJO decliners beat advancers 99 to 89 — a sharp reversal from Friday's 141/52 — and only 36.7% of constituents sit above their 50day. The All Ords breadth was worse (206/241), and the Small Ords fell -0.35% with 81 decliners against 63 advancers. An index rising on negative breadth because of two bid stocks is a narrow tape: the deal premiums are locked in and will not repeat tomorrow.

Energy (XEJ +2.21%) was the cleanest genuine trend. Coal led — New Hope, Yancoal (+5.71%) and Whitehaven (+5.17%) — after China's September coal output fell 6.8% y/y and Newcastle futures firmed to US$154.60/t, with the NHC-Malabar deal adding a consolidation premium across the group. Woodside (+2.69%), Santos (+2.31%), Karoon (+3.87%) and Beach (+4.02%) rode Brent's Friday spike toward US$110. The one energy loser was the one with refining exposure: Ampol (-2.51%) doubled its Lytton margin to US$23.92/bbl, but flagged ~150ML lower Q4 output — the market paid for volume, not margin.

Technology (XIJ -1.31%) imported Friday's Nasdaq rout. NextDC entered a trading halt over a power supply delay at its M3 Melbourne data centre — the first hard operational crack in the ASX's AI-infrastructure trade — and the read-through hit data-centre adjacent names: Digico Infrastructure REIT (DGT -5.12%) and SKS Technologies (SKS -5.07%) sit on the losers list with no announcements of their own. Weebit Nano (-7.64%) fell after an investor update and Mesoblast (-11.06%) on broker downgrades. Materials barely moved (XMJ +0.11%) as BHP's +0.72% was offset by South32 (-2.59%, September quarter) and PLS (-2.68%, price-target cuts) despite China's Q3 GDP beating at 4.5% — property investment down 20.6% is why the beat did not reach iron ore equities.

RBA context (no Watch section — next meeting 03/11/2026 is 15 days away): cash rate 4.60% after the 29/09 hike, the fourth of 2026, with the Board flagging further tightening may be needed. Assistant Governor Kent spoke today and Deputy Governor Hauser speaks Wednesday. AU 10-year at 5.24% keeps the yield-sensitive end of the market (REITs, long-duration tech) capped, and is exactly why insurers — whose float earns that yield — are rising up the Smart Money table. The forward set-up: the NextDC halt resolution, the Sarytogan halt, Westpac's post-close 'items impacting FY26' (16:50) and Metro Mining's guidance update (17:14) are tomorrow's catalysts, and the late-October quarterly window is now in full swing (26 quarterly/4C/5B filings today). The level to watch is XJO's 50day near 8,881 — reclaim it on positive breadth and the Neutral Bear label can turn; fail there on another 89/99 tape and today was simply a takeover-premium mirage.
