Session Summary

Friday 14/08/2026 saw the XJO give back -0.80% to 9,115.2 and the XAO -0.73% to 9,313.2 — a shallow, rotation-driven pullback rather than a risk-off event. Info Technology (XIJ +2.83%) topped the board on Wall Street's record close, while Materials (XMJ -2.58%) single-handedly anchored the index lower. A resources-led decline is the mirror image of a resources-led rally: same sector, opposite sign.

The materials drag is fundamentally sourced. Iron ore sits near 14-month lows around US$95/t (62% Fe) on soft Chinese steel margins; the lone offset — a widening strike at BHP's Port Hedland export hub — is a supply, not demand, story and hasn't lifted equities. Integrated miners (DVP -6.71%, SRL -5.62%, MI6 -5.13%) and gold spec names led the sector down. SRL's fall still leaves it +10.67% on the week — profit-taking in strong names, not capitulation.

The offset was reporting season and offshore tech. IPG +13.10% (Appendix 4E + FY26 results, lodged 08:24-08:35, price-sensitive) was the clean earnings winner; SEK +9.13% and CAR +5.47% extended post-result runs. Large-cap software tracked the US Nasdaq record — WTC +5.55%, XRO +5.54%, XYZ (Block) +6.07%. On the debit side, SKG -8.30% and QBE -1.62% show the market is not paying up indiscriminately for results: reactions are being earned line by line.

Breadth confirms the pullback rather than contradicting it — XJO advancers trailed decliners 67/127 (0.53:1), yet 67% of the 200 remain above their 20day MA and T2108 sits at a healthy 71%. The market is thinning at the surface while its internals stay firm. With the RBA out until late September, the real test is whether iron ore stabilises and reporting-season beats keep offsetting the resource complex. Watch NextDC — its FY results webcast invite (17:17, post-close) flags results imminently.
