📝 Session Summary

Materials (XMJ +3.71%) and Energy (XEJ +2.67%) carried the ASX 200 up 71.4 points (+0.82%) to 8,763.8, more than reversing Wednesday's -0.39%. It was a resources squeeze rather than a macro re-rating: FMG (+3.90%), RIO (+3.53%) and BHP (+3.39%) all rallied while iron ore eased 0.5% to about US$88.30/t. The bid came from positioning after XMJ's -5.70% 20day drawdown, not from the commodity. Gold miners did have the metal behind them — gold futures +1.39% to US$3,598.50 lifted CMM +7.12%, GMD +7.20%, EVN +6.40% and RMS +6.33%, with ASX200 names OBM (+10.09%, exploration update 08:51) and CYL (+8.89%) at the top of the institutional board.

Energy rode oil: WTI +4.29% to US$84.19 and Brent +3.7% (about US$94.76) lifted WDS +3.25%, KAR +3.89%, coal (NHC +3.77%, WHC +3.68%) and uranium (PDN +7.14%). That oil move is not good news for rates. October CPI printed 4.9%, above expectations, two weeks before the 8 December RBA meeting; the cash rate is 4.85% after a 6-3 split vote to hike on 3 November. CommBank expects a hold, but a renewed oil spike is exactly the input that keeps a sixth hike in play.

Defensives funded the move. Consumer Staples (XSJ -1.36%: WOW -2.06%, A2M -3.02%, CBO -4.67%) was the weakest sector, with Discretionary (-0.71%), Comm. Services, Utilities, Healthcare (RHC -6.67%, CU6 -6.41%) and Real Estate (LLC -9.79%) all red — only 5 of 11 sectors closed higher. Insurers slipped (QBE -1.45%, IAG -1.51%, SUN -1.95%) as ASIC sued a major insurer over complaints handling, a direct hit to the 60day insurance leadership.

Structure did not improve. XJO's regime was downgraded to Neutral Bear on an up day, and the index remains below its 50MA (~8,812) and 200MA (~8,831). Breadth was thin for a +0.8% session: 106 advancers to 85 decliners (1.25:1), only 14 stocks up 4% or more, and just 32% of the ASX 200 above its 20MA. The Small Ords is the weak link — Bearish, 12.41% below its 200MA, with 20 new 20day lows against one new high.

Into Friday: the US closed mixed after the Nasdaq 100 briefly touched bear-market territory, and ES/NQ futures are now +0.79%/+1.07%. US cash markets are shut tonight for Thanksgiving, so the ASX trades without a cash-market lead. Watch the halts (NEU, TER, LEG, BAK, LAC, RCM) and MFD's post-close board overhaul. Whether miners hold today's gains with iron ore flat will tell you if this was a turn or a squeeze; a close above ~8,831 is the bar for the former.

The commodity tape did not explain the materials rally. Iron ore eased 0.5% to about US$88.30/t and LME base metals were hammered on 25/11 (copper -2.76%, zinc -3.94%, tin -6.43% in the 1day-delayed 7d feed), yet FMG, RIO and BHP all rose 3.4-3.9%. When the majors rally against their own commodity, the buyer is a positioning trade covering a 20day XMJ drawdown of -5.70%, and those moves fade unless the commodity follows.

Gold is the exception — the move had price behind it. Gold futures +1.39% to US$3,598.50 and silver +2.72% produced the cleanest equity response of the day across the ASX200 gold names. Oil is the macro risk: WTI +4.29% and Brent +3.7% feed straight into Australian inflation 12 days before the 8 December RBA meeting, with October CPI already printing 4.9% above expectations.
