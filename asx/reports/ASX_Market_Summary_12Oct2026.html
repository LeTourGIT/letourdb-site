Session Summary

The ASX 200 added +0.76% to 8,783.2 — a second straight gain and just shy of a one-month high — but the rally was built almost entirely on index heavyweights. Financials (XFJ +1.56%) and Materials (XMJ +1.52%) did the lifting: WBC +2.70%, NAB +2.26%, MQG +2.05%, ANZ +1.96% alongside BHP +2.45%, FMG +2.87%, RIO +1.60% and BSL +3.51%. Seven of eleven sectors closed red.

Resources: iron ore firmed ~0.7% to ~US$91.20/t on expectations of Chinese mill restocking — enough to lift the majors, but positioning rather than a breakout (iron ore -7.49% over 20days in the 7d feed). Gold miners rode Friday's +1.5% gold futures move (NST +2.50%). The other half of Materials was ugly: lithium and rare earths were sold with no company news — PLS -3.71%, LYC -3.14%, ELV -4.80%, GLN -9.26%, IXR -13.39%, BRE -4.89%. Even ARU fell -3.03% on a binding offtake. That is de-risking of the critical-minerals trade, not a fundamental event.

Banks are trading the higher-for-longer margin story: with the cash rate at 4.60% and two of the Big 4 economists calling another hike on 03/11, NIM support is outweighing credit fears. BOQ (+2%) reports Thursday 15/10 — the first hard read on margins in this cycle — and US big-bank earnings on Tuesday are the cross-check.

Communication Services (XTJ -1.94%) was the worst sector as the global telco sell-off reached Australia: SpaceX's ~US$8bn purchase of an 800MHz spectrum portfolio puts Starlink in direct competition with mobile carriers. TLS -2.52%, TPG -4.90%. Real Estate (XRE -1.38%) and Staples (XSJ -0.56%) bled as money rotated toward cyclicals.

Breadth is the warning: XJO advancers/decliners 98/95 and XAO 189/255 — more stocks fell than rose on a +0.67% All Ords day. ASX 200 new 20day lows (25) still edge highs (24) and T2108 is 32%. Forward catalysts: the after-close MSB annual report, seven unresolved trading halts, US CPI Wednesday and BOQ Thursday.
