📝 Session Summary

The ASX 200 fell 56.0 points (-0.64%) to 8,727.9 and the All Ords lost 0.59% to 8,869.0. The damage came from one place: Financials (XFJ -1.41%) were the worst sector as CBA slid 2.16% to $151.17 into its AGM, NAB fell 1.78%, WBC 1.34% and BOQ 2.99% the day before its full-year result. Five of 11 sectors still rose — but breadth tells the truer story at 51 advancers to 140 decliners.

Resources added the second leg. Materials (XMJ -0.88%, 5day -3.45%) were hit by a gold-miner sell-off — EVN -3.16% after its September quarterly (08:41, price sensitive), GMD -4.33%, PDI -4.60%, NEM -1.95% — with COMEX gold flat near US$4,186 and the AUD firming to 70.03c. A stronger dollar against a flat gold price is a direct margin squeeze in AUD terms; Smart Money shows Gold dropping from rank 24 to 62 in nine sessions. BHP fell 0.69% on news of ~300 Pilbara job cuts and RIO 0.32% after its Q3 production report (08:23). Iron ore at US$90.65 is down ~7% for the month.

Energy and uranium split hard. XEJ rose 0.80% as Brent added 0.6% to US$106.32 on Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia and vessel strikes near Hormuz — KAR +6.15%, TBN +8.33%, WDS +1.28% and STO +1.35% (a 20day high). Inside the same sector Paladin collapsed 9.34% to $7.28 after its September quarterly (08:57, price sensitive), the worst ASX 200 performer and -21.72% over 5day. A ~US$11.8 Brent-WTI spread says the Middle East premium sits in seaborne crude — the benchmark that matters for Australian LNG pricing.

The defensive bid held. Real Estate (XRE +0.95%) led on Goodman +3.55% to a 20day high, and Communication Services (XTJ +0.85%) on Telstra +1.29% and Superloop +5.44%. Austal (+10.13%) was the top ASX 200 stock with no ASX announcement — treat it as flow until a cause is sourced. Smart Money confirms the shift: Multiline Utilities jumped from rank 59 to 2, Property & Casualty Insurance from 63 to 15, Integrated Oil & Gas from 42 to 4.

Regime has deteriorated: XJO is back to Bearish, only 26% of the index is above its 50MA and T2108 sits at 31%. Small caps are oversold (XSO RSI-15 29). Tomorrow's catalysts: BOQ's FY result — the first bank print of the season after today's bank sell-off — the Regis Resources halt, after its 11:50 McPhillamys court win, and Bougainville Copper's half-year lodged after the close.
