📰 Key Headlines

🥇 Gold breaks US$4,000. Bullion fell below US$4,000/oz overnight (first since Nov 2025) on sticky US inflation and a firmer USD — now -8%+ YTD though still +20% on 12 months. ASX gold miners bore the brunt as fixed-cost operating leverage turned a ~6% bullion move into double-digit equity falls.

🏁 FY2026 closes +2.8%. The ASX200 ended the financial year up 2.8% despite today's soft finish — a year of grinding gains rather than a trend, with the index still sitting below its 200MA.

🇺🇸 US records overnight. Dow closed above 52,000 for the first time (Alphabet joined the index, +4%); S&P 500 +1.2%, Nasdaq +2.07%. SCOTUS ruled Fed Governor Lisa Cook stays in post, removing a near-term policy tail risk. VIX eased to 17.50.

⛏️ Corporate deals. Euroz agreed to sell its capital-markets arm to BMO; the ACCC launched legal action against Amazon over alleged unfair Prime advertising and subscription terms.

🛢️ Hormuz risk live. Strait of Hormuz strikes again threaten shipping — a live oil/inflation risk feeding directly into the RBA's reaction function ahead of its 28-29 July meeting.
