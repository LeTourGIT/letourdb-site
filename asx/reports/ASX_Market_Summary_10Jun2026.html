📰 Key Headlines
🏢SDF +36.2% — Dragoneer $7.7B Takeover Bid (M&A)
Dragoneer Investment Group (US PE) launched a $6/share indicative offer for Steadfast Group (SDF), the largest PE insurance deal on the ASX in years. The board unanimously recommends acceptance in the absence of a superior proposal. SDF had fallen ~34% over 12 months to $3.95 pre-halt; the $6 offer represents a 52% premium. Amwins to separately acquire the underwriting agency business. Deal highlights the persistent private equity appetite for ASX insurance infrastructure at compressed public market valuations — a signal for other sector names (SUN, AUB, MPL).
⚠️US-Iran Escalation — Hormuz Tail Risk Elevated
US launched overnight strikes on Iranian targets following the downing of a US helicopter. Iran has signalled further retaliation is possible. WTI paradoxically fell on diplomatic jawboning by Trump ("framework agreement within days") — but Hormuz tail risk affecting ~13M barrels/day of global supply remains unresolved. For Australia, the direct consequences are: (1) energy import cost inflation if Hormuz is blocked; (2) LNG export premium; (3) re-escalation of the RBA fourth-hike debate if oil spikes above $100 sustained. Tonight's US CPI is the overlapping data risk.
⚠️RBA June 16 — Near-Certain Hold at 4.35%
Three consecutive hikes in 2026 (Feb +25bp, Mar +25bp, May +25bp) have taken the cash rate from 3.60% to 4.35%, fully reversing 2025's cuts. April unemployment at 4.5% (up from 4.3%) gives the board a clear rationale for June pause. Consumer confidence fell 3.5% in June — fourth decline this year. Statement language is the key watch: any framing of August as "live" will be the signal for market participants. The 16 June decision at 2:30pm AEST will be the market-moving event of the week for rate-sensitive ASX sectors.
📉Gold Official Bear Market — ASX Gold Complex Under Pressure
Gold is now down >20% from January's record ATH (~$5,380+), entering official bear market territory. The proximate causes: a strong USD (safe-haven demand from Iran escalation), no Fed rate cuts on the horizon, and profit-taking by institutional gold allocators. NST -3.5% today; the Elliott Management activism saga (shareholder pushing for sale process, chair rejecting) adds governance overhang to NST specifically. The XMJ -7.32% 5day return encapsulates the scale of the gold-driven commodity rout. Iron ore at $101.37/t (5day -3.58%) adds to the pressure on the diversified miners.
