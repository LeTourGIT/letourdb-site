Session Summary

Friday 3 July delivered a broad +1.37% XJO advance to 8,844.4, but the internals tell a rotation story, not a uniform melt-up. The session was driven almost entirely by a violent gold repricing: bullion +2.51% to US$4,185 overnight — soft US jobs → lower Fed-hike odds → weaker USD — lit a fuse under every ASX gold name on the board.

Genesis Minerals (GMD) +16.7%, Catalyst Metals (CYL) +13.83%, Northern Star (NST) +11.75%, Chalice (CHN) +11.3%, Capricorn (CMM) +10.13% and Evolution (EVN) +8.83% led — with NST and CMM printing fresh 20day highs. Several names (GMD, VAU, CYL, BOE) also carried operational updates, layering fundamentals onto the bullion beta. Materials (XMJ +2.53%) was the headline sector, but Healthcare (XHJ +2.67%) actually topped it — a classic defensive-plus-gold pairing.

The mirror image was technology. PEXA (PXA) collapsed -21.29% (linked to a Bloomberg report), Objective Corp (OCL) -13.46%, DXN -13.64%, Qoria (QOR) -11.11% and Macquarie Technology (MAQ) -6.03%. The XIJ index printed +0.26% only because a couple of large-caps masked the carnage beneath. This is the ASX faithfully importing Wall Street's overnight AI/semis unwind (SOX -6.7%, Micron -7%, Tesla -7.5%) into its own growth complex.

Iron ore was the notable resource laggard: Fortescue (FMG) -3.16% as iron ore hovers near US$99-100/t under China's lower-grade supply controls — a reminder that "materials up" today meant GOLD, not the bulk iron-ore majors. AUD/USD at 0.6946 (+0.36%) is a mild tailwind for AUD-reported miner margins and not yet at a level that compresses USD revenue meaningfully.

Structurally the tape is healthier than a month ago — XJO back above both its 50MA (+1.34%) and 200MA (+0.73%), breadth clean (Adv/Dec 147/42, 3.5:1). But the XTO-Bullish / XSO-Neutral-Bear split and a 20day trend still Down argue this is a large-cap, defensively-led grind rather than a durable broad-based bull leg.
