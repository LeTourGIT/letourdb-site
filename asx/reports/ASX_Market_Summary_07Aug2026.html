→ This is the week's dominant ASX-specific risk, but the rate itself is a non-event — a hold is fully priced. The signal lives in Governor Bullock's statement language. Since February the RBA has added 75bp to 4.35%, fully reversing 2025's easing; the soft June trimmed-mean (3.6%) removed the immediate hike case that Westpac alone still flagged. A hawkish hold (retaining explicit tightening conditionality) supports the AUD and keeps pressure on rate-sensitive REITs/utilities/consumer lenders (ZIP -3.38% today). A genuinely neutral step-back could reprice the short end and lift the very consumer-discretionary names the Smart Money table is already accumulating. Resources and energy — USD-revenue, oil-levered — sit insulated either way.
