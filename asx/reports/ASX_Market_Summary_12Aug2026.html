Session Summary

The ASX 200 gave back 0.45% to 9,209.4 — the session after the RBA held at 4.35% and the morning Commonwealth Bank delivered a record $10.9b full-year cash profit. The reaction was a textbook sell-the-news: CBA -0.69%, Financials (XFJ -0.59%) the heaviest large-cap drag, and only two of eleven sectors green. The Bullish regime label held, but the tape beneath it was defensive.

Utilities (XUJ +2.64%) was the only genuine bid, carried by AGL +5.95% on its FY26 result and FY27 guidance (lodged 08:26, price-sensitive). With CBA sold and the RBA reaffirming a hawkish bias, capital concentrated in the single sector with a fresh positive catalyst rather than broadening out.

Reporting-season dispersion was the real signal. SEK -14.31% (FY26 miss) and PMV -11.01% (soft Premier Retail update) anchored the losers and dragged a retail cohort (SUL -5.48%, TPW -5.99%) with them, while BVS +12.54% (Appendix 4E beat, new 20day high) and HLI +7.09% rewarded the beats. The market is paying up for results and punishing misses violently — stock selection, not index beta, is driving August returns.

Breadth contradicted the headline regime: XJO decliners led advancers 129 to 69 (0.5:1). The damage is shallow — new 20day highs (30) still beat lows (12) and 61% of XJO names hold their 200MA — but a sub-1:1 advance/decline line on a red day is a non-confirmation worth logging against the Bullish label. Beneath the surface, M&A stayed active: FleetPartners +5.94% on takeover offers, SEG halting then agreeing to buy MediaWorks, and Growthpoint selling $268m of assets.
