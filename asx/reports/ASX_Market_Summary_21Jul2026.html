Session Summary

Tuesday 21 July closed effectively flat — XJO +0.02% to 8,793.3, XAO +0.02% to 8,976.9 — but the flat headline masks a defensive, two-speed session. Only 4 of 11 sectors finished green, and XJO decliners edged advancers 103 to 90. The two leaders (IT +3.26%, Materials +1.33%) simply carry enough index weight to mask a broadly red board.

IT led on data-centre/AI capex demand: NXT +7.74% and CAT +8.36% drove the sector even as US semis faded overnight — the ASX expressing the AI-infrastructure thesis through data centres rather than chips. Materials was carried entirely by precious metals: gold +0.83% and silver +2.11% overnight lit up EVN +5.63%, S32 +6.62%, SBM +5.88% and SLS +5.58%. This is a safe-haven rotation, not a broad resources rally — iron ore slipped a second day (~US$98.6/t on soft Chinese steel margins) and LTR -5.10% shows lithium remains the sold end of mining (-14.79% over 5 days).

The red side told the rates-and-risk story. Financials -0.71% saw platforms and lenders hit — HUB -4.18%, NWL -4.05%, JDO -4.71%, HLI -10.11% — as rising bond yields pressured rate-sensitive models. Healthcare -1.05% was the outright laggard (MSB -4.53%, CU6 -3.88%), and Consumer Discretionary -0.84% (APE -3.82%) reflects caution into the 11 August RBA meeting, where the cash rate sits at 4.35% and Westpac still forecasts hikes to 4.85% while CBA/NAB/ANZ see a hold.

Underneath, the defensive posture is unmistakable: Utilities +0.22%, Energy +0.40% and Real Estate +0.58% show cash parked in must-invest, lower-beta corners while the Hormuz oil premium and RBA overhang cap risk appetite. AUD/USD 0.7022 (+0.36%) is a mild tailwind but too small (<0.5%) to move miner-revenue maths materially.
