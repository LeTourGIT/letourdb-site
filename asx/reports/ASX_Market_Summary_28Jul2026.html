Session Summary

Tuesday 28 July closed the XJO +0.60% to 8,947.8, a firm session that defied a savage tech sell-off across Asia. The leadership tells the story: Consumer Discretionary (XDJ +2.68%) and Comm. Services (XTJ +2.55%) led while Materials (XMJ -1.36%) was the only red sector. This is a rotation out of resources and into cyclicals and defensives — the inverse of the resources-led bounces seen earlier in the quarter.

WEB (Web Travel Group) +17.08% to A$3.29 was the standout, driven by an 08:37 price-sensitive 1H27 guidance upgrade paired with a share buy-back — a fundamental catalyst, not flow, that pulled the entire Leisure & Recreation industry into the Smart Money top four (#4, from #44). VEA (Viva Energy) +8.61% on its own 1H26 trading update lifted Energy (XEJ +1.60%) even as crude softened overnight, a company-specific rather than sector-wide move.

WTC +8.06% rode the global software rebound (SAP, ServiceNow) but couldn't rescue IT (XIJ only +1.20%) because semiconductors were routed: WBT -11.70% and SLX -8.35% imported the Nvidia/memory weakness directly. The day's other clear theme was a defense sell-off — DRO (DroneShield) -13.22% despite a 09:48 price-sensitive announcement of material contracts and a product release, a textbook sell-the-news after a large run (5day -16.8%), with EOS -7.52% sympathising.

Materials was dragged by a gold and junior-mining pullback (SRL -9.20%, LIN -8.88% after an ASX price query, MTM, MEI, VMM) with iron ore soft near $98/T keeping the majors capped. Context is the calendar: this is the heavy late-July quarterly-reporting window — 215 announcements lodged, 170 price-sensitive, overwhelmingly Appendix 4C/5B activity/cashflow reports from small-cap resources and technology. Treat the resources volatility partly as a scheduled event, not a macro signal.

Forward-looking, the Smart Money table is the tell: Homebuilding surged to #1 (from #67 nine days ago) and Insurance Funds to #2 — rate-cut-sensitive domestic cyclicals leading capital rotation into the 10-11 August RBA meeting. Two post-close items to carry forward: MTM's quarterly (16:57) and MC Mining's CEO handover (16:56) land in tomorrow's session, and KKO is in a trading halt pending an announcement.
