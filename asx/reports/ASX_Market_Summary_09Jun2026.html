📝 Session Summary — 09/06/26

Tuesday returned from the long weekend into post-NFP turbulence — but the damage was surgically contained at -0.24%. The surface number conceals a fascinating internal split: XJO Adv/Dec 116:76 (large-cap selectivity in force) vs XAO 193:266 (decliners dominated the broader universe). Institutional capital was hunting specific quality names while the broader market drifted. This is not a healthy market — it is a market where only the best-positioned stocks survive the macro cross-currents.

Pro Medicus (PME) +9.06% to a new 20-day high at $165.79 was the session's defining move. A global healthcare SaaS re-rate — driven by US peers' valuation recovery off Friday's lows — lifted PME through technical resistance. PME at a 20-day high within a Neutral Bear XJO regime is a classic "quality outperformance in a weak market" signal. With a 30% 10day RS gain and 14.77% 10day industry ROC, the institutional positioning in this name is self-reinforcing.

Materials (XMJ -2.32%) bore the heaviest single-sector damage, continuing Friday's commodity rout. The most significant structural event: gold breached its 200-day moving average on Friday for the first time since October 2023, ending a 660-day streak (the 3rd longest on record). This is not recoverable in a day. The gold-to-200MA thesis has driven significant flows into GOR, EMR, NST, WAF over the past two years — that thesis has materially changed.

SRG Global (SRG +2.88%) was the other major institutional accumulation confirmation, hitting a 20-day high after upgrading both FY26 and FY27 earnings guidance. Smart Money #5 in Construction & Engineering (ROC +8.59%, holding since last week) is now validated by the stock's actual price action. DDR (Dicker Data +1.34%) quietly advanced further, cementing Computer Hardware's extraordinary Smart Money #1 position with the highest 10day industry ROC in the dashboard at +25.67%.
