📝 SESSION SUMMARY

Thursday 17 September printed a green headline — the XJO rose +0.41% to 8,732.4, a second straight up-day off Monday's 8,672 low — but the internals told a very different story. Advancers (94) trailed decliners (97): the gain was purely cap-weighted, carried by the banks, while the median ASX 200 stock fell. This is a narrow, low-quality bounce inside an intact Bearish downtrend, not a reversal.

The driver was offshore and structural. The US Federal Reserve's first rate hike in three years to ~4% forced a global re-pricing higher in bond yields, and on the ASX that expressed as a defensive, rate-winner rotation. Financials (XFJ +1.42%) led every sector — banks and insurers are the classic higher-for-longer NIM beneficiaries — with Healthcare (XHJ +0.98%) and Real Estate (XRE +0.66%) the other green pockets. Materials, IT and Energy all fell.

The session's sharpest moves were in the gold miners — to the downside. WAF −7.37%, PNR −7.45%, PDI −6.02%, RMS −4.14% and CMM −3.42% dragged XMJ to a 5day loss of −5.45%, the worst weekly damage on the board. None carried a same-day announcement: this was crowded-trade profit-taking. The irony is that gold names remain the 3-month RS leaders (SBM [H], TCG, ALK, RSG) — the structural uptrend is intact; today was positioning unwinding into the Fed.

At the other end, defence was the standout theme: EOS +7.99% and DRO +6.77% directly confirm Smart Money's number-one-ranked Aerospace & Defense surge. Uranium (BOE +7.54%, VR8 topping the RS table) and med-tech (EIQ +14.56% on an FDA filing update, AYA +10.98%, 4DX +7.34%) rounded out the leadership — a barbell of hard-asset and idiosyncratic-catalyst names standing against a soft, defensive tape.

Net: respect the two-day hold above 8,700, but do not mistake it for a bottom. T2108 at 30.5%, only 38.5% of the XJO above its 200MA, and new 20day lows (29) swamping highs (9) all say the damage runs deep. The 29 September RBA meeting — now carrying materially higher hike odds after the Fed's move — is the circuit-breaker that decides the next leg.
