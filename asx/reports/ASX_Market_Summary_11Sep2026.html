📝 Session Summary

Friday 11 September delivered the ASX 200's fourth straight decline — XJO -0.89% to 8,741.2 — and the cause was macro, not local. Brent crude's surge to a four-month high near US$110, driven by Houthi strikes on Saudi facilities and fresh Middle-East shipping-lane attacks, reignited inflation fears and pushed global bond yields to multi-year highs (US 10-year ~4.90%). Asia sold in sympathy — Nikkei -2.8%, Kospi -2.3% — making this a synchronised, yield-driven risk-off rather than a stock-picking session.

The internal rotation was textbook defensive. Financials (XFJ +1.08%) was the only clearly green sector, and the driver was insurers — IAG +4.23%, SUN +3.65%, QBE +2.86%, AUB +3.33%, CGF +2.92% — whose investment income mechanically improves as bond yields climb. Utilities and Industrials held roughly flat as bond-proxy havens. This is nearly the mirror image of a risk-on tape, where Materials leads and Financials lags.

Everything high-beta was sold. Materials (XMJ -3.63%) was crushed as the entire resources complex de-risked: uranium (DYL -9.87%, PDN -9.59%, BMN -8.24%), lithium (LTR -8.55%) and copper (CSC -8.12%) all fell 8–10% — and copper did so even as LME copper sits near record highs, underlining that this was position-cutting, not a demand signal. IT (XIJ -2.06%) fell as the rising discount rate hit long-duration growth. Only Energy (XEJ, +2.4% over five days) was rewarded for the oil move.

Two moves were genuinely company-specific. Echo IQ (EIQ -9.65%, -60% over five days) tumbled on an FDA rejection — the week's standout blow-up — and GQG Partners (-9.50%) fell on a soft month-end FUM update lodged pre-open. On the upside, Immutep (IMM +27.27%) led the board on a refreshed development strategy for its efti lead asset, and Corporate Travel (CTD +15.49%) rebounded on flow with no same-day ASX filing.

Breadth confirmed the damage: XJO advancers 41 versus 150 decliners (1:3.7), 16 stocks down more than 4% against a single gainer, only 37% above the 200MA, T2108 at 28%, and 70 fresh 20-day lows against just 2 highs. RSI-15 near 30 flags an oversold market primed for a technical bounce — but with US CPI landing tonight and the RBA's 28–29 September meeting ahead into a hardening inflation backdrop, the oil-and-yields impulse remains firmly in control of the tape.
