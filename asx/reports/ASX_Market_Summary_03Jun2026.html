📝Session Summary

Wednesday 3 June delivered a constructive but internally divided session. XJO added +0.70% to 8,785.7 as Energy and Materials led, anchored by the overnight AI chip catalyst (Marvell +32%, HPE +25%) and selective gold strength via Northern Star's resource upgrade. The advance was broad in price but not in participation — 84 advancers versus 104 decliners in XJO confirms the gains were concentrated in specific sectors while the balance of the market consolidated.

The day's macro set-piece was the Q1 GDP print: +0.3% q/q, missing the +0.5% consensus and slowing sharply from Q4 2025's +0.9%. The annual rate of 2.5% also missed 2.7% expectations. Three consecutive RBA hikes are doing exactly what they were designed to do — real GDP per capita fell 0.1% in the quarter. The silver lining was business investment: machinery and equipment surged 16.3% q/q on data centre capex, contributing 0.7 percentage points to GDP alone. The AI infrastructure build-out is now visible at the national accounts level, which validates the NXT and Megaport thesis structurally.

Northern Star (NST) was the institutional standout at +5.3%, following a resource and reserve estimate update that confirmed 88.9 million total resource ounces after mining depletion — an 18.2 million ounce addition that materially extends the mine life and capital return profile. This is the kind of structural de-risking that re-rates a mid-cycle gold miner independently of the spot price, and the Smart Money Gold rotation (rank climbing from #70 to #27 over five days) confirms institutional accumulation was already underway. Acrow (ACF) +9.5% on an Industrial Access FY revenue milestone confirmed the broader infrastructure theme has domestic ASX expression beyond the Hormuz-related global capex narrative.

Megaport (MP1) remains in a trading halt after announcing four AI infrastructure contracts worth A$459M TCV and a A$827.3M fully underwritten equity raise to fund GPU and networking infrastructure. The dilution creates a near-term overhang on XIJ but the contract wins confirm that enterprise demand for AI compute capacity is real and accelerating — the same story NXT has been building for 18 months, now validated by a competitor's contract book. When MP1 relists, expect significant price discovery both ways.
