Session Summary

Monday 25 May delivered a third consecutive session of gains for the ASX, with XJO adding +0.40% to 8,692 on a day US markets were shuttered for Memorial Day. The driver was entirely geopolitical — Trump's Saturday social media post declaring the US-Iran deal "largely negotiated" sent US equity futures surging Monday morning AEST, lifting the ASX cleanly at the open on thin volume. The Hormuz peace trade is now the dominant cross-market macro variable, with oil behaviour the direct transmission mechanism to Australian inflation and RBA rate expectations.

Coal was the session's defining narrative. WHC surged +8.70%, NHC +4.73%, and YAL +7.48% — an extraordinary triple-header in a single session that represents fundamental re-pricing, not momentum chasing. Smart Money Coal rank has surged from #56 to #9 in just nine sessions. The market is pricing a complex optionality thesis: coal's LNG-substitution premium holds whether Hormuz reopens or not, because (a) if the deal closes, oil falls but Asian thermal power demand remains structurally elevated; (b) if the deal collapses, oil stays high and LNG substitution continues driving coal demand. Both scenarios are bullish for Newcastle thermal.

Energy (XEJ -2.41%) was sold aggressively — the largest single-sector decline — as the Hormuz peace trade directly unwinds the oil-price premium embedded in WDS, STO, and Beach Energy. This is the Smart Money rotation confirmed: Integrated Oil & Gas dropped from #4 to #6 in a single session while Coal surged to #9. The pair trade (long coal, short ASX LNG-exposed energy) is active.

The week's critical macro event is Wednesday's April CPI print (27/05, 11:30am AEST). March CPI at 4.6% was the shock that drove the RBA's May hike to 4.35% (8-1 vote). April CPI will capture peak Hormuz fuel costs — transport inflation ran at +8.9% in March. Consensus is approximately 4.2-4.5%. A reading above 4.6% reignites June hike pricing (Westpac still forecasts June + August). A reading at or below 4.0% — aided by any Hormuz moderation visible in April fuel data — gives the RBA cover to hold as CBA projects. XJO is sitting at 50MA (-0.05%): a benign CPI result through Wednesday could produce the first sustained 50MA break and initiate structural repair of the sell-off.
