Session Summary

Tuesday 7 July saw the XJO ease −0.31% to 8,803.9 — a third soft session in four — as a resources rout overwhelmed a genuine bid in Tech and Financials. Decliners beat advancers 122 to 69 on the XJO (0.57:1); breadth was materially weaker than the modest headline decline suggests, a classic index-masking-internals tape.

Materials (XMJ −2.64%) was the wrecking ball. Liontown ASX200 −7.55% led a broad lithium/gold/uranium liquidation — Boss Energy −8.86%, Deep Yellow −6.87%, Galan −6.76%, Wildcat −6.73%. The driver is macro, not stock-specific: the hawkish Fed pivot has repriced the USD sharply higher, and a strong USD compresses every USD-denominated commodity at once. Iron ore near US$97/t (Simandou ramp + soft China steel) removes the one offset the complex had.

The other side of the rotation was orderly and institutional. Info Tech (XIJ +2.01%) led every sector — WiseTech ASX50 +5.65% (even as chair Richard White stepped down but stayed on), NextDC +3.6% and CAR Group +2.99% — the hyperscaler/cloud-capex thesis intact. Financials (XFJ +1.25%) firmed with Netwealth +6.73%; a higher-for-longer curve modestly improves bank NIMs. This is textbook new-financial-year rotation out of the resource complex into growth and quality.

Under the surface, Smart Money is decisively domestic-defensive: Homebuilding surged to #1 (from #61 nine sessions ago) and Insurance Funds to #3 (from #77) — capital rotating toward rate-and-consumer plays insulated from the USD/commodity axis. Healthcare (XHJ +5.2% over 5day) remains the standout medium-term sector even after a flat session.

AUD at 0.6945 (−0.21%) near multi-month lows is the tell: this is fundamentally a USD-strength story, and Australia sits on the wrong side of it for its biggest export earners. The real test is whether Tech and Financials leadership can keep offsetting the resource drag — and whether XSO’s Neutral Bear print is a warning the large-cap index has yet to heed.
