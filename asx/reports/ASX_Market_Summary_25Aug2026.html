📝 Session Summary

Tuesday 25/08/2026 saw the XJO add +0.68% to 9164.6 on a genuinely broad tape — advancers beat decliners 134/53 (2.5:1), T2108 at 61%, and 42 new 20-day highs against just 15 lows. Yet the index level buries the real story: this was the sharpest single-day earnings dispersion of the FY26 season so far, with the biggest winners and losers sitting inside the same sectors.

EOS (Electro Optic Systems) +23.02% was the standout. Its half-year report and revenue update (lodged 08:25, price-sensitive) re-rated the defence thesis in one session. ARB +13.87% and ANN +8.16% (a 5-year high) confirmed the market is paying up hard for genuine FY26 beats. Against them, Industrials also produced MND -11.96% (Monadelphous, margin disappointment) — so XNJ closed +0.58% masking a ~35-point spread between its best and worst constituent.

The precious-metals complex was the day's leadership engine. Gold's push to a 3-month high (spot ~US$4,651) on USD softness carried VAU, GMD [H] and EVN, and lifted Smart Money Gold to rank 4 and Uranium to rank 3 (BOE +4.12%). Lithium was the mirror image: LTR -8.68%, PLS -6.03% (Bell Potter flagged the stock 'may have peaked' after its result) and ELV -5.82% — a clean rotation out of lithium and into gold/uranium within Materials, netting XMJ to just +0.30%.

Energy (XEJ -0.78%) was the only red major sector as Brent (~-1.35% to ~US$93) and WTI (-1.6%) gave back gains on profit-taking ahead of the US Iran-sanctions plan. With XJO 2.45% above its 50MA and above every key average, the structural backdrop is firmly constructive; the risk here is idiosyncratic single-name earnings shocks, not macro, while the reporting calendar stays this heavy (WES, WOW, HVN, LOV all due this week).
