📝 Session Summary
Tuesday 19 May snapped the prior session's geopolitical panic with a +1.17% XJO recovery to 8,604.7 — but the catalyst was a postponement, not a resolution. Trump's Truth Social announcement that he was calling off a scheduled Tuesday military strike on Iran, after direct appeals from Qatar, Saudi Arabia and UAE, triggered an immediate repricing of Hormuz escalation risk. WTI fell from $112+ toward the $107–108 zone intraday; Brent eased below $110. For the ASX, that transmission is direct: every dollar off oil narrows the inflation argument for another RBA hike in June, and rate-sensitive sectors traded accordingly.
Consumer Staples (XSJ +3.00%) led the market — not a typical leadership sector for genuine risk-on rallies, which flags this as a defensive rotation with a dovish rate overlay rather than growth optimism. Woolworths surged +4.28% on a US bank upgrade to $37 price target, a mean-reversion trade after WOW's painful 6.9% drop in late April following a weak Q3 update. Metcash +2.23% also benefited. Comm Services (XTJ +2.66%) and Healthcare (XHJ +1.87%) followed — both beneficiaries of lower rate expectations. Financials (XFJ +1.71%) confirmed the rate read: banks bouncing as June hike probability deflated, with CBA +1.12% to $162.67.
The one sector that refused to participate tells the real story: Materials (XMJ -0.07%) flat-to-red for the fourth consecutive session, accumulating a ~7.5% pullback from its recent high. BHP and RIO are not being sold on China demand concerns (iron ore held near $110) — they are being sold as the 30% rally from March lows that over-extended. The XSJ leading while XMJ drags is the structural inversion from the 1 May session, when LTR's Kathleen Valley approval had XMJ +2.09% as the vanguard. The rotating leadership signals a market searching for its next directional catalyst rather than confirming a trend.
The XAO regime flip to Neutral Bear is today's most analytically significant data point. When the XAO was Neutral Bull and XJO Neutral Bear (a divergence that existed briefly in late April and early May), there was a case for medium-term MA structure holding. That cushion is now gone. Three of four major indices are aligned Neutral Bear. XSO's persistence in Neutral Bull is the only outlier — and with XSO at -7.55% below its 200MA, the regime label flatters the underlying damage. The June RBA meeting on 16 June is the next circuit-breaker event in either direction.
