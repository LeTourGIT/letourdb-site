📝 Session Summary

Monday 07/09/2026 looked quiet — XJO +0.06% to 9010.9, XAO +0.05% to 9200.6 — but the internals were narrower. Decliners beat advancers on the 200 (85/106), All Ords (180/251) and 100 (32/64); only the Small Ords were near even (70/71), and new 20-day lows outran highs on the XJO (13 vs 6). This was a mega-cap-propped flat tape, not a broad advance.

Energy (XEJ +1.78%) led, and the driver is offshore: Brent has pushed to ~$97 (+11% on the month) as the US and Iran trade strikes on tankers and Tehran threatens a restricted maritime zone beyond the Strait of Hormuz. That risk premium lifted the ASX energy complex — Whitehaven (WHC +7.03%, ASX100, 20-day high) and Tamboran (TBN +9.09%) on its first Beetaloo gas sales — and, unhelpfully for the RBA, it is exactly the imported-inflation channel the Board has flagged three weeks out from its 29 September decision.

Underneath, this was resources-and-agri-up, tech-retail-property-down. The speculative resource tier ran on catalysts — Iondrive (ION +29.41%) on a US$243m NPV upgrade, plus gold explorers (SLS, BNZ, LRV). Bubs (BUB +30.00%) was the standout, its permanent US FDA infant-formula approval a genuine fundamental re-rating; Ingenia (INA +14.79%, ASX200) jumped on rejecting a takeover approach. The other side: Info Tech (XIJ -2.60%, 5-day -6.37%) was the worst sector, with retail (SUL, MYR) and property (Peet -7.37%) soft as the Bathla $3.4b developer collapse kept housing-sector stress in the headlines.

Gold is the quiet structural story. The gold price eased (~$4,477, -0.82%), yet gold miners own relative-strength leadership across both tiers — spec (ION, NMG, BNZ, AUC) and institutional (VAU, SBM, RSG, GMD, ALK). Miners holding RS leadership on a down-price day says the pullback is being bought, not sold.
