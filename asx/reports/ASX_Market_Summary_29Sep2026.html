Session Summary

RBA decision day delivered exactly what was priced — a 25bp hike to 4.60%, the fourth of 2026 and a 15-year high — and the ASX 200 still finished +0.34% at 8,709.3, its second straight gain. The path mattered more than the close: the index chopped either side of flat into 2:30pm, dipped red on the statement's hawkish language, then caught a late bid once Governor Bullock confirmed the Board only weighed hold vs +25bp. Classic sell-the-rumour, buy-the-fact on a fully-priced event.

This was a stock-specific session, not a macro one. Information Technology (XIJ +4.61%) was the day's standout, almost entirely on two upgrades: Codan (CDA +23.93% to a record $64.43) lifted its FY27 outlook on surging demand for its unmanned-systems communications gear with H1 NPAT set to more than double, and Megaport (MP1 +9.44%) landed three AI-infrastructure contracts worth ~$979m TCV and raised FY27 revenue guidance to $720-810m. Treat the XIJ print with care — the sector is still -9.72% over 20 days and -43% over 1 year; one day of earnings-led squeeze does not repair that.

Materials (XMJ +1.02%) supplied the institutional ballast: BHP +1.39%, RIO +0.82%, S32 +2.87% and Sunrise Energy Metals (SRL +9.19%, no filing — critical-minerals flow). Perenti (PRN +8.84%) added A$200m of drilling and underground contracts. Gold miners were mixed (RMS +3.49%, PNR +6.32% on its annual Resource & Reserve statement, but NST -0.77%, EVN -0.30%) after bullion fell ~4% on Monday on the US yield spike.

The losing side is where the rate message lives. Utilities (XUJ -0.82%) and Energy (XEJ -0.72%, lowest since mid-August per Reuters) were the worst sectors; KAR (-6.75%) extended Monday's production-guidance collapse; and five REITs sit among the 18 weakest names on the movers sheet. Financials (XFJ -0.13%) looks benign only because QBE (+1.21%) and MQG (+0.86%) offset CBA (-0.83%).

Breadth improved but did not confirm. XJO advancers beat decliners 115/74 (1.55:1) after 85/111 on Monday, yet new 20-day lows (44) still outnumber highs (9) almost five-to-one, only 38.5% of XJO stocks sit above their 200MA and T2108 is 26.5%. That is an oversold, washed-out tape attempting to base — not a trend change. The level that matters: hold the 8,665 floor through tomorrow's August CPI and the range survives; lose it and the Bearish regime gets a fresh leg lower.
