📝 Session Summary

The ASX 200 added 0.46% to 8,850.1, but this was a one-engine rally. Materials (XMJ +2.78%) did all the lifting -- BHP +4.26%, RIO +2.53%, S32 +2.58%, MIN +2.09%, NST +2.09%, with rare earths (LYC +3.46%, ILU +3.75%) and uranium (PDN +6.69%) joining in -- while six of eleven sectors closed red. Iron ore was flat at US$90.60/t (-0.11%) and the AUD unchanged at 0.7025, so this was not a commodity-price move: it was capital rotating out of rate-sensitive sectors and into USD earners the day before a CPI print that could seal a fifth RBA hike. No Section 2b announcement explains BHP's move -- it is flow, not news.

Structurally the day mattered more than the headline number. XJO reclaimed both its 50MA (+0.10%) and 200MA (+0.23%) -- it was below both yesterday -- and the 10MA>20MA flag is Up, so short-term momentum is repairing even though the Excel regime still prints 'Neutral Bear' (the MA stack has not fully re-ordered). But breadth refused to confirm: XJO Adv/Dec was a flat 99/94, only 33% of the index sits above its 200MA, and the gain was cap-weighted rather than broad. A BHP-led reclaim of the 200MA on 1.05:1 breadth is a fragile reclaim.

The size split is stark. XTO (ASX 100) sits +1.13% above its 200MA with RSI 58 and a 5day trend that flipped Up today; XSO (Small Ords) is in a full 'Bearish' regime, -7.29% below its 50MA and -11.21% below its 200MA with RSI 36. Small caps did bounce harder (+1.11%, Adv/Dec 100/47) and XSO new 20day lows collapsed from 31 to 8 -- an oversold relief move, not a trend change. When large caps lead and small caps sit in Bearish structure, the market is defensive at its core.

Financials (XFJ -0.59%) and Comm. Services (XTJ -0.80%) were the drags: NAB -1.73%, BOQ -2.84%, WBC -0.96%, REA -3.28%. The banks had run +2.5% over 5days; trimming them ahead of CPI and WBC's 02/11 result is risk management, not a call on credit. Consumer Discretionary was hit by a stock-specific shock -- AOV -10.46% on a revised FY27 outlook (08:24, PS) dragged auto-aftermarket peers APE -4.69% and ARB -3.79% -- a crack in the market's strongest 60day industry (Auto Parts, 60day ROC +16.01%).

Forward look: Q3 CPI at 11:30am Wednesday is the swing factor for every rate-sensitive sector, then the Fed Wednesday night US time, then RBA on 03/11. NOX remains in a trading halt (08:57) -- resolution is a live catalyst. Post-close filings from PFM (4C) and DGR (Takeovers Panel orders) are not yet in the price. The September-quarter reporting window is in full swing: 86 quarterly filings today, 56 of them from Materials.
