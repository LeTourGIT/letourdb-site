📝 Session Summary

The ASX 200 surged +2.61% to 9,065.1 — its best session since June and its highest close since September — as Wall Street's blue-wave rally landed in Sydney. This was a resources-and-tech day: Materials (XMJ +7.55%) delivered the institutional weight while IT (XIJ +10.09%) delivered the fireworks. Gold miners led the resources leg after spot gold jumped +3.6% overnight on a weaker US dollar — CYL +18.34%, PNR +18.48%, EVN +14.21%, RRL +13.18%, NST +9.92% — and the bulks followed with FMG +9.16%, RIO +6.98% and BHP +6.19% as iron ore and copper bounced. MIN +11.19%, LTR +11.67% and PLS +8.85% put lithium back in the leadership group.

NextDC (NXT +50.84% to $9.85) is the session's single biggest story and it is corporate, not macro: a trust owned equally by Goodman (GMG +7.03%) and Digital Realty bought up to 9.9% at $9.50 — a 45.5% premium — in an after-market raid, and NXT's 09:59 price-sensitive response called it opportunistic. The stock had lost ~40% in late October after pausing developments and cutting revenue forecasts; closing ABOVE the $9.50 raid price tells you the market is pricing a control premium, not just the stake. XRO +12.55%, REA +11.20% (Q1 FY27 numbers), 360 +9.69% and WTC +7.48% turned it into a sector-wide tech re-rating.

The losers are a clean macro map. Energy (XEJ -4.76%) was the only sector to lose more than 1.6%: Brent's ~9% collapse hit WDS -6.02% (Capital Markets Day 08:59 offered no offset), STO -5.41%, VEA -4.63% and the coal names. Financials (XFJ -0.29%) were dragged by NAB -5.37% on its FY26 result — following WBC's soft reception on 02/11, with ANZ due Monday 09/11. Two days after the RBA's fifth 2026 hike to 4.85% (18-year high), bank margins are not the free lunch the bulls assumed. APE -13.22% (09:44 trading update) and the David Jones administration are a warning that rate pain is now reaching discretionary retail.

Breadth confirms the move without qualification: XJO advancers 168 vs 29 decliners (5.8:1), 65 stocks up 4%+ vs 6 down 4%+, 79 new 20day highs vs 7 lows, and 79% of the index above its 20MA. That is a thrust day. The question for Friday is follow-through: US futures are already softening (NQ -0.37%), MQG reports tomorrow (06/11), and the AI-oversight risk that hit CoreWeave overnight is the one headwind NXT's re-rating has not priced.
