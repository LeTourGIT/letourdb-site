📝 SESSION SUMMARY

The ASX 200 closed +0.92% at 8,789.3 — a third straight gain off the 25/09 low of 8,665.0 — on the day after the RBA lifted the cash rate to 4.60%. The catalyst was not the hike (priced) but August CPI: headline 4.0% landed below the 4.1% consensus and the trimmed mean rose just 0.2% m/m. Swaps cut November hike odds to roughly 20-25%, and equities immediately repriced toward a terminal-rate narrative. The ASX 200 VIX fell 5.6% to ~10.3, a one-month low.

Leadership was the textbook inverse of the hiking cycle. Real Estate (XRE +3.68%) led every sector with LLC +11.30%, CHC +6.43%, GMG +3.86% and SCG +2.64%; Consumer Discretionary (XDJ +2.27%) followed on WES +3.04% and DMP +6.32%; and REA +5.85% carried Communications (XTJ +1.94%). Financials (XFJ +0.27%) lagged with the Big 4 essentially flat — a lower terminal rate caps the NIM upside the market had been paying for, and banks are now passing hikes to borrowers.

Resources did not lead (XMJ +0.67%, 5day -1.97%). NST +6.35% [ASX20] rode gold's rebound (spot +1.62% on 29/09), RIO +1.56% and FMG +1.04% firmed as China's factory PMI snapped a two-month contraction, but BHP (+0.31%) barely moved. Lithium sold the news: LTR (-2.11%, 5day -11.43%) lodged its Kathleen Valley Expansion FID pre-open (08:17, price sensitive) and still closed lower. The uranium complex was liquidated with no company news — BOE -11.52%, CXU -12.86%, AGE -10.0%, LOT -8.33%, DYL -5.26%, and RS leader VR8 -11.59% — a positioning unwind, not a fundamental event.

Breadth confirmed the day but not the trend. XJO Adv/Dec was 144/53 (2.7:1) and new 20day highs (23) overtook 20day lows (14) — a sharp reversal from 9/44 yesterday. But all four indices carry a Bearish regime, XJO sits 1.98% below its 50MA and 0.41% below its 200MA, T2108 is 31.5% and only 40.5% of XJO stocks hold their 200MA. On the XAO, 20day lows (46) still outnumber highs (40). XSO's 5day trend stayed Down (+0.70% today vs XTO +0.93%): small caps are lagging a large-cap-led relief rally.

September closes -3.16% (9,076 → 8,789.3), the first monthly loss in six, though the quarter scrapes out +0.12%. The level that matters is the 200MA near 8,825: a close above it would be the first piece of regime repair; failure there turns this into a bear-market rally into the 50MA (~8,967). External risks cluster in the next 72 hours — US core PCE, ISM and Friday payrolls with the US 30-year at 2002 highs. Post-close, DGR lodged Takeovers Panel interim orders (16:38, price sensitive) — tomorrow's catalyst.
