Session Summary

A -0.07% close on the XJO (9,146.8) is the least informative number of the session. Underneath, the market made a sharp rotation. Financials (XFJ +1.10%) absorbed a -2.08% washout in Materials (XMJ). CBA (+2.89%) and WBC (+1.93%) did most of the lifting as gold, lithium and iron-ore names were sold at the same time. The index recovered ~42 points from its midday low. The breadth did not: XJO decliners outnumbered advancers 116 to 75, and across the XAO the count of stocks down 4% or more jumped from 5 to 47.

Resources took the hit on three fronts with no company-specific news. Gold miners fell (EVN -4.21%, NEM -2.80%, NST -2.24%) after spot gold dropped 1.08% on 11/11 and futures slipped another 0.29% today. Lithium was hit hardest: PLS -8.06%, ELV -8.35%, LTR -6.99%, MIN -5.23%, IGO -5.12%, with no filing behind any of them. That is sector liquidation, not news. Iron-ore majors followed (RIO -2.14%, FMG -2.40%, BHP -1.29%) on talk of Chinese steel curbs, with ore at US$92.15. The AUD has fallen 2.26% over five days, which should cushion AUD-denominated miner revenue by roughly 1% at the 0.7%-per-cent rule. It did not help. When USD strength and lower commodity prices arrive together, the FX cushion loses.

Results day split by quality of guidance. Xero (+6.08%) raised its outlook and announced an A$1bn buyback, but gave back half of an intraday +12.7% once Nvidia's after-hours slide hit the AI-adjacent bid around lunch. Orica (+4.47%) beat on its highest earnings in 15 years and was the only large-cap materials name to rally. Aristocrat (-1.32%) combined an in-line FY26 with a CEO transition and was sold. GrainCorp (-0.57%) did the same with its own CEO succession. Wagners (-5.01%) was punished on its FY27 guidance, while Inghams (+2.24%) printed a new 20day high on its trading update.

The speculative tape produced two real catalysts. G8 Education (+90.00%) entered a process deed with Majority Capital, putting a scheme on the table, and Personal Services went straight to rank 1 in Smart Money. Clarity (CU6 +23.49%) won the first FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation in PSMA PET. Sell-the-news hit the explorers: Brightstar (-7.43%) on a 305m @ 2.66g/t intercept and Minerals 260 (-8.52%) on its works approval. In this tape, good drill results are being used as exit liquidity.

Forward look. Paladin resumes trading after its A$525M raise at A$7.80, a 10.3% discount; uranium peers (DYL -7.39%, BOE -9.39%) have already marked themselves down. MPK, AT4 and M79 remain in halt, each a pending catalyst. Motio's first court hearing result (16:12) landed after the close. Overnight the Nvidia board decision is the key event, with NQ futures already -0.38%. TNE and CAT report 17/11 and IFT 18/11. The XJO/XSO regime split is the line to watch: if small caps keep leaking, the large-cap bid needs banks to hold, and banks are already 3.3% higher over five days (CBA).
