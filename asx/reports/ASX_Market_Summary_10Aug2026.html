📝 Session Summary

The ASX 200 eased 0.33% to 9,232.6, snapping back from Friday's record but doing so in an orderly, single-sector fashion. The entire index decline is attributable to Financials (XFJ -2.27%), the sole materially red sector, and within it to Westpac -5.88% after a soft 3Q26 update flagging declining home-loan applications -- landing 24 hours before the RBA and amplified by the ABC's parallel "end of the low-rate era" framing. IAG -3.24%, Judo -3.43% and FleetPartners' peers dragged the sector broadly lower.

Underneath that headline, the tape was risk-on. Rare earths and critical minerals were the day's engine: SRL +16.07% on a US$400m US Office of Strategic Capital conditional loan -- the ASX expression of the US "strategic-minerals" investment wave -- with LIN +18.28% (consolidating 100% of the SARECO MREC), ARU +7.69% and a cluster of juniors (MTM, MEK, GL1, AT4). Materials (XMJ +1.22%, 5day +8.54%) and Precious Metals rotating hard into Smart Money confirm this is a broad complex re-rate, not a single-stock spike.

M&A was the second theme: Tabcorp lobbed a scheme bid for BetMakers (BET +33.33%), oOh!media signed a scheme with I Squared, and FleetPartners (+12.10%) drew rival non-binding indicative offers from SG Fleet and Element. Healthcare (XHJ +1.45%) leaned on AVH +31.82% and PNV +8.00%. The session's worst single stock was ARF -21.95% on disappointing Arena REIT FY26 results, a REIT-specific shock that fed the rate-sensitive weakness.

Breadth stayed constructive despite the red index: XJO advancers led 108/81 (1.33:1), new 20day highs (57) swamped lows (6), and T2108 held a healthy 74.9%. The regime is intact and Bullish; the pullback is event-positioning, not a trend break. The real test is tomorrow's SMP tone and whether small-caps can finally reclaim their 200MA.
