📝 SESSION SUMMARY

Resources led the market lower, and the commodity tape does not explain it. XMJ fell -2.03% (6.09% down over 20 days) while iron ore actually firmed +1.1% to ~US$92.15/t intraday. BHP (-2.02%), RIO (-2.86%) and FMG (-1.37%) were sold on positioning and discount rate, not on price. Fortescue's 09:04 September-quarter update (shipments down ~6%) added a company-specific drag, but the breadth of the selling was the story. Lithium (PLS -4.64%, MIN -4.39%, ELV -4.04% despite its LG Energy Solution offtake) and gold (EVN -2.21%, NST -1.40%) were hit just as hard. When a sector falls on a day its key commodity rises, the market is de-grossing, not repricing.

The catalyst is global rates. The Fed minutes leaned hawkish, the US 30-year hit a 24-year high, and AU's own 30-year printed a record 5.89% on Wednesday. That is a direct tax on capital-intensive producers and long-duration growth. It explains why the worst performers were the high-beta, cash-burning or richly valued names: WBT -11.52% (the ASX 200's worst), LOV -9.15%, EOS -8.16% and MP1 -8.02%. None of them lodged an announcement. These were exits, not news.

The rotation was textbook defensive. Staples (+1.42%), Energy (+1.36%) and Utilities (+1.34%) led, and six of eleven sectors rose, yet the index lost 67 points. Materials and Financials carry the weight. Banks extended Wednesday's slide (CBA -1.50%, WBC -1.55%), while the new-20day-high list reads like a bunker roster: QBE, RMD, FPH, ASX, MTS, BGA, SDF. Smart Money confirms it, with seven industries surging 30+ ranks inside nine days, all in consumer staples, utilities, insurance, oil and domestic consumer services.

At the stock level, Maas Group (MGH -22.38%) was the institutional casualty. The 12:53 response to an ASX price query followed reports that Firmus Technologies may cut the price of its 23/10 float, in which MGH holds 3.2%. Combined with Wednesday's -5.7%, MGH has lost 28.6% in five sessions; the AI-infrastructure premium is coming out of anything adjacent to data centres. At the other end, Alvo Minerals (ALV +210%) re-rated after a strategic placement and Bluebush drilling results lodged at 10:07/10:08, a [Spec] story on a $18M company.

Forward look: the RBA does not meet until 03/11 (cash rate 4.60% after the 29/09 hike), with ~76% priced for a hold and minutes due next week. Watch four trading-halt resolutions (CPM, BM1, AM3, OMA), BOQ's result on 15/10, and Firmus IPO pricing ahead of 23/10 as the MGH swing factor. The only post-close price-sensitive item is CBA's CBAPJ hybrid suspension (17:28), which is technical, not earnings-related. With ES/NQ soft and 43 new 20day lows in the XJO, expect any Friday bounce to be sold until breadth stops deteriorating.
