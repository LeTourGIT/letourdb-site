📝 Session Summary

Thursday 16 July 2026 closed effectively flat — XJO 8,840.7 (+-0.00%), XAO 9,036.9 (+0.03%) — but the flat headline masks a sharp sector rotation. This was a resources-led pullback: Materials (XMJ -1.58%) and Energy (XEJ -1.53%) were the only meaningfully red sectors and dragged the index back from earlier strength.

The offset came from a clean rotation into domestic financials and consumer names. AMP +9.83% (ASX100, new 20day high) led the market on a record FUM ($14.5bn); REA +6.61% and Life360 +5.03% carried consumer/tech-media; Lovisa +4.93% and Tabcorp +5.20% rounded out the domestic-discretionary bid. Banks rose 0.2-1.0% with Macquarie at a record — a mirror of the US, where financials (BlackRock +7.3%) printed records overnight. Capital is rotating out of USD-commodity cyclicals into domestic earners.

The resources damage was broad, not a single name: AIC Mines -17.79% (production/cost miss), EQ Resources -7.55%, Wildcat -5.75%, Elevra Lithium -4.65% and Firefly -4.62% — lithium, tungsten and copper-gold juniors all sold. Iron ore sitting sub-$100 ($98.88, delayed) removes the recent BHP/RIO tailwind even as BHP posted record iron ore output and faces a historic WA worker strike. The XSO Bearish flip is the same story at index level — the speculative resources tail is where the selling concentrated.

Beneath it all sits the semiconductor read-through: the overnight US chip rout (Micron -8%) hit ASX tech's soft underbelly — Weebit Nano -6.46%, Audinate -6.81%, and XIJ's 5day at -2.51%. Financials-led + resources-sold + chips-fragile is a defensive-rotation tape dressed up as a flat close.
