📝 Session Summary

The ASX 200 recovered from a soft open to close +0.47% at 9,019.3, with the All Ords up a matching +0.45%. Crucially, this was a rate-sensitive, defensive-led advance — not a resources rally. Utilities (XUJ +2.10%) topped the board, followed by Industrials (+1.36%), Consumer Discretionary (+1.10%) and Consumer Staples (+1.03%). That ordering is a peak-rate signal: with the RBA decision now a week away and the majors unanimous on a hold, capital rotated into the sectors that benefit most from a rate ceiling.

Materials was the dog that didn't bark. Despite an enormous Diggers & Dealers announcement flow — dozens of gold and copper drilling results and presentations — XMJ managed only +0.30% and is flat over five days. The reason is at the big-cap end: FMG -3.84% and CIA -2.93% on soft iron ore (~US$98/t, near a six-month low) dragged the index while the exploration excitement stayed trapped in the Bearish small-cap tail.

The single biggest move on the board was corporate, not macro: FPR +17.31% after FleetPartners received a non-binding indicative takeover offer from SG Fleet — with a parallel Austral/Hammer Metals bid and a Steadfast consortium update confirming that M&A premia are alive across financials and mid-cap resources. Energy (XEJ -1.19%) was the only real red, consolidating after a ~20% monthly crude rally rather than signalling any demand deterioration.
