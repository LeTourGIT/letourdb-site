📝 Session Summary

The ASX 200 added 24.2 points (+0.27%) to 9,153.2 — a third straight gain, the highest close since 25/08, and now 1.3% below the 06/08 record close of 9,271.6. It did so against a hot-CPI retreat on Wall Street: the index opened -0.3% at 9,103, was flat by midday and finished near its highs.

Leadership was idiosyncratic and domestic. IT (XIJ +1.52%) topped the board on Life360's +10.05% rebound after Tuesday's -15.3% post-Q3 rout, and Utilities (XUJ +1.30%) rode a Smart Money surge in Electric Utilities (rank 76 → 5 in nine sessions). Ten of eleven sectors closed green; Financials (XFJ -0.35%) were the lone loser as CBA faded its Q1 trading update (-1.21%) and GQG collapsed (-19.59%).

Corporate action defined the stock tape. IDP Education (+16.29%) confirmed a revised Blackstone approach at $3.00 cash (09:38, PS) — 20% above the $2.50 proposal rejected in September — yet closed at $2.57, a 14% discount to the offer. That spread prices real completion risk on a non-binding proposal for a business whose student volumes fell ~25% in 2026. GQG's October FUM update (11:02, PS) was the mirror image: FUM down ~12%, outflows nearly tripled and ~US$11bn lost to investment performance after the July tech pivot — the stock hit 2020 lows.

Materials (XMJ +0.77%, +10.31% over 5day) split along commodity lines. Lithium was bid without a single company filing to explain it — PLS +6.53%, ELV +9.70%, CXO +8.00%, VUL +5.74% — which reads as positioning flow into the battery complex. Gold miners went the other way (BC8 -5.04%, BNZ -4.02%, SX2 -3.33%, OBM -3.05%), catching up to bullion's -2.59% slide on Tuesday's US CPI. Iron ore stays parked near US$92.5 — the majors were not the story.

Under the surface the rally narrowed: XJO advancers fell from 173:22 on Tuesday to 123:66, and new 20day highs from 63 to 45. That is digestion after a +3.6% 15day run, not distribution — not one XJO stock fell more than 4% and new lows held at 2. Tomorrow brings Aristocrat, Xero, Orica and GrainCorp results [EARNINGS], plus the open's verdict on VAU's post-close scheme vote result.
