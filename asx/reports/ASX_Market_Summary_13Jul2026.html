Session Summary

A flat headline, a negative tape. XJO closed +0.03% at 8,808.5 and XAO −0.01%, but internals were decisively soft: XJO advancers trailed decliners 74/118 (0.63:1), the All Ords worse at 156/292. This was an index held aloft by a few large-caps, not a broad advance — and the XTO (above all MAs) versus XSO (Neutral Bear, −7.45% below 200MA) regime split confirms the speculative tail is bleeding.

Energy was the story under the surface. With US–Iran strikes rocking the Strait of Hormuz, oil stayed bid and ASX energy ran — Ampol (ALD) +4.17%, Viva (VEA) +3.56%, Stanmore coal (SMR) +4.68%. The Smart Money table confirms a genuine multi-day rotation: Oil & Gas Refining vaulted to #1 (from #52 nine days ago), with Integrated and E&P oil both top-8, and Energy was the best 5day sector (+4.10%).

The offset was a gold-miner rout. Spot gold fell (−1.29% on the delayed feed) and the ASX gold complex was dumped: Kingsgate (KCN) −12.83% led the whole market lower, joined by St Barbara (SBM) −5.32%, Forrestania (FRS) −5.0% and Viridis (VMM) −7.42%. Gold cut both ways, though — Dateline (DTR) +33.33% and Genesis (GMD) +3.70% bucked it on stock catalysts. Materials (XMJ −0.65%) wore the damage; iron ore itself was quiet at $98.72 (+0.15%, delayed).

Tech was the worst sector (XIJ −2.49%), dragged by a semiconductor unwind — Weebit (WBT) −10.6%, Silex (SLX) −7.89% — mirroring US chip profit-taking even as broad US indices closed green. Utilities (−1.63%) and Staples (−0.91%) fell on rate/defensive rotation. Financials (+0.67%, best 5day +3.0%) firmed ahead of US bank earnings week. AUD slipped to 0.6935 (−0.28%), a mild tailwind for USD miners.
