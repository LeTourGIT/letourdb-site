US markets ripped on Monday: Nasdaq Composite +2.13%, S&P 500 +1.48% (record area) and the Dow +1.32% to a fresh record, after President Trump canceled planned strikes on Iran and revived negotiations -- sending oil down ~5% and collapsing the inflation-risk premium. ⚠️ RBA Watch — Meeting in 7 days

MEETING10-11 August 2026 (decision Tue 11 Aug, 2:30pm AEST)
CASH RATE4.35% · 3 hikes in 2026 (Feb / Mar / one prior), held at 4.35% in June
CONSENSUSHOLD -- CBA, NAB, ANZ and Westpac now all forecast no change
INFLATIONJune-quarter CPI 3.8% headline / ~3.6% trimmed mean -- still above the 2-3% band
RISK BALANCEFinder survey: 55% of economists still see at least one further hike in 2026; Bullock frames June as 'a pause, not a pivot' and remains 'vigilant on upside inflation risks'

The 11 August meeting is 7 days out and, unlike earlier in the year, the consensus has firmly converged on a hold at 4.35% -- all four majors now agree following the softer June-quarter CPI. That shift is doing real work under today's tape: rate-sensitive Real Estate (+1.41%), Financials (+1.93%) and the Homebuilding/Insurance surge in Smart Money all reflect a market pricing the top of the cycle. The residual risk is upside: trimmed-mean inflation at 3.6% is still above band and a minority of economists keep a hike live. Insulated on a hawkish surprise would be USD-revenue resources and energy; most exposed are REITs, consumer discretionary and utilities.
