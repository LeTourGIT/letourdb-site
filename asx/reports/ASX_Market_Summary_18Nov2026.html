Session Summary

The ASX 200 fell 0.69% to 8,821.8, its fifth straight loss and about 3.6% below the 9,147 close of 12/11. It closed 0.19% under its 200day MA and just 0.04% above its 50day MA. That band, 50MA ≈ 8,819 to 200MA ≈ 8,839, is where a pullback either holds or becomes a trend change. With the 50MA already below the 200MA, the burden of proof is on the bulls.

The -0.69% headline understates the damage. XJO breadth was 34 advancers to 155 decliners (0.22:1). Across the All Ords there were 72 new 20day lows against 16 highs. The Small Ords lost 1.48% and now sit 10.48% below their 200MA. Meanwhile XTO (top 100) still shows T2108 62% versus 41% for the All Ords. Money is hiding in the largest names while the rest of the market is sold. That profile fits a correction still in progress, not one that is finishing.

The overnight lead set the tone: the Dow -1.64%, the S&P 500 at its lowest close since July, VIX ~25.5. IT (XIJ -2.31%) was the worst sector as the US software 'AI disruption' sell-off hit TNE (-6.12%), XRO (-3.81%) and TUA (-6.46%). Materials (XMJ -1.08%) extended a brutal week: the sector is -10.36% over 5day, with BHP -10.02%, FMG -12.52% and RIO -8.55% over the same span. Copper fell 1.83% to US$5.89 and iron ore sits at US$90.62. Gold miners took the worst of it today: OBM -9.66%, PDI -7.05%, BNZ -6.80%, EVN -5.32%. Only Energy (+0.46%) and Utilities (+0.12%) closed green.

At the stock level the market sold good news. TEA fell 9.49% on the day it upgraded FY27 guidance (15:12). CTT dropped 12.70% after reporting 'enhanced profitability' (09:16). EML fell 25% on its Q1 FY27 update (10:01). Only hard M&A paid: NXL +45.28% on a process deed with Haveli Investments and GG8 +43.40% on the Cosmos acquisition and A$60m placement. When upgrades get sold, holders are raising cash, not re-rating fundamentals. That is the behaviour of a de-risking market, and it usually needs a forced-selling flush before it stops.

Forward look: no post-close announcements were lodged, so tomorrow's stock catalysts are scheduled ones. NUF and AAC report on 19/11 and SHV on 20/11. The LIO trading halt (12:16) resolves within two sessions, and the NXL scheme terms are the M&A follow-through to watch. The macro tests are whether US futures hold flat through the US cash session after Nvidia, and whether XJO can defend ~8,819. The RBA (4.85%, next decision 08/12) sits behind everything, and today's firmer wage growth gives the hawks no reason to stand down.
