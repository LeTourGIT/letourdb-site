📝 Session Summary

The ASX 200 slipped -0.33% to 8,816.9, a modest headline that hides a busy session underneath. Futures pointed 77 points lower at the open and the index was down as much as 1.1% intraday before defensives clawed most of it back. Wall Street's tech bounce was irrelevant: the tape was set by three local catalysts — NAB's restructuring, BHP's copper miss and an energy give-back — and the close left the XJO back below its 200day MA, one session after reclaiming it.

Financials (XFJ -0.48%) took the morning hit. NAB (-1.75%) announced up to 2,500 job cuts (~6% of staff) by mid-2027 with a ~$300m pre-tax restructuring charge ahead of its 5 November full-year result. Markets read a bank cutting costs this hard into a hiking cycle as a margin-defence signal, not a growth one. ANZ and WBC (-0.79%) followed, while CBA (+0.14%) held — the market still pays up for the highest-quality bank when credit risk rises (unemployment is at its highest since 2021 and mortgage arrears are climbing).

Materials (XMJ -1.16%) and Energy (XEJ -1.31%) were the anchors. BHP (-1.59%) reported September-quarter copper down 19% to 400.7kt — a miss against ~423.6kt consensus after Escondida was suspended following a fatal accident and Carrapateena lost a conveyor. Iron ore output beat (71.9Mt), but the realised price fell 9%, to US$76.27/wmt. The nugget: Escondida's outage is a global supply hit, which is why COMEX copper is up (+0.46%) on the same news that hurt BHP — volume loss outweighed price for the producer. Gold (BNZ -13.20%, PDI -5.12%), rare earths (SRL, MI6, BRE) and coal (SMR, YAL) all de-risked without announcements, and refiners (ALD -6.22%) gave back Monday's energy surge.

The offset was a clean defensive rotation. Consumer Staples (XSJ +1.14%) and Healthcare (XHJ +0.87%) led — COL +1.85%, COH +5.17%, CSL +1.16% — with staples at their highest since April 2023. Smart Money confirms the move is structural, not one-day: Insurance Funds, Life & Health Insurance and Natural Gas Utilities are all SURGE moves, and the 20day-highs list reads like a defensive watchlist (COL, WOW, EDV, WES, IAG, CGF, CSL). This is the market positioning for an RBA hike in November with the cash rate already at 4.60%.

Breadth is the warning. XJO advancers/decliners were 69/115 and 20day lows (33) overtook 20day highs (32) after a 43/17 split on Monday; across the All Ords decliners beat advancers 281/159. The Small Ords (Bearish, RSI-15 32.34, -11.0% vs 200MA) are where the damage is concentrated. With September CPI next week and the November RBA meeting behind it, the path of least resistance for small caps and rate-sensitives remains down until inflation surprises lower. Watch tomorrow: NextDC's halt resolution, LNQ's post-close drill result, and Deputy Governor Hauser at 8:45am.
