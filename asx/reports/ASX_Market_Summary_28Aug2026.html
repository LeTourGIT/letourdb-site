Session Summary

Friday 28 August delivered a classic reporting-season split tape. The large-cap indices finished green — XJO +0.60% to 9,092.3, XAO +0.55%, XTO +0.68% — while the Small Ordinaries fell 0.37%. Beneath the headline gain the market was not broad: XAO decliners (224) edged advancers (220), the Small Ords ran 53 up against 90 down, and new 20day lows outnumbered new highs on all four indices. Index strength was carried by a handful of large-cap winners rather than genuine breadth.

FY26 full-year results are the entire story, and single-name dispersion was extreme. Dicker Data (DDR +20.66%) surged on its H1 result and FY26 guidance update; St Barbara (SBM +20.30%) leapt on FY26 numbers. At the other end, PEXA (PXA −17.43%), Saluda Medical (SLD −24.41%), Cobram Estate (CBO −14.85%) and WAM Capital (WAM −18.54%) were punished on their releases, and Broken Hill Mines (BHM −10.06%) fell on a A$90m underwritten capital raising — dilution, not operations. Every one of these moves ties to a same-day lodgement: the market is repricing fundamentals stock-by-stock, not trading macro.

The relative-strength board carries the cleaner structural signal, and it is wall-to-wall gold. The ASX200/300 RS leaders are almost entirely gold miners — SBM, VAU, GMD, RRL, RSG, PRU, WGX — with bullion near US$4,654/oz. This is a broad institutional re-rating of Australian gold producers, not a single-stock spike, and it is the most durable theme on the tape. Information Technology (XIJ +2.31%) led sectors on the overnight AI read-through, with WTC and NXT the ASX vehicles.

Real Estate (XRE −0.86%) was the weakest sector — the clean expression of rate-hike anxiety, with the major banks now openly tipping an RBA hike ahead of the 28–29 September meeting (a month out, so no dedicated RBA Watch this session). Layered on top, ASIC’s warning of the “first significant cracks” in Australian private credit, and a major private-credit fund suspending redemptions, add a specific financial-plumbing risk to monitor into the back half of reporting season.
