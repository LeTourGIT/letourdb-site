Session Summary

Wednesday 29 July was a CPI-driven relief rally. The June print landed at 3.8% headline (down from 4.0%), and — the number that mattered — trimmed mean 3.6% undershot both the 3.7% consensus and the RBA's own 3.8% May projection. The read-through is clean: an August hike is off the table, the cash rate holds 4.35% into the 10-11 August 2026 meeting, and the entire rate-sensitive complex re-rated in a single session. XJO closed +1.01% at 9,038.6.

Healthcare (XHJ +4.24%) led every sector, powered by CSL +7.15% on its enhanced plasma manufacturing update — the first hard operational catalyst for the blue-chip in months — which dragged HLS +7.14% and EBO +5.06% with it. But the more revealing leadership was consumer-discretionary: TPW +8.36%, LOV +7.88%, APE +5.27%, DMP +5.28%, NCK +4.81% and REIT SGP +4.96%, a textbook lower-for-longer basket.

The Smart Money table confirms the theme with force: Homebuilding is entrenched at #1, and Insurance Funds, Auto Parts, Leisure, Home Furnishings and Appliances all surged double-digit ranks over the week. RIO +3.67% on its 2026 half-year results gave Materials (XMJ +1.12%) institutional ballast, though iron ore near US$99/t on softening Chinese steel demand caps the bulk-price upside.

The clear losers were the AI/semiconductor complex: MP1 −8.40% and WBT −8.33% (Weebit's ReRAM) are the local expression of the global chip washout that took the Kospi down 10.8% overnight. LTR −7.92% on an underwhelming June quarterly, and DVP −17.56% — which drew an ASX price-and-volume query post-close — were the standout single-name blow-ups.

Structure check: this is a large-cap rally. XJO/XAO/XTO are Bullish with XTO overbought, yet XSO remains Bearish, 7.95% below its 200MA. Only 45% of XJO names sit above their 200MA, and quarterly −25% movers (32) still outnumber +25% movers (27). The dovish CPI is a genuine tailwind, but breadth beneath the mega-caps has not yet confirmed.
